Claremont School Board, August 18, 2021

Regular meeting two weeks before school opened. The board approved the elementary and middle school handbooks, held the Stevens High School handbook for a transcript section, adopted an amended 2021-22 COVID-19 decision-making matrix by roll call (four yes, one abstention, one no), debated a $20,000 vaccination-incentive motion that was never voted, and approved a list of staff nominations.
