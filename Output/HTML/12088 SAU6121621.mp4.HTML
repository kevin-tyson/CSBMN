SAU 6 Board, December 16, 2021

Regular meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity board of School Administrative Unit 6, with one member attending virtually. After a brief nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(a), the board adopted a revised SAU budget of about $1,795,945 (up 1.34%), debated how the SAU's 3% merit pool had been paid without a written evaluation process, and voted to extend the superintendent's contract by five years on terms still to be agreed. The superintendent opened by explaining why Claremont schools would be closed the next day. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

HIGH A member attended virtually, but the budget, the contract extension and every other vote except the nonpublic motions were taken by voice

Rob Lovett Jr. answered "Present. Virtually." (0:03:07). RSA 91-A:2, III(e) then requires: "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote." The board used roll calls to enter and leave the nonpublic session (0:09:41, 0:11:23), but took voice votes on the agenda (0:03:35), the recess (0:13:19), the SAU budget (1:13:32), the five-year superintendent contract extension (1:15:06) and adjournment (1:15:59). No count was announced for the budget or the contract, so neither the public nor the minutes could show how each member voted. Paragraph III(a) also requires the reason the member could not attend in person to be stated in the minutes, which are missing (flag 3). In mitigation: a physical quorum was present, the chair checked that Lovett was still connected (1:13:25), and no opposing voice is heard on any of these votes. The flag is HIGH because III(e) is mandatory and the two most consequential votes of the night were taken without it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(a), (e) (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1); NHPR, June 10, 2021 (state of emergency ends June 11, 2021; reporting)

MEDIUM SAU staff received merit payments and above-budget raises decided by administrators, without the evaluation process the board had required

RSA 194-C:5, III provides that the SAU board "shall fix the salaries of all school administrative unit personnel". On this recording the administrators describe salary decisions the board did not make: increases above budget for three positions after employees said they were underpaid (0:54:56), totalling "about $8,500" by Sprague's count (0:54:13); and merit payments "over the last two years" (0:57:47) for which "There hasn't been a written evaluation piece" (0:57:39), where "we made the judgments on it. Everyone received the same amount." (0:59:35). The superintendent says "We didn't even know we had a merit pool until we were reaping the budget" (0:59:14), and the assistant superintendent for finance that the evaluation tool adopted years earlier was one "we didn't know about" (0:58:12). Mitigations on the record: the board itself budgeted the merit pool, and the assistant superintendent for finance says it now carries "the specific direction and vote from the board that nothing would be paid out of it without the implementation and presentation of the evaluation process" (0:53:05). Whether the earlier payments were within amounts the board had fixed cannot be told without the SAU's salary resolutions and payroll records, none of which is public; graded MEDIUM as a control weakness.

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, III (SAU board fixes SAU salaries; 1996, 298:3, unamended)
