Claremont School Board, October 2, 2024

Regular board meeting, 46 minutes long, with the superintendent absent. The board approved a two-year tuition agreement to take Unity's high school students at Stevens High School, filling the draft's blank tuition rate by a floor amendment of $16,000; heard the Stevens summer credit-recovery results (136 students invited, 128 of 234 credits recovered); and agreed by consent that its NHSBA delegate would follow the association's recommendations on the 2024 resolutions, with the chair's opposition to two of them noted. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on October 2, 2024, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

MEDIUM A tuition contract approved with its rate left blank, filled in by a floor amendment that conflicts with the contract's own escalator

Exhibit A, the draft Unity tuition agreement posted with the agenda, carries a DRAFT watermark and reads at ¶4(a): "Claremont shall charge Unity a base tuition rate of________. The base tuition shall be adjusted each year based upon the percentage increase in the Claremont School District's approved operating budget." Sprague moved acceptance, then said "There's no amount." (0:03:20). The figure came from memory of a meeting about a year earlier (0:03:20), and the amendment fixed it as "16,000 per year for the duration of the agreement" (0:04:12), which on its face displaces the annual adjustment; the motion does not say whether $16,000 is per student or in total. The exhibit has further defects that went unmentioned: its term runs from July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026, so it was approved three months into its own term; ¶4 has no subparagraph (b); the numbering runs 1 to 16, then 27, 21 and 22; and ¶2 sends approval to "the NH Department of Education" while ¶22 says the State Board of Education "pursuant to RSA194:22". RSA 194:22 makes the state board the approving body. The vote itself was properly moved, seconded and recorded. Graded MEDIUM as a control weakness in a multi-year financial commitment, not as a violation of a stated rule.

Sources: Exhibit A, Unity-Claremont Tuition Agreement (draft), ¶¶2, 4(a), 22; RSA 194:22 (tuition contracts; approval by the state board); approved minutes 10.2.24, item III.1

MEDIUM The Policy subcommittee met the day of this meeting and planned another; no notice or minutes of either were found

Skillen: "We had a meeting today to prepare some policies to bring to the next meeting, and we're going to have another meeting next week on Wednesday" (0:34:43). A subcommittee of a school board is a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which ends "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", so each meeting needed posted notice and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. The board's agenda lists dates only for Finance subcommittee meetings, and no Policy subcommittee notice or minutes for October 2024 were found in this meeting's packet, the October 16 packet, or a Drive title search for the period; a search negative is not proof that none exists. Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH because the record mitigates it: the subcommittee's work was reported in public session and its policies came to the full board.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (subcommittees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, II (2024 codification; notice and minutes)

OBSERVATION The draft minutes were dated within the five-day window but reached the public share two business days later

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting", which made October 9, 2024 the deadline. Drive dates show when the public could read the minutes online, not whether a copy was available at the SAU office, so this is recorded as an observation.
