MEDIUM The business office's own schedule put Claremont's FY2020 audit 27 months and its FY2021 audit 17 months after year-end

Asked for an update, the assistant superintendent for finance said the Unity and SAU audits would be done within weeks and work was starting so that “the Claremont one” would be done “by the end of September” (0:36:33); “Those are the 2020s.” (0:36:58); FY2021 by November and FY2022 by February (0:37:01). On that schedule Claremont's FY2020 audit (year ended June 30, 2020) would finish about 27 months after year-end and FY2021 about 17 months after. For an entity spending $750,000 or more in federal awards, the Uniform Guidance due date is the earlier of 30 days after the auditor's report or nine months after year-end (March 31, 2021 and March 31, 2022), with OMB M-21-20 allowing six more months for fiscal years ending through June 30, 2021. Claremont's federal expenditures for those years are not stated in this record, so the single-audit deadline is not confirmed to apply; the flag stays MEDIUM. Mitigation on the record: the schedule was being met, FY2022 was planned within nine months, and two state grant-compliance findings had been cleared (0:37:21).

Sources: 2 CFR 200.501(a), 2022 CFR edition ($750,000 threshold); 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1), 2022 CFR edition (earlier of 30 days or nine months); OMB M-21-20, App. 3 sec. IX (six-month delay through June 30, 2021 year-ends)

OBSERVATION The weapons row of the high school discipline matrix, as read aloud, should be checked against the mandatory firearm expulsion

RSA 193:13, IV provides that a pupil who brings or possesses a firearm in a safe school zone without written authorization “shall be expelled from school by the local school board for a period of not less than 12 months,” and ¶VII requires the board to adopt a policy allowing the superintendent to modify that requirement case by case. As read aloud by a board member, the matrix's weapons row lists “up to ten days of out of school suspension a possible expulsion hearing” with mediation or a student panel as options (0:12:47). The administration said the law governs serious offenses and the restorative option applies only on re-entry (0:08:04), and the column was relabeled at the meeting (0:14:06). The handbook itself is not in the packet folder, so this page cannot say whether the matrix distinguishes firearms from other weapons or cites the statutory expulsion. The point is noted for a reader with the handbook in hand.

Sources: RSA 193:13, I(a), IV and VII, 2022 codification (10-day suspension; firearm expulsion of not less than 12 months; case-by-case modification policy; source note ends 2020, 38:1)
