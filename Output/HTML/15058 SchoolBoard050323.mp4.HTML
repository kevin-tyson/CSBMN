Claremont School Board, May 3, 2023

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, six of seven members present, and the board's first use of a consent agenda. The board approves an American Sign Language course with a full-time instructor over one dissent, hears the special education director's annual report (identification rate "a record high for Claremont at 26%"), agrees to be listed as a host of the NH School Funding Fairness Project's May 25 presentation, and hears the single candidate for a school-board seat on the CCTV board. The packet's special education report carries an appendix naming individual students; this page reproduces none of it (flag 1). HIGH The public packet's special education report lists individual students by full name and grade under program headings that indicate a disability category

The packet document Special Education -Director’s Report- Claremont School District 5.3.2023.pdf, created in the district's public packet folder on April 26, 2023, ends with an appendix (labelled Appendix E) that lists students by full name and grade in columns headed by self-contained program and by categories such as behavior and cognitive delay. A name with a program placement and disability basis is personally identifiable information from education records: the FERPA regulations define PII to include "The student's name;" and require signed parental consent before disclosure except as 34 CFR 99.31 allows, and the IDEA confidentiality rule requires parental consent before PII is disclosed outside participating agencies (regulations, 2022 editions). None of the 99.31 exceptions reaches publication in a public board packet. The file was still readable in the district's packet folder when this page was built on 2026-10-03. Nothing on the recording refers to the appendix; the board discussed only aggregate figures. This page reproduces no name, grade or count from that appendix, and readers should not seek it out; the district can remove or redact the file.

Sources: 34 CFR 99.3 (education records; personally identifiable information), 2022 edition; 34 CFR 99.30(a) (written consent before disclosure); 20 U.S.C. §1232g(b)(1) (no federal funds for a policy or practice of releasing education records); 34 CFR 300.622(a) (IDEA: parental consent before disclosure), 2022 edition
