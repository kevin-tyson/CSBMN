Claremont School Board, November 20, 2024

Regular meeting, six of seven members present. The board accepted a consent agenda that included minutes of the unrecorded November 14 special meeting, went into a brief nonpublic session, heard the superintendent's report and an update on the three instructional coaches, and ratified the teachers' (SREA) contract with 2.5% cost-of-living increases in each of two years, without counsel present and without the contract in the public packet. MEDIUM The teachers' contract was ratified without being posted, without its cost stated, and before counsel arrived

The agenda lists "SREA Contract Ratification (Discussion and Vote) (Exhibit A)". The packet folder for this meeting holds six documents and no Exhibit A, so the public could not read the agreement the board ratified; a member said "I read it this afternoon" (0:52:18). The board's by-law 4.03(d) assigns posting "online any and all agenda materials not-exempt from public dissemination by RSA 91-A in conjunction with the posting of any board or committee agendas". On tape the terms are given in outline (2.5% cost-of-living increases in each of two years, higher extra-duty and mentor pay, paraprofessional raises, 0:45:09 to 0:46:45), but no dollar cost is stated for either year. Under RSA 273-A:3, II(b) "Only cost items shall be submitted to the legislative body" for approval, so the voters, not the board, would fund the cost items; the public record of the board's vote still carries no figure for them. The board chose to ratify before counsel, listed as presenter, could join (0:51:27 to 0:52:27). Graded MEDIUM as a process gap: the ratification itself is within the board's authority, and the approved minutes record mover, seconder and the main terms.

Sources: Agenda, item IV.2; Packet folder 8. CSB 11.20.24 (six documents); Claremont School Board By-Laws, 4.03(d) (adopted June 5, 2024); RSA 273-A:3, II(b) (cost items to the legislative body)

MEDIUM The nonpublic motion named no exemption, and the unsealed nonpublic minutes have not been found

RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires a motion to enter nonpublic session to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II", and the board's own by-laws (Appendix D) say "It is vital that a specific reference to one of these grounds be made on the record". The motion as spoken was "RSA nine one. Dash a colon three." (0:11:36), with no paragraph or letter. The chair's lead-in had said item a (0:11:16) and the agenda printed the text of II(a), which mitigates it; the approved minutes then record the motion as "RSA 91-A:3(a)", a citation without the paragraph that holds the exemptions. The roll call required by I(b) was taken (0:11:45). The minutes add "No need to seal the minutes", so RSA 91-A:3, III required those minutes to be public within 72 hours; the by-laws' Appendix D repeats that duty. No nonpublic minutes for this date have been found: the Meeting Minutes share's Unsealed Minutes folder was created January 13, 2025 and lists only 2025, 2026 and 2027 subfolders (checked October 3, 2026). The district website as it stood in 2024 cannot be inspected, so this is "not found", not "never released".

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(a), III; By-Laws, Appendix D; Approved 11.20.24 minutes, item IV.1

OBSERVATION The consent agenda approved minutes by the wrong date and was corrected 49 minutes later without a vote

The posted agenda listed "Minutes Approval- 10.2.24 & 11.14.24"; the October 2 minutes had already been approved on October 16. The consent agenda was accepted "Seeing no opposition" (0:01:48), and only at 0:50:24 did the assistant superintendent note the date should be 10.16, because "we have to submit it as evidence" (0:51:01). The correction was "noted and changed" with no new vote, and the approved minutes fold it into the consent item as though it were made at the time. The chair also said the agenda "was, modified multiple times, so the number did not get there" (0:37:08), and the instrument disposal listed as "(Discussion/Vote)" was settled by consent. The by-laws' rule 2.10 describes consent items as moved "en masse, by consent"; no rule found requires a fresh vote for a clerical date correction, so this is an observation about the reliability of the record.

Sources: Agenda, item II.a; Approved 11.20.24 minutes, item II.a; By-Laws, 2.10

OBSERVATION The board approved, without comment, minutes of an unrecorded November 14 meeting that misletter its nonpublic exemption

The consent agenda approved draft CSB Meeting Minutes 11.14.24.pdf, a Claremont School Board meeting called to order at 8:16 p.m. on November 14, after the SAU 6 meeting in the same building. Five members were present; the meeting's only business was a nonpublic session from 8:19 to 8:59 p.m. The motion is recorded as "RSA 91-A: 3, II (a & d)", but the minutes then print the second ground as "(b) Consideration of the acquisition, sale, or lease of real or personal property", which is the text of II(d) under the wrong letter, cut before its qualifying clause ("which, if discussed in public, would likely benefit a party or parties"). The minutes record no vote to seal, so RSA 91-A:3, III required the nonpublic minutes to be public within 72 hours; none has been found. No recording of that meeting exists in the Cablecast gallery as checked by this project in August 2026, and MAP.md has no section for it. The draft itself was posted November 20, within five business days.

OBSERVATION The contract was ratified knowing it names a health plan whose cost had just risen 15%

Crawford raised the 15% increase reported at the previous day's Finance Committee session and asked whether the contract's language could accommodate a change of carrier (0:48:13). The approved minutes record the business administrator noting the contract "does specifically say SchoolCare". The administration's answer was that the union might agree to reopen it if a change saved its members money (0:48:54, 0:49:54). That is a stated risk, accepted on the record, that the FY26 budget would carry; no rule is engaged.

Sources: Approved 11.20.24 minutes, item IV.2; Finance Committee, November 19, 2024

POSITIVE School-by-school attendance, truancy and discipline data published in the board packet every month
