HIGH No default budget figure was disclosed at the budget hearing

Claremont votes by official ballot under RSA 40:13. In the text in force in January 2017, RSA 40:13, XI(a) read in full: "The default budget shall be disclosed at the first budget hearing held pursuant to RSA 32:5 or RSA 197:6." This was the budget hearing the superintendent had scheduled for January 11 as the required public hearing (December 21 page). On the recording the default budget is discussed only in the abstract: it “would be” lower than a budget with an $850,000 increase (0:05:42), and after the reductions the superintendent says “the fall budget to be announced” (1:21:19), which in context reads as default budget. No figure is stated by anyone at any point in the hearing. If the default amount appeared on a slide or handout that the recording does not capture, the statute may have been met; the slides do not survive (flag 3). Graded HIGH because the requirement is mandatory, the hearing is the one the statute names, and the only statement on the record says the figure was still to come.

Sources: RSA 40:13, XI(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 190:1-3, 8-10, eff. Sept. 9, 2014; re-read 2026-10-02)

MEDIUM Nonpublic session entered for personnel and its minutes sealed without a stated finding

The chair asked for “a motion for nonpublic 91 a for personnel” (2:17:29). RSA 91-A:3, I(b) required that "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II." Personnel is not one of the paragraph II grounds; II(a) (dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline of an employee), II(b) (hiring) and II(c) (reputation) are distinct, and the motion names none. After the session the board voted by roll call to seal the minutes (2:19:15 to 2:19:43). RSA 91-A:3, III allows nonpublic minutes to be withheld only "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session" that determines that divulging them would likely affect a person's reputation, render the proposed action ineffective, or concern terrorism. The roll call was recorded, but no determination was stated. Both are documentation gaps; neither shows what was discussed.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(a)-(c), III (2016 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017)

POSITIVE The hearing met its statutory window and gave the public time, answers and a second turn
