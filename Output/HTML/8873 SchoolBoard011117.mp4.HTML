Claremont School Board — January 11, 2017

Public hearing on the fiscal year 2018 school budget, followed by a short regular board meeting with a nonpublic session, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. The administration presented about $850,000 in recommended reductions; most of the public testimony defended the Owls alternative program and the Stevens High School physical education position that the recommendations touched. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

MEDIUM The default budget amount was not stated at the budget hearing; the superintendent called it "to be announced"

Claremont adopts its budget by official ballot, with a deliberative session on February 8. In an official-ballot district the default budget had to be disclosed at the first budget hearing: RSA 40:13, XI(a), as it read in January 2017, says "The default budget shall be disclosed at the first budget hearing held pursuant to RSA 32:5 or RSA 197:6." At 0:05:42 the superintendent said only that there "would be a default budget lower then the budget being proposed", and in his closing snapshot at 1:21:19 the transcript reads "the fall budget to be announced. But I can tell you it's going to be less than article two." (fall is most likely the ASR's rendering of "default"). No default budget figure is read aloud anywhere on the recording.

This is graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH for three reasons: the PowerPoint and handouts are not available and may have carried a figure the speaker did not read; this page cannot confirm that January 11 was the district's first budget hearing, although the December 21 and January 4 recordings both describe the January 11 session as the public hearing; and the statute's disclosure duty may have been met in a posted document this project cannot see.

Sources: RSA 40:13, XI(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, eff. Sept. 9, 2014, so the text in force in January 2017): default budget disclosed at the first budget hearing.

MEDIUM Nonpublic session entered "for personnel" with no statutory exemption named, and its minutes sealed with no stated reason

At 2:17:29 the chair asked for "a motion for nonpublic 91 a for personnel." In January 2017 RSA 91-A:3, I(b) required that "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II." Personnel is not one of the paragraph II grounds; the employee-specific grounds are II(a) (dismissal, promotion, compensation, discipline) and II(b) (hiring), and II(c) covers reputation. The roll call was taken, which the same paragraph requires, and all audible answers were yes.

On return, the board voted by roll call to seal the minutes (2:19:15). RSA 91-A:3, III allowed nonpublic minutes to be withheld beyond 72 hours only if, "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session", the body determined that disclosure would likely affect adversely the reputation of a person other than a member, render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism. The vote itself appears to meet the two-thirds requirement (seven members, no audible no), but no determination is stated on the recording. Whether the minutes recite one cannot be checked, because they are not online.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(a)-(c) and III (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017, so the text in force on this date).

OBSERVATION The board's Saturday, January 7 budget work session shaped these figures but has no recording or minutes available online

Five members met on Saturday, January 7 to go through the budget line by line (Sprague at 2:22:17: "five of us were here Saturday"; Irish at 1:09:51: "67 pages front and back, 1300 plus lines"). Five of seven is a quorum, so under RSA 91-A:2, I that session was a meeting, with the notice and minutes duties of RSA 91-A:2, II. The superintendent folded "some of the work that the school board accomplished on January 7th" into tonight's recommendations (0:14:40), and Irish put the board's resulting reduction total, $897,254, on the record at 2:09:02. The session was set up in public on January 4 (see the January 4 page), where posting it was discussed.

No Cablecast recording of January 7 is listed in MAP.md, and the district's online shares begin in July 2022, so neither its minutes nor its notice can be inspected from here. Under the project's missing-records rule this is graded as an observation about online availability: the absence is explained by the archive's start date, not shown to be a failure to keep minutes.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I and II (2016 codification; source note ends 2016, 29:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017, so the text in force in 2017): meeting of a quorum; notice and minutes.

POSITIVE The hearing met the statutory calendar, and the board heard every speaker, answered questions on the spot and offered second turns

RSA 32:5, I (as in force in 2017) required the budget hearing "not later than 25 days before" the meeting; January 11 is 28 days before the February 8 deliberative session. RSA 40:13, II-a required official-ballot budget hearings "on or before the third Tuesday in January", which in 2017 was January 17. The seven days' notice the statute also requires cannot be checked from here.

The hearing format itself went beyond what the law required. The chair set neutral rules (1:24:33), promised a second turn to anyone who wanted one, and let principals, teachers and the business administrator answer speakers' questions directly. Eleven people spoke from the floor, including an Owls teacher and four former Owls students; one traveled about an hour. The superintendent had declined to give the press details before this hearing "So the community and the board can hear it" at once (0:26:19). A statutory public comment period for school boards (RSA 189:74) did not exist until 2022.
