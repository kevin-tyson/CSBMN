Claremont School Board, May 5, 2021

Regular meeting at which the board adopted the 2021-26 strategic plan and, after a long presentation by Stevens High School's principal and guidance chair, voted to separate competency reporting from course grades and return to letter grades (no D grades, a "CR" credit-recovery mark, a 70 cutoff). Also heard: a student COVID-19 vaccination clinic, summer credit recovery, prom and graduation plans, and a push to bring elementary realignment forward. Built from the Claremont Community Television (CCTV) / Claremont Community Media Center (CCMC) recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. The presentation laid out a specific new scale: letter grades from A plus to C minus, "Notice no D's.", "a 70 as a cutoff" and a "CR for credit recovery" worth zero (0:40:48, 0:41:23). The superintendent then told the board, "We're not talking about the the exact grading format, just the separation of the competencies from from the grade" (1:08:08), and "It's conceptual." (1:17:34). The motion as made was "to accept the proposed new grading system, which separates the competency based evaluation from" a numerical or letter grade (1:35:52, 1:36:07), and the assistant superintendent said it would apply to "all schools" (1:17:33) from next year (1:18:27, 1:18:28). A reader of the recording cannot tell whether the board approved the separation only, leaving the scale for later, or the scale as presented; minutes would normally settle that. No statute or rule examined for this page requires public input on a grading change, so this is an observation about the record's clarity, not a finding.
