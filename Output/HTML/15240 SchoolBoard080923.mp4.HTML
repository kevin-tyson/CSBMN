Claremont School Board, August 9, 2023

A single-item meeting, held at the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center and called a special meeting by the chair, on Special Education Director Ben Nester's proposal to restart an alternative high-school program. After about forty minutes of public comment the board voted 4 to 3 (minutes) to house the program at the technical center for one year, to be re-evaluated by January 31, 2024, while the special education director, relaying a state CTE official, and the vice chair described non-CTE use of the building as out of compliance. MEDIUM The board placed a non-CTE program in the technical center while its officials described that use as out of compliance and before the legal opinion the chair had requested arrived

RSA 188-E:3, II, as codified in 2023, provides that a regional CTE facility built or renovated with state grants "shall become the property of the school district or public academy, for use by the career and technical education center exclusively" (statute). Miles read that sentence aloud (1:14:36). Nester relayed a state CTE official's view that a non-CTE program would be "a finding of noncompliance" with no listed sanction (1:16:40) and that it would block state funding of a future renovation, a roughly 75 percent share by his account (0:09:23); Nester and Sprague both said the building was already out of compliance (1:16:24, 1:16:37). The chair told the meeting she had asked board counsel for "an official legal opinion" and that it could not be expedited (1:43:13); the board voted 4 to 3 the same night (minutes). Mitigation on the record: the stay is limited to one year with a re-evaluation by January 31, 2024 (1:07:20); the program uses rooms Koski described as empty (1:49:47). Whether the SRVRTC building was built or renovated under this section is not documented in this meeting's record, though every speaker treated it as covered. The chair also named the district preschool and CCTV as existing non-CTE tenants (1:09:34), and Hawkins's request to review them was left for a future agenda. The flag is MEDIUM rather than HIGH because the statute states a use restriction with no sanction, the state's view reaches the page only second-hand, and counsel's opinion was still pending.

Sources: RSA 188-E:3, II (2023 codification; source note ends 2021, 210:2, eff. Oct. 9, 2021); minutes, item III.1
