Claremont School Board, April 17, 2024

MEDIUM Board members were still signing the MS-22 thirty-six days after the March 12 vote

RSA 21-J:34, II requires the governing body's report certifying the appropriations voted to be "filed within 20 days of the close of the meeting" (statute). The district's official-ballot vote was held March 12, 2024, so twenty days ran to April 1. This agenda carries "Member signing of the MS22" under Other Business, and the chair defers it to after adjournment (1:35:48); the minutes repeat the agenda line. Signatures gathered on April 17 point to a certification completed after the statutory period. Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH because the record does not show when the Department of Revenue Administration received the form or whether an earlier submission was made; the DRA filing record would settle it.

Sources: RSA 21-J:34, II (2023 codification; source note ends 2007, 182:2, eff. April 1, 2007); agenda, item VII

OBSERVATION A summer practice of hiring first and ratifying in August, built into the board's own calendar

RSA 189:39 provides that "Superintendents shall nominate and school boards elect all teachers employed in the schools" (statute). Sprague describes a past practice under which the board "authorized the superintendent to to hire without necessarily without board approval" so that "He's able to tender contracts, offer people contracts before the fact", with ratification later; Crawford: "The ratification comes in August." (1:18:32 to 1:19:25). The pre-populated agenda in the packet schedules "Ratification of nomination of professional staff" at the second August meeting. Nothing was decided tonight, and Sprague undertook to ask counsel. Whether a contract tendered before the board's vote complies with RSA 189:39 depends on the terms of the delegation and the ratification vote, neither of which is in this record, so no breach is asserted.

Sources: RSA 189:39 (1971, 371:2, never amended); Exhibit A, Pre-Populated Agenda, August

POSITIVE A public hearing on a gift below the statutory threshold, and the superintendent's report posted for the public

Under RSA 198:20-b, III(a) as amended in 2023, a school board must hold a prior public hearing only "For unanticipated funds in the amount of $20,000 or more" (statute). The board noticed and held a hearing on a $5,000 gift anyway (0:08:02), opened it by motion, invited the public, and accepted the gift by a recorded motion and second. Pratt also told the board his written report would be posted on the district website the next day (0:10:10), giving the public the same document the board received.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, III (2023 codification; source note ends 2023, 38:1, eff. July 18, 2023)
