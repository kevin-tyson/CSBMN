Claremont School Board, December 1, 2021

HIGH A member attended online, but every vote was taken by voice rather than roll call

Joshua Lambert was online for the roll (0:00:52). Once a member participates remotely, RSA 91-A:2, III(e) applies: "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote." All three votes were voice votes: the agenda ("All those in favor? Aye. Opposed?", 0:01:24), the November 3 minutes (0:02:04) and adjournment (0:35:56). Paragraph III(c) also requires that each remote member "must be able to simultaneously hear each other and speak to each other"; the chair told Lambert "I don't know if you can hear me" (0:29:46) and Whitney answered "He's trying." (0:29:58), and no word from him is captured anywhere on the recording. Paragraph III(a) requires the reason remote attendance was not reasonably practical to be stated in the minutes, which are missing (flag 3). In mitigation: six members were in the room, so the physical quorum of III(b) was met, and no opposing voice is heard on any of the three votes. The flag is HIGH because III(e) is mandatory and was not followed on any vote.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(a)-(c), (e) (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1); NHPR, June 10, 2021 (state of emergency ends June 11, 2021; reporting)

HIGH Board subcommittees met, one of them over Zoom, with no notice or minutes on record

Three board subcommittees are reported as having met. Whitney: "The Capital Improvement committee met over zoom last night" (0:19:38), that is, Tuesday, November 30. Horsky: the Policy committee "did meet with the ads" and has a draft pay-to-play policy (0:22:34). The chair refers to "the subcommittee meeting" on the budget (0:32:03), and two more budget committee sessions were set for December 13 and 14 (0:33:35). RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) makes "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body" of a school board a public body, so each session needed 24-hour notice and minutes open to inspection within five business days under RSA 91-A:2, II. A committee that met "over zoom" also engages RSA 91-A:2, III(b): "Except in an emergency, a quorum of the public body shall be physically present at the location specified in the meeting notice"; the emergency-order relief had ended in June. The recording does not say whether any members of that committee sat together, so the III(b) point rests on Whitney's words alone. Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1); RSA 91-A:2, II and III(b) (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION Tentative agreements are to be discussed "in nonpublic", a session type the statute gives no ground for

The chair said of the two tentative agreements, "we'll discuss it in nonpublic and then, come back and vote in public" (0:18:41). RSA 91-A:3, I(a), in the text then in force, bars nonpublic sessions "except for one of the purposes set out in paragraph II", and paragraph II lists no collective-bargaining ground. Collective bargaining is handled instead by RSA 91-A:2, I(a), which excludes "Strategy or negotiations with respect to collective bargaining" from the definition of a meeting; whether a board review of an agreement already reached is "strategy or negotiations" is a fair question. The chair was right that the ratification vote must be public, and his plan to give members the text before the vote is sound practice. RSA 273-A:3, II(b) then sends the cost items to the annual meeting, which fits "we'll need it for the budget" (0:19:06). The session itself takes place at a later meeting; this is noted so that page can be read against it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(a) and II (2019 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1 and 280:1); RSA 91-A:2, I(a) (2019 codification); RSA 273-A:3, II(b) (cost items to the legislative body; 2013, 244:1)
