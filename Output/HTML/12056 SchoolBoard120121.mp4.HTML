MEDIUM A member attended online but apparently could not take part, and every vote was a voice vote

Joshua Lambert attended online (0:00:52: "Josh? Josh is online."). He has no attributed speech. When the chair turned to him at 0:29:46 ("Josh, you've been I don't know if you can hear me. Do you have anything to say? You're good."), a member answered "He's trying." RSA 91-A:2, III(c), as in force in 2021, required that "Each member participating electronically or otherwise must be able to simultaneously hear each other and speak to each other during the meeting, and shall be audible or otherwise discernable to the public in attendance". III(e) required that "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote"; the agenda, the November 3 minutes and adjournment were all voice votes (0:01:24, 0:02:04, 0:35:56). III(a) required the reason for remote attendance to be stated in the minutes, which are not available.

Mitigation: a quorum of six was physically present, as III(b) requires, and the chair checked on the remote member twice (0:29:46 and 0:34:58). No business turned on his vote. The COVID-19 state of emergency, under which Emergency Order #12 had relaxed these rules, expired June 11, 2021. Graded MEDIUM: the connection problem is audible on the tape, but its extent cannot be measured from the transcript.

MEDIUM The Capital Improvement committee met entirely over Zoom, outside the state of emergency

Whitney reported at 0:19:38: "The Capital Improvement committee met over zoom last night", that is, November 30, 2021, with the facilities director presenting. A committee of the school board, or an advisory committee to it, is itself a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which reaches "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto." RSA 91-A:2, III(b) required that "Except in an emergency, a quorum of the public body shall be physically present at the location specified in the meeting notice", with any emergency declared by the chair and its facts recorded in the minutes. A meeting held over Zoom has no physical quorum unless members gathered somewhere, which the report does not suggest, and no emergency is described.

Graded MEDIUM because the process gap is probable rather than certain: the committee's membership, its notice and whether any members met in person are not in any available record, and no minutes of the committee are posted. By December 2021 the Governor's emergency order that had allowed fully remote meetings had lapsed with the state of emergency on June 11, 2021.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, so in force in 2021): committees and advisory committees are public bodies; RSA 91-A:2, III(b) (2019 codification): quorum physically present except in an emergency; NHPR, June 10, 2021 (reporting).
