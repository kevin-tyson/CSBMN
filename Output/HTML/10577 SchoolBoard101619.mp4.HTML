RSA 91-A:2, II, in the text in force from January 1, 2019, required that minutes include "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", that "The names of the members who made or seconded each motion shall be recorded in the minutes", and that minutes be "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting" (statute text). On this recording the movers of the agenda, the October 2 minutes and adjournment are unidentified voices, and the opening roll call cannot be read (flag 3), so minutes are the only record that could settle attendance and who moved what.

MEDIUM under the project's severity rule, because the record mitigates the gap: the board approved minutes of this meeting at its next meeting on November 6, with Zullo abstaining because she had been absent (November 6, 2019 page), so minutes were written. No public copy survives.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019)

MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed on a voice vote with no statutory reason stated

After the legal-advice session the board voted to “seal the minutes for 83 years.” (0:29:51). The motion gave a duration (probably garbled in the transcript) and no reason. The chair called “All those in favor?”, one Aye. is transcribed, and the meeting moved on (0:30:00). RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes and decisions to be "publicly disclosed within 72 hours of the meeting, unless, by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that" disclosure would harm the reputation of a non-member, "render the proposed action ineffective", or pertain to terrorism (statute text). Nothing on the recording states which determination the board made, and the tally needed to show a two-thirds vote is not audible.

MEDIUM: a documentation gap rather than a confirmed violation, because the minutes may record a determination the recording does not. A session limited to legal advice under II(l) is not itself a ground for withholding the minutes; the III determination is what does that work.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, II(l) and III (2019 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017; 280:1)

POSITIVE Public progress report on an in-district program for students who had been placed out of district

Six weeks into the PRIDE program, the director of special education reported that two students had returned from out-of-district placements (0:47:32); members asked how progress would be measured (0:51:28); staff gave an early figure with its limits stated (0:55:50) and agreed to return with academic and behavioral data after the first semester (1:07:23).
