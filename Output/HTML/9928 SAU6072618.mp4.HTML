MEDIUM The nonpublic minutes were sealed "For ten years" with no statutory finding stated and a roll call that cannot be checked

The chair moved to seal "the minutes of the non public meeting" (0:07:41) "For ten years." (0:07:48); Benware seconded, and a roll call began (0:08:41 to 0:08:50). No reason was stated on the recording. RSA 91-A:3, III, as in force in 2018, requires nonpublic minutes and decisions to be disclosed within 72 hours unless the body, "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session", determines that disclosure would likely affect adversely the reputation of a person other than a member, render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism; the information may then be withheld "until, in the opinion of a majority of members, the aforesaid circumstances no longer apply". Two things in the motion as heard do not fit that text. It states none of the three determinations, and it fixes a ten-year term where the 2018 statute ties the seal to the circumstances (the ten-year review rule of RSA 91-A:3, IV did not exist until October 3, 2023). The roll call is captured only for three members before the chair moves on (0:09:17), so the two-thirds vote cannot be confirmed from the recording. A determination may have been understood: the session concerned a hire still under negotiation, and "render the proposed action ineffective" would fit. The page grades this MEDIUM because the finding the statute requires is not on the record, and the sealed minutes, if they survive, cannot be checked against it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and III (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017; 280:1)

The assistant superintendent reported that "with the ten that we're outstanding in November, six of them are completed" (0:37:03), across "four different entities", Cornish, the SAU, Claremont and Unity (0:38:01, 0:38:25); completed audits each include "a letter to the school board", and she proposed to discuss "The letters of June 28th and such" in September (0:37:34, 0:38:01). On June 28 the business administrator had said the SAU's own 2016-17 audit had just been received (see the June 28, 2018 page). The recording does not say which entities and fiscal years the four remaining audits covered. Where an entity spent $750,000 or more in federal awards in a year, 2 CFR 200.501(a) required a single audit, and 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1) required the reporting package "within the earlier of 30 calendar days after receipt of the auditor's report(s), or nine months after the end of the audit period"; for a fiscal year ending June 30, 2017 that is March 31, 2018. Without knowing which audits were open, this page cannot say any deadline was missed, so the point is an observation. The Federal Audit Clearinghouse submission dates for Claremont, Unity and SAU 6 would settle it.

Sources: 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1) (2018 annual CFR edition); 2 CFR 200.501(a) (2018 annual CFR edition; $750,000 threshold)

POSITIVE The interim superintendent was nominated, introduced, questioned and approved in public, after a nonpublic session entered on a stated exemption and a roll call

The board went into nonpublic session on a motion naming RSA 91-A:3, II(a) and (b) and held a roll call, as RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires (0:05:58, 0:06:08). It then returned to public session for the decision: the chair summarized the search, the 16 applications, the three interviews and the candidate's certification (0:09:17 to 0:10:58); the candidate spoke for several minutes and took members' comments (0:11:54 to 0:21:02); and the nomination was moved, seconded and voted in public (0:11:18, 0:21:46).
