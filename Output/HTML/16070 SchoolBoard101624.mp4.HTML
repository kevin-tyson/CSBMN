Claremont School Board — October 16, 2024

Sixteen days after the date by which every dollar of federal ARP ESSER money had to be obligated, this board heard the word ESSER twice: once to explain where a van came from, and once when the Finance chair asked for the final disposition and was told the office was still assembling the numbers. The same evening it voted to retain up to $350,000 of the FY24 surplus (on three sheets of paper the agenda calls Exhibit B and the public packet does not contain), adopted a federal grants policy that is an association template with the drafting instructions still in it, and seated two student board members after three months of an empty masthead.

The federal deadline
Sixteen days past. The U.S. Department of Education’s grantee memorandum of 12 June 2024 states that “all ARP ESSER and ARP EANS funds, including reverted ARP EANS funds, must be obligated by September 30, 2024”, and that all funds must be liquidated “within 120 calendar days after the close of the obligation period, or by January 28, 2025”, citing 2 CFR 200.344(b). Neither date is spoken here and neither appears in any packet document (flag 4).
Money
FY24 closed, on the business administrator’s account, at a fund balance of $497,000, plus $114,000 of additional revenue, for a $611,000 surplus. She gave the board a retention ceiling of $466,664.43; the approved minutes print $469,000 (flag 7). The board voted to retain up to $350,000. On 18 September the same board was told the FY24 balance was $544,145.29; nobody reconciles the two figures here, and the worksheet that would is the exhibit the packet does not contain (flag 1).
