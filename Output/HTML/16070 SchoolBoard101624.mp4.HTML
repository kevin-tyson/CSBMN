Claremont School Board, October 16, 2024

Regular board meeting. The board voted to retain up to $350,000 of the FY24 year-end balance to offset the FY26 tax rate, after the business administrator said she needed the decision "to finish the dough 25" [DOE-25], a report due September 1; adopted three policies on first reading (EHB-R, DAF and JJJ); heard the Tech Center's new assistant director and the student board members; and, by consent, gave teachers the November 26 professional day off and named a negotiator for the administrators' union. A parent's public comment on how an elementary school handled a bathroom incident drew no board response. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on October 16, 2024, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

HIGH The FY24 annual financial report (DOE-25), due September 1, was still unfinished on October 16 and waiting on a board decision

The business administrator opened the retention item: "you guys need to make up your mind on how much money you're going to keep out of the fund balance for me to finish the dough 25." and "I do need that taken care of this evening" (1:08:33). The DOE-25 is the annual financial report RSA 198:4-d requires of every school district, and the statute as it read in 2024 says "This report shall be submitted on or before September 1 of each year." It contains no extension provision. On her own account the FY24 report was unfinished six and a half weeks after the deadline, and one input to it, the board's retention vote, was not taken until this meeting. The record does not show whether a partial filing was made or an extension was sought from the department. Graded HIGH as a missed statutory deadline on the business administrator's own statement; the mitigation is that the board acted the same night.

Sources: RSA 198:4-d (2024 codification; annual financial report by September 1; source note ends 2021, 44:5); approved minutes 10.16.24, item IV.3 ("the Board must decide how much fund balance to retain")

MEDIUM A $350,000 retention voted on worksheets the public could not see, with figures that do not agree

The agenda lists the item with "(Exhibit B)", but the posted packet holds Exhibits A and C to G and no Exhibit B; Henry handed members "three sheets" (1:09:17). The figures given aloud do not reconcile. Henry stated a maximum of "$466,664. And $0.43" (1:10:19); from then on she and members used 469 (1:16:20), and the approved minutes print "The maximum that can be retained is $469,000". She described the cap as the local education and state education taxes "times to point 5%", which reads as 2.5 percent. RSA 198:4-b, II allowed 2.5 percent before September 27, 2020 and 5 percent since (2020, 38:25), measured on the net assessment as RSA 198:5 defines it. If 2.5 percent was applied, the ceiling the board was shown was half what the law allowed; whether her base equals the statutory net assessment cannot be checked without the worksheet. The year-end balance itself moved: on September 18 the business administrator reported a surplus of $544,145.29 from Exhibit B to that meeting (show 16021), and here the fund balance is 497,000 (1:09:17), with no explanation of the difference on either recording. The vote itself was within the statutory cap on any reading, properly moved and seconded, and the board asked for a public tutorial (1:32:02). Under RSA 198:4-b, II(a) the board must hold a public hearing, with newspaper notice at least 7 days ahead, before it expends retained funds, which the motion's FY26 tax-offset condition will require. Graded MEDIUM as a control and transparency weakness in a financial decision.

Sources: RSA 198:4-b, II (5 percent of net assessment; hearing before expending; 2020, 38:25); RSA 198:4-b, II (2019 codification; 2.5 percent); Drive folder: 6. CSB 10.16.24 (no Exhibit B); Exhibit B to the September 18 meeting, FY24 Budget Report 6-30-24

OBSERVATION A district-wide LED lighting contract, financed as a lease, already signed with no board vote in this record, and held up by the missing audits

Pratt said completion waits on the lender because "we have certain audits that aren't done" (1:02:02). Sources: RSA 32:8 (no liability without an appropriation; 1993, 332:1); approved minutes 10.16.24, item IV.2

OBSERVATION A parent's report that elementary girls were made to view a soiled bathroom stall drew no board discussion

Gallagher described an assembly of girls in grades two through five after which "the students were made to line up in the hallway outside of the bathroom in question and single singly, go observe the fecal matter smeared on the wall of the bathroom stall." (0:11:34); she said only parents who emailed were contacted and asked whether a policy governs such incidents (0:12:40). The chair thanked her and moved to the superintendent's report (0:14:02); neither the superintendent nor any member responded on the record, and the superintendent's report that followed did not mention it. The board's Policy BEDH, §B.5, provides for "Board Discussion Regarding Citizens Comments" after the comment period closes. A board may properly decline to discuss a personnel or student matter in public, and the administration had answered the parents who wrote, so this is an observation: the public record shows the complaint and no follow-up.

Sources: Policy BEDH, §B.5 (board discussion of citizens' comments); approved minutes 10.16.24, item II.b

OBSERVATION Two decisions taken by consent outside the agenda: a professional day turned into a day off, and the board's bargaining negotiator

Neither item appears on the posted agenda. The superintendent asked "permission" to give teachers November 26, a scheduled professional-development day, off (1:56:36, 1:56:59); the chair declared the board "unanimously in support" (1:59:38) with no motion. Unanimous consent is a recognized way to act, and the minutes record both items, so this is an observation about public notice of business taken up.

Sources: CSB Agenda 10.16.24 (item V lists only the community forum); RSA 273-A:5, I(e) (refusal to negotiate in good faith)
