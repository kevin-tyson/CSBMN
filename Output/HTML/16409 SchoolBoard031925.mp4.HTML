Claremont School Board, March 19, 2025

The first meeting after the March 2025 election: two new members, a vice chair elected in his absence, new subcommittee assignments, and a long debate on policy JBAB (Transgender & Gender Nonconforming Students), which the agenda listed for removal. Sixteen members of the public made seventeen comments before the debate, and five more spoke before the vote. On a 3–2 voice vote the board tabled any action on JBAB for review by the policy committee and counsel within three months, leaving the policy in force. It also adopted or advanced eight other policies, seven of them tied to the federal Title IX rule. Built from the dialogue transcript, the agenda, the packet and the approved minutes. MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed for six months with no statutory finding stated, and no unsealed copy found

RSA 91-A:3, III provides that "Minutes and decisions reached in nonpublic session shall be publicly disclosed within 72 hours" unless withheld "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session" on a determination that disclosure "likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person", would "render the proposed action ineffective", or pertains to terrorism (statute text; this paragraph was last amended by 2023, 189:1, eff. Oct. 3, 2023, so it governs this meeting). The recorded vote is there: on return to public session the chair moved "to seal the nonpublic minutes for six months" (0:45:48) and the minutes record a roll call with all five present in favor. What is missing is the determination: neither the motion on tape nor the approved minutes states which of the statutory grounds applied. The session was entered under II(a) and II(c), which suggests the reputation ground, but the record does not say so. The motion to enter the session is also thin on its face: the minutes record only "a motion to move into non-public", and on tape the citation is unintelligible (0:43:32); the agenda text quoted in the minutes carries the exemptions. Finally, a six-month seal would have run out around September 19, 2025, and MAP.md's scan of the Unsealed Minutes share (most recently 2026-10-01) lists no 3/19/25 nonpublic minutes. Graded MEDIUM: a documentation gap rather than a missing vote.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) (motion stating the exemption) and III (72-hour disclosure; 2/3 vote with a finding); approved minutes 3.19.25, item IV.1

OBSERVATION JBAB's athletics section conflicts on its face with RSA 193:41, in force since August 2024, and the policy stays in force

JBAB section H, adopted September 21, 2016, reads: "Transgender and gender nonconforming students shall be permitted to participate in interscholastic athletics in a manner consistent with their gender identity" (Exhibit B). RSA 193:41, II(b), added by 2024, 228:1, eff. Aug. 18, 2024, provides: "Athletic teams or sports designated for females, women, or girls shall not be open to students of the male sex", and ¶III fixes eligibility by "the student's biological sex on the student's official birth certificate" (statute text). The district's counsel wrote that JBAB "has not been reviewed since the adoption of RSA 193:41" and that "there are aspects of Policy JBAB that do not presently comply with state law" (Upton email, packet). The chair made the same point aloud (1:02:35). The board voted 3–2 to leave the policy in force and refer it for review within three months (1:34:52). A statute governs over a conflicting district policy whether or not the policy is amended, so the practical risk is staff reliance on the policy's text. This page does not assess the chair's separate statement about "parental involvement and notification"; no provision in force on March 19, 2025 was verified for it. Whether the three-month review happened is a question for the June 2025 record.

Sources: RSA 193:41, II(b) and III (school athletics; 2024, 228:1, eff. Aug. 18, 2024); Exhibit B, policy JBAB; Policy JBAB Upton Email 3.19.25.pdf

OBSERVATION Seven Title IX and related policies taken on emergency reads, with members disputing whether the policy committee had reviewed them

The assistant superintendent presented the changes as a return to the federal Title IX rule followed "until August of 2024" (1:47:02), that is, the 2020 rule, effective August 14, 2020 (85 FR 30026), which governed until the 2024 rule took effect. He said NHSBA recommended skipping the three-reading process (1:37:07) and that tabling ACAC would leave him out of compliance in investigations (1:40:04). Members disagreed on the record about whether the policy committee had reviewed the drafts (1:39:34, 1:40:04), and Madden said he had no summary of the implications (1:44:14) and had asked for one (1:45:01). Three policies (ACN, GBAM, IHBCA) were adopted on a single reading; four moved to second read. The board's own reading rule was not verified for this page, so the item cites only the federal rule the policies track. Which version of Title IX applied in March 2025 is a question of federal law outside this page's scope.

Sources: 85 FR 30026 (2020 Title IX rule; effective August 14, 2020); packet Exhibits C to I
