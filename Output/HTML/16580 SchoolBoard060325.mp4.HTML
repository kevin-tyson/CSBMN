Claremont School Board, June 3, 2025

Regular board meeting with five of seven members present. The draft 6.3.25 DRAFT CSB Meeting Minutes.pdf sits in the June 18 packet folder 24. CSB 6.18.25, and Drive records it as created on June 16, 2025 at 13:02 UTC (modifiedTime the same minute). Counting business days after Tuesday, June 3 (8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday, excluding holidays, as RSA 91-A:2, II defines them): June 4, 5, 6, 9 and 10. The limit of the evidence: createdTime shows when the file reached this public share, not when it was written or whether a copy could be had at the SAU office. The approved minutes (25. Approved CSB Meeting Minutes 6.3.25) were created June 20, 2025, two days after approval.

MEDIUM The policy subcommittee meets the afternoon of each board meeting; no notice or minutes of the June 3 session were found

Hawkins: "I just want to say that we met this afternoon" (1:33:16), and later "we do meet every the day of every board meeting before the board meeting." (1:50:25). She reports that the technology director "was actually at our meeting today just to answer any questions" (1:33:51), and the superintendent refers to "we were talking earlier at the policy subcommittee meeting." (0:56:59). A subcommittee of a school board is a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which ends "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", so each session needs 24 hours' posted notice and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. The June 3 board agenda notices the Finance subcommittee's June 13 meeting but not this one. No notice or minutes of the June 3 session were found in the packet folder or by Drive title searches; the district's Claremont Policy Sub Committee folder exists but could not be listed from here, so this is "not found", not "does not exist".

Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH under the project's severity rule because the record mitigates it: the subcommittee's recommendations were presented and voted in public that evening. The same pattern appears at the May 21 meeting.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (subcommittees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, II, 2024 codification (notice and minutes); CSB Agenda 6.3.25 (Finance subcommittee date noticed; no policy session)

OBSERVATION A member proposed making state-test opt-outs harder; state law requires a district form and bars penalizing exempted students

William Madden: "I think we should make it uncomfortable to opt out." and "Why don't we make them sign a form and they got to come into the school and sign the form?" (0:19:21). The same transcript segment, labeled as Madden, also carries the reply "We can't exactly put up barriers to a state rule." and the participation figure "We had 95% participation." (0:19:46); the approved minutes credit that reply to Assistant Superintendent Koski. RSA 193-C:6, as amended effective October 1, 2024, provides that "A school district shall develop a form to be signed by the parent or legal guardian of any student exempted" and that "A school district shall not penalize any exempted student" (statute text). No motion was made, and the administration's answer on the record matches the statute. Recorded here because a future change to the opt-out process would have to fit within that section.

Sources: RSA 193-C:6 (parental exemption from the statewide assessment; source note ends 2024, 350:3, eff. Oct. 1, 2024); Approved minutes 6.3.25 (Koski's reply)
