Claremont School Board, September 2, 2026

Regular meeting at the tech center. The superintendent reported that the district's FY26 annual financial report (DOE-25), due September 1, is on a 30-day state extension with a second one planned, and that 8 of the 18 corrective actions needed to finish the FY22 audit are written; the treasurer described checks found outside an approved manifest. The board waived its new sealed-bid procedure to buy a $146,342 bus, approved the Stevens and middle school handbooks 5 to 2 and the elementary handbook unanimously, sent back both athletics handbooks, adopted three policies on first presentation, and went into nonpublic session on tuition agreements.
