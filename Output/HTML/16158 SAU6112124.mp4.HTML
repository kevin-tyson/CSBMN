SAU 6 Board, Strategic Plan Presentation, November 21, 2024

Single-item meeting of the SAU 6 joint board (Claremont and Unity). With no quorum at 6:30 p.m., District Management Group's 44-minute presentation of the SAU 6 Strategic Plan 2025-2030 ran before the meeting was called to order; once seven of twelve members were present the chair gaveled the board in, and the plan was adopted on an uncounted voice vote about six minutes before adjournment. MEDIUM The draft minutes reached the public share on the fourteenth business day

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting". Counting business days as that paragraph defines them and excluding Thanksgiving (November 28, a holiday under RSA 288:1; the day after Thanksgiving is not on that list), the deadline was Friday, November 29, 2024. The draft's Drive file was created at 4:00 p.m. Eastern on December 12, 2024, in the December 12 packet folder, about two and a half hours before that night's meeting: the fourteenth business day. The board then approved the minutes "as presented" that evening, so the public first saw them online only as they were adopted. Graded MEDIUM under the project's severity rule because the meeting was noticed and packeted and the minutes exist; Drive dates show when the public could read them online, not whether a draft was available at the SAU office sooner.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2024 codification; 5 business days); RSA 288:1 (legal holidays); 11.21.24 DRAFT SAU6 meeting minutes (Drive createdTime 2024-12-12, 21:00 UTC)

OBSERVATION Forty-five minutes of presentation and discussion took place before the meeting was called to order, with a quorum present for at least part of it

The chair opened with a welcome and no call to order (0:00:00); the presenter twice said she did not know whether a quorum was present (0:02:01, 0:44:50). During that time five members took part: Hawkins, Ruggeri and Crawford read plan text, Sprague and Whitney discussed priorities, and the chair answered an audience question. Under RSA 91-A:2, I a meeting is the convening of a quorum to discuss or act on a matter within the body's jurisdiction, so the later part of the presentation was a meeting whether or not it had been gaveled. The purposes of the chapter were served in substance: the session was noticed on the agenda, open, televised and recorded, and the draft minutes disclose the sequence and summarize the discussion. The cost is to the record: the clerk's roll was taken only at 0:45:45, so the record does not show who was present when, and the gaveled meeting lasted from 0:44:57 to 0:50:45, under six minutes.

OBSERVATION The adopted plan differs from its own presentation on how chronic absence is defined, and two of its terms deserve a second look

The presenter defined chronically absent students as those "who have missed 10% of or more of the school year" (0:15:14). The plan the board adopted "as presented" defines it in its appendix as "having 10 or more unexcused absences in a school year" (plan p. 20), a count of unexcused days that differs from a share of all absences, so the baseline for one of the plan's own measures depends on which definition the district uses. The plan also sets a goal to "Decrease the number of bullying and harassment investigations" (p. 13), which counts the district's responses to reports rather than incidents; the presenter framed it as showing that students "will then feel safe in school". And it states that "the Board of Education will provide essential guidance and oversight" (p. 16), a name the SAU 6 board does not use. None of these was raised before the vote (0:47:16 to 0:48:51). No rule is cited: the plan is a policy document within the SAU's remit, and these are points for the progress monitoring the chair proposed.

Sources: SAU Strategic Plan_2025-2030 (pp. 13, 16, 20)
