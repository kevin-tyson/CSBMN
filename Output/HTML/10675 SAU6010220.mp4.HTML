Summary of a public hearing on the FY2021 SAU 6 budget followed by a regular meeting of the SAU 6 board (the Claremont and Unity school boards sitting jointly), generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript. HIGH The FY2021 SAU budget was adopted on January 2, 2020, after the statutory January 1 date

RSA 194-C:9, I, in the text in force on this date, provides: "At a meeting held before January 1, the school administrative unit board shall adopt a budget" for the next fiscal year, to be apportioned among the member districts. The board adopted the FY2021 budget tonight (0:25:06), and the business administrator said the allocation between Unity and Claremont would follow that approval (0:07:59). On December 5 the board had chosen this date knowing that the budget is usually finished in December, on the belief that a public hearing was required first; RSA 194-C:9 requires none (December 5 page, flag 1). Mitigation, stated fairly: the dollar figure was fixed by a vote on December 5 (0:04:45), there was no change to it here, the delay was two days, and nothing on the record shows harm to either district's budget process. Nothing on the recording suggests the districts had adopted the alternative procedure of RSA 194-C:9-a, which RSA 194-C:9, III excepts. Graded HIGH because on the face of the record a statutory deadline was missed.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I and III (2019 codification; source note 1996, 298:3; 2003, 279:1); RSA 194-C:9-a (2019 codification).

OBSERVATION SAU policies were moved to second reading on the day most members first saw them

The draft SAU policies were emailed with a December 31 date but, by members' accounts, arrived the morning of the meeting (0:23:03, 0:23:35). A Unity member objected that "we really haven't done a first read" (0:22:12) and asked for a three-reading process; the board moved the set to second reading anyway, with the understanding that changes could follow (0:23:59). The drafter himself urged members "do not rubber stamp this" (0:20:24). No statute sets a reading procedure for board policies; this concerns the board's own practice. It also matters for later pages: policies adopted from this set would be the SAU's first of its own, so their adoption history starts here.

Sources: No statute applies; board practice only.

OBSERVATION Six months into the superintendent's contract, the board said it had not yet set goals with him

With an evaluation expected before the March elections, members said they had agreed an evaluation tool but no procedure (0:27:59), that nobody had "sat down with Mike to set goals" (0:39:01), and that joint goal-setting "is actually a requirement in the contract" (0:39:42). The superintendent said the board had no such process "for any of the previous superintendents and last decade or so" (0:41:32). The contract was not available to check. The board agreed to hear the entry-plan report on February 20 and to discuss the process further; how the evaluation itself is held is governed by RSA 91-A:3, II(a), which lets a board consider an employee's compensation or discipline in nonpublic session unless the employee requests an open meeting, as a member noted (0:31:53).

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, II(a) (2019 codification); the superintendent's contract was not available.

POSITIVE The SAU board held a public hearing on its budget that the statute does not require, and asked that hearing notices state their subject

RSA 194-C:9 requires the SAU board to adopt a budget but prescribes no public hearing. The board held one anyway, on camera, with the chair noting for the record that no member of the public had come (0:06:00), and a member later confirmed that hearing notices on the agenda now state what each hearing is about (0:58:29). The hearing was also the reason the adoption slipped past January 1 (flag 1); the practice was sound, the scheduling was not.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9 (2019 codification; no hearing requirement).
