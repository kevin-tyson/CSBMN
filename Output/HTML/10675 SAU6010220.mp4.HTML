HIGH The SAU 6 board adopted its FY21 budget on January 2, 2020; RSA 194-C:9, I requires adoption “At a meeting held before January 1”

RSA 194-C:9, I provides: “At a meeting held before January 1, the school administrative unit board shall adopt a budget”. The statute's exceptions do not reach SAU 6. ¶II covers units “comprising only one district”, and SAU 6 has two. ¶III covers districts that have adopted RSA 194-C:9-a, whose alternative, a separate warrant article under RSA 194-C:9-b, “shall not apply to a school administrative unit that includes a city”, and SAU 6 includes the City of Claremont. The board's own record treats tonight's vote as the adoption: the business administrator said the allocation to the districts would follow “once this board approves the budget” (0:07:59), and the regular agenda carried “the fiscal year 21 budget approval” (0:24:52), voted at 0:25:34. On December 5 the board had only moved the figure to a hearing.

This page grades the point HIGH because the record contains nothing that brings the adoption inside the statute: it is a missed statutory date, by two days. The December 5, 2019 page grades the scheduling decision MEDIUM, because on that date the miss was a choice still in prospect and the board was asking for the deadline; tonight it is a completed fact. Mitigating, and stated here for fairness: no member of the public came to the hearing that caused the delay (0:06:00); the superintendent described starting the year “with hardly anyone in the saw” [SAU] (0:41:08); and RSA 194-C:9, I's further step, certifying each district's share “Prior to January 15”, remained possible. The statute states no consequence for late adoption, and none appears in this record.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I-III (2019 codification; source note 1996, 298:3; 2003, 279:1, eff. Sept. 16, 2003); RSA 194-C:9-b, IV (2019 codification; not available to an SAU that includes a city)

OBSERVATION Members said the superintendent's contract requires joint goal-setting that had not happened six months in

Six months into Superintendent Tempesta's tenure, Erickson said she was not sure the board had ever sat down with him to set goals, Whitney said that “is actually a requirement in the contract”, and Erickson said it was supposed to happen within six months (0:39:01 to 0:41:52). The superintendent answered that his entry plan was shared with everyone and is online (0:40:00, 0:42:46). The contract is not in this record, so the requirement and its timing cannot be checked here; this is the members' characterization, recorded as an observation. Two procedural points from the same discussion: Petrin said the evaluation discussion “would have to be in nonpublic” and Zullo answered that it need not be if the superintendent chooses otherwise (0:31:46, 0:31:53). RSA 91-A:3, II(a) permits a nonpublic session on the dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline of an employee, and even then not where the employee “(1) has a right to a meeting and (2) requests that the meeting be open”; nothing in it requires a nonpublic session, so Zullo's point is consistent with the statute. And Erickson objected that the process was being settled although “it's not on the agenda for today” (0:28:58); RSA 91-A:2, II requires notice of a meeting's time and place, not an itemized agenda, so the objection goes to good practice rather than the statute.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, II(a) (2019 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1 and 280:1); RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification)

POSITIVE A public hearing the statute does not require, and hearing notices that now state their subject

RSA 194-C:9 sets no hearing requirement for an SAU budget; the board held one, presented the figures in full and recorded that no one came (0:06:00). At the close, Benware confirmed that the district's posted hearing notices now say what each hearing is about, and Erickson pointed to the wording on the notice (0:58:29, 0:58:40). That answers the complaint a resident made at the Claremont board's December 4 hearing that its posting “doesn't say what it's about” (show 10638; see the December 4, 2019 page). RSA 91-A:2, II requires notice of time and place only, so stating the subject goes beyond the minimum. The cost of holding the hearing, a missed adoption date, is flag 1.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9 (2019 codification; no hearing requirement); RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification; notice of time and place)
