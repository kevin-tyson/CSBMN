Claremont School Board, May 6, 2026

Regular board meeting with all seven members present and the incoming superintendent, Dr. Timothy Broadrick, attending as a guest. Four citizens spoke on a lunch-account refund, middle-school staffing and the alternative Academy program. Broadrick said the 2025-26 calendar the board approved had only 172.5 school days, which is why the district needs a State Board waiver to end school on June 12. The interim business administrator reported just over $6 million in cash, said he had pulled almost 1,000 invoices for the auditors and could not find copies of some, and said the auditors will disclaim an opinion on the July 1, 2025 opening balance. The board appointed Kerry Kennedy director of curriculum, instruction and assessment and Amy Savage human resources director, both effective July 1, reappointed a member to the CCTV board, adopted two safety policies, and sealed the minutes of a 40-minute nonpublic session until September 1, 2027. MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed for 16 months with no statutory finding stated

After a 40-minute nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(b), (l) and (c) (hiring, legal advice, and a student-transportation suspension added that day; 1:17:31, 1:44:51), the board voted by roll call "to seal the minutes until September 1st, 2027." (1:46:11, 1:46:25). RSA 91-A:3, III allows nonpublic minutes and decisions to be withheld beyond 72 hours only "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session" and only where it is determined that disclosure "likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself", or would render the proposed action ineffective, or pertains to terrorism. The roll-call vote satisfies the first condition. Neither the motion on tape nor the approved minutes states which finding the board made, so the record does not show the second.
