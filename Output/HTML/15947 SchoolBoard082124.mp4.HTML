Claremont School Board, August 21, 2024

First recorded board meeting of the 2024-25 school year, with all seven members present. The board went into a nonpublic session, ratified the year's professional staff list, funded a Girls on the Run program at Bluff, and, after a long debate, approved an "acting assistant CTE director" post at the tech center that the superintendent had already offered to a district employee. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on August 21, 2024 (the 2024 codification of RSA 91-A, whose source notes end with 2023 session laws), or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

HIGH The motion to enter nonpublic session stated no exemption, the one the chair named cannot apply, and no nonpublic minutes were located

RSA 91-A:3, I(b): "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II which is relied upon as foundation for the nonpublic session." The motion on tape is "I'd like to make a motion to move to a nonpublic session." (0:34:50); the minutes record it the same way. The chair had introduced the item as "Roman numeral two, J" (0:34:32), and the agenda heading prints both II(j) and II(c). II(j) covers "confidential, commercial, or financial information that is exempt from public disclosure under RSA 91-A:5, IV in an adjudicative proceeding pursuant to RSA 541 or RSA 541-A"; a school board's regular meeting is not such a proceeding, so II(j) is unavailable on its face. II(c), reputation, may have fit; nothing on the record says which ground was used. The board then declined to seal ("Is there any motion to seal the minutes? No.", 0:35:53), so RSA 91-A:3, III required the nonpublic minutes and decisions to be "publicly disclosed within 72 hours of the meeting". The approved public minutes record only the entry and exit votes. No minutes of this nonpublic session were found by Drive title search across the district's shares (a search negative is not proof of absence; the minutes may exist and be unposted). Mitigation: the entry and exit votes were roll calls, and the agenda gave the public notice of the two grounds considered. The August 7 minutes approved tonight cite their own nonpublic session as "RSA 91-A:3, I & II (1)", a numbering that does not exist in the statute.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(c), II(j) and III (2024 codification; source note ends 2023, 189:1); CSB Agenda 8.21.24 (2).pdf; approved minutes; 8.7.24 Draft CSB Meeting Minutes (3).pdf

The amendment changed the title to "acting assistant CTE director", and the chair restated the main motion with "the change acting assistant CTE director" (1:16:06). The approved minutes record the amendment correctly but then the final vote "to approve the position with the amendment in the title to 'assistant'", dropping the word the board had just voted on and Petrin had voted against. RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to include "a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions"; on the final decision, the official record contradicts the recording. The roll-call tallies themselves (5-2 on the amendment, 6-1 on the main motion) are recorded with each member's name.

MEDIUM Subcommittee minutes reached the public three to four weeks late, and one subcommittee met that afternoon without a located notice

A committee or subcommittee of a school board is a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which reaches "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof", so its minutes are due within five business days. The Finance Subcommittee met July 22 (Crawford, Sprague, Whitney; minutes in calendar-notes form) and the Capital Improvements Committee on July 29 at the SAU office, 3:30 to 4:01 p.m. Both sets of minutes first appear in this packet, uploaded August 19, 2024 (Drive createdTime and modifiedTime both that morning), the 20th and 15th business days after the meetings. No notice for that meeting was located; a draft of its minutes, titled 1. draft SAU6 Exploratory AdHoc Sub-Comm 8.21.24, was found only in the project maintainer's own Google Drive (see footer), not in a district share. The by-laws adopted June 5 send subcommittee material to the district website, which the chair says is not yet up (1:26:30). Graded MEDIUM because minutes exist for all three meetings; the defects are timing and notice.
