Claremont School Board, Superintendent Candidate Interview (Broderick), April 6, 2026

An evening public forum, moderated by board chair Candace Crawford, closing the sole finalist's full-day visit to the district. The candidate, who signs his own name Timothy Broadrick (the district and CCTV spell it Broderick), answered about a dozen submitted and floor questions on curriculum, accountability, reconfiguration ("Don't make another change for the next 12 months"), school choice, state funding, staffing, financial controls and whether he would relocate. No board business was transacted on tape. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript, the April 1 and April 15 minutes and Drive searches. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

MEDIUM A board-run forum in the hiring process, attended by at least two members and reportedly most of the board, has no posted notice, agenda or minutes

RSA 91-A:2, I defines a meeting as "the convening of a quorum of the membership of a public body ... for the purpose of discussing or acting upon a matter" within its jurisdiction, and excludes "A chance, social, or other encounter not convened for the purpose of discussing or acting upon such matters". The forum was organized by the board as part of its own hiring process, run by its chair, and attended at least by Mr. Madden (1:09:04); the candidate said "most members of your school board, I think are in the room right now" (0:56:09). If four of seven members were present, it was arguably a meeting, which requires notice posted at least 24 hours ahead and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. No notice, agenda or minutes for April 6 was found in the district's shares (MAP.md section 299; rechecked this run). Applying the project's severity rule, the limb is MEDIUM rather than HIGH because the record mitigates it: the forum was announced in open session on April 1 with its date, time and place (see the April 1 page) and in the April 1 minutes, the public was invited, and CCTV recorded it in full. Whether a quorum attended is the fact that would settle the question; members did not deliberate or vote on tape.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I and II (definition of meeting; notice and minutes); 4.1.26 Approved minutes (future dates: "April 6th 5-7 candidate forum")

POSITIVE The finalist faced an open public forum, with questions taken from the floor and online, recorded for broadcast

No statute requires a school board to put a superintendent finalist before the public. The board announced the forum in open session five days ahead, invited residents who could not attend to submit questions (0:01:16; see also the April 1 page), alternated those with floor questions, and had CCTV record it for broadcast the next morning. The chair read pointed questions as submitted, including ones on board overreach and the candidate's residence (0:55:36, 1:02:37), and the candidate answered each one.
