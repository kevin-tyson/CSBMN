SAU 6 Board — June 4, 2019

Meeting of the joint SAU 6 board (Claremont and Unity members) under Acting Superintendent Cory LeClair, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. The board went into nonpublic session with counsel, took a split roll-call vote on a motion the recording does not capture, rejected a motion to reserve the assistant superintendent post from the incoming superintendent's summer hiring authority and then gave him that authority without limit, authorized a press release on "additional matters that we discovered", and received the SAU's FY2018 audit, which carries an adverse opinion. OBSERVATION A split roll-call vote after the nonpublic session, and a press release on "matters that we discovered", are unintelligible from the public recording

When the recording resumes after the nonpublic session, a motion has already been made and seconded ("Jason to have a second point.", 0:05:18) and the clerk calls a roll on it (0:05:46 to 0:06:21): McCormick and Zullo are heard voting no, everyone else yes. What the motion was is not on the tape. Later the acting superintendent announced a press release about "Additional matters that we discovered" (0:22:38), and the board authorized the chair, counsel and LeClair to issue it (0:23:13, 0:23:32) without the matters being described in public. The nonpublic session itself was properly moved (flag 5). In 2019 RSA 91-A:3, III required minutes of a nonpublic session to be publicly disclosed within 72 hours unless the body voted, by a recorded two-thirds vote in public session, to withhold them on a statutory ground; no such vote is on the recording. RSA 91-A:2, II required the public-session minutes to record "final decisions". Those minutes exist (approved July 25, with a correction to this very roll call) but are not online, and no nonpublic minutes or press release were located, so the decision cannot be identified from the public record.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (2019 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1 and 280:1): nonpublic minutes disclosed within 72 hours unless withheld by recorded two-thirds vote with a statutory determination; RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification): minutes record final decisions.

OBSERVATION The SAU's FY2018 audit carries an adverse opinion, and SAU 6 entities had 17 audits outstanding

The acting superintendent presented the annual financial audit of SAU 6 as of June 30, 2018 (0:23:54). She said it identifies "significant deficiency and material weaknesses" (0:24:16) and that "it's an adverse opinion which is not what we strive for" (0:32:10). She reported that of "17 outstanding audits" five remained, with Claremont and Unity FY2018 possibly unfinished at her departure (0:25:51, 0:26:16). An adverse opinion means, in her own reading, that the statements do not fairly present the accounts or do not follow generally accepted accounting principles (0:31:08). The figures are the speaker's; the audit report was not located online, and she confirmed it was not on the website but could be provided (0:33:25). No audit-timing rule specific to an SAU in 2019 was verified for this page, and the SAU's federal expenditures, which decide whether the Uniform Guidance single-audit deadline applies, were not established, so no statute is cited. In mitigation, the audit and its opinion were explained in open session in plain terms, and the chair asked on the record how the public could see it.

Sources: Cablecast show 10407 at the times given (speaker's account; audit report not located).

OBSERVATION The board declined to reserve the assistant superintendent decision and gave the incoming superintendent unrestricted summer hiring authority

LeClair described summer permission to employ as letting the superintendent fill "the vacant positions at the Saw level" with the positions "presented to the board afterward" (0:06:41); the open posts were business administrator, assistant business administrator and HR director, and assistant superintendent (0:08:27). Zullo moved to except the assistant superintendent post "until the board has a conversation around that" (0:10:38), saying the question "should be had on camera" (0:14:05). The motion failed on roll call, with only Bader, Sprague and Zullo heard voting yes (0:20:38 to 0:21:43), and Lovett's motion to let Mr. Tempesta employ "as he sees fit over the summer" passed by voice (0:21:47, 0:22:02). On July 25 the new superintendent brought Donna Magoon to the board as assistant superintendent and the board approved her, so the post was filled with a board vote after all. Members' views of the board's authority differed on the record ("gross overstep" at 0:12:37; "micromanaging" at 0:11:00). RSA 189:39, which requires school boards to elect teachers on the superintendent's nomination, concerns teachers; no statute governing appointment of SAU central-office staff was verified for this page, so the delegation is recorded as an observation, not a concern.

Sources: RSA 189:39 (superintendents nominate, school boards elect teachers; source note ends 1971, 371:2); SAU 6 Board, July 25, 2019.

POSITIVE The nonpublic motion read both exemptions aloud and was carried on a recorded roll call

The motion to enter nonpublic session cited RSA 91-A:3 and Lovett read the text of paragraph II(a) (employee dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline) and II(c) (matters likely to affect adversely the reputation of a non-member) into the record (0:02:14, 0:02:22).
