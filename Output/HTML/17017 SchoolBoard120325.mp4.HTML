Claremont School Board, December 3, 2025

Regular board meeting of three and a half hours, all seven members present and the clerk absent. The board approved five sets of minutes going back to September 17, the Stevens High School program of studies, up to $3,500 for CodeMonkey licenses (conditioned on first exhausting grant options), and a two-installment payment plan for an $868,000 SchoolCare assessment; it adopted policies DJ, JJE and BBAA, withdrew GBEC, appointed William Madden and Michael Petrin to the superintendent search, heard a review of state assessment results, and argued for most of an hour over whether to look beyond a traditional superintendent. A motion to approach the teachers' union about reopening its contract was withdrawn.
