The department may have an administrative practice the statute does not show; this page cannot confirm it. OBSERVATION Held checks and a large unpaid assessment framed FY26 as a cash problem rather than a balance-sheet problem

"The old practice of holding checks did continue to occur during during 2025, 2026." (0:05:03). The superintendent said the late July checks the treasurer reported were not held deliberately but waited on training (0:05:28). Of the $868,000 mid-year assessment "that nobody budgeted for" (0:06:18), the district has paid one third; "We have not yet paid the other two thirds." although the full amount was encumbered (0:06:43); "It's not a balance sheet issue." (0:07:08) and "cash management is really the area that ultimately the district slipped up on." (0:07:15). He said he would establish the cash position before releasing checks (0:08:08). Under modified accrual (GASB 34, ¶79) an incurred obligation belongs in the fund statements whether or not the check is mailed, which is why a held check distorts cash rather than expenditures; and RSA 197:23-a ties payment to board orders and the treasurer's books. No rule is shown to be broken on this record; the FY26 audit will be the test.

Sources: GASB Statement 34, ¶79 (governmental funds: modified accrual); RSA 197:23-a (treasurer pays only on board orders; keeps a fair and correct account)

OBSERVATION Grant revenue anticipated in the year-end report but never claimed is described as a past practice

Dr. Broadrick: he must "figure out how much grant revenue from fiscal 26 that was anticipated" (0:18:36); in the past "the business administrator would say, oh, we're going to get $2 worth of grant funds for last year. They would submit their 25 report anticipating that revenue and." Crawford: "Never submit the reports." (0:19:37, 0:19:50). Grants for 2024-25 must be claimed first (0:19:08). Under GASB 33 a reimbursement grant is revenue only when allowable costs have been incurred and the other eligibility requirements met, and in a governmental fund only when available; revenue that is never claimed cannot be available. This is the superintendent's description of past practice, not a figure in a filed report, so it is an observation; the FY26 DOE-25 and audit would settle it.

Sources: GASB Statement 33, ¶20-21 and ¶30d (grant revenue recognized only when eligibility requirements, including allowable costs incurred, are met, and in governmental funds when available)
