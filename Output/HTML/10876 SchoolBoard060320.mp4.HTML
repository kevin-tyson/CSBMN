Claremont School Board, June 3, 2020

Second remote meeting of the COVID-19 closure, held by video conference with six of seven members. District counsel briefs the board on the governor's ten-person limit for the drive-through graduation at the speedway; the superintendent expects a hybrid reopening in the fall paid for in part by federal relief money; the business administrator recommends renewing the no-cost Abbey Group food contract and projects a food-program surplus of about $60,000 for the year. The board approves the February 23 retreat minutes and corrects and approves the May 6 minutes, and sets a goal-setting retreat for August 9 in place of a July meeting. MEDIUM No agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting survive in any district share

MAP.md records no Drive material for this meeting. The recording is now the only public account of the graduation briefing, the reopening plan and the food-contract recommendation. RSA 91-A:2, II (in force from January 1, 2019) requires minutes that include "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions" and that are "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting" (statute text).

MEDIUM, not HIGH, because the record mitigates the gap: the chair works from a numbered agenda (0:02:49), and the board approved the June 3 minutes on August 5, 2020 (August 5, 2020 page).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1)

OBSERVATION Board members formed a working group on the food contract and planned a capital improvement committee session, and no notice or minutes of either survive

The business administrator asked for "a group from the board that I can work with" on renewing the Abbey Group contract and proposed meeting "for next Monday or next Tuesday" (1:12:43); the chair named himself, Whitney and the absent Benware (1:12:16, 1:12:37). Later the chair proposed a capital improvement committee meeting "so that we can have a discussion prior to next board meeting" (1:38:46). RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) makes a public body of a school board "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto" (statute text), so a committee of the board must give notice and keep minutes under RSA 91-A:2.

Three of seven members is not a quorum of the board, but the statute reaches committees as such; the question is whether this group was a committee of the board or an informal staff consultation. It was formed by the chair at a board meeting to prepare a board decision, which points toward a committee. Whether either session took place, and whether it was noticed, cannot be checked: the corpus holds no recording, notice or minutes for either, and there are no 2020 district records online.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1); RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION Remote-meeting practice slipped in three ways: the statement of who was present came after three votes, a member lost the connection during a briefing, and there was no public comment period

RSA 91-A:2, III(c) requires a member participating remotely to "identify the persons present in the location from which the member is participating" (statute text). Here that statement came only after the agenda, the retreat minutes and the May 6 minutes had been voted, at a member's prompt (0:07:57). Emergency Order #12 did not waive that sentence; it waived III(b)'s physical quorum and III(c)'s audibility-at-a-location requirement (order text).

Towle lost her connection during counsel's graduation briefing and said "I lost internet connection and I never could pick it up" until the student representative had finished speaking (0:34:48); no vote was taken while she was out. The order's conditions concern public access, and the record shows no public access problem; it also shows no citizens' comment period at all, at a meeting the public could reach only by phone or video. No statute required one in 2020: RSA 189:74's 30-minute comment period dates from 2022, 333:1, eff. Sept. 6, 2022. The March 4, 2020 in-person meeting did offer one (March 4, 2020 page).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(c) (2019 codification); Emergency Order #12 (Mar. 23, 2020); RSA 189:74 (2022, 333:1, eff. Sept. 6, 2022; not law in 2020)
