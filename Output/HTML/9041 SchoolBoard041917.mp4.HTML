MEDIUM An unbudgeted $11,787 payment toward a city track, funded from health insurance savings, with no appropriation identified

The superintendent proposed using $11,787 to help the city resurface the Monadnock track, a request "three weeks old" that "We didn't talk about it when we were doing this year's budget" (2:26:59). The business administrator said the money was left in the health insurance account because the guaranteed maximum rate came in below budget (2:36:52). The board voted on the chair's motion (2:33:13, 2:39:28); no ayes are audible. RSA 32:8, in force in 2017, provides that no school board "shall pay or agree to pay any money ... for any purpose in excess of the amount appropriated by the legislative body for that purpose, or for any purpose for which no appropriation has been made, except as provided in RSA 32:9-11". RSA 32:10 lets the governing body move an unexpended balance "to that appropriation" when it becomes "necessary to expend more than the amount appropriated for a specific purpose", and requires records from which "any citizen" can "ascertain the purposes of appropriations to which, and from which, amounts have been transferred". The record does not say which appropriation the payment was charged to, or whether a transfer was recorded. If an athletics or facilities line covered resurfacing a track the district uses, a transfer into that line would fit RSA 32:10; a payment straight from the health insurance line would not. The minutes and the district's FY2017 transfer records would settle it.

Sources: RSA 32:8 (2016 codification; source 1993, 332:1); RSA 32:10, I and II (2016 codification; source note ends 2004, 113:1, the text in force until Aug. 15, 2017)

OBSERVATION The superintendent offered to relay members' emails to one another

Asking members to send him their priorities for budget briefings, the superintendent said: "You can't you can't communicate over the email with your board members, but you can share it with me and I can share it with you." (2:22:06). RSA 91-A:2-a, as in force in 2017, required public bodies to deliberate "only in meetings" and provided that "Communications outside a meeting, including, but not limited to, sequential communications among members of a public body, shall not be used to circumvent the spirit and purpose of this chapter". Passing a list of requested briefing topics to the board is ordinary staff work. A hub that collects each member's views and shares them with the others is the kind of sequential communication the statute names, and it would become a problem if it carried discussion of a pending decision. The same evening, the superintendent advised against the board's meeting "without administration" (1:47:10); members said those budget sessions were public meetings held on a Saturday (1:57:26, 1:57:38). This page could not check how those sessions were noticed or minuted.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2-a, I and II (2016 codification; source 2008, 303:4)
