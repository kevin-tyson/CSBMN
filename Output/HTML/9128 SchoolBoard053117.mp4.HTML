OBSERVATION The food-service committee that would recommend the vendor may itself be a public body, and no record of its meeting survives

The superintendent said the committee would meet "after tonight's meeting", with two board members and four faculty members, and "come back with a recommendation" to the board, which would decide (0:21:50, 0:22:21, 2:07:34). He formed it by asking "people who would be interested in serving" (2:07:07). RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) makes a public body of a school district board "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", and a public body must give notice and keep minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. Whether a committee convened by the superintendent to advise the board is an "advisory committee thereto" is not settled by anything verified for this page, so this is an observation. If it is a public body, its meeting needed notice and minutes, and none survive. The June 21 page (show 9171) records the committee's recommendation.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (source note ends 2008, 354:1, so the text in force in 2017); RSA 91-A:2, II (2016 codification)

OBSERVATION A written meal-charge policy was federally required by July 1, 2017; the board was told it would work on one "this summer"

Asked how vendors treat students without lunch money, the superintendent told the board "There is one that you'll be working on this summer. It's called meal charging. That's in response to a recent law" (0:41:27). Two rules were in play. RSA 189:11-a, VIII, in force since July 2, 2016, provides that a school lunch meal payment policy implemented by a school board "shall ensure that all students have access to a healthy school lunch" and that "no student will be subject to different treatment from the standard school lunch meal". USDA's memo SP 23-2017 restates that a written meal charge policy "is required for SFAs no later than July 1, 2017", under SP 46-2016. A policy worked on over the summer would come after that date. The June 21 meeting (show 9171) sets the second reading for July 19. This is graded an observation here because the record does not show whether the district already had a written charge procedure in place; the June 21 page carries the scheduling finding.

Sources: RSA 189:11-a, VIII (2017 codification; source 2016, 48:1, eff. July 2, 2016); USDA FNS memo SP 23-2017 (Mar. 23, 2017): written meal charge policy required no later than July 1, 2017
