Claremont School Board — November 29, 2017

Budget study session of the Claremont School Board, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes are posted in the district's online shares. With no administrators present, the seven members worked line by line through the superintendent's first-draft FY2019 budget, starting at Stevens High School and moving through the middle and elementary schools, marking lines whose budgets far exceeded past spending and finding two apparently duplicated salary entries ($16,584.75 for a paraprofessional and $39,802 for the truant officer). The questions were to be sent to the business administrator by email. The session ended with a motion to enter nonpublic session "for personnel", after which the recording stops. MEDIUM The nonpublic motion said only "personnel", and no roll call is on the recording

At the start Zullo asked to add a nonpublic session for "RSA 9183 personnel" [as transcribed] (0:01:23); at the end Pierce moved "to go into nonpublic" and the chair added "For personnel." (2:47:35). RSA 91-A:3, I(b) as it read in 2017 required that the motion "shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II" and be decided by roll call. Personnel is not an exemption: paragraph II separately covers (a) an employee's dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline, (b) hiring, and (c) matters affecting a non-member's reputation, and I(c) confines the session to the matters set out in the motion. The earlier 9183 may be a garble of 91-A:3, but no paragraph is audible. A second is reported (2:47:41); the operator is told to shut off (2:47:51) and no roll call is heard, so the vote itself may have happened off the recording. Graded MEDIUM as a probable process gap rather than HIGH because the recording ends before the vote and no minutes are available to confirm how it was taken.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), I(c), II and III (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017).

OBSERVATION The question list was to be assembled by email with every member's "blessing" before going to the administration

Irish said he would send the spreadsheet to all members, take additions by email, and "Everybody says, okay, I'll send it's strictly informational and then I'll send it to Mike once I get everybody's blessing", adding "Just don't go back and forth" (2:46:25, 2:46:50). RSA 91-A:2-a (in force since 2008) requires public bodies to "deliberate on matters only in meetings" and bars communications outside a meeting, including sequential communications among members, from being used to circumvent the chapter. Much of the list was built in this public session, and Irish expressly tried to keep email one-way; but collecting each member's approval of the final list by email sits close to the line the statute draws. Recorded as an observation.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2-a, I-II (source note 2008, 303:4 only, so in force in 2017).

OBSERVATION Members were unsure which hires need board approval

Zullo said people had been hired "that we haven't approved well, even known about" (0:49:09); Rapp said the board approves a hiring list; Irish replied "But we don't have to" (0:49:05) and later that restructuring had happened "without the board's involvement" (0:50:12). RSA 189:39, unchanged since 1971, provides that "Superintendents shall nominate and school boards elect all teachers". The positions discussed here were mostly non-teaching (secretaries, curriculum and program roles), for which this page verified no comparable statute, so no finding is made; the exchange is recorded because the board's own understanding of its approval role was unsettled on the record.

Sources: RSA 189:39 (source note ends 1971, 371:2; unchanged since).
