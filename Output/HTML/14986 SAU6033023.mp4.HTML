MEDIUM The SAU 6 chair was chosen by unsigned paper ballot, and no member's vote is on the record

At 0:01:39 a paper vote is requested and agreed; the minutes say "Frank Sprague requests a paper vote" and "Ben Nester collects the voting slips and totals the counts." The result, "Vote is six five" (0:06:44), is all that survives: the recording does not say how anyone voted and the minutes give totals only. RSA 91-A:2, II, as codified for this date, provides: "Except for town meetings, school district meetings, and elections, no vote while in open session may be taken by secret ballot" (statute). Whether a board's choice of its own chair is one of the "elections" the exception covers is not settled by the text; read as public elections, the exception does not reach it, and the vote should have been by voice or roll call, or by signed ballots read into the record. The vice-chair vote that followed was by voice (0:07:05). Because the meaning of "elections" is open, this is graded MEDIUM, not HIGH.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2022 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019); draft minutes, item II

MEDIUM The board organized two days before the statutory window and chose no secretary or treasurer

RSA 194-C:5, I requires the SAU board to "meet between April 1 and June 1 in each year" and to "organize by choosing a chairperson, a secretary, and a treasurer" (statute). This meeting, on March 30, held the election of officers and chose a chair and a vice chair, an office the statute does not name; the agenda listed only "a. Chair b. Vice Chair". No secretary or treasurer was chosen. The treasurer's office was live: Horsky says he has been signing vouchers in "the treasurer hat" (1:05:54) and he became chair tonight. The minutes still list a "Secretary Roll Call of Attendance". Mitigation: the next meeting, April 13, falls inside the window, and the board's own calendar draft puts officer elections at the first April meeting. At that meeting the board elected Arlene Hawkins treasurer and again chose no secretary (SAU 6 Board, April 13, 2023, flag 3).

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, I (1996, 298:3, eff. Aug. 9, 1996; unamended); agenda, item II; proposed recurring agenda items

The packet folder 5. SAU SB 3.30.23 and the agenda were created on Drive on March 30, 2023 at about 2:05 p.m., for a 6:30 p.m. meeting. OBSERVATION Subcommittees formed without members or a vote, and a misreading of when a subcommittee meeting is a meeting

The policy subcommittee was created with no members (1:31:30). The superintendent-evaluation committee was filled by volunteers (1:51:41), while the draft CBI before the board says the board "will elect a subcommittee of 3-5 members". In the CBI debate a member says a five-member subcommittee of a twelve-member board meets "without it being an official meeting" (1:45:34). That is not the test. RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) makes "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto" a public body in its own right, so a quorum of the subcommittee convened on its business is a meeting under RSA 91-A:2, I, with notice and minutes, whatever its size relative to the full board. Another member states the rule correctly at 1:47:10, and Erickson says the evaluation committee has minutes for its meetings. Those minutes are not among the documents in this packet. The August 23 retreat may be held at a member's house (1:56:40); a retreat of a quorum is a meeting of the full board and needs the same notice.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (public body includes committees and subcommittees); RSA 91-A:2, I and II (meeting; notice and minutes); CBI revision proposed
