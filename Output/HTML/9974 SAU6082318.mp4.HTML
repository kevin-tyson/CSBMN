SAU 6 Board, August 23, 2018

A meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity board of SAU 6 that hired the New Hampshire School Boards Association to run the superintendent search for a $12,000 fee on an 8-0 roll call with one abstention, deferred the makeup of the search committee to September 13, set the SAU budget calendar with a November public hearing, tabled strategic planning until January on a 5-3-1 vote, and went into nonpublic session. Three of the five Unity members were absent. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. OBSERVATION Whatever its makeup, the superintendent search committee will be a public body

The board debated a community committee against a board subcommittee (0:51:07 to 1:31:21) and deferred the choice to September 13. The method described has the committee set criteria in a half-day session (0:19:23), screen applications and telephone interviews (0:34:06), and score recorded finalist interviews as a group (0:38:19); Bill said he has let "anybody who wanted to" watch and score the videos (1:26:27).

RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) includes in the definition of a public body "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto". A search committee of the SAU board, advisory or not, therefore owes 24-hour notice and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. Candidate deliberation can be taken into nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(b), "The hiring of any person as a public employee", but only on a motion stating that exemption and a roll-call vote under I(b). None of this was discussed on the record; it is a reviewer's checklist for the September meetings.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1); RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification); RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and II(b) (2017 codification)

OBSERVATION A recess was voted while, by a member's count, the board lacked a quorum

While the tabling motion was being reworded, two or more members were out of the room and Horsky said the board was "one shy of corn [quorum]" (1:48:34). The chair asked whether a recess needed a roll call; Sprague said a headcount would do (1:49:24, 1:49:31); a voice vote was taken (1:49:48) and the board resumed about six and a half minutes later (1:56:31), when the motion was restated and passed with nine members voting.

RSA 91-A:2, I defines a meeting as "the convening of a quorum of the membership of a public body". A body without a quorum present cannot act, though a recess decides nothing of substance. With twelve members and nine present, three absences from the room would leave six, one short of a majority, which matches Horsky's count. The business that followed was transacted with a quorum.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I (2017 codification)

POSITIVE The SAU budget calendar includes a published budget and a public hearing that the statute does not require

O'Neill built the calendar back from RSA 194-C:9 (1:32:25): a first look on September 13, approval of a budget for publication on October 18, newspaper publication "between 21 and 14 days before" a public hearing, and final adoption after the hearing (1:32:55 to 1:36:39). The chair caught that the November 9 date fell on a Friday and moved it to November 8 (1:34:18); Towle offered to help publicize a hearing that last year drew perhaps two members of the public (1:36:01, 1:36:51).

RSA 194-C:9, I, as it read in 2018, requires only that "At a meeting held before January 1, the school administrative unit board shall adopt a budget", apportioned between the districts; it says nothing about publication or a hearing. Publishing the budget in summary and holding a hearing before adoption goes beyond the statutory minimum.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I (2017 codification; source note 1996, 298:3; 2003, 279:1)
