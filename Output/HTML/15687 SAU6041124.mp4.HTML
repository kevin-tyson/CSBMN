HIGH The annual organization chose a chair and vice chair but no secretary or treasurer, and the treasurer seat stayed empty for a year

RSA 194-C:5, I requires the SAU board to "meet between April 1 and June 1 in each year" and to "organize by choosing a chairperson, a secretary, and a treasurer" (statute). Item II of this agenda, "Election of Board Officers", lists only "a) Chair" and "b) Vice Chair", and the draft minutes record only those two elections (0:00:54 to 0:03:08); vice chair is not one of the statutory offices. The board's only later gathering inside the window, the May 29, 2024 retreat, recorded a self-assessment and SWOT discussion and no election (draft retreat minutes), and the SAU 6 meeting scheduled for June 13 is not in the record. The SAU's own April 10, 2025 draft minutes then record that "Arlene Hawkins noted that the SAU Board currently has no treasurer" after the auditor said the treasurer should review the check register, and the board elected Candace Crawford treasurer that night. The chair calls clerk Noelle Kronberg Our secretary (0:03:20), but she is the board's employed clerk named on the masthead, and no record shows the board choosing her as an officer. The 3/31/24 statement carries a $1,500 "School Board Secretary" expense on a line with a $0.00 budget.

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, I (annual organization; 1996, 298:3, never amended); draft minutes, item II; 5.29.24 SAU6 Retreat draft minutes; 4.10.25 draft SAU6 minutes, item IV

MEDIUM By the chair's account the SAU had no Title IX grievance policy under the 2020 regulations until this night

The 2020 Title IX rule, effective August 14, 2020, required recipients to "adopt, publish, and implement grievance procedures" and to follow the formal-complaint grievance process at 34 CFR 106.45. Presenting policy ACAC, the chair says "These regulations went into effect in 2020, but we did not implement. We have no policy that is this regarding this, other than that G.B. a" (0:18:58), and the NHSBA advised adoption "on an emergency basis" (0:19:33). Koski's point that the regulations bind "Whether we have a policy or not" (0:19:56) is correct, and the board fixed the gap tonight by adopting ACAC unanimously. What the record does not show is how the SAU handled complaints in the intervening years. This rests on the chair's statement; the earlier policy GBAA was not in the packet.

Sources: 85 FR 30026 (May 19, 2020), 34 CFR 106.8 and 106.45, effective August 14, 2020

OBSERVATION A six-month seal with a stated reason that tracks the statute loosely, and a term that ran out in October

RSA 91-A:3, III allows nonpublic minutes to be withheld by a recorded two-thirds vote in public session where it is determined that disclosure would likely affect a non-member's reputation, "render the proposed action ineffective", or pertain to terrorism. The vote was a roll call in public session (0:12:39), recorded in the minutes as "all present voting in favor", which clears two-thirds. That reason fits the ineffective-action ground, though neither record states the determination in the statute's terms; the chair recited the statutory grounds correctly later the same night (0:30:22). This is graded below the MEDIUM given to the December 14, 2023 seal, where no reason at all was stated. The six-month term set by the board ran to about October 11, 2024; whether the minutes were released then is outside this record.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (2023 codification); draft minutes, item IV

OBSERVATION A transfer policy that cites a statute naming districts, not SAUs, adopted while lines ran over budget with no transfers made

Policy DBJ as presented says "All transfers of appropriations will be made consistent with the provisions of RSA 32:10", and the chair called it "consistent with RSA 3210 [32:10]" (0:34:55). RSA 32:10 lets a governing body transfer between appropriations, but RSA 32:1 states chapter 32's application in terms of towns, districts, school districts and village districts and does not name school administrative units, whose budget authority sits in RSA 194-C:9. The policy also cites "RSA 282-A:71,III", an unemployment-compensation provision not checked for this page.
