OBSERVATION Labeled an SAU 6 meeting, but the recording shows a community forum with no board business

MAP.md files this recording as an SAU 6 Board meeting, and the Cablecast title reads “SAU6 Meeting - Elementary School Restructuring Proposal - 9/20/21”. The recording itself shows a forum run by the superintendent, open to the public in person and online: no call to order, no roll call, no motion and no vote. The superintendent had announced it to the Claremont board on September 15 as a forum (September 15 page), and here he describes the format: “We set out to be remote and we also set out to be in person.” (0:00:15). Under RSA 91-A:2, I, as in force in 2021, a meeting is the "convening of a quorum of the membership of a public body" (statute text); no board member is identified on the recording, so nothing shows that a quorum of either board convened, and the minutes duty of RSA 91-A:2, II attaches only to meetings. That is why this page carries no missing-minutes flag. If board members attended in numbers, that is not visible here.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I and II (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION The evidence offered was preliminary: an unweighted survey and no cost or transportation model

The superintendent described the survey as “incredibly incomplete”… and without identifying information (0:30:45, 0:31:05); a participant said she could respond twice (0:51:07), and another that its questions admitted only one answer (0:40:58). The proposal is described as “supposed to be neutral or less money” (0:40:32), but no cost figures were shown, transportation, which he called “our biggest concern”, had not been modeled (0:35:25), and the capacity estimate was called “the preliminary run in sketch of it” (0:57:29). Several numbers in the configuration slides are garbled in the transcript (0:20:08). None of this breaches a rule; the superintendent presented it as a starting point. It records that, as of this forum, the public had no cost analysis to weigh, and the board’s earlier talk of putting restructuring to voters had never become a motion (0:27:49, 0:28:30).
