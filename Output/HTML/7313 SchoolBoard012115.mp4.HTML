OBSERVATION Restraint-and-seclusion policy still at second reading after the statutory duty took effect

RSA 126-U:2, as amended effective September 30, 2014, required "Each facility and school" to "have a written policy and procedures for managing the behavior of children" that describes "how and under what circumstances seclusion or restraint is used". Before that amendment the section already required a written policy on restraint (2010, 375:2, eff. September 1, 2010). On January 21, 2015 the board approved policy JKAA only on second reading, with a third reading to follow (2:26:03, 2:26:37). The recording does not show whether an earlier restraint policy was in force, so this is an observation about timing, not a finding that the district lacked a policy.

Sources: RSA 126-U:2 (2015 codification; 2014, 324:4, eff. Sept. 30, 2014); RSA 126-U:2 (2014 codification; 2010 text)

OBSERVATION Transportation policy advanced while a resident's dispute hearing was pending
