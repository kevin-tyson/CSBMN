Claremont School Board, Budget Public Hearing, January 15, 2025

Statutory public hearing on the proposed FY2026 budget ($42,933,564 on the warrant; default $42,772,778) and the support-staff and administrators' contracts, followed by a short board meeting. Three members of the public spoke at the hearing, in four turns, and all three addressed the suspension of the district preschool program. No budget vote was taken; the board set it for January 21. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid. They are not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule or document it rests on, and a reader should check the cited source and the recording before relying on it.

MEDIUM Default budget: the eliminated-positions reduction is not shown, and no default-budget form is in the hearing packet

RSA 40:13, IX(b) defines the default budget as the prior year's operating appropriations adjusted for debt service, contracts and mandated obligations, “and reduced by one-time expenditures contained in the operating budget and by salaries and benefits of positions that have been eliminated in the proposed budget” (statute text). Henry read the definition aloud (0:02:24) and stopped after the one-time-expenditures clause. Her account of the reductions names the bond payment, out-of-district tuition and transportation and other contractual items (0:02:47, 0:03:26), and nothing for eliminated positions, in the year the board suspended the preschool classroom (0:08:29) and made FTE adjustments for teachers and para positions (0:28:21).

RSA 40:13, XI(a) also requires a default budget form that identifies each change by account code, including “Reductions for eliminated positions and benefit expenditures as defined under subparagraph IX(b)” (statute text). The hearing packet holds the agenda, the budget summary, the V8 proposed budget and the December 18 draft minutes; no default-budget form was found in it. The default figure itself was disclosed at the hearing, as XI(a) requires. At the January 21 meeting (show 16286) Henry said the defaults were ready to submit to the Department of Revenue Administration, so a form was likely prepared; none was found in the hearing packet. Whether the $42,772,778 default already excludes the preschool and other eliminated positions cannot be determined from these records; IX(b) excludes vacant positions under recruitment and redefined positions, so some of the FTE changes may not count. The default budget form, if one was completed, would settle it. Graded MEDIUM as a probable process gap: the default cannot be amended by the voters (RSA 40:13, XI(b)).

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) (default budget definition), XI(a) (disclosure at the first hearing; the default budget form), XI(b) (not amendable); Claremont Proposed Budget FY26 V8 1-13-25.pdf

OBSERVATION The 5% goal is stated two ways, and the record meets one and not the other

Sprague told the hearing the Finance Committee's goal was “to limit the tax impact of the budget to a 5% increase” (0:05:11). The approved minutes record it as “The primary goal was to limit the budget increase to 5%.” The figures in the record are an 8.14% increase in the general fund ($36,497,266 to $39,468,564) and 7.44% in the total appropriation ($39,962,266 to $42,933,564), both from the budget summary, and Henry's 2.1% net of new revenue (0:18:06). On the tax rate, Henry gave Article 2's impact as 27 cents (1.9%) for the budget and 42 cents (2.9%) for the first-year teachers' contract (0:23:18), about 4.8% together on her own percentages, which would meet a 5% tax-impact goal; the two other contracts bring the total to 89 cents. In December the Finance Committee had worked toward a 4% total increase (its December 18, 2024 session, show 16226, not this meeting). Read as Sprague said it, the goal appears to have been met for Article 2; read as the minutes record it, it was not. Not a rule question; noted so the deliberative-session figures can be read against it.

Sources: Claremont Budget Summary 1-13-25.pdf; approved 1.15.25 minutes

OBSERVATION Budget summary: one change cell does not foot, and the hearing slide misstated the warrant total

In the budget summary posted for the hearing, the “Total other Funds” row shows a change of “$ (32,751)” and “-2.68%,” the debt-service change alone. Its own columns run from $1,222,574 to $1,704,659, an increase of $482,085, because the new “Teachers Contract” line ($514,836) carries no change entry. The general-fund and total-budget increases ($2,971,297; 8.14% and 7.44%) are correct and include the contract. The sheet also carries a stray “8,228,280” in its header. Separately, Henry's slide gave the Article 2 total with the wrong leading figure; Pratt caught it and she corrected it to $42,933,564 on the record (0:22:50). RSA 32:5, IV requires comparative columns, and the sheet has them; the defect is arithmetic in one cell, not a missing disclosure.

Sources: Claremont Budget Summary 1-13-25.pdf; RSA 32:5, IV (comparative columns; 2023 codification)

OBSERVATION December 18 minutes approved by “no objection” although the agenda says a vote is required

The board-meeting agenda lists “Consent Agenda (vote required)” with the December 18 minutes. The chair asked “Is there any objection to accepting the minutes of December 18th, 2024?” and declared “Seeing none, we'll accept consent agenda as approved” (1:18:01). There was no motion, second or vote; the minutes record “Consent approval” and name no mover. Approval by unanimous consent is a common parliamentary device and no statute requires a recorded vote to approve minutes, so this is noted as a departure from the board's own agenda wording rather than a rule violation. The adjournment was also by consent (1:27:29; minutes “Consent adjournment 7:57pm”).

POSITIVE Hearing held inside the SB 2 calendar, default disclosed with figures that reconcile, and the final vote deferred past the testimony

RSA 40:13, II-a(c) requires SB 2 budget hearings on or before the third Tuesday in January (January 21 in 2025); this hearing was held January 15, and the 17 days to the February 1 deliberative session do not offend RSA 32:5, I because II-a opens “Notwithstanding any other provision of law” (statute text). The default budget was disclosed and presented for questions at this first hearing, as RSA 40:13, XI(a) requires, and Henry's starting point, reduction and increase ($39,962,266 less $6,622,120 plus $9,432,632) reconcile exactly to the $42,772,778 she announced (0:02:47). The public had three-minute turns plus a second turn, and board members answered questions on the spot. The board then declined to finalize the budget the same night (1:27:23) and set the vote for January 21, after public testimony had closed, which is the order RSA 32:5, I describes (“after the conclusion of public testimony shall finalize the budget”).

Sources: RSA 40:13, II-a(c), III, XI(a) (hearing calendar; default disclosure); RSA 32:5, I (hearing; finalize after testimony; 2023 codification)
