Claremont School Board — December 18, 2024

A year earlier, on 20 December 2023, this board moved $36,313,407.97 to its budget hearing. On the third Wednesday of December 2024 it moves nothing, and the chair says on the record why: two collective bargaining agreements are unratified and “we have to be pretty mum on what our bottom line is going to be.” The only FY26 figure spoken all evening is the default budget, $39,791,261, produced between two meetings held the same day, disclosed with no worksheet, no comparison and no document, and $2,981,517 lower than the default budget the same officer presented at the public hearing twenty-eight days later. The evening’s one vote about money is for something that was not on the agenda when the public read it: an $857,592 seven-year lease, added at the table, moved with the wrong company name, and authorised the same night against a contract that is in no public share. The Finance Committee, which had spent the afternoon in the same room with five of the same people, left no minutes at all.

The budget calendar
Claremont votes by official ballot, so RSA 40:13, II-a (which opens “Notwithstanding any other provision of law”) fixes the January dates. For 2025: notice of budget hearings by 14 January, hearings on or before 21 January, warrant and budget posted by 27 January, first session between 1 and 8 February. The agenda’s hearing date of 15 January 2025 is inside that window and is not a breach of RSA 32:5, ¶I’s twenty-five-day interval: II-a displaces it. What the packet does instead is give two different dates for that hearing in two of its own documents (flag 3).
