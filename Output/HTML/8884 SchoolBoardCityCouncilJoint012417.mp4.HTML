OBSERVATION The 4% annual stabilization cut described at the meeting was the law in force; the dollar projections are the speakers' own

The mayor said the stabilization grants "were going to be reduced by 4% on an annual basis" under law already passed (0:02:01). RSA 198:41, IV(d), as amended through 2016, paid each municipality its education grant plus a percentage of its fiscal 2012 stabilization grant: "96 percent for fiscal year 2017", reduced by 4 percent of the 2012 amount for each fiscal year after that. The description at the meeting matches the statute. The superintendent's figures (almost $400,000 lost this fiscal year, over $1 million by 2020, $5.8 million over 18 years, 0:05:40, 0:06:10) were not checked against a state aid schedule and are reported as his statements. HB 525 was pending in the House Education Committee on the meeting date; this page does not report its outcome.

Sources: RSA 198:41, IV(d) (2016 codification; source note ends 2015, 276:139; 2016, 8:4, 5; 85:9, eff. July 18, 2016)

OBSERVATION Lead screening proposed as a condition of registration, by analogy to vaccination, without a stated legal basis

The chair proposed that "all kids that come in had to have been screened to be able to register", comparing it to vaccination (0:45:32, 0:45:35). The vaccination rule he cited rests on statute: RSA 141-C:20-a bars admission to school unless a child shows immunization, partial immunization or an exemption. No statute in force in January 2017 that makes lead screening a condition of school admission was found for this page, and RSA 193:1, I places a duty on parents to "cause such child to attend the public school", which a registration bar would have to sit alongside. The mayor's own framing was a screening goal, and the record shows no legal advice on whether screening could be required rather than offered. The policy came back for a first reading on March 15, 2017 (see the March 15, 2017 page); whether it made screening a condition of enrollment should be read in the adopted text.

Sources: RSA 141-C:20-a (2016 codification; source 1987, 193:7; 1995, 310:183): immunization required before school admission; RSA 193:1, I (2016 codification; source note ends 2013, 215:1): parent's duty to cause attendance
