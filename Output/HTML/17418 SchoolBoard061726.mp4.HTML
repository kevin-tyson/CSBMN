MEDIUM Federal grant controls: a recurring monitoring finding on time cards, and about $2 million in FY2026 grant money not yet claimed

Dr. Broadrick reported that federally funded pay requires "certain language" on time cards and that it was added that day (0:26:55, 0:28:41). Whitney said the gap "has been a reoccurring finding from when we've had the fiscal monitoring reports" and that the board had been "assured that it's been done" before (0:28:09, 0:28:24). Federal cost rules require that "Charges to Federal awards for salaries and wages must be based on records that accurately reflect the work performed", supported by internal control (2 CFR 200.430(g)(1), as revised effective October 1, 2024; earlier awards carry the same standard at 200.430(i)). On the same evening the written report states that "for several years the school district has not successfully claimed all the federal funds to which it was entitled" and that "about $2 million" for 2025–26 "can not yet be claimed" (report text); the business administrator says the state has asked him to rebuild every grant "from 2021 to the present" (0:37:01). Mitigation: the money is reimbursement for FY2026 spending, most programs allow costs through September 30 and claims after that (0:33:00), Angell calls it a cash-flow matter (0:34:27), and the superintendent has a written action list and an August 1 target (0:32:10). MEDIUM because a monitoring finding recurred and the claims are late; it becomes a loss only if the claims are not filed in time.

Sources: 2 CFR 200.430(g)(1) (documentation of personnel expenses); 2 CFR 200.303 (internal controls); 20 U.S.C. §1225(b) (carryover limited to one additional year); Claremont SB report, June 17, 2026.

MEDIUM Nonpublic session on "negotiations" cited II(a) and II(c), and the minutes were sealed without a stated finding

The agenda titles the item "Non-Public Meeting: Negotiations RSA 91-A:3, II(a&c)" and prints the text of II(a) (agenda text); the chair states the same grounds on tape (1:10:38) and the roll call is taken, which meets RSA 91-A:3, I(b)'s form. The fit is less clear. Strategy for collective-bargaining negotiations is excluded from the definition of a meeting by RSA 91-A:2, I(a), so it needs no nonpublic exemption at all; II(a) covers "the dismissal, promotion, or compensation of any public employee"; II(c) covers matters likely to harm the reputation of a person "other than a member of the public body itself". The board had just appointed a member to the maintenance negotiation team (1:09:55) and dropped an administration-contract item from public session (1:09:55), so either subject is possible; the record does not say which, and RSA 91-A:3, I(c) confines discussion to the matters in the motion. The seal was voted in public by roll call (1:12:32, 1:12:39), as RSA 91-A:3, III requires, but neither the motion on tape nor the minutes state the required determination (that disclosure would likely harm a person's reputation, render a proposed action ineffective, or concern terrorism preparedness). The seal lapsed on July 1, 2026; a Drive search on 2026-10-03 did not locate released nonpublic minutes for this session, and MAP.md lists none, but Drive search has missed files before, so this is "not found", not "does not exist".

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I(a) (bargaining strategy is not a meeting); RSA 91-A:3, I(b), I(c), II(a), II(c), III (motion, scope, grounds, sealing with a finding); 6.17.26 CSB Meeting Agenda.
