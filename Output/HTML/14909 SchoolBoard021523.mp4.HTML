Claremont School Board, February 15, 2023

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, all six sitting members present, one week after the deliberative session added $1,000,000 back to the proposed FY2024 budget. The administration presents its grade-reconfiguration case (universal pre-K, instructional coaching, class-size data); the board approves the 2023-24 calendar and, on a 4 to 2 roll call, adds $600,000 to the default budget for positions that had been shifted to ESSER and Title funding. OBSERVATION The default budget was raised $600,000 after the deliberative session, and the public record does not show the line items or the amended form

RSA 40:13, XI(b) allows the governing body to adjust the default budget "acting on relevant new information at any time before the ballots are printed, provided the governing body ... completes an amended default budget form" (statute). The board's action fits that window: the motion passed on a recorded roll call (1:41:15) and the superintendent was finalizing the default budget for signatures the next day (1:48:06). The stated basis was that the money funds letter-of-agreement positions moved to ESSER and Title funding (1:10:13), which Skillen described as "positions that were redefined" (1:38:26); IX(b) says eliminated positions "shall not include ... positions redefined in the proposed operating budget, as determined by the governing body" (statute), which supports her reading. What the record lacks: the motion was a round $600,000, no member or administrator named the positions or account codes it covers, and the amended default budget form XI(a)(2) requires (changes "by account code, and the reasons for each change") is not in this meeting's packet. Whitney's objection, that restoring positions only to decide later not to fund them would "artificially inflate the default budget" (1:22:18), is the question the form would answer. The resulting figures, $36,942,948 in default appropriations and $15,845,186 to be raised by taxes, are the chair's on tape (1:39:11).

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) and XI(a)-(b) (2022 codification; source note ends 2019, 192:2, unchanged since); minutes, item III.2

OBSERVATION The default-budget definition read to the board stopped before the clause that decided the motion

Asked to explain the calculation, the business administrator chose to read the statute "as opposed to my interpretation of it" (1:27:48). His reading ends at "positions that have been eliminated in the proposed budget" (1:34:24) and continues with a description of one-time expenditures; it does not include the next sentence's exclusion, "eliminated positions shall not include vacant positions under recruitment or positions redefined in the proposed operating budget, as determined by the governing body", and nothing on the recording mentions XI(b)'s adjustment power. Both bear directly on the motion before the board: the exclusion is the basis Skillen gave, and XI(b) is the authority for acting at all. He closed with "it's up to the board's interpretation of these words" (1:35:43). His remark that the law "was updated in 2018" (1:35:17) is consistent with the source note (2018, 241:1, 2 and 313:1, 2). The project has recorded the same truncation in Claremont's printed default-budget worksheets.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) and XI(b) (2022 codification)

OBSERVATION The legal advice the motion rested on was given off the record, and a member was unsure whether that session was public

Three members relied on counsel's advice: Sprague recalled "talking with ... our attorney" at an earlier meeting and added "I'm assuming that's a public wasn't a non wasn't a non public" (1:11:06); Whitney cited "what I understood from Matt Upton" (1:19:02); Skillen sized her motion at $600,000 rather than $1,000,000 on "explanation from our district's attorney" (1:38:26). Consultation with legal counsel is excluded from the definition of a meeting by RSA 91-A:2, I(b) (statute), so receiving the advice privately is lawful. The recording does not say when or in what setting it was given, and the February 15 minutes do not mention it. A reader cannot tell from the record what counsel advised beyond these members' summaries.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I(b) (2022 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1)
