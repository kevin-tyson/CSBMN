Claremont School Board, Public Hearing & Meeting, January 7, 2026

A short public hearing at which the board voted to apply for and accept a $49,775 New Hampshire Charitable Foundation grant for middle-school band and chorus, followed by the regular meeting. The interim business administrator said he saw "a path forward with wiping out the deficit" and would not recommend a deficit warrant article. After a public-comment period of 39 minutes in all, most of it from teachers and parents asking for stability, the board voted 4–3 to send both four-school budgets ($46,211,951 current configuration; $44,844,373 aligned) to the January 20 hearing and rejected the chair's modified three-school budget 3–4. It also ratified the administrators' tentative agreement and elected a 0.6 FTE middle-school music teacher. MEDIUM The motion for nonpublic session did not state a lettered exemption, and the minutes cite a different basis than the one spoken

RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires a motion to enter nonpublic session to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II". As spoken at 0:28:09, the motion cited "the RSA 91 A on three Roman numeral two as related to litigation" with no letter, and, for the administrators' negotiations, "RSA 91 a colon to the numeral. I mean one excuse me, letter A as related to contact negotiations". RSA 91-A:2, I(a) is not a paragraph II exemption: it excludes collective-bargaining strategy from the definition of a meeting. The minutes and the nonpublic form then record the session as "RSA 91-A:3, II (a & e)", a basis no one stated on the record, and the consent-agenda notes write "Non-public RSA (e&a)". RSA 91-A:3, I(c) confines discussion to the matters set out in the motion. Mitigation: the subjects (litigation, CAA negotiations) were stated aloud, the roll call was taken in public, the nonpublic minutes list who was present, and II(e) plausibly fits a pending special-education cost dispute. The motion was also made by the interim superintendent and adopted by the chair; the minutes credit it to the chair.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), I(c), II(a), II(e) (motion must name the exemption; discussion confined to it); RSA 91-A:2, I(a) (collective-bargaining strategy is not a meeting); 1.7.26 nonpublic minutes

MEDIUM A decision reached in nonpublic session reached the public share about 17 hours after the 72-hour window

The nonpublic minutes record a 6–1 roll-call vote, Madden opposed, "to authorize Ms. Kennedy to direct the district's attorney to pursue litigation regarding the district's responsibility to pay court ordered out of state special education costs for a student under state custody" (nonpublic minutes).
