Claremont School Board, December 16, 2020

Regular board meeting held in person and by video during the COVID-19 state of emergency. MEDIUM The board voted on making Stevens High School fully remote, and the result was never announced

The chair's motion to support the administration's proposal, including "going remote for third quarter at the high school" (1:24:10), went to a roll call at 1:33:04. The transcript captures only the clerk calling "Rebecca." (1:33:21) and then nothing for about 53 seconds; no tally and no result are stated. The chair's next words, asking that the item return "if there were some substantive changes" (1:34:15), suggest the motion did not carry, but the recording does not say so, and the January 6 meeting approved these minutes without discussion. Ten minutes later, in the adjournment roll call, the clerk could not hear the remote member Zullo (1:37:05) and the recording ends before her answer. RSA 91-A:2, III(c) required that members participating remotely "be able to simultaneously hear each other and speak to each other", III(e) required a roll call on every vote, and II required the minutes to record final decisions. A roll call whose answers the clerk could not hear, on the meeting's main decision, with no announced result, is a probable process gap rather than a confirmed breach; the minutes approved on January 6 would settle it and are among the records flag 1 finds missing.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II, III(c) and III(e) (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION Board members heard staff on the Stevens proposal at a roundtable held outside a meeting, with attendance capped to avoid a quorum

The superintendent said board members, including the chair, sat in on staff roundtable forums that day and that "We can't have the whole board in would be an odd meeting. We can only have three." (0:40:02). Petrin and Sprague both draw on what was said there (1:09:39, 1:06:32). Under RSA 91-A:2, I a "meeting" is "the convening of a quorum"; with seven members a quorum is four, so three members attending was not a meeting, and keeping the number at three was the lawful way to do it. RSA 91-A:2-a, II adds that communications outside a meeting "shall not be used to circumvent the spirit and purpose" of the chapter. Nothing on the record suggests deliberation among the three, and the superintendent summarized what staff said for the public (0:40:24). The point is that the teachers' testimony that shaped the debate was heard by part of the board in a session the public could not attend.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I (2019 codification); RSA 91-A:2-a, II (source note 2008, 303:4, unamended)

OBSERVATION A remote member says she was sending written notes during the deliberation

Towle: "I've been sending in notes as we've been discussing, I, you know, I figured everybody was reading them" (1:29:31). The recording does not show whether these went to a public chat that viewers could read or privately to members or staff. RSA 91-A:2, III(c), in the text then in force, ends: "No meeting shall be conducted by electronic mail or any other form of communication that does not permit the public to hear, read, or otherwise discern meeting discussion contemporaneously". If the notes were visible only to members, part of the deliberation took place where the public could not follow it; if they went to a public chat, there is no issue. The district's video-platform chat log, if kept, would settle it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(c) (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION Two weeks after the board approved the Goshen agreement, its tuition terms were described as still under negotiation

RSA 91-A:3, II(k) provides that a negotiated pupil tuition contract "shall be made public prior to its consideration for approval". The legal question is recorded on the December 2, 2020 page; this flag records the later evidence.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, II(k) (2019 codification)
