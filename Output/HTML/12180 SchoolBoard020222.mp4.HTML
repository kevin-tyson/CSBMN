Claremont School Board, February 2, 2022

Regular board meeting held at the Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center one week before the district's deliberative session. The board and a larger than usual audience discussed the proposed fiscal 2023 reductions at the tech center, the proposed reduction of the preschool to three classrooms, a school board candidate forum and the district's COVID protocols, then voted to enter a nonpublic session, where the recording ends. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting were located, although later minutes show the board approved this meeting's minutes on June 15, 2022. OBSERVATION The preschool discussion turned on legal duties and a state ratio that no one cited

Petrin says the district does "not have to run a pre-K program" and can deliver services to identified children elsewhere; the coordinator agrees "from a legal standpoint" (1:18:03, 1:18:24). Federal law requires a free appropriate public education for children with disabilities from age 3 and education with nondisabled children "to the maximum extent appropriate"; it does not require a general preschool, so the statement is broadly consistent with the statute, but the least-restrictive-environment duty is the reason the half-typical model matters, which the administration's recommendation reflects (1:05:28). POSITIVE A member explains on the record why he will not deliberate by all-board email or on Facebook

Horsky tells the audience that board members cannot respond together on Facebook "because the moment we do, it's considered a meeting", and that he does not answer emails addressed to the whole board because a reply "creates a discussion which creates a quorum" (1:36:17). That is the concern RSA 91-A:2-a addresses: public bodies deliberate only in meetings, and communications outside a meeting, including "sequential communications among members of a public body", may not be used to circumvent the chapter. Stating the practice publicly helps residents understand why members do not answer them collectively.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2-a, I and II (2022 codification; source 2008, 303:4, unamended)
