HIGH A member took part remotely, but every vote was taken by voice, not roll call

Rob Lovett Jr. participated from outside the room throughout (0:00:39, 0:02:02, 1:30:32). Governor Sununu's Emergency Order #12, which relaxed remote-meeting rules, ended with the state of emergency at midnight on June 11, 2021, and the 2021 bill to extend the relief (SB 95) did not pass, so on July 21 the ordinary text of RSA 91-A:2, III governed. It allows remote participation only when attendance is "not reasonably practical", requires the reason to be "stated in the minutes of the meeting", and provides that "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote." Whether the minutes stated a reason for remote attendance cannot be checked, since none survive. Graded HIGH because the roll-call rule is mandatory and the departure is on tape; the votes were procedural and none was contested, which limits the practical harm.

OBSERVATION Education Freedom Accounts described in terms that do not match the enacted statute

The chair told the public that departing students take "the state money and the local money", about $20,000 each (2:19:58), and the vice chair cited an income limit of "something like $43,000 per family" (2:31:03), which the finance administrator said he had not known (2:31:36). RSA ch. 194-F, enacted in the 2021 state budget (2021, 91:431) and not in force until August 24, 2021, sets the grant at "the per pupil adequate education grant amount under RSA 198:40-a, plus any differentiated aid", which is state aid, not local money, and limits eligibility to households with income "less than or equal to 300 percent of the federal poverty guidelines", a test that scales with household size rather than a single dollar figure. The speakers' figures may describe local cost per pupil or a particular household size, but as stated they overstate the money that leaves the district and misstate the eligibility rule. This is a speaker characterization corrected against the statute, not a finding about the board's conduct. The chair publicly corrected both points at the next meeting (see the August 4, 2021 page, flag 5).

Sources: RSA 194-F:1 (eligible student; 2022 codification, source note 2021, 91:431, eff. Aug. 24, 2021); RSA 194-F:2 (grant amount)
