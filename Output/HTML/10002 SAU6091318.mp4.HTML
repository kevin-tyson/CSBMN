MEDIUM No agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting survive in any district share

MAP.md records no Drive material for this SAU 6 meeting: neither share holds a year folder older than 2022, and the Meeting Minutes share starts at 2023. The meeting set up the superintendent search committee and reviewed the unit's draft budget, and the recording is now the only public account. RSA 91-A:2, II, as in force throughout 2018, required minutes including "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", open to public inspection "not more than 5 business days after the meeting". This page applies the MEDIUM limb of the project's severity rule because the record mitigates the gap: members had an agenda (0:01:35), a recording secretary took minutes (2:12:29), the chair said SAU 6 minutes were being posted on the web (0:03:26), and the board approved this meeting's minutes "as written" on October 18, 2018 (October 18, 2018 page). What is missing is the public copy today, not proof that minutes were never kept. The 2018 rule did not yet require minutes to name the mover and seconder; that sentence took effect January 1, 2019.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification; the text for all of 2018)

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, III (2017 codification; source note 1996, 298:3, never amended)

OBSERVATION The new search committee is itself a public body; confidentiality has to run through the Right-to-Know exemptions

The board created a seven-member committee of four board members, one SAU staff member and two district staff members (2:16:10), after hearing advice that it must be "advisory only", that non-board members "sign a confidentiality agreement", and that the search requires confidentiality (1:47:57, 1:48:24). RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) defines a public body to include a board's "committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", so the committee carries its own notice and minutes duties under RSA 91-A:2 whether or not its members form a quorum of either board; Horsky's quorum concern (1:41:47) decides only whether a parent board is also meeting. Applicant confidentiality is available through a nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(b) ("The hiring of any person as a public employee"), entered by a motion naming the exemption and a roll call; a private confidentiality agreement does not replace that procedure. Nothing on this recording shows how the committee later met, so this is an observation for the pages that follow.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1, so the text in force in 2018); RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification); RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and II(b) (2017 codification)

Horsky's remark that he was "going off what was tight" [typed] (2:53:33) suggests a prepared text carried a typo. The board did land on a real exemption and took a roll call, so the procedure was met as far as the tape shows. The tape ends during the roll call (2:55:28), and with no minutes surviving, the motion's final wording, the vote, what was decided, and whether those minutes were sealed by the recorded two-thirds vote RSA 91-A:3, III requires cannot be checked.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(a) and III (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017; 280:1)

OBSERVATION The chair named a candidate for governor while introducing the board's letter to candidates

Introducing a letter on school funding to be sent to all candidates, the chair said "Molly Kelly is a strong proponent, I think, for state for school funding." (2:29:03) and then cautioned "We have to be really careful as board members about speaking publicly about supporting stand" (2:29:27). RSA 659:44-a bars electioneering only by a "public employee, as defined in RSA 273-A:1, IX", and RSA 273-A:1, IX(a) excludes "Persons elected by popular vote", so the statute does not reach an elected board member. The remark is recorded here for completeness; the board's stated plan was to send the same letter to every candidate (2:29:27), and it was deferred to October 18.

Sources: RSA 659:44-a, I and III (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 176:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017); RSA 273-A:1, IX(a) (2019 codification; source note ends 2014, 13:1, 2)
