SAU 6 Board, November 9, 2023

Regular meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity board, chaired by Arlene Hawkins. The board appointed Noelle Kronberg its clerk, heard the business administrator's first pass at the FY2025 SAU budget, adopted policy GBE, named a three-member policy subcommittee, and adopted four objectives with key results drafted by facilitator Dr. David Ryan for the superintendent's evaluation cycle. A minutes amendment about the suspended superintendent evaluation was accepted in softened words its mover called "a little whitewash". MEDIUM The superintendent evaluation subcommittee was to meet off camera right after the board; no notice or minutes were located

The chair reported that the evaluation subcommittee (chaired by Atonya Hart) was meeting with Superintendent Tempesta and facilitator David Ryan "Right after this meeting" (0:24:33), and at adjournment: "it's going to be a meeting after this in one of the other classrooms" (1:02:20). The minutes confirm the subcommittee was "meeting with Superintendent Tempesta and David Ryan". A subcommittee of the SAU board is a public body: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) reaches "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", and RSA 91-A:2, II requires notice at least 24 hours ahead and minutes open to inspection within five business days. The board's own agenda does not notice the session. No subcommittee minutes were found in this meeting's packet folder or in the document lists MAP.md records for the later SAU 6 folders (December 7 and 14, 2023; January 11, February 15, April 11 and September 12, 2024), and a Drive title search for evaluation documents created between November 2023 and June 2024 returned nothing, a result that by itself proves little. The Claremont Meeting Minutes share has never held an SAU 6 file. This page applies the MEDIUM limb rather than HIGH because the record shows the session announced and scheduled, not that a quorum of the subcommittee convened: the tape ends at adjournment. If it did convene, the absence of notice and minutes is unmitigated.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (subcommittees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, II (2023 codification: 24-hour notice; minutes within five business days)

OBSERVATION A minutes correction about not following the superintendent's contract was recorded in softer words

A member, Marjorie Erickson per the minutes, asked that the October 12 minutes record "the discussion that we had as to why we're not following the superintendent's contract or our approved policy on the topic" (0:07:02) and, as proposed wording, "the current methodology being followed by the school board not aligning with the superintendent's contract or the policy that was approved" (0:07:33). The chair recast it as "a discussion about the board vote to suspend" the superintendent evaluation; the member replied "It's a little whitewash, but I'll accept it." (0:08:21). The approved minutes read: "Marjorie Erickson wishes for the minutes to include a note under section IV (School Board Work Session) that there was a discussion pertaining to a vote to suspend the superintendent evaluation." RSA 91-A:2, II requires only "a brief description of the subject matter discussed", and the board adopted the wording with the member's assent, so this is not a violation. The October 12 draft minutes in this packet show what was suspended: a motion "to suspend the application of Policy CBE - Superintendent Evaluation and Goal Setting for this evaluation cycle since we are already past the guidelines". The board terminated Tempesta's contract on January 11, 2024.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (minutes: brief description of subject matter and final decisions); _SAU 6 unapproved School Board Agenda minutes 10.12.23.docx (3).pdf
