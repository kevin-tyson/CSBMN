MEDIUM No agenda, packet or minutes of this joint meeting are publicly available from the district

The record shows an agenda and a packet existed: the chair says “Your missing from your agenda is approval of the minutes” (0:01:27) and the superintendent says “the presentation is what's in your packet” (1:51:43). The joint body also keeps minutes: it approved those of its January 30 meeting at 0:01:58. None of these documents is in the district's public Drive shares, which do not reach back before July 2022. A joint meeting is two public bodies; the council side's notice and minutes would be held by the city clerk, and this page did not inspect the city's records, so it makes no finding about them.

RSA 91-A:2, II, in the 2017 codification that governed all of 2018, requires minutes that include the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the business, open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting. The mover-and-seconder requirement did not exist until January 1, 2019 and is not applied here.

Severity limb: MEDIUM, not HIGH, because the record mitigates the absence. The meeting was agendaed and packeted and the body demonstrably makes minutes; what is missing is a public copy reachable today, not evidence that the minutes were never written.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification; minutes within 5 business days)

OBSERVATION A joint Medicaid letter was to be signed off outside the meeting, with no motion or vote

At 2:05:04 the mayor proposed drafting a letter to the House Finance Committee supporting Medicaid expansion funding and asked whether the bodies would sign off once they had seen it; the draft would go out the next day through the council clerk and the superintendent (2:06:55, 2:07:16). The only response on the recording is an unidentified “I think that's okay.” No motion was made and no vote taken by either body.

RSA 91-A:2, I(d) excludes from the definition of a meeting only the circulation of draft documents which, when finalized, are intended only to formalize decisions previously made in a meeting, and RSA 91-A:2-a, II bars communications outside a meeting, including sequential communications among members, used to circumvent the chapter. Neither body recorded a decision that the letter would formalize. Whether the letter was sent, and over whose names, is not in the record, so this is an Observation, not a finding.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I(d) (2017 codification; draft documents formalizing prior decisions); RSA 91-A:2-a, I-II (deliberation only in meetings; no circumvention by outside communications)
