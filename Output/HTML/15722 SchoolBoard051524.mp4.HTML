Claremont School Board, May 15, 2024

A regular meeting at which the board adopts Eureka Squared and Into Math for K–8 ($85,729.28 within an existing $120,000 allocation), votes $50,000 of unbudgeted money for pre-bond architectural planning at the technical center (recorded in the minutes as $54,000), authorizes the chair to sign the teachers' union 403(b) sidebar, accepts the retiree list, and hears the interim superintendent announce an SREB program-of-studies project and a District Management Group strategic plan, with the business administrator projecting about $1 million left in the FY2024 budget. MEDIUM The board voted $50,000 for architectural planning; the minutes record $54,000

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to record "final decisions". The agenda listed the item as "Contract estimate $54,000, unbudgeted". On the recording Crawford moves "the expenditure of the $50,000" (0:58:17) and the chair puts the question as "approving $50,000" (0:58:35). The Lavallee Brensinger proposal in the packet (Exhibit H, dated April 1, 2024) itemizes fees of $15,000, $5,000, $8,000, $8,000, $7,500, $500 and $6,000, which total $50,000 before reimbursable expenses, and Herzog told the board "The actual amount is for $50,000" (0:53:56); at the April 17 meeting (show 15693) he had put the contract at about $54,500. The approved minutes nevertheless record "a motion to approve the spending of the $54,000 as presented for the retainment of architectural services". The board approved the minutes in that form. The motion also named no appropriation: Herzog said the money was "not in this year's budget" and the funding was to come from year-end money (0:55:50); RSA 32:10 lets the board transfer between appropriations within the total voted, but the record does not show which lines the $50,000 came from.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (minutes include final decisions); RSA 32:10 (transfers by the governing body); Exhibit H; approved minutes, item IV.3
