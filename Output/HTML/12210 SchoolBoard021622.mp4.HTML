Claremont School Board, February 16, 2022

Unscheduled board meeting, called after what the chair described as "some urgency over the board over the weekend", with one action item: revising the district's COVID-19 decision-making matrix. On roll-call votes the board amended the motion to keep masks on buses and follow state rules for students returning from isolation, then adopted it 5 to 2, making masks optional in school from Monday, February 21, 2022. OBSERVATION An unscheduled meeting, labeled an "Emergency Session" by CCTV, with no notice or minutes to show how it was called

The chair says "this was not a scheduled meeting" and that the urgency arose "over the weekend" (0:02:36); the Cablecast title calls it an emergency session. RSA 91-A:2, II requires notice of the time and place at least 24 hours in advance, excluding Sundays and legal holidays, "Except in an emergency", which exists where "immediate undelayed action is deemed to be imperative by the chairman or presiding officer"; if a meeting is held as an emergency, "The minutes of the meeting shall clearly spell out the need for the emergency meeting". A weekend request and a Wednesday meeting leave time for ordinary 24-hour notice, and an agenda was before the board (0:01:32), so the meeting may well have been noticed normally and the CCTV label may be informal. The posted notice and the minutes would settle which; neither was located.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification; notice, emergency exception and minutes requirement)
