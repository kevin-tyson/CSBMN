Claremont School Board, July 19, 2023

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, six of six sitting members present, at which the board filled the seat Steven Horsky vacated on June 21: three applicants spoke and Candace Crawford was appointed 4–2 over Kevin Tyson by show of hands. The board also added cetirizine to the nurses' over-the-counter list, discussed raising free-and-reduced lunch applications, tabled a communications-consultant proposal and formed a disruptive-behavior subcommittee. OBSERVATION The three letters of interest reached the public packet folder the morning after the appointment vote

Late in the meeting Jennifer Gallagher reported a complaint that only the candidates' names were public, not their letters (“I received a complaint regarding our process of appointment”, 1:49:39). The chair answered that “the letter of intent was attached to the school board agenda on the Wednesday before this Wednesday” (1:49:56) and then that if it was not public “it was absolutely an oversight” (1:50:49); Arlene Hawkins said she had found “the listing of just the three names” (1:51:04). Drive metadata supports the complaint. In the 16. CSB 7.19.23 folder, Exhibits A, E, F and G were created on Wednesday, July 12, 2023, and the agenda on Friday, July 14; the three letters (Exhibits B, C and D) were created on Thursday, July 20, 2023, at about 11:11 a.m. Eastern time, roughly 15 hours after the vote. Metadata cannot exclude an earlier copy that was later removed, but the superintendent's own explanation at the next meeting points the same way: the letters were not attached “because we didn't get those redacted in time” (show 15228; see the August 2, 2023 page; the August 2 minutes record “an issue redacting the personal information on each letter of intent prior to the meeting”). No statute requires a board to post its packet, and the letters were public records open to inspection on request under RSA 91-A:4, I, so this is an observation rather than a rule-based finding. Its weight is practical: the public heard each candidate's five-minute statement but could not read the letters, which the chair cited in nominating Tyson (0:54:10, 0:54:43). Disclosure: one of the three letters is Kevin Tyson's, the maintainer of this project.

Sources: RSA 91-A:4, I (public records open to inspection); Drive metadata for the folder's files (appendix); the August 2, 2023 minutes.

OBSERVATION A new disruptive-behavior subcommittee was formed by consensus, with its membership left unclear

Gallagher proposed “a disruptive behavior subcommittee meet with Ben and then the court players and then report back to the board” (1:44:21); the chair asked her to chair it (1:45:51) and asked Chelsea to post it on the web page. No motion or vote was taken, and the record does not settle the members (see flag 2). Under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) a public body includes “any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof” (statute), so once the subcommittee meets it carries the notice and minutes duties of RSA 91-A:2, II. On August 2 it had not yet met (August 2, 2023 page). This is noted so its later meetings can be checked against those duties.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d); RSA 91-A:2, II.

POSITIVE The vacancy was filled in public, on published questions, by a named hand vote

The chair read the governing statute into the record (0:25:48), whose ¶II reads “The school board shall fill vacancies occurring on the school board” and whose ¶II(a) provides that appointees “shall serve until the next district election” (statute); she then stated the term as “until the March 2024 election” (0:29:33), which matches. She summarized advice from district counsel Matt Upton on what the board could not do, among them “We cannot rank choice vote” (0:27:57). The three written questions (Exhibit F) and the process memo (Exhibit E) were posted to the public folder on July 12, a week ahead. Each candidate spoke in public for five minutes and took follow-up questions under a motion Sprague moved and the chair seconded (0:30:39, 0:31:34). Nominations were made with stated reasons and seconds, and the vote was by show of hands; the minutes name the mover and seconder of each motion and nomination and the four members voting yes, as RSA 91-A:2, II requires for motions. RSA 671:33 itself requires none of the published questions, the candidate presentations or the named vote. The process departed from the July 11 memo, which called for voice votes on nominations and appointment, but the chair announced the changes before the item began and invited motions to modify them (0:28:19).

Sources: RSA 671:33, II and II(a) (source note ends 2021, 42:1 and 91:318; in force on July 19, 2023); RSA 91-A:2, II; Exhibits E and F.
