MEDIUM Minutes of three earlier Policy Subcommittee meetings are still not public

This meeting's own minutes say the minutes of the subcommittee's June 17, August 19 and October 7, 2026 meetings "will need to be approved before the 18th." The chair opened on the same point: "I think these minutes, if possible, using our handy dandy robot friends and the drafts we already have, I think, you know, before the 18th, we need them" (0:00:12). A subcommittee of a school board is a public body (RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d)), and RSA 91-A:2, II requires its minutes to be "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting"; a draft satisfies that, and approval is not a precondition. As of November 13, 2026 no Policy Subcommittee minutes for those dates were found in the Meeting Minutes share's 2027 Meeting Minutes folder, in the 5. 8.19.26 -- Policy Subcommittee packet folder (which holds only the agenda) or in the district's empty Claremont Policy Sub Committee folder; the 2026 year folder was not rechecked this run. In mitigation, the minutes of this meeting were posted in its packet folder on November 12, 2026 (Drive creation date), the fifth business day after the meeting with Veterans Day excluded, and the chair told the board that evening the minutes "will be submitted" (11.4.26 DRAFT CSB Minutes).

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (subcommittees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, II (minutes within five business days; drafts suffice); CSB Policy Sub-Committee Meeting Minutes (1).pdf

OBSERVATION The annual investment-policy review is the board's duty, and DFA was set aside

The chair noted that DFA (investments) "says we should review it annually" (0:41:12) and recalled "We passed DF a revision in October, I think, or last October" (0:46:04); the closing summary put DFA among items "not different enough to change ... so we don't do anything with those" (0:55:22). RSA 197:23-a, IV says "At least yearly, the school board shall review and adopt an investment policy for the investment of public funds." The duty runs to the board, not the subcommittee. If DFA was last adopted in October 2025, the board's 2026 review and adoption is due; a subcommittee decision that no edits are needed does not by itself meet it. The record here does not show when the board last adopted DFA.

Sources: RSA 197:23-a, IV (yearly review and adoption of an investment policy)

POSITIVE Signature authority for checks and contracts tightened (DGA)

The subcommittee agreed to bring the board a DGA revision built on the superintendent's draft (0:36:30, 0:40:34). As the minutes record it, checks on the general fund or any special fund "require the signature of the school district treasurer who is authorized to sign only after approval of a manifest by the school board or a duly authorized subcommittee thereof," and "Any multi-year contract or any contract binding the district to an expense greater than $5,000 may be signed only by the superintendent or business administrator, unless the superintendent delegates such authority in writing." The check language tracks RSA 197:23-a, I, under which the treasurer pays out money "only upon orders of the school board." The superintendent tied the $5,000 line to the three-quote procurement threshold so purchases are checked "before the auditors catch it at the end of the fiscal year" (0:42:56). The chair asked for the open-ended "other district personnel" language to be struck, which the superintendent questioned and the chair said matched Mr. Lavalette's concern (0:40:19).

Sources: RSA 197:23-a, I (treasurer pays only on the board's orders); CSB Policy Sub-Committee Meeting Minutes (1).pdf

POSITIVE Bullying policy brought into line with the 2026 change in state law (JIC)

The superintendent reported that "all they changed in the law is the, imbalance of power element" and that the district's policy still refers twice to an alleged victim's perceived imbalance of power; striking those two references would "come into compliance with the only change in state law" (0:53:57, 0:55:08). 2026 N.H. Laws ch. 205 (HB 131) §4 repealed RSA 193-F:3, I(b), the "imbalance of power" clause of the definition of bullying; the act was approved July 2, 2026 and that section took effect 30 days after passage. The chair queued the change for the November 18 board meeting (0:55:22). The district's policy has lagged the statute since about August 2026; the gc.nh.gov page for RSA 193-F:3 may not yet show the repeal.

Sources: 2026 N.H. Laws ch. 205 (HB 131), §§4–5 (repeal of RSA 193-F:3, I(b); effective date); RSA 193-F:3
