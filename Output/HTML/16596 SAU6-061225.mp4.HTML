SAU 6 Board, June 12, 2025

Regular meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity SAU board. The superintendent presented the FY25 budget report without line-item transfers; the treasurer reported that a former board member was still the signer on the SAU account; the board set three meetings for 2025-26; and it met in nonpublic session and sealed those minutes for a year. MEDIUM The nonpublic minutes were sealed for one year with no recorded determination of the statutory ground for withholding them

After the nonpublic session the recording resumes on a fragment, "For one year.", followed by a second and a roll call answered Yes by each voice (0:23:58 to 0:24:11). The draft minutes record the motion as "Heather Whitney made a motion to seal the minutes for 1 year, Rocco Ruggeri seconded the motion" with "all present voting in favor" (minutes text). RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes to be "publicly disclosed within 72 hours" unless, "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person" other than a board member, or one of the section's other listed grounds applies (statute text). The vote margin satisfies the two-thirds requirement: all eight members present voted yes. What neither the minutes nor the surviving recording shows is the determination itself, the finding that names which statutory ground supports withholding. The motion's full wording is not on the recording, which joins the moment of entering nonpublic session to the end of the sealing motion.

The nonpublic session was entered under RSA 91-A:3, II(a) and (c), and (c) is the reputation ground, so a determination of the same kind may have been intended. The flag is a documentation gap, not a finding that the minutes were wrongly sealed. A sealing for one year would expire about June 12, 2026; this page did not locate unsealed minutes. The April 10, 2025 draft minutes record the same form of motion ("made a motion to seal the minutes for 1 year") with no determination either (April 10 minutes text).

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (72-hour disclosure; 2/3 recorded vote with a determination to withhold; source note ends 2023, 189:1); Draft minutes 6.12.25 (seal motion)

OBSERVATION A former board member was still the signer on the SAU account two years after leaving the board, and no one was signing checks

Reporting on the auditor's April advice, the chair said "we were told that it cannot be a member of the board who signs the checks. So we don't have anybody currently signing the check." (0:15:16). Candace Crawford, elected SAU treasurer on April 10, 2025, then reported that a former board member "was the signer on the account still", that "he was signing on the checks, and I was told as a board member I can't sign the check." and that "he hasn't been on the board for two years, and he's still his signature is what's on the checks and on the account, and that needs to be straightened out." (0:15:53). She added "But I haven't gotten any update." The chair asked for an update by September (0:16:29). The surname is transcribed by the ASR and is not confirmed by any document reviewed, so it is not printed here. The draft minutes reduce this to "Candace Crawford noted that the previous treasurer and the electronic signature is a former Board member" (minutes text).

RSA 194-C:5, I requires each SAU board to organize "by choosing a chairperson, a secretary, and a treasurer" (statute text). No verified statute or rule tells an SAU who may hold signature authority on its accounts, so this is an observation. What the record supports is narrower: on June 12, 2025 the signature on the SAU's checks and account belonged, by the treasurer's account, to a person who had left the board about two years earlier, and the board itself said no current signer existed. The same item records that Madden and Ruggeri had been approved to review manifests and vouchers and were still waiting for direction on how (0:16:52, 0:17:01).

The April 10, 2025 draft minutes, posted in this meeting's packet, set out what was intended: the auditor said "the treasurer should review the check register"; the business administrator said "whoever is on the account should be the electronic signature and the one to review the check register and it will be the treasurer"; and the board elected Crawford treasurer and named Ruggeri and Madden "to review and sign vouchers" (April 10 minutes text). Two months later the account had not been changed to match.

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, I (SAU board chooses a chairperson, secretary and treasurer); Draft minutes 6.12.25 (item 2); Draft minutes 4.10.25 (treasurer and voucher signers elected)

OBSERVATION The chair described the SAU as "being dissolved" on July 1, 2026; the mechanism the record identifies is Unity's withdrawal

The withdrawal process for a member district is RSA 194-C:2, IV, and Unity is the district withdrawing: the April 10, 2025 draft minutes record that Unity's town vote to withdraw passed and give "Last day will be June 30, 2026 and first day of separation will be July 1, 2026" (April 10 minutes text). Later district records state both readings, and this page does not decide whether the unit itself ends; it records what was said. The board agreed to meet in November, January and May on the second Thursday (0:21:06). A May meeting falls within the April 1 to June 1 window in which RSA 194-C:5, I requires an SAU board to meet and organize (statute text: "shall meet between April 1 and June 1 in each year").
