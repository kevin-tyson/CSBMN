Claremont School Board — January 27, 2021

Remote meeting summary generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. Two weeks after extending remote learning, the board heard a letter from the district's nurses urging it to stay remote, a coach's plea to restore winter sports, and the superintendent's recommendation to return, then voted unanimously by roll call to reopen all schools in person on Monday, February 1, with winter sports. It also accepted seven retirements, appointed two members of the CCTV board, and heard how the annual meeting would run under HB 1129. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

MEDIUM In a fully remote meeting the agenda was adopted by voice vote because the clerk had not logged in

The meeting was held by video conference: the chair looks for members' raised hands on his screen ("I'm sorry, I can't see Heather.", 0:51:26) and telephone callers are told how to unmute. When any member participates other than in person, RSA 91-A:2, III(e) as it read in 2021 required that "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote." Emergency Order #12, in force for the state of emergency, waived only the physical-quorum requirement of III(b), on public-access conditions; it did not touch III(e). At 0:02:22 the chair, having begun "All those in favor?", continued "Yeah, we can do I. We don't have Mary here." and the amended agenda was carried on a chorus of Aye with no record of who voted. On January 13 the same chair had stopped himself in the same situation ("Oh, I can't do it that way. Can you roll call, please?", show 11262; see the January 13 page). The mitigation is real: every later vote at this meeting, including the retirements vote the chair called himself while the clerk was offline, was taken by roll call, and an agenda vote decides little. MEDIUM, as a lapse in a binding procedure on one low-stakes vote.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(b), (e) (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1, text in force until 2023): physical quorum except in an emergency; roll-call votes when a member participates remotely; Emergency Order #12 (March 23, 2020, under Executive Order 2020-04): III(b) waived for the state of emergency, subject to public-access conditions.
