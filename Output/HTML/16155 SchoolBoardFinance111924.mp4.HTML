OBSERVATION Three different descriptions of catastrophic special-education aid, none matching the statute in force

In the 2024 codification, RSA 186-C:18, III has the state pay 80 percent of a child's special-education costs above 3½ times the estimated state average expenditure per pupil and all costs above 10 times, with reimbursement prorated when the appropriation falls short; it contains no minimum funding floor and no 90 percent figure (the floor was added in 2025). On tape the business administrator said the state is "supposed to fund up to 90%" (1:09:08), the student-services director said that above the threshold "the state is supposed to kick in everything" (1:10:20), and a member summarized it as 100% above the thresholds (1:12:27). So "They're not they're not paying what they're obligated to pay." (1:12:54) is a characterization: proration at 67.5% in FY2025 is what the 2024 text provides for. The 67.5% and 87% figures Whitney cited match reporting by the New Hampshire Bulletin four days earlier. The practical point stands: the district absorbs the difference.

Sources: RSA 186-C:18, III, 2024 codification (source note ends 2023, 79:141, 142); New Hampshire Bulletin, November 15, 2024 (reporting, not law)
