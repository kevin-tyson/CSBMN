Claremont School Board — March 15, 2017

Summary of the Claremont School Board's organizational and regular meeting the day after the March 14, 2017 district election, generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript. The newly elected members took the oath, the board re-elected Brian Rapp chair and Chris Irish vice chair, the clerk read the election results, two food service workers questioned the planned food service RFP, the business administrator presented the report of appropriations voted ($35,183,247), and the board held a short nonpublic session and moved its lead screening policy to second reading. OBSERVATION The principal search committee's membership was not public; whether the Right-to-Know Law reached it is not settled here

The superintendent described a ten-member screening committee of Bluff faculty, parents, a community member and one administrator that met March 13 and was reviewing applications (0:30:29, 0:30:58). Asked whether its members would be listed online, he said he had not made them public, citing candidate confidentiality, and offered to post the names (0:34:05 to 0:34:57). The law then in force treated as a public body any committee or subordinate body of a school board, or an advisory committee to it. A committee convened by the superintendent to screen candidates the board will then elect is close to that line; the recording does not show who appointed or charged it. The page records the question without resolving it. The offer to publish the names is in the district's favor, and the hiring itself went to the board.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1, so the same text governed 2017): public body includes "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto".
