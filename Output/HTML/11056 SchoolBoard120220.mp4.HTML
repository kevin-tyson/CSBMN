Claremont School Board — December 2, 2020

Regular meeting summary generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. A hybrid meeting held during the COVID-19 state of emergency: the board approved an anchor-school tuition agreement with the Goshen School District, rejected by 4 to 3 the administration's proposal for two weeks of remote learning after the Christmas break (the chair first announced the motion as failing), and tabled the second-semester instructional plan to December 16. OBSERVATION The decisive 4 to 3 vote was announced as failing, one remote vote was read off a screen, and the draft minutes then recorded the wrong outcome

On Benware's motion that students return as calendared with no two-week remote period after the Christmas break, the roll call (1:31:25) was interrupted when Rob Lovett Jr., participating remotely, deferred his answer and asked what the motion meant. After Whitney explained it (1:33:37), the chair said "We don't know what you voted Rob."; a member answered "That's a yes I believe." and another "Yes. On the screen." (1:34:13). The chair announced "the motion does not pass" (1:34:18), was corrected by Benware, and said "Oh did pass. He said yes okay okay." (1:34:26). Two answers on the roll are unlabeled in the transcript and the chair's own answer is not transcribed; the individual votes cannot be read off the recording. Two weeks later, as Petrin read them aloud on the December 16 recording, the draft minutes recorded the motion as "defeated, by four against three", and Petrin had them corrected to "approved by a vote of 4 to 3" (December 16 page, flag 5). RSA 91-A:2, III(c) as it read in 2020 required that a remotely participating member "shall be audible or otherwise discernable to the public"; a vote seen on a screen by members in the room may not have been discernible to viewers. The later tabling roll call (1:51:14) also ends without Towle's answer or an announced result. The outcome itself was corrected before the minutes were approved, which is why this is an observation.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(c), (e) (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1, in force until 2023, 188:1): remote members audible or discernible to the public; all votes by roll call; dialogue rows at 1:31:25 to 1:34:32; the December 16, 2020 recording, show 11106 (minutes correction, linked above).

OBSERVATION The board approved a Goshen tuition agreement whose text was still under negotiation and was not public

The superintendent told the board that "the actual contract is under negotiation" and that he would speak only "to the public portions" (0:16:14); "This is by no means a done deal." (0:19:22).
