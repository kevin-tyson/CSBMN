Claremont School Board, December 2, 2020

Regular board meeting held in person and by video during the COVID-19 state of emergency. The board approved a tuition agreement with the Goshen School District, voted down the administration's proposal for two weeks of remote learning after the winter break, and tabled the second-semester instruction plan. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

MEDIUM No agenda, notice, packet or minutes for this meeting survive in any district share

MAP.md records no Drive material: the district's shares hold no year folder older than 2022, and the Meeting Minutes share starts at 2023. The recording is the only public account of a meeting that approved a tuition agreement and decided the January return date. RSA 91-A:2, II, in the text in force in 2020, required minutes including "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", the names of the persons who make and second each motion, and inspection "not more than 5 business days after the meeting"; paragraph II-b required a body with a website to post its approved minutes there or post where they may be reviewed. This page applies the MEDIUM limb of the project's severity rule because the record mitigates the gap: the board amended and approved these minutes on December 16, 2020, so they were produced. What is missing is any public copy now. The meeting notice is also missing, and with it the evidence that the district met Emergency Order #12's conditions: public access by telephone, "public notice of the necessary information for accessing the meeting", and a way for the public to report access problems. No call-in information is read out on the recording (0:00:40).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II and II-b (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1); Emergency Order #12 (March 23, 2020)

MEDIUM The board approved the Goshen tuition agreement while the superintendent described the contract as still under negotiation

Before the vote the superintendent said he would speak only to "the public portions" because "the actual contract is under negotiation" (0:16:14), called it "by no means a done deal" (0:19:22) and asked for "a motion and to pursue going forward" (0:21:44). The motion actually made was "to, approve the tuition agreement with the Goshen School District" (0:26:00). RSA 91-A:3, II(k), in the text then in force, provides that "A contract negotiated by a school board shall be made public prior to its consideration for approval by a school district", together with related nonpublic minutes and records, and that approval "shall occur only at a meeting open to the public at which, or after which, the public has had an opportunity to participate." The publication sentence sits inside the nonpublic-session exemption, so it can be read as reaching only contracts negotiated under that exemption; this page does not decide that question. The vote was in public session and the proposed tuition rates were stated aloud and said to be on the website. Nothing on the recording shows that a contract text was made public before the vote, and the superintendent's description suggests a final text did not yet exist. A member of the public who asked by chat to put a question was told "the issue has gone by" (0:27:16). Whether a written agreement was posted, and what the board approved, would be settled by the agenda and the December 2 minutes, neither of which survives. On December 16 the superintendent said the Unity and Goshen boards had also voted, that signed copies were still to come back, and that the agreement would then be posted online (see the December 16, 2020 page), which points the same way: the text was published after approval, not before.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, II(k) (2019 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1 and 280:1)
