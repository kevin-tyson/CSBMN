Claremont School Board, October 1, 2025

Regular meeting six weeks into the district's cash crisis. The board interviewed candidates for the vacant seat, tied 3 to 3 and laid the appointment on the table; approved a calendar change and union agreement for the Bluff closure; asked the legislature for a state-run adequacy revolving loan fund; heard the interim business administrator project a $1,974,757.42 deficit at June 30, 2026; and heard the draft FY2022 audit, which disclaims an opinion. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on October 1, 2025, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

HIGH The FY2022 single audit reached the board as a draft about 30 months after its federal due date, and the draft disclaims an opinion

Angell: "These are going to be the fiscal year 2022 financial statements. And yes, they are with four years behind." (2:49:38). He told the board the district "expends greater than $750,000 of federal awards" (2:50:38); the draft's Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards totals $4,276,509 for the year. For a fiscal year ending June 30, 2022, 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1) required the single-audit reporting package by the earlier of 30 days after the auditor's report or nine months after year end, that is, March 31, 2023. On October 1, 2025 the report was still a draft awaiting counsel's legal letter (2:50:19). The draft (Exhibit C- 9-30-25 CSD Revised Draft FY2022 Audit report) issues a "Disclaimer of Opinion" on the financial statements because "extensive turnover in management and those charged with governance" and "inadequate or missing documentation" prevented the auditors from verifying balances, and a separate disclaimer on compliance for "All Federal Programs" (finding 2022-018). Whitney said the 2016, 2017 and 2019 audits were each completed about three years late (3:06:10), so the lateness is a pattern, not a one-year event. Mitigation on the record: the current administration and auditor are clearing the backlog and plan one engagement for FY2023 to FY2025 (2:54:18). RSA 21-J:19 is permissive as to timing, so the state-law side of the lateness is a control weakness; the federal deadline is not permissive.

Sources: 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1) (due nine months after period end); 2 CFR 200.501 (single audit at $750,000 for this fiscal year); Exhibit C, draft FY2022 audit report (independent auditor's report; SEFA); RSA 21-J:19 (audits by independent accountants; timing permissive)

HIGH The audit finds the FY2022 tax rate was reduced with $1,059,738 more fund balance than existed

Angell: "In the audit, it's showing us a deficit of $198,888" and "$1.2 million was returned to the taxpayers" (3:00:38); "So your tax your tax bill was artificially reduced." (3:01:04). The draft's own figures are more exact than the spoken ones. Note 2-A: "In fiscal year 2022, the District applied $1,030,475 of beginning General Fund unassigned fund balance" while the restated beginning balance was $(29,263), so "the District applied $1,059,738 more fund balance than was available, effectively reducing the tax rate beyond its actual capacity." Note 15 shows the beginning unassigned balance had been reported as $133,150 before a $162,413 accounts-payable restatement. Note 2-C reports a General Fund unassigned deficit of $198,888 at June 30, 2022, resulting "from prior years’ fund balance returns that exceeded the amounts available". Under GASB 54, unassigned fund balance is the residual available for spending; using more of it than exists to set a tax rate misstates the resources behind the levy. The $1.2 million on the recording does not match the $1,030,475 in the draft; the page reports both. Graded HIGH as a material misstatement documented by the district's own auditor; the restatement itself is the correction the standards call for.

Sources: Exhibit C, draft FY2022 audit report (Notes 2-A, 2-C, 15; Schedule of Changes in Unassigned Fund Balance); GASB Statement 54, ¶6–17 (fund balance classes; unassigned); N.H. Admin. Rules, Rev 1102.03 (districts follow GAAP, GASB first)
