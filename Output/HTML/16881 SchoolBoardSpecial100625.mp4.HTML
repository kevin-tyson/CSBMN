Claremont School Board, Special Meeting, October 6, 2025

A twelve-minute special meeting held at 5:30 p.m., before the SAU 6 board's special meeting the same evening. HIGH The agenda listed citizens' comments, and the board voted without holding them

The posted agenda carries "Citizens Comments-Please reference CSB Policy BEDH- Public Comment & Participation at Board Meetings." On the recording the chair takes the roll and hands the floor to the superintendent in the same breath (0:00:47); no invitation to the public is made before the vote (0:06:29). The only later invitation asks people in the room to send the chair items for future agendas (0:11:19), which is not a comment period. RSA 189:74 requires a school board to "provide the opportunity for the public to comment on school district matters at a meeting of the school board held under RSA 91-A:2" for no less than 30 minutes, with exceptions only for emergency meetings and meetings held solely for a nonpublic session. This meeting was noticed about 76 hours ahead (agenda uploaded October 3, 2025) and transacted business in public, so neither exception applies, and the statute does not distinguish special from regular meetings. Policy BEDH likewise requires comment at all board meetings. The question the project's anchor notes leave open, whether 30 minutes is a floor on elapsed time, does not arise here, because no opportunity was offered at all.

Sources: RSA 189:74 (public comment at board meetings; two exceptions); Policy BEDH (public comment and participation); CSB Agenda 10.6.25 (item II.b)

MEDIUM No minutes exist for a meeting that created a position

The board created a district-wide substitute secretary position by voice vote (0:06:29). No minutes for October 6 were found: MAP.md records that the approved December 3, 2025 minutes list the minutes approved during the clerk's absence and omit this date, and a Drive title search for this date's minutes returned nothing on 2026-10-03 (a negative from search alone is not proof). RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes within five business days that record members present, the subject matter, final decisions, movers and seconders, and, since August 22, 2025, the start and end times and the name of the person who produced them. Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH under the project's severity rule because the meeting was noticed and agenda'd and only the minutes are missing; the clerk's maternity leave, stated at the October 1 meeting, is context but not an exemption.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (minutes contents and five business days; source note ends 2025, 112:1, eff. Aug. 22, 2025)

OBSERVATION A full-time position was created with no stated cost, no job description and no audible second, during a declared deficit

Asked the cost, the superintendent answered "So it's the full salary" and "And then some benefits." without a figure (0:04:12, 0:04:18). The job description was still being worked out with the union (0:05:41), so the motion was amended to approve the position "pending a job description" (0:05:59). No second is heard before the vote. Five days earlier the interim business administrator had projected a deficit of $1,974,757.42 at June 30, 2026 (see the October 1 page). The agenda's consent items, including the manifest, were also not taken up. No rule fixes the content of a position-creation motion; the point is what a reader of the record cannot learn from it.

Sources: CSB Agenda 10.6.25; Claremont School Board, October 1, 2025

POSITIVE Three administrative departures were announced in open session before posting
