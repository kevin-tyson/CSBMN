Claremont School Board & City Council, Joint Meeting, January 30, 2018

Quarterly joint meeting of two public bodies, the Claremont School Board and the Claremont City Council, chaired jointly by the school board chair and the mayor. It heard a proposal to test household pets for lead, previewed the governor's ceremonial signing of the lead bill, and set community-wide priorities for 2018. No agenda or minutes survive in the district's online shares, so the order of business below is reconstructed from the recording's dialogue transcript. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

MEDIUM No agenda or minutes for the school board side of this meeting survive in any district share

The mayor's references to "the second agenda item under New Business" (0:05:39) and "next on the agenda" (0:24:28) show that a written agenda existed, and the school district clerk, who says she has served "as secretary for too many years" (1:10:34), called the school board roll (0:01:02). Neither document is in the district's shares: MAP.md records that no share holds a year folder older than 2022. RSA 91-A:2, II, as it read in 2018, required minutes with "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", open to inspection "not more than 5 business days after the meeting". This page applies the MEDIUM limb of the project's severity rule: the recording shows a recording secretary at the table, and the gap matches the reach of the district's online archive rather than anything specific to this meeting. The City Council's own minutes belong to the city clerk and were not checked, so this flag makes no finding about them.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification; source note ends 2017, 165:1 and 234:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2018: the text in force for all of 2018)

OBSERVATION Neither body's attendance can be fully read from the recording, and the priority "consensus" was not a vote

Only two school board answers are legible ("Michelle Pierce here" and "And Brian here", 0:01:02, 0:01:11). The council roll (0:01:17) names five members and the mayor and carries three unnamed answers, which would put the council at or near its full nine, but the names cannot all be recovered. Under RSA 91-A:2, I a meeting is "the convening of a quorum" of a public body, so each body's quorum matters separately. Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I and II (2017 codification; in force for 2018); Claremont City Charter sec. 16 and 21 (nine councilors; majority is a quorum), as recorded in the project's anchors file
