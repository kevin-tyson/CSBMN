Quarterly joint meeting of the City Council and the School Board, presided over by the mayor: updates on childhood lead screening and the SB 247 lead bill, district programs for young children and families, workforce development, and the community's response to an August 2017 incident. OBSERVATION Personal details of a small group of student parents and their infants were described on camera

Presenting the One for All child-care program for high school students who are parents (0:51:35), the assistant superintendent gave the number of infants enrolled, their ages, how many student parents were expected to enroll in which months, how many parents were recent graduates, and health and family circumstances of the infants' births (0:52:03, 0:52:37). A board member then described a recent graduate who had a baby and crossed the stage at graduation (1:17:30). This page does not repeat those details.

In a district of about 1,800 students, a combination of facts about a small group of infants and their student parents may let people in the school community recognize individuals. FERPA's regulations define personally identifiable information to include information that, alone or in combination, is linked or linkable to a specific student so that a reasonable person in the school community could identify the student with reasonable certainty, and define disclosure as release or other communication of information from education records by any means. Whether these remarks drew on education records, and whether anyone was identifiable, cannot be determined from the recording. The speakers' purpose was to show the program's value; the point for review is how much individual detail a public presentation needs. No finding of violation is made.
