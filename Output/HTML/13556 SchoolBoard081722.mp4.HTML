MEDIUM FY2020 audits for all three SAU entities were still in progress, and the board had not received the auditors' reports

In August 2022, more than two years after the fiscal year ended June 30, 2020, the administration described the FY2020 audits of “the three entities” as on the auditors' schedule rather than complete (0:07:51), with FY2019 done. The vice chair said the board had not received the auditors' paper report: “No, we just knew that we were on schedule as of last meeting.” (0:09:35). Where an entity spends $750,000 or more in federal awards in a year, the Uniform Guidance requires the single-audit package within the earlier of 30 days after the auditor's report or nine months after the period ends (March 31, 2021 for FY2020); OMB M-21-20 allowed a further six months for fiscal years ending through June 30, 2021, which would still have fallen in September 2021. The record does not state Claremont's FY2020 federal expenditures, so whether the single-audit deadline applied is not established here, and the flag stays at MEDIUM. Mitigation on the record: the superintendent said the auditors could be asked to present and the reports provided (0:09:26), and the delay was described as cleanup of an inherited backlog (0:08:45).

Sources: 2 CFR 200.501(a), 2022 CFR edition ($750,000 single-audit threshold); 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1), 2022 CFR edition (earlier of 30 days after the auditor's report or nine months after period end); OMB M-21-20, App. 3 sec. IX (six-month delay for fiscal year-ends through June 30, 2021)
