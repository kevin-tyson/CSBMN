Claremont School Board, June 21, 2023

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, all seven members present at the call to order. The board opens with a nonpublic session that the minutes say ran until 8:00 p.m.; on returning, Steven Horsky resigns from the board effective immediately. The board then approves a $90,000 addition to the Stevens High School roof, raises school meal prices for FY24, hears the food-service coordinator on lunch debt and free and reduced meal applications, appoints Joella Merchant to the CCTV board, adopts policies IGA and BG and withdraws five older policies. MEDIUM A roughly 90-minute nonpublic session with no seal vote, and no nonpublic minutes located

RSA 91-A:3, III, as it read on this date: "Minutes and decisions reached in nonpublic session shall be publicly disclosed within 72 hours of the meeting, unless, by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation" of a non-member, render a proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism (statute). The board entered nonpublic session after the roll (0:01:43) and, per the minutes, returned "at 8:00PM". No motion to seal was made on the recording after the return (0:03:21 to 0:05:36), and the public minutes record none. Unsealed nonpublic minutes therefore had to be public by Saturday, June 24, 2023. They are not in the June 21 packet folder, not attached to the public minutes, and were not found in the district's shares by this project; the district's Unsealed Minutes folder was created only in January 2025. A Drive search is not proof that no minutes exist, which is why this is MEDIUM and not HIGH. The public minutes say nothing about what was discussed or decided. This page does not infer a connection between the session and the resignation that followed it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (2022 codification; source note ends 2021, 163:1 and 172:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2022); minutes, item II

OBSERVATION The nonpublic motion did not state its exemptions; the chair read three, one of them the tuition-contract ground

RSA 91-A:3, I(b): "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II", and "The vote on any such motion shall be by roll call" (statute). The motion on tape is "To make a motion. We go into nonpublic." (0:01:08); after the second the chair supplies the grounds as transcribed, "91-A3, 2B91A colon 32C91-A colon three two K" (0:01:12), before the roll call. The roll call met the statute. The minutes cite "RSA 91-A3 B, C, and K", without the paragraph number. The three grounds are II(b), "The hiring of any person as a public employee"; II(c), matters likely to affect adversely the reputation of a person "other than a member of the public body itself"; and II(k), a school board's consideration of a pupil tuition contract under RSA 194 or 195-A, whose own text requires that the contract be made public before approval "together with minutes of all meetings held in nonpublic session". Nothing in the public record says which ground applied to what.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(b), II(c), II(k) (2022 codification)

OBSERVATION A food-service contract renewal listed as "Vote Required" went without a vote

Federal rules allow a school food authority's contract with a food service management company "a duration of no longer than 1 year", with "options for the yearly renewal" not exceeding "4 additional years" (7 CFR 210.16(d), 2023 edition). Jack describes this as "year two" of a five-year bid (0:25:29), with a projected deficit of $36,061 that the district would absorb (0:26:26), and says the state was still reviewing the contract. The agenda heads the item "Food Service Contract Approval (Vote Required)"; the board voted only on meal prices, and Jack advised "I don't think we need to vote on it" (0:39:10). The record does not show who, if anyone, exercised the renewal for the district, or whether the contract returned to the board after the state's review.

Sources: 7 CFR 210.16(d) (2023 annual edition); agenda, item IV.2
