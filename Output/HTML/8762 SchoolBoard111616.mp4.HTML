Claremont School Board, November 16, 2016

OBSERVATION "Equal access": the statute as amended in 2016 does not use the word, but it does bar a more restrictive policy

The superintendent described RSA 193:1-c as one "which basically talks about equal access" and warned that removing equal would be "wordsmithing the RSA" (1:15:43, 1:20:25); the vice chair read the statute and said it "does not say equal" (1:22:17). RSA 193:1-c, I, as amended effective March 26, 2016, provides that "Nonpublic, public chartered school, or home educated pupils shall have access to curricular courses and cocurricular programs offered by the school district in which the pupil resides", and that district policy "shall not be more restrictive for non-public, public chartered school, or home educated pupils than the policy" for its own pupils. The vice chair's reading of the text is correct: the word equal does not appear. The superintendent's underlying point, that access may not be narrower than for enrolled pupils, has support in the second clause. The board deferred the wording (1:24:11).

Sources: RSA 193:1-c, I-II (2016 codification; source note "2002, 202:1 ... 2016, 4:1, eff. March 26, 2016")

POSITIVE Deliberative session date corrected into the statutory window and moved off a City Council night

The superintendent caught that the agenda's February 1 date for the deliberative session was too early (0:02:32) and told the board the session must fall "between February 4th through February 11th" (0:29:49). RSA 40:13, III, as it read in 2016-17, required the first session of an official-ballot annual meeting to be held "between the first and second Saturdays following the last Monday in January, inclusive of those Saturdays". The last Monday in January 2017 was January 30, so the window ran from Saturday, February 4 to Saturday, February 11, as he said. The board then chose Tuesday, February 7 instead of February 8 because the latter was a City Council night (0:30:36, 0:32:18). Keeping the date clear of the council's schedule is a step beyond the statute toward letting residents attend both.

Sources: RSA 40:13, III (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 7:1-4 and 190:1-3, 8-10, eff. Sept. 9, 2014, so the text in force in 2016-17)
