SAU 6 Board, May 16, 2019

Meeting of the SAU 6 board (Claremont and Unity members): the incoming superintendent, Michael Tempesta, is introduced; the acting superintendent and the assistant director of business and finance resign; the board debates and then drops an exit interview; the acting superintendent describes three open positions and unsettled Claremont and Unity year-end figures; the board approves a draft data governance plan and a three-year Primex rate cap, and goes into nonpublic session, where the recording ends. OBSERVATION The SAU board approved a draft data governance plan that the statute required each school board to approve, and its security gaps were described on air

RSA 189:66, V, as amended by 2018, 252 and in force on this date, required: “Each local education agency shall develop a data and privacy governance plan” to be “presented to the school board for review and approval by June 30, 2019”, and updated annually. The SAU 6 board approved “the data governance plan draft as presented.” (0:40:45). Whether the Claremont and Unity school boards also reviewed and approved a plan by June 30, as the statute's wording points to, is not in this record. The plan's required contents are an inventory and review of software, access and privacy policies, a breach response plan, and service-provider standards; staff training is not among them, so the director's statement that training was not legally required (0:35:32) is consistent with the text.

The director was candid that the plan describes requirements rather than protections in place, and in doing so described on the televised record specific weaknesses: unlocked server racks in classrooms (0:33:41), no backup generators (0:34:04), no cybersecurity expert (0:37:59), and a student information system “hosted locally and open to the internet.” (0:38:24). Those statements are a reason to ask what was done afterward, and whether describing them in public was weighed against the risk.

Sources: RSA 189:66, V (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 252:1, 2, eff. Aug. 11, 2018)
