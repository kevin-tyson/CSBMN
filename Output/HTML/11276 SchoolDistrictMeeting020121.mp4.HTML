MEDIUM The minutes record a statement this recording does not contain and omit the session’s main business

The district’s minutes of this session (2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting) state: “The Moderator recognized Board Member, Michael Petrin. Mr. Petrin stated that he wanted to recognize the members that served on the Budget Committee with him … Board Chair Frank Sprague, Jason Benware and himself who worked mornings for fifteen days to provide a Budget that would serve the Students and not increase the Budget.” (quoted from the minutes). Petrin is not heard on this recording. The budget subcommittee is recognized here by the superintendent (0:13:39), who describes the work as “two, two weeks or three weeks”. Petrin did recognize the subcommittee at the February 4 session, describing at least ten hours over five meetings (show 11281, dialogue rows 41 and 42), which is neither the speaker nor the figure the minutes give for February 1.

The minutes also leave out the roughly 25-minute budget and default-budget presentation by the superintendent and Seaman (0:13:23 to 0:38:02), and they are signed by the clerk, whom the vice chair reported as not present (0:04:26). RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes that include “a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions” (statutory wording, 2019 codification). The minutes do record the only decision correctly, with mover and seconder (Benware, Towle). Graded MEDIUM: an accuracy defect in the official record of a legislative-body session, without a misstated decision.

Sources: 2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting; RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification); Dialogue/11281 SchoolDistrictMeeting020421.mp4.CSV (2/4/21 Petrin remarks).

OBSERVATION The minutes reached the district’s public share twenty-one months after the session

Google Drive metadata for the minutes PDF shows it created on 2022-11-03, inside the packet folder for the November 3, 2022 special district meeting. RSA 91-A:2, II makes minutes open to public inspection “not more than 5 business days after the meeting” (statutory wording). The Drive date measures when the document reached this share, not when it was written or first made available (html_briefing.md §22c), and the clerk may have held it for inspection at the SAU office. This session itself approved the minutes of the February 5, 2020 session a year after that session, so this district’s meeting minutes wait for the next district meeting to be approved, which may explain why these were filed with the November 2022 packet. The record on hand cannot settle whether the five-day rule was met.

Sources: 2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting (Drive metadata: created 2022-11-03, modified 2022-11-03); RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification).

OBSERVATION The minutes vote was open to anyone on the call, while written comments had to be checked against the checklist

The moderator announced that written submissions must carry the voter’s name, address and ward and “will be verified against the checklist for eligibility”, and that “No live comments or questions from registered voters will be allowed”. For the 2020 minutes she then had every participant unmuted for a voice vote (0:10:06) and took it as unanimous on hearing no nays (0:10:59). Nothing on the record checked who on the Zoom or phone line was a Claremont voter. The item was routine and uncontested; the point is the inconsistency within the process, not a rule violation, since the chaptered text of House Bill 1129 was not read for this page.

Sources: Dialogue/11276 SchoolDistrictMeeting020121.mp4.CSV; 2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting; HB 1129 (2020), bill status page (chaptered text not read).

POSITIVE The default budget and both tax rates were explained in full before any comment period opened

At the January 13 budget hearing the default budget had not yet been calculated (see that page’s flag 1). Here Seaman explained how the default is built under the official-ballot law, what it drops and adds back (0:34:52 to 0:36:13), and gave both rates: $24.53 for the recommended budget and $24.50 for the default (0:33:45, 0:37:30). RSA 40:13, XI(a) requires the default to be disclosed at the first budget hearing; this session, the first part of the annual meeting, went beyond that minimum by putting the comparison before voters at the start of the written-comment window. The session was reachable by Zoom, telephone, CCTV Channel 8 and a viewing room at Stevens High School (0:07:28).

Sources: RSA 40:13, XI(a) (2019 codification); Emergency Order #12 (March 23, 2020); Dialogue/11262 SchoolBoard011321.mp4.CSV (1/13/21 hearing).
