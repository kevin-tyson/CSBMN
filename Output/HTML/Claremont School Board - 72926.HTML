Claremont School Board, Goal Setting Meeting, July 29, 2026

Annual goal-setting work session at the Dow Administration Building, facilitated by Superintendent Timothy Broadrick around his nine-domain "foundation" framework. Five of seven members attended. The board brainstormed needs on chart paper and talked through each domain: special education, business administration, curriculum, human resources, technology, facilities, school operations and the board's own role. No votes were taken on the recording, which opens with the workshop under way and ends at a break before a noticed nonpublic session. OBSERVATION The chair's description of catastrophic special-education aid does not match the statute

The chair said "the law that they were supposed to pay 95% of anything over 70,000" (0:21:24). RSA 186-C:18, III sets its threshold as a multiple of the state average expenditure per pupil ("exceed 3 and 1/2 times the most current state average"), not a dollar figure, and, as amended in 2025, directs the department to distribute "not less than 80 percent of the district's entitlement" (statute text); no 95 percent figure appears in the section. The superintendent's statement that aid was once fully funded but "in the last 5 to 7 years we've slipped" (0:21:38) is his characterization. Nothing turned on the figure at this session, which took no votes.

Sources: RSA 186-C:18, III (special education catastrophic aid)
