Claremont School Board, January 21, 2020

The public hearing on the FY2021 budget and warrant articles, followed by a regular meeting. The superintendent opened by postponing the elementary reorganization for a year; sixteen members of the public spoke, most of them about it. The board then sent a $38,202,661 budget and its warrant articles to the deliberative session after rejecting, 2 to 4, a motion to cut the two $500,000 reserve articles, and adopted policy DAF and the 2020-21 calendar. OBSERVATION The current year's out-of-district special-education line was set at $380,000 against an $800,000 estimate, and the business administrator forecasts an overrun

The business administrator says the elementary out-of-district line for the current year (FY2020) "was budgeted at 380,000", although the special-education director's estimate had been "$800,000", and actual spending was 750,000 in FY2019 and "$790,000" in FY2018 (0:12:10 to 0:13:16). "I don't know why 380 was put in there". He forecasts about $600,000 for FY2020, "So we're going to be over budget this year." No rule cited here forbids an under-estimated line; RSA chapter 32 lets the governing body move money between lines within the total appropriation, so the overrun is not itself a violation. The observation is that the FY2020 budget voters approved understated a known cost by roughly $220,000 to $420,000, and that FY2021's $520,000 rests on the success of new in-district programs. The FY2020 year-end budget-to-actual report would show how the overrun was covered.

Sources: the recording (above); no statute verified for this flag

POSITIVE The budget hearing met the statutory date, presented the default budget, and heard every speaker before the board finalized the budget

RSA 40:13, II-a requires budget hearings "on or before the third Tuesday in January", and this hearing was held on that day. RSA 40:13, XI(a) requires the default budget to be "disclosed and presented for questions and discussion at the first budget hearing"; the business administrator presented it with each component (0:36:16 to 0:38:31) and board members answered a citizen's question about it (0:54:56). RSA 32:5, I contemplates that the body "after the conclusion of public testimony shall finalize the budget"; the chair kept testimony open until no one else rose (1:36:39), deferred an off-topic complaint to citizens' comments rather than cutting it off (1:32:58), and the board voted only after reopening citizens' comments in the regular meeting. Beyond the statutory minimum, the superintendent answered the night's main public concern by postponing the reorganization before the hearing began (0:02:27).

Sources: RSA 40:13, II-a and XI(a) (2019 codification); RSA 32:5, I (2019 codification)
