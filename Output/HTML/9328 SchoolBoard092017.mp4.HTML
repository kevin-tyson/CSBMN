Claremont School Board — September 20, 2017

Summary of a regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript. With the chair and vice chair apparently absent, Frank Sprague presided. A grandparent objected to the district's lead screening policy and the board agreed to revisit it; the superintendent spoke about the community's response to a late-August incident; the Stevens High School scheduling committee reported and the board voted to let the superintendent relax its January 2017 memorandum of understanding with the teachers' union; the board accepted a $478 National Honor Society gift, approved Washington, D.C. and Italy trips, and adopted the nonacademic survey policy on final read.
