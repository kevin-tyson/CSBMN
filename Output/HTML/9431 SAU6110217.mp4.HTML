OBSERVATION The budget vote and the attendance behind it cannot be read from the recording

The SAU 6 budget was adopted on a voice vote: “Any further discussion? All those in favor?”, “All those against any abstainers. Okay.”, “Motion is passed.” (1:15:43 to 1:16:03). No count is heard. The roll call at 1:07:21 is garbled (“Chris Irish, Michael. Patrick, grumpy. Ryan. You're right here. Rebecca Zullo here. Robert de here. I treat absent here. Bruce Howard here.”), so which members of the two boards were present cannot be established either. RSA 194-C:8 provides that weighted votes "shall only be used upon the demand of a majority of the members of any board"; no demand is heard, so a simple voice vote was permissible. The point is that a vote fixing both districts' assessments has no recoverable tally and no surviving minutes (flag 1).

Sources: RSA 194-C:8 (weighted voting; source note 1996, 298:3, unamended); RSA 91-A:2, II (2016 codification)

POSITIVE A public hearing on a newspaper-posted budget, two months before the deadline

RSA 194-C:9, I, as codified in 2017, requires the SAU board to adopt its budget "At a meeting held before January 1" and imposes no hearing or notice requirement. The board held a public hearing on the version “that was posted in the newspaper” (0:05:20), kept post-hearing revisions separate so that the hearing matched what the public had seen (0:05:07, 0:14:54), presented the changes before voting, and adopted the budget on November 2. The hearing drew no public comment, and the chair asked staff to avoid acronyms for newer members and the public (0:03:33). The nonpublic session that followed was entered on a motion adopting a named exemption and a roll call (1:16:12 to 1:17:00), as RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I (2017 codification; source note 1996, 298:3; 2003, 279:1); RSA 91-A:3, I(b) (2016 codification)
