Claremont School Board (part 2 of 2) — December 6, 2017

Second part of the December 6 regular meeting, summarized from the CCTV recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes are online, and the opening of the meeting is on separate recordings. The administration walked the board through its proposed reductions line by line: a security grant, an unfilled maintenance position, an elementary section, a school psychologist, special education teachers, Stevens English and world-language positions, and a $42,800 school resource officer line that had been left out. Administrators said the reductions would hurt; two resignations were accepted. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.
