OBSERVATION Special education reductions were weighed against the free appropriate public education requirement

Ben stated the legal floor: "our obligation is to provide a free and appropriate public education by law to kids with disabilities" (0:16:31). Asked whether one special education teacher could go, he said "I think we can get by" (0:23:20); the special education administrator said "We could live with one." (0:26:44) and warned that a psychologist cut would push the team toward compliance work (0:08:06). Federal law makes a free appropriate public education available to all children with disabilities, and 34 CFR 300.323(c)(2) requires that services be made available in accordance with the child's IEP. Staff did not say the cuts would breach those duties; they said the margin would be thin. A reviewer weighing the final budget should look at whether the special education reduction was made and how caseloads changed.

Sources: 20 U.S.C. 1412(a)(1)(A) (free appropriate public education); 34 CFR 300.323(c)(2) (2017 annual edition)
