Claremont School Board, September 1, 2021

Regular meeting held as the school year began. The board tabled COVID-19 vaccination incentives on the superintendent’s advice, approved the Stevens High School program of studies with a transcript addendum, amended its COVID-19 decision matrix 4-3 by roll call (masks strongly recommended rather than required at the moderate level), heard a state surveillance-testing proposal, and approved seven staff nominations. OBSERVATION A "non meeting" was planned for the superintendent’s evaluation; RSA 91-A has no such category for a quorum

Whitney proposed “to have a non meeting” on the superintendent’s evaluation (1:19:58), at which the superintendent would review his strategic plan with each board (1:18:55), and later: “We do a non meeting first and then have the school board meeting afterwards okay.” (1:33:28). Under RSA 91-A:2, I, as in force in 2021, a meeting is the "convening of a quorum of the membership of a public body" to discuss or act on matters within its jurisdiction (statute text). Its exclusions cover collective-bargaining strategy, consultation with legal counsel, a party caucus and circulating draft documents; an evaluation discussion is not among them. If a quorum of the board gathers to discuss the superintendent’s performance, the law treats it as a meeting that must be noticed; private discussion would need a nonpublic session entered on a motion stating a paragraph II exemption and a roll-call vote (RSA 91-A:3, I(b)). The September 15 recording opens with the board already seated and shows nothing about a session beforehand (September 15 page), so this page cannot say whether the plan went ahead or in what form.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I (2019 codification; meeting defined; four exclusions); RSA 91-A:3, I(b) (2019 codification; motion stating the exemption, roll call)
