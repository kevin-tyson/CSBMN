Claremont School Board, November 3, 2021

Regular meeting on the elementary restructuring with universal pre-K, a common schedule for the middle school, Stevens and the tech center, and whether to leave the Region 10 career and technical education agreement. OBSERVATION The Maple Avenue roof was replaced without a decided funding source, and reserve money had not always reached the trustees

Answering the questions raised on October 20 (October 20, 2021 page) and by a citizen tonight (0:07:38), the assistant superintendent for finance said that “we have not taken any money as of yet.” (1:40:08) He had planned in July to ask the board to release money from the fund for the roof (1:42:22), the roof was then installed (1:42:45), and he “held off in July on asking for any distribution” (1:43:33) while CARES Act eligibility was explored. Result: “So we have the cost of the maple roof we have incurred. There's still a couple ways to pay for that.” (1:44:13), either a board-approved draw or savings within the budget (1:44:13 to 1:45:53). POSITIVE Reserve money is now held by the trustees and released only on a documented board vote

The assistant superintendent described the control he put in place: when voters approve a reserve article “we're supposed to actually take that cash and send it to the trustees of the trust funds, which which I have done since we've been here.” (1:40:08) To release money “I need to provide to them the motion and the approval from the board in order for those dollars to come out of those funds.” (1:40:44) He confirmed “nothing has been distributed from the capital fund, nothing has been distributed from the Special Education Fund.” (1:40:44), and that the one draw, “approximately $175,000, which was for the two busses and the van.” (1:41:09), came from the transportation fund under the board's approval last year, with the motion and documentation sent to the trustees. “There is no way without a board vote for any of those funds to be distributed from that expendable trust.” (1:47:17) Whitney: “Well, thank you for safeguarding that.” (1:47:32) A control that ties every disbursement to a recorded board motion, explained in public in answer to a citizen's question, is the practice the October 20 discussion asked for.
