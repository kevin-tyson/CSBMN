MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed for six months with no statutory determination on the record

RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes and decisions to be disclosed within 72 hours "unless, by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism." The vote itself meets the statute: a roll call in public session (0:03:44), all six members present voting yes. What is missing is the determination. Crawford's motion (0:03:35) states only a duration, and the minutes record "seal the minutes of the non-public session for 6 months" with no ground. In a tuition negotiation the "render the proposed action ineffective" limb is the obvious candidate, and the board may well have meant it, but nobody said so. RSA 91-A:3, II(k) adds its own release rule for this kind of session: a negotiated contract "shall be made public prior to its consideration for approval by a school district, together with minutes of all meetings held in nonpublic session". So the six-month seal gives way to the earlier of its own expiry or the contract coming forward for approval.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, II(k) and III (2023 codification; source note ends 2023, 189:1, eff. Oct. 3, 2023); approved minutes, item III.1

OBSERVATION The nonpublic citation drops the section number, and the agenda's paraphrase drops "tuition"

The agenda heads the item "Non-Public Session RSA91-A:,II (k)" and the minutes record the motion as made "under RSA91-A:II(k)". Both omit the section, 91-A:3. The agenda's paraphrase, "Consideration of a school board of entering into a student or pupil contract", also omits the word that limits the exemption: II(k) reaches "a student or pupil tuition contract authorized by RSA 194 or RSA 195-A, which, if discussed in public, would likely benefit a party or parties whose interests are adverse to those of the general public or the school district". The motion on tape (0:02:03) is too garbled to show what was said. RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires the motion to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II". The paragraph letter was stated and fits the subject, a Croydon tuition arrangement, so the defect is one of form; it is an Observation because the exemption relied on is identifiable from the record.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and II(k) (2023 codification); agenda, item III.1

OBSERVATION Members told not to rely on the budget's prior-year comparison column

Crawford tells the board that the binder's percent-variance column should not be the focus "because ... last year's figures, there were a lot of anomalies in the way the budget was put together last year" (0:18:50), and that items "were in one category that shouldn't have been" and have been rearranged (0:20:05). Hawkins reports "the same anomalies" in the SAU budget (0:20:21). RSA 32:5, IV requires budget forms to carry comparative columns, so the prior-year comparison is the one the statute makes the public rely on; reclassifying lines between years makes that comparison harder to read unless the changes are disclosed with the budget. No figure in this meeting's record shows a misstatement, so no rule or accounting standard is applied. The binder was not placed in the packet; the 12/20/23 packet's Updated Claremont School District Budget 12.20.23.pdf is the document to check.

Sources: RSA 32:5, IV (2023 codification; comparative columns); Dialogue/15453 SchoolBoard120623.mp4.CSV

POSITIVE Minutes name every mover and seconder, and a member corrected the prior minutes to record a seal vote

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to include "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", and since January 1, 2019 "The names of the members who made or seconded each motion shall be recorded in the minutes." The December 6 minutes name the mover and seconder of every motion, including all three nonpublic-session motions, and say each roll call was taken by the clerk with all present voting yes. Hawkins also had the November 15 minutes amended (0:01:05). The November 15 draft recorded a one-year seal only for that night's later nonpublic session under II(a) (moved by Whitney, seconded by Sprague); its earlier session under II(c), on page 2, item II, showed entry and exit but no seal vote. Hawkins's correction adds that second seal with its mover, seconder and unanimous roll call. Where the dialogue file's speaker labels disagree with the December 6 minutes on two motions, the minutes are the better evidence.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2023 codification; mover and seconder clause from 2018, 244:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019); approved minutes

POSITIVE The Finance Committee's budget interviews were televised

Crawford reports that the committee's line-by-line sessions with administrators "have been recorded on CCTV" (0:11:06), and Hawkins says she watched them (0:17:27). RSA 91-A requires a committee of the board to meet in public and keep minutes; it does not require recording or broadcast, so televising the sessions goes beyond the minimum. The limit of that practice is the other side of the same record: no minutes of any of these Finance Committee sessions have been located in either district share (MAP.md sections 213, 216 and 218 and the finance-committee pages), so the recordings are the only public account of them.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (a committee of a public body is itself a public body); RSA 91-A:2, II (2023 codification)
