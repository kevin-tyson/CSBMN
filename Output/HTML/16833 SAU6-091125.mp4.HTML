SAU 6 Board, September 11, 2025

A 31-minute meeting of the joint SAU 6 board (Claremont and Unity members). After a nonpublic session cited under RSA 91-A:3, II(a) and (c), the board accepted Superintendent Chris Pratt's resignation, offered the interim superintendency to Kerry Kennedy, and approved three consulting contracts with TMSolution (superintendent mentoring at $1,500 a month, communications at $155 an hour, and an operational review at $175 an hour capped at $25,000), all by voice vote. Approval of four sets of minutes was tabled because members could not open the files. No minutes of this meeting have been located. MEDIUM The nonpublic session was cited under II(a) and (c), but a member describes it as an interview of the interim candidate, which is hiring under II(b)

The agenda cites RSA 91-A:3, II (a & c) and the motion "RSA 91-8 colon three comma two A and C" (0:03:24): (a) is "The dismissal, promotion, or compensation of any public employee or the disciplining of such employee", (c) is reputation. Back in public session, the board accepted the superintendent's resignation and offered the interim post to Kerry Kennedy without discussion, and Madden said the board "just had an extensive interview with our new candidate" (0:16:04). Interviewing a candidate is "The hiring of any person as a public employee", the separate exemption at II(b), which the motion did not cite. Paragraph I(b) requires the motion to state the exemption relied on. Part of the session may have fit (a), since Pratt's departure followed his placement on leave; without minutes the content cannot be checked. Graded MEDIUM as a probable mismatch between the stated and the described purpose.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and II(a)–(c) (motion states the exemption; hiring is II(b)); SAU6 School Board Agenda 9.11.25 (1).pdf, item III.1
