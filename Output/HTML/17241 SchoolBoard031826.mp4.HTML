HIGH The motion to enter nonpublic session stated a lettered exemption for only one of two items, while the subject announced for the session was the superintendent hiring

The agenda noticed one nonpublic item, Non-Public Session 91-a:3 II(a), Requested by Kerry Kennedy, and printed the text of II(a) (dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline of an employee). Minutes earlier, the leadership search subcommittee chair reported at 1:56:58: “We are discussing it in nonpublic. We have made a recommendation and we have to decide how to go forward. I guess we'll do that in nonpublic”, and at 1:57:40: “The third candidate is a very, very strong candidate, and that will be discussed this evening”. The chair then framed the motion at 1:58:13: “We have two items under the nonpublic, 91-A colon. Three, part two a as requested by Miss Kennedy, and 91 a colon three subset two for the school board”. Heather Whitney said So moved (1:58:53), a second was announced (1:58:57), and the clerk called the roll from 1:59:03. The approved minutes record the motion as one under RSA 91-a:3 II(a) only.

RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires that a nonpublic motion "shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II which is relied upon", and I(c) confines "All discussions held and decisions made" to "the matters set out in the motion" (statute text). The second item, as spoken, cites paragraph II with no letter, which identifies no exemption. The exemption that fits the announced subject is II(b), "The hiring of any person as a public employee" (statute text); a candidate who has not been hired is not yet a public employee whose compensation II(a) would reach. As minuted, the motion covered II(a) alone, so a discussion of the candidate would fall outside it. Mitigation: the chair did announce two items on tape, the board had used II(a) and II(b) together for the same search on March 4, and what was discussed cannot be confirmed because the nonpublic minutes were sealed (flag 2). The page grades this HIGH because the defect is on the face of the motion and the minutes.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b)-(c), II(a)-(b), III (nonpublic motion must state the specific exemption; discussion confined to it; seal only by 2/3 recorded vote in public session with a statutory determination); 3.18.26 agenda, Non-Public Session item; 3.18.26 approved minutes, Non-Public Session

MEDIUM The nonpublic minutes were sealed for four weeks with no statutory determination recorded

The approved minutes record that after the board returned to public session at 9:49 p.m., Michael Petrin made a motion to seal the minutes for 4 weeks, Don Lavalette seconded, by roll call, all present in favor. RSA 91-A:3, III allows nonpublic minutes to be withheld beyond 72 hours only "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session" in which "it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism" (statute text). The minutes record the vote and the period but no determination. A short, time-limited seal tied to a pending hire is consistent with the "render the proposed action ineffective" ground, but the record does not say so.

The vote happened after the recording ended, so the minutes are the only account (flag 11). Whether the nonpublic minutes were released when the four weeks ran out could not be checked: the 2026 Non-Public folder in the Unsealed Minutes share returned no listing through the Drive API on October 3, 2026.

RSA 194:61, III gives an approved chartered public school a right of first refusal on an unused district facility, but the notice must state "the expiration date of the right of first refusal which shall be 60 days after the date of the notification to the charter school administrator", and a school that does not act "shall forfeit such right"; the price is set by appraisal at fair market value (statute text).
