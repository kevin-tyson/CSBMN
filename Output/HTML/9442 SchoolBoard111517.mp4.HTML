Claremont School Board — November 15, 2017

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes are posted in the district's online shares, and the recording begins partway through the evening. With the chair absent, Vice Chair Chris Irish presided. A Ward 1 parent challenged 23-pupil kindergarten classes at Bluff School, students presented extended learning opportunities, the technology department defended its staffing, and the superintendent handed out a first-draft FY2019 budget that did not include the board's directive of $1.2 million below FY2018, prompting a sharp exchange and his commitment to deliver that budget on December 6. The board set a budget study session for November 29. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

OBSERVATION A class-size remedy was to go to the board in nonpublic session, and the superintendent said the district does not follow the state board

Responding to the Bluff kindergarten complaint, the superintendent said a resolution would be presented "to the school board and nonpublic" (0:14:04) and would cost money (0:18:34); he cited not wanting to stigmatize "a group of children" (0:18:12). RSA 91-A:3, II as it read in 2017 allows nonpublic sessions only for listed matters, such as (a) an employee's dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline, (b) "The hiring of any person as a public employee", and (c) matters likely to affect adversely the reputation of a person other than a member; the motion must state the exemption and pass by roll call (I(b)). A staffing change that spends district money is ordinarily public business, though a discussion of identifiable pupils could fit II(c). Whether any such session occurred, and on what motion, is not on this recording. The superintendent also said "We don't follow the Board of education. We follow the state fire marshal" (0:12:11). The citizen's square-footage figure and any State Board classroom-size rule were not verified for this page, so the statement is recorded, not assessed.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and II(a)-(c) (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017).
