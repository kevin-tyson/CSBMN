SAU 6 Board, December 1, 2022

Regular meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity board of School Administrative Unit 6. The board went line by line through the proposed FY24 SAU budget ($2,046,679.61, up 13.96%), including 6.5% raises, cell phone and mileage stipends, a new Human Resources Director and fully employer-paid health insurance, and sent it back for revision with a list of action items; no budget vote was taken. A citizen who described himself as a former board chair asked the board not to give SAU staff raises in light of the November organizational study.
