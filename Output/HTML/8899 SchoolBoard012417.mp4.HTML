OBSERVATION The default-budget comparison stated after the restoration does not reconcile with the figures given

Before the vote the proposed operating budget was 31,000,001 93006 [$31,193,006] (0:06:56); after it, 31,000,002 83006 [$31,283,006] (1:01:58). The business administrator then gave the default budget as 31,000,207 288 [$31,207,288] and said it "Was $14,282 higher than the proposed budget. Now it's going to be $104,282 higher." (1:05:14). The first comparison checks ($31,207,288 less $31,193,006 is $14,282). The second does not: adding $90,000 to the proposed budget puts it about $75,718 above the default, not $104,282 below it. The figures are fused ASR numbers and may be misheard, so this is an observation, not a finding; the posted MS-27 default budget form would settle it. The chair's point that next year's default would be built on this year's budget matches the statute: RSA 40:13, IX(b) defines the default budget as the prior year's appropriations, adjusted for debt service, contracts and other obligations, less one-time expenditures.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) and XI(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 7:1-4 and 190:1-3, 8-10, so in force until August 2018)
