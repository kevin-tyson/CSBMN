Claremont School Board, December 17, 2025

Regular meeting, six of seven members present, at which the administration first showed the FY 2026-27 budget: $44,861,008 (up 3.89%) for a four-school model the interim superintendent recommends, or $43,968,104 (up 1.83%) for a three-school model, against $43,179,564 this year. No decision was taken; about eighteen members of the public spoke against more school moves, and the comment period was extended. The board also accepted ski-program gifts, set tuition rates, accepted five retirements, restored two Stevens High School positions, adopted two policies, raised the clerk's stipend and met in nonpublic session under a defective citation. HIGH The motion to enter nonpublic session cited RSA 91-A:2, III, which holds no exemption, and the agenda and minutes attach different texts to the same letters

RSA 91-A:3, I(b): "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II which is relied upon as foundation for the nonpublic session." On tape, Board Member Sprague moved "We go to nonpublic session under RSA 91, dash a colon, two comma three, for the purpose of discussing, a matter of which the public body has supervision, control and so on." (2:53:47). No lettered exemption is spoken, and RSA 91-A:2, III is the electronic-participation paragraph ("A public body may, but is not required to, allow one or more members of the body to participate in a meeting by electronic or other means"). The paperwork compounds it. The agenda heads the item "RSA 91-A:2, III" and then quotes, as (a), the collective-bargaining exclusion from the definition of a meeting in RSA 91-A:2, I(a) ("Strategy or negotiations with respect to collective bargaining"), followed by the tuition-contract exemption that is actually RSA 91-A:3, II(k). The approved public minutes record the motion as "under RSA 91-A:2, III (a & k)" but print under the heading the text of RSA 91-A:3, II(a) (dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline of an employee). So the same (a) means collective bargaining in the agenda and employee compensation in the minutes, and the cited paragraph contains neither. In mitigation, the separate nonpublic-session form ticks RSA 91-A:3, II(a) and II(k) correctly, the entry vote was by roll call as I(b) requires (Yes six times, 2:54:11 to 2:54:16; minutes: "Roll call vote taken by Noelle Kronberg, all present voting in favor"), and the matters recorded (personnel, board-clerk compensation, administrators' negotiations) fit II(a) at least in part. Two further points from the nonpublic minutes: no tuition-contract matter is recorded despite the II(k) tick, and "administrators' negotiations" is collective-bargaining strategy, which RSA 91-A:2, I(a) removes from the definition of a meeting rather than listing among the II exemptions. Graded HIGH because the motion as made and as minuted does not state an exemption under paragraph II, which is the requirement I(b) puts on its face.

OBSERVATION The "Default Budget" item produced no default-budget figure, and the business administrator's account of the law is narrower than the statute's text

Agenda item 4 was "Default Budget & Options Summary". No default-budget amount was stated. Asked whether the default would represent the current five-school budget, the interim business administrator said "when I reviewed the laws, it's going to have to mirror the model to get selected. So if the school board decides that it's going to do a for school model, I have to do a for school default budget." (2:17:55). RSA 40:13, IX(b) defines the default budget as "the amount of the same appropriations as contained in the operating budget authorized for the previous year, reduced and increased, as the case may be, by debt service, contracts, and other obligations previously incurred or mandated by law, and reduced by one-time expenditures contained in the operating budget and by salaries and benefits of positions that have been eliminated in the proposed budget." The chosen model can move the default through the eliminated-positions clause, but the statutory starting point is last year's appropriations, not the chosen model.
