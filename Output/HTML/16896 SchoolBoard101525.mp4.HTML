Claremont School Board, October 15, 2025

Regular board meeting with the chair absent and Vice Chair Michael Petrin presiding, one member on Zoom and the board clerk on leave. The board heard a presentation on two paid school-psychology interns, was told of the former business administrator's unpaid disciplinary suspension, appointed an interim middle-school principal, voted to publish staff salary and benefit data, and was asked to sign the FY2025 DOE-25 and MS-25, due September 1, on the night of the meeting. The interim business administrator gave two tax-rate estimates, 9.18% and 6.08%, depending on special-education aid. These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on October 15, 2025, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

HIGH The FY2025 DOE-25, MS-25 and revised revenue estimate, due September 1, were completed the morning of October 15 and signed that night

Angell at 1:40:18: "I, I emailed copies of them to you this this morning. Unfortunately, I've been working on it for like the last week, and I finished it this morning. So I'm sorry about normally I get this done in August". At 1:02:31 he says his focus "up until today was to make sure the end of year report was, finished, estimated revenues is finished". The MS-25 in the packet prints its own deadline, "DUE TO THE NH DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE Not Later Than September 1, 2025", and each page carries the print stamp "10/15/2025 11:00 AM"; the DOE-25 file was placed in the packet folder at 17:08 UTC on October 15 (Drive metadata). RSA 198:4-d, as in force from July 1, 2025, requires the district's financial report "on or before September 1 of each year" and contains no extension provision; RSA 21-J:34, V requires school districts to "submit financial reports on or before September 1 of each year", and ¶III requires the revised estimate of revenues "by September 1 of each year". On the record these filings were 44 days past due when the board signed them. Mitigation stated on the record: the business administrator had been on leave since August 22 (1:13:21) and Angell had been brought in after the crisis; the filings were completed before the tax rate was set, and Angell planned to "file everything tomorrow" (1:45:30). The deadline still passed, and the board received the documents, which carry a chair's certification under penalty of perjury, the morning it was asked to sign them.

Sources: RSA 198:4-d (2025 codification; financial report on or before September 1); RSA 21-J:34, III and V (revised revenues and financial report by September 1); FY 25 MS-25.pdf; FY 25 DOE-25.pdf

MEDIUM The public minutes reached the district's minutes folder about six weeks after the meeting

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to be "promptly recorded and open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting", which for this meeting was Wednesday, October 22, 2025. The draft public minutes were created in the 2025 Meeting Minutes folder at 03:24 UTC on November 26, 2025 (Drive metadata), the evening of November 25 in Claremont and about 28 business days after the meeting, counting Veterans Day as a holiday. Drive creation dates show when a document became visible in the public folder, not when it was written. Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH under the project's severity rule because the record mitigates the gap: the clerk's maternity leave is stated on tape (2:08:44), the agenda itself advertised for a transcriber at $300 a meeting, and Kennedy said the transcriber would work from "the video footage" (2:09:50). The recording was available throughout. The gap is still well beyond the statutory period.
