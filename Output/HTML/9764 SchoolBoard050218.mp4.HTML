The superintendent asked approval of “the $1,000 grant to be used for the field trips” and Benware moved to accept it (1:30:37). Under RSA 198:20-b (2017 codification), a board may accept unanticipated money only where the district has adopted the enabling article (¶I); for amounts under $5,000 it shall post notice of the funds in the agenda and shall include notice in the minutes (¶III(b)). OBSERVATION HIPAA cited as the barrier to student lice data; HIPAA's privacy rule excludes education records

Members asked for counts of cases by school and year (0:40:19, 1:09:19). The superintendent answered that “the hippo laws, which which prevent public bodies from having access to information about students” make that a challenge. The federal privacy rule's definition of protected health information excludes individually identifiable health information In education records covered by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, so student health information held by the district is governed by FERPA, not HIPAA. Whether aggregate counts could be shared under FERPA is not decided on this page; the point is that the statute named on the record is the wrong one.

Sources: 45 CFR 160.103, “protected health information” (2)(i) (2017 CFR edition)
