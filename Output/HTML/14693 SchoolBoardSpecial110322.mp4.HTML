MEDIUM The meeting approved two sets of old minutes although RSA 197:3-a bars “any other business”

The warrant called this meeting under RSA 197:3-a, a single-session special meeting limited to an increase or reduction of appropriations in response to a change in RSA 198:41 aid. Paragraph VII ends: the legislative body shall not “act on any other business at the meeting.” The agenda nonetheless carried, and the meeting voted on, approval of the minutes of the February 9, 2022 deliberative session (amended at 0:06:28) and of the February 1, 2021 virtual deliberative session (0:07:03). The warrant itself named only Article 1.

Paragraph VIII excuses compliance with RSA ch. 197 and several other chapters for meetings under this section; it does not relax ¶VII. Nothing in the record suggests the votes on the minutes affect the article’s validity, and approving a prior session’s minutes is housekeeping that the deliberative sessions themselves had missed, as the moderator explained. That is why this is graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH. Separately, ¶VII speaks of approving or disapproving the proposed increase; whether a floor amendment that attaches spending conditions without changing the amount fits within it is not addressed by the text, and this page does not decide it.

Sources: RSA 197:3-a, VII and VIII (2013, 197:1; no other business; chapters excused); 11.3.22 Agenda Special District Meeting; warrant

MEDIUM Minutes reached the public share three months later and omit the main motion’s mover and the amendment vote

A district meeting is a public body (“Any legislative body” of a school district, RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d)), so RSA 91-A:2, II applies: minutes open to inspection within five business days, naming members, persons appearing, and the mover and seconder of each motion. The only minutes located are a draft with a Drive createdTime of 6 February 2023, filed in the packet for the February 8, 2023 deliberative session, 95 days after the meeting. createdTime dates this copy’s arrival in the share, not its writing.

On content: the motion to move the article was made and seconded from the floor at 0:13:42, and the minutes name neither person. The tape shows three separate votes at the end (move the question, the amendment, the article as amended); the minutes record the first and the last only. They do name the movers and seconders of the amendment, the motion to move the question and both minutes approvals. The minutes are also dated “November 3, 022” in their page headers.

Limb applied (html_briefing §5): MEDIUM, because the meeting was noticed and documented and minutes exist; their timing and two content gaps are the issue.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (any legislative body of a school district); RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification; minutes within 5 business days; mover and seconder); draft 11.3.22 Special District Minutes

OBSERVATION Approval of the 2021 deliberative-session minutes came twenty-one months late

The moderator explains at 0:07:03 that the February 1, 2021 minutes “should have been approved at the February 9th, 2022 meeting” and were missed. A copy of those minutes was in this meeting’s packet (2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting). RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to be available, not approved, within five business days, so late approval is not itself a breach; it is recorded here because a member also found the February 2022 minutes omitted two attendees (0:06:28).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (availability, drafts suffice); 2.9.22 District Meeting Minutes; 2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting

OBSERVATION The printed rules of procedure describe an SB 2 deliberative session, not this single-session meeting

The 11.3.22 Rules of Procedure handed to voters say “Claremont is an SB2 community”, “All Warrant Articles are subject to the Deliberative Session Procedures”, “Amendments, if carried, will be placed on the ballot in lieu of the original Warrant Article” and “Warrant Articles do not require a motion to be passed on to the voters.” Under RSA 197:3-a, I this meeting was held “in a single session for deliberating and voting, and without regard to the provisions of RSA 40:13”: there was no ballot, and the vote in the room was final. Someone corrected course on tape at 0:13:22 and a motion was made.

The sheet also tells voters that any one voter who doubts a non-counted vote may require a counted show of hands. Where RSA 40:4-b governs, the remedy is a secret ballot on the request of seven voters. Whether RSA 40:4-b reaches a school district meeting held under RSA 197:3-a was not verified, so this is an Observation, and no vote was questioned at this meeting.

Sources: RSA 197:3-a, I (single session, without regard to RSA 40:13); RSA 40:13 (official ballot); RSA 40:4-b (questioning a vote)
