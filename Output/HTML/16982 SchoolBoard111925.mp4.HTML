Claremont School Board — November 19, 2025

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, built from the CCTV recording, the dialogue transcript, the posted agenda, the draft minutes and the packet folder. Much of the evening went to the interim superintendent and acting student services director counting staff: the middle school against the state minimum standards, then each special-education program against the one-to-one support students’ IEPs prescribe. The board also reinstated the truancy officer 4–2, defeated a head-lice policy revision 2–3 and heard a general-ledger cash balance of $307,000. HIGH District officers stated that one-to-one supports prescribed by students’ IEPs are unstaffed in several special-education programs

34 CFR 300.323(c)(2) requires each public agency to ensure that “special education and related services are made available to the child in accordance with the child’s IEP.” Asked at 0:41:50 whether the missing paraprofessionals are required by students’ IEPs, the answer on the recording is “Yes they are.” Program by program (0:42:22 to 0:52:13; minutes, “Student Services Staffing”): the K–1 room has five of six one-to-one paraprofessionals, “prescribed by IEPs, still need 1 more” (minutes); the grades 2–3 room has seven of eight; Visions has “7 paras needed, 4 filled” (minutes) and no teacher; Camp at the middle school has no special educator on staff and no paraprofessional, though the acting director said a self-contained program must have both; Academy’s two paraprofessionals are both leaving; Pride has an open post. The finance report shows the effect: contracted services at −$701,000 against $145,000 of unspent special-education salary (1:05:43). Limits: the record shows vacancies against prescribed supports on one date. It does not show that any student went without services; staff described contracted help being sought (0:49:08), a behavior analyst supporting one room, and specialized instruction pushed in where a post is empty. HIGH because the district’s own officers stated on the record that IEP-prescribed supports are not staffed, across several programs at once.

Sources: 34 CFR 300.323(c)(2) (services made available in accordance with the IEP; read on eCFR 2026-09-25); RSA ch. 186-C (special education; cited at chapter level); 11.19.25 DRAFT CSB Minutes.

MEDIUM Draft minutes of three autumn meetings entered the public share weeks after the five-business-day deadline, and the chair apologized for it
