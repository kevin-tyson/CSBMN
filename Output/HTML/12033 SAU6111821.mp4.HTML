SAU 6 Board, November 18, 2021

Regular meeting of the SAU 6 board (Claremont and Unity) at which the proposed FY2023 SAU budget was presented: no staffing change except a half-time receptionist, the merit pool held back pending evaluations, three salaries raised above budget, $100,000 of last year's surplus committed to the SAU office, and audits two years behind. The superintendent reported that Claremont had tabled its elementary restructuring. The board then moved into nonpublic session on a motion that named no exemption. HIGH Nonpublic session entered on a motion that named no statutory exemption, by a voice vote, with a member attending remotely

At the end of the public meeting the chair said “We're gonna we gotta move. Closing activities after the nonpublic.” (0:37:22) Someone asked “Any of the subsections. Or can we just.” (0:37:33) Horsky then moved “to go into public session. State and personnel.” (0:37:40) (the context shows he meant nonpublic). The chair prompted “So you got the RSA.” (0:37:49) and the assistant superintendent for finance supplied “91-A colon three. There you go.” (0:37:52) The chair took a second from Stone and called “Set any discussion? All in favor?” (0:37:57), where the recording ends.

RSA 91-A:3, I(b), as it read on this date (the 2019 codification's text, unchanged by the 2021 amendments in these passages), requires the motion to enter nonpublic session to state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II relied on, and the vote on it to be by roll call. “State and personnel.” (0:37:40) is not an exemption: paragraph II separately lists, among others, the dismissal, promotion, compensation or discipline of a public employee (II(a)), the hiring of a public employee (II(b)), and matters likely to affect a non-member's reputation (II(c)). The question about “Any of the subsections.” shows the requirement was raised in the room, and the motion went forward without one. The vote was called as a voice vote; the recording ends before any roll call could be heard, so a roll call taken afterwards cannot be ruled out, but none was called. Separately, Horsky was attending remotely (flag 3), and RSA 91-A:2, III(e) requires every vote at such a meeting to be by roll call. No minutes of the session survive, so whether the nonpublic discussion stayed within an exemption, whether any decision was made, and whether minutes were disclosed within 72 hours under RSA 91-A:3, III cannot be checked.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(a)-(c) and III (2019 codification; these passages are unchanged through the 2021 amendments); RSA 91-A:2, III(e) (2021 codification): all votes by roll call when a member participates remotely
