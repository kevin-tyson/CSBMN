Items flagged for review

MEDIUM The motion to enter nonpublic session states no exemption on the recording; the minutes say it cited RSA 91-A:3, II(c)

The agenda noticed the session under RSA 91-A:3, II(c) (reputation), and the board voted to move it to the top of the agenda (0:01:27). When the chair called for it she asked only, "Can I have a motion to move into nonpublic?" (0:02:22), and Bonnie Miles replied, "I can make a motion to move into nonpublic." (0:02:42). Frank Sprague seconded and the roll was called (0:02:51). No exemption is spoken in the motion, the second or the roll call. The approved minutes record the same motion as made "under RSA 91-A:3 ©" (the district's rendering of (c)).

RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires that the motion "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II" and be decided by roll call. The board's own by-laws (Appendix D) say "It is vital that a specific reference to one of these grounds be made on the record before entering a non-public session." The roll call and the agenda notice satisfy part of the rule; the motion as heard does not. Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH because the agenda the board was working from names the exemption and the minutes record it, so the ground is on the written record, though not in the motion as spoken. (Whether that agenda was posted online before the meeting cannot be shown from Drive; see flag 3.)

MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed for five years without the determination RSA 91-A:3, III requires

After the board came out of nonpublic ("we're out of nonpublic at 728", 0:04:31), the chair moved: "I would like to make a motion to seal the minutes for five years." (0:04:47). Michael Petrin seconded and the roll was called; every member present voted yes (0:04:59 to 0:05:03). Five members were present, so the two-thirds vote was met. Neither the motion nor the approved minutes ("Heather Whitney made a motion to seal the minutes for 5 years") states any of the statutory grounds.

RSA 91-A:3, III lets a body withhold nonpublic minutes only where, "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism". The board's by-laws (Appendix D) add: "The decision to seal minutes of non-public sessions must affirmatively state one of the statutory bases listed above." The vote is recorded; the determination is not. A fixed five-year term is the board's choice: the statute itself withholds only "until, in the opinion of a majority of members, the aforesaid circumstances no longer apply."

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and III (motion states the exemption; sealing needs a recorded 2/3 vote and a determination); Claremont School Board By-Laws, adopted June 5, 2024 (Exhibit E of the June 5, 2024 packet) (Appendix D, sealing minutes); 16. Approved CSB meeting minutes 2.5.25

OBSERVATION The $14,000 donation was accepted without a hearing; the $20,000 hearing threshold is the statute's, not the policy committee's

Frank Sprague asked, "Isn't that a public hearing?" (0:06:58). The chair replied that the policy committee had changed the amount ("We changed the amount for a policy committee.", 0:07:03) and added "We did pump it way up to prevent a lot of public hearings." (0:07:08). The approved minutes put it as the policy committee having "recently adjusted the amount for donations that need to go to public hearing (under $20,000)".

The $20,000 figure is set by RSA 198:20-b, III(a), as amended by 2023, 38:1 (effective July 18, 2023): at $20,000 or more the board must hold a prior public hearing with seven days' newspaper notice. Below it, the board "shall post notice of the funds in the agenda and shall include notice in the minutes," which this board did (the agenda names the foundation and the amount; the minutes record the acceptance). A board may set a lower notice amount, not a higher one. The acceptance itself appears to comply; the explanation given on the record misplaces the source of the threshold. RSA 198:20-b, I also conditions the board's authority on a district meeting article; that article was not checked for this page.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III (unanticipated funds; $20,000 hearing threshold; notice in agenda and minutes below it); CSB Agenda 2.5.25.pdf; 16. Approved CSB meeting minutes 2.5.25
