SAU 6 Board, November 14, 2024

Regular meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity board, eight of twelve members present and the clerk absent. MEDIUM This meeting's draft minutes reached the public share three weeks after the five-business-day deadline

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes "promptly recorded and open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting", which made Thursday, November 21, 2024 the deadline. The draft's Drive file was created December 12, 2024 at about 4:00 p.m. Eastern, inside the packet folder for the December 12 budget hearing; it was not in the folder for the SAU 6 meeting held on November 21 itself. This is graded MEDIUM, not HIGH, under the project's severity rule: the minutes exist, are detailed, and record a mover and seconder for every motion; Drive dates show when the public could read them online, not whether a copy could be inspected at the SAU office earlier. The same night the board approved the September 12 minutes, whose own draft had reached the share only on November 8, eight weeks after that meeting (see the September 12 page).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2024 codification; 5 business days; movers and seconders); 11.14.24 draft minutes (Drive createdTime 2024-12-12)

MEDIUM Nine sets of nonpublic minutes became public records this night, and none has been located in a public share

RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes to be disclosed within 72 hours unless a recorded two-thirds vote taken in public session seals them on a statutory finding. The motion to seal this night's nonpublic minutes for nine years was made and seconded (0:06:35) but never voted; members called them "Generic to two sentences" (0:06:47), and the draft minutes say "Mr. Ruggeri withdrew his motion, minutes were not sealed". So the 72-hour clock ran from this meeting. The board also voted to release eight sealed sets from 2018 and 2023 (0:42:40), and the chair said "These minutes will be posted within 72 hours on the website." (0:42:43). None of the nine has been found: the Claremont Meeting Minutes share holds no SAU 6 file (MAP.md), and that share's Unsealed Minutes folder, created January 13, 2025, lists only 2025 Non-Public, 2026 Non-Public and 2027 Non-Public (checked October 3, 2026; Drive listings can under-report, so this is a search result, not proof). The SAU 6 packet folders in MAP.md list no nonpublic minutes either. The limit of that check: the SAU 6 website as it stood in late 2024 cannot be inspected from here, so this is "not found", not "never posted". MEDIUM because the votes and the promise are on the record and only the publication is unverified.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (72-hour disclosure unless sealed by recorded 2/3 vote in public session); 11.14.24 draft minutes, items III and IV.4

MEDIUM Three SAU fiscal years remain unaudited while the board plans to spend a "little over $300,000" surplus

The business administrator put the SAU's surplus at "a little over $300,000", then qualified it: "because of course, we haven't been audited on the other three years that I have listed." (0:14:05). Exhibit A shows the Business Office "Auditors" line at $0.00 actual in FY23 and FY24, with $10,000 budgeted in FY25 and FY26. The chair had asked for an audit-presentation timeline in the packet and it was not there (0:07:44); the agenda carries "Audit Dates" only as a future item. RSA 194-C:4, II(a) lists audits among the services every SAU must provide. No located statute sets a deadline for an SAU audit (RSA 21-J:19, the audit statute for school districts, is permissive and does not name SAUs), so this is graded MEDIUM as a control weakness rather than a missed deadline. The practical point is that the fund balance the board was told to "take the full 180 out of" (0:32:09) has no audited figure behind it.

Sources: RSA 194-C:4, II(a) (audits among SAU services); RSA 21-J:19 (permissive; towns, school districts, village districts); Exhibit A, SAU6 FY26 Proposed Budget V1, line 100.41.2520.383

OBSERVATION The April 11, 2024 six-month seal is not on the unsealing list, though its term had run

The April 11, 2024 draft minutes record a roll-call vote "to seal the minutes for six months to allow time for the negotiations to finish". Six months ran out about October 11, 2024. The list read this night (0:41:47, 0:42:17) covers four 2018 and four 2023 sets and nothing from 2024, and the recording does not say whether the April 2024 minutes were reviewed and kept sealed or simply not reached. RSA 91-A:3, III keeps minutes sealed only while the statutory circumstances apply, and RSA 91-A:3, IV(a) lets a body review that by majority vote; neither turns a time-limited seal vote into automatic release, so this is an observation. A reader should ask the SAU whether the April 2024 nonpublic minutes are now public.

Sources: 4.11.24 draft SAU 6 minutes, item IV; RSA 91-A:3, III and IV(a)

OBSERVATION The printed 7.23% nets revenue against a gross base; the gross increase is 12.25%

Exhibit A's totals are $2,489,151.00 for FY25 and $2,794,151.54 proposed for FY26, an increase of $305,000.54, or 12.25%. The sheet then subtracts $125,000 of revenue ($50,000 of contracted services and $75,000 of grant indirect costs) and prints "Total Budget Increase $180,000.54" and "Total Budget % Increase 7.23%". That percentage divides a net increase by the gross prior-year budget, so it is neither the gross nor a like-for-like net change. On tape the 7.23% is described as one that "would put people into panic" (0:14:29) and "not truly a 7.23% increase" (0:16:05); the gross dollar figure comes out only when the superintendent asks (0:33:23, 0:33:32), and 12.25% is never said aloud. No statute or GASB standard governs the arithmetic of a proposed budget worksheet; this is noted so a reader of the exhibit knows which number is which.

Sources: Exhibit A, SAU6 FY26 Proposed Budget V1, totals page

POSITIVE The board reviewed old seals and released eight sets of nonpublic minutes, some sealed since 2018

RSA 91-A:3, IV(a) lets a public body review sealed minutes and release them by majority vote once the reasons for sealing no longer apply, and II(m) requires that vote to be taken in public. The board did exactly that for four 2018 and four 2023 sets (0:41:47, 0:42:40), adding the item openly by amendment at the start of the meeting.
