Claremont School Board (part 1 of 2), January 3, 2018

First of two recordings of the regular meeting held after the budget hearing: the board reconsidered its December 20 budget, debated whether to add the $307,153 cost of the newly ratified teachers' agreement, rejected that addition 3-4 on a roll call, and began debate on sending $31,148,256.95 to the deliberative session. It also accepted a $2,977 grant and a $2,000 donation. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. RSA 91-A:2, II, in the text in force for all of 2018, required minutes that "shall include the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", open to public inspection "not more than 5 business days after the meeting". OBSERVATION A nonpublic session held before the hearing is on no recording, and its basis cannot be checked

The chair opens this recording with "Need a motion to come out of nonpublic, which we had before our budget hearing." (0:00:08) and notes the roll call was taken "when we opened the meeting earlier" (0:00:34). That earlier portion was not recorded. RSA 91-A:3, I(b), as it read in 2018, required that "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II" and be decided by roll call; RSA 91-A:3, III required nonpublic minutes to be disclosed within 72 hours unless sealed by a recorded two-thirds vote taken in public session. None of the motion, the exemption cited, the roll call, a sealing vote or the nonpublic minutes can be seen from here, and the subject of the session is never stated on tape. Nothing on the recording suggests the session was improper; the gap is that nothing public lets a reader confirm it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and III (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017; 280:1)

OBSERVATION The teachers' agreement came to the board in nonpublic session, and its ratification vote is on no recording

The superintendent told the hearing the board "approved and ratified" the agreement on December 20 (part 1 of the hearing), and the business administrator puts its 2018-19 cost at $307,153. Here an administrator says the agreement "came to you in a non public meeting after the last board meeting that was held at the tech Center, we went over into the other room, and that was one of two items discussed in that." (0:12:51). The collective-bargaining exclusion from the definition of a meeting, RSA 91-A:2, I(a) in the 2018 text, reaches only "Strategy or negotiations with respect to collective bargaining", and RSA 91-A:3, II lists no exemption for ratifying a bargaining agreement, so the ratification vote belonged in public session. The December 20 recording (Cablecast show 9507) ends at the roll call to enter nonpublic, so whether the board returned to public session and voted there cannot be determined from the recordings, and the minutes that would settle it are not public. The ratification's cost is the reason the board reconsidered its budget at this meeting.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I(a) (2017 codification, in force for 2018); RSA 91-A:3, II (2017 codification)

OBSERVATION The contract's cost items go to voters inside the operating budget, on the strength of an unidentified court decision

The business administrator said Claremont is "one of the only, if not the only district that is required to put SRE or our teachers contract in the made [main] budget" (0:13:19), and the chair attributed this to "a Supreme Court decision" (0:14:39); neither identified it, and this page has not located it. RSA 273-A:3, II(b) provides that "Only cost items shall be submitted to the legislative body of the public employer for approval at the next annual meeting of the legislative body". Placing the $307,153 inside the operating-budget article means voters cannot approve or reject the contract's cost separately, and the board's choice tonight was whether to absorb it within the December 20 total (a deeper cut elsewhere) or raise the total. Whether that placement satisfies RSA 273-A:3, II(b) is a legal question this page cannot settle. The board's decision after the hearing to finalize a figure is itself consistent with RSA 32:5, I, which directs the governing body "after the conclusion of public testimony" to "finalize the budget".

Sources: RSA 273-A:3, II(b) (source note ends 2013, 244:1, eff. Sept. 22, 2013); RSA 32:5, I (2017 codification)

OBSERVATION The default budget figure is garbled in the transcript; the speaker's own comparison puts it near $31.65 million

The business administrator's figure is transcribed "It's 35,111,787" (0:25:04). That cannot be right as transcribed: it would sit about $3.7 million above the operating budget, yet seconds later he says the default is "191, 377 more than the operating budget" (0:25:26). Added to the $31,455,410 operating figure he had just given, $191,377 yields $31,646,787, which shares the transcript's last three digits; the transcription probably misheard the leading digits. This is the page's arithmetic, not a figure stated on tape. A default budget above the proposed operating budget is lawful: RSA 40:13, IX(b), as it read in 2018, built the default from the prior year's appropriations adjusted for debt service, contracts and other obligations, less one-time expenditures, and the superintendent explained at the hearing why Claremont's ran higher. The consequence Sprague names (0:24:49) is real: if voters rejected the lower operating budget, the higher default would be the fallback, as O'Neill had just said (0:23:05).

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) (2017 codification; in force until August 2018)

OBSERVATION The board struck its scheduled review of the hearing testimony before voting on the budget

The agenda carried "a review of citizens comments from the public hearing" (0:01:53), which the board removed on the vice chair's motion (0:01:28) minutes after roughly two dozen people had testified. The vice chair himself later said "I wish we'd talk more about this during the public hearing." (0:20:54). Sources: RSA 32:5, I (2017 codification)

POSITIVE Each member's vote on adding the contract cost is on the public record by roll call

The chair put both the motion to call the question and Herzog's amendment to a roll call (1:02:46, 1:04:02), and the clerk called each member by name. In 2018, RSA 91-A required roll calls when a member participated remotely (RSA 91-A:2, III(e)) and to enter nonpublic session (RSA 91-A:3, I(b)); an in-person budget vote could be taken by voice. Recording each member's position on the $307,153 question, on a 3-4 vote, goes beyond the minimum and lets voters see who stood where before the deliberative session.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(e) (2017 codification: roll call when a member participates remotely); RSA 91-A:3, I(b) (2017 codification)
