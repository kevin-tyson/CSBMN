Claremont School Board, Budget Public Hearing, January 2, 2019

Public hearing on the proposed FY2019-20 budget: three members of the board's budget subcommittee presented an operating budget of $31,828,063 and five warrant articles, the business administrator explained a default budget that for the first time came in below the proposed budget, and three members of the public asked questions. After a break the board sent all warrant articles to the deliberative session and adopted a group of policies on second and final reading. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no notice, agenda, budget handout or minutes survive in the district's online shares. OBSERVATION A board member's questions were cut off during the public hearing

During the public-question period a board member challenged the alternative program, asking "So why are they being segregated?" (0:23:00), and later said she would "step back off as a board member" to ask her questions (0:43:53). The chair ruled her out of order, saying it was "not our role during public to to argue with taxpayers" (0:47:03), and a colleague suggested she take questions to the special education director (0:47:15). RSA 32:5, I gives the hearing to "public testimony", so a chair may keep it for the public, and the board had voted the program forward on December 19. The exchange is noted because members do speak during this hearing as presenters, and the member said "Some of the board members are just seeing this today." (0:46:14). No rule verified for this page governs members' speech at the hearing.

POSITIVE The default budget was presented line by line at the first hearing, and the board presented its own budget with published rules for public questions

RSA 40:13, XI(a), as it read on this date, required only that "The default budget shall be disclosed and presented for questions and discussion at the first budget hearing". The business administrator did that and more: he handed out the line-item default budget and the statutory language, explained the 2018 change that removes eliminated positions from the default budget, and gave the result, $179,000 below the proposed budget (0:35:09 to 0:37:16). The rule he described matches RSA 40:13, IX(b) as amended in 2018. The three budget-subcommittee members presented the budget themselves instead of leaving it to the administration (0:00:10), the chair read rules giving each resident three minutes and a second turn (0:01:14), and staff answered every question asked from the floor, including the press.
