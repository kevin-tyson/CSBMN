OBSERVATION The board took a position on pending legislation by consensus, without a motion or vote

During the superintendent's report, a member asked that "the board ask the chairman to make those phone calls on our behalf" to Claremont's two representatives on SB 191 (0:38:21). Another member began a motion, members said it could not be made (0:44:21 to 0:44:38), apparently because the item was not an action item, and the chair accepted a consensus instead (0:44:39, 0:44:41). The decision was real: the board was to speak through its chair on a bill in front of the House the next day, and a member listed the calls among the evening's follow-ups (2:52:22). RSA 91-A:2, II required minutes to record "final decisions"; a consensus has no mover, no second and no count, so the public record of who supported it is the recording alone. Declining to take a formal vote on an item not on the agenda is a defensible instinct, and nothing verified for this page bars a board from asking its chair to advocate. The point is that the decision was made without the record a vote would have created.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2016 codification: minutes include "a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions")

OBSERVATION A $150,000 tech center roof: contractor engaged by the business administrator, funding from year-end surplus, no board vote on tape

The business administrator said he had "already hired" Melanson to roof the tech center "for the same price as last year's" (1:40:30), that the job "is going to cost 150,000" against a plan of $100,000 a year, and that "we have the ability with year end surplus to pay for that this year" (1:42:30). He also said the tech center roof "was passed through a warrant article" (1:38:31). He disclosed that he works with the same contractor "all over the state and several other districts", described it as the low bidder each time, and gave a $40,000 saving on the Maple roof (1:40:07, 1:40:20). No contract or bid result came before the board on this recording. RSA 32:8, in force in 2017, bars a school board from paying "for any purpose in excess of the amount appropriated by the legislative body for that purpose" except under RSA 32:9-11; if the warrant article appropriated less than the contract price, the difference would need a recorded transfer under RSA 32:10. The record does not give the warrant article's amount, the bids, or who signed the contract, and this page verified no statute requiring a school district to bid such work. The disclosure of the business relationship was made openly on camera.

Sources: RSA 32:8 (2016 codification; source 1993, 332:1); RSA 32:10, I (2016 codification; text in force until Aug. 15, 2017)
