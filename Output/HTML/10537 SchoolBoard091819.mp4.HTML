OBSERVATION $20,000 private gift accepted after the prior public hearing the statute requires; notice not verifiable

For unanticipated money of $5,000 or more from a private source, RSA 198:20-b, III(a) requires the board to hold a prior public hearing, noticed in a newspaper at least seven days ahead, and the board may accept only if the district has adopted the enabling article under ¶I. The board held the hearing at 0:00:25, heard no one, and accepted the Byrne Foundation's $20,000 in public session at 0:43:54 on Benware's motion. That sequence matches the statute. What this page cannot confirm is the newspaper notice or the enabling article; the chair says only that the item was “warned” at the previous meeting and announced (0:02:53), which refers to the agenda, not the hearing notice. The chair attributes the hearing requirement to “Board policy”; the statute imposes it independently. The opening's “20 $0” is a transcription error for the $20,000 in the motion.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(a), 2019 codification (enabling article; prior hearing and 7-day newspaper notice at $5,000 or more)

OBSERVATION State-aid figures under the 2019 budget veto are members' estimates; the stabilization phase-out they describe was statutory

With the state on a continuing resolution, members gave competing figures for Claremont's loss: “about 4.3 million” over two years against a post-veto “1 in 1 point four” (Benware, 0:24:59); a stabilization loss of “about a quarter of a million” a year (Sprague, 0:25:20); “950 zero 0 to $1.1 million short” (Benware, 0:25:58). The page cannot check these against a document; none was shown. The mechanism Sprague and Benware describe is in the statute as last codified before this meeting: from fiscal 2017 a municipality's stabilization grant is 96 percent of its 2012 amount, reduced by 4 percent of the 2012 education grant each year after. This page does not establish whether the 2019 budget, then still being negotiated, changed that schedule; the October 2 meeting reports a compromise.

Sources: RSA 198:41, IV(d), 2018 codification (96 percent for FY2017, reduced by 4 percent a year; source note ends 2016, 85:9)
