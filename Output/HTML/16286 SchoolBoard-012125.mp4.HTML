MEDIUM Seven-year seal voted with no statutory finding on the record

After the nonpublic session Petrin asked “Can I make a motion to seal the minutes for seven years?” (0:05:53); Hawkins seconded and the roll call was unanimous. RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes to be disclosed within 72 hours “unless, by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism” (statute text). The vote met the procedural half: it was a recorded roll call, in public session, and unanimous among the five present. Neither the motion nor the minutes (“Michael Petrin made a motion to seal the minutes for 7 years”) states which determination the board made. The session was entered under II(c), the reputation exemption, so a reputation finding is the likely basis, but the statute asks for the determination, not an inference from the agenda. Graded MEDIUM as a documentation gap.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (72-hour disclosure; seal only on a 2/3 determination; 2023, 189:1); approved 1.21.25 minutes

MEDIUM Motion to enter nonpublic session cited only RSA 91-A:3

RSA 91-A:3, I(b): “Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II which is relied upon as foundation for the nonpublic session” (statute text). On the recording the chair introduces “a nonpublic session per RSA 91” and “Dash a colon three” (0:03:39, 0:04:16), and the motion is “We move into nonpublic session. The RSA 91-A colon three” (0:04:24). Neither names paragraph II or a lettered ground. The agenda item, which survives only as reproduced in the minutes, cites “91-A: 3 (c)” and prints the text of II(c), so the board and the clerk had the ground before them; the minutes' citation itself omits the paragraph number. The roll-call requirement of I(b) was met. Graded MEDIUM: the statute puts the exemption on the face of the motion, and the motion as spoken does not carry it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(c) (motion must state the exemption; reputation ground); approved 1.21.25 minutes
