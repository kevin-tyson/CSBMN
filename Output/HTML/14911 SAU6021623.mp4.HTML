SAU 6 Board — February 16, 2023

A regular meeting of the joint Claremont–Unity SAU #6 School Board: a separate public body from the Claremont School Board, which had met the night before. Generated from the meeting recording's dialogue transcript, the four-document posted packet and the draft minutes. Four things make this night worth reading: the board adopted a written superintendent-evaluation process, tool and calendar after its chair said on the record that the board "haven't really been very good at that in the last few years"; a member spent twenty minutes pressing for a legal waiver before a teacher-coaching grant went forward; the chair stated that the SAU had missed its December budget hearing, and the FY24 budget was in fact adopted on January 17, past the deadline RSA 194-C:9 sets; and a nonpublic session was voted, held and sealed for "not longer than 99 years", while the microphone kept running. Body
SAU #6 School Board: the joint board of the Claremont and Unity school districts. It is a "public body" under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which names school administrative units expressly. It employs the superintendent and the central-office staff and adopts the SAU budget; the schools themselves answer to the two district boards. Not the Claremont School Board. The chair here is Marjorie Erickson of Unity; the chair of the Claremont board, Michael Petrin, sits at this table as an ordinary member.
Board composition
Eleven seats as printed on the agenda masthead: six Claremont (Petrin, Heather Whitney, Sprague, Miles, Skillen, Horsky) and five Unity (Erickson, Popescu, Bator, Ruggeri, Hart). That is one fewer than the twelve named on the same masthead on December 1, 2022: Joshua Lambert's Claremont seat had gone and was not refilled, matching the vacancy the Claremont board itself carried until the March 14, 2023 election. Ten of the eleven attended; Garry Bator was absent and was known to be leaving: "if we have outgoing board members that we know are outgoing, such as Gary" (0:35:54).
