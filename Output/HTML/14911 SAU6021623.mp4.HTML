SAU 6 Board, February 16, 2023

Regular meeting of the SAU 6 board (Claremont and Unity), ten of eleven members present, chaired by Marjorie Erickson of Unity. The board hears the TeachUNITED coaching grant, adopts a superintendent-evaluation procedure built at its January retreat, gets a status report on the Lupini organizational study, sends an exit-interview policy to second reading, holds a nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(a) and seals its minutes, and confirms Angela Vivian as HR director over one No vote. MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed "for not longer than 99 years" or a member's death, with no statutory determination on the record

RSA 91-A:3, III, as it read on this date, lets a public body withhold nonpublic minutes beyond 72 hours only by a recorded two-thirds vote of the members present, taken in public session, that determines divulgence "likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism" (statute). The vote here was in public and unanimous by roll call (1:35:15 to 1:35:56), which meets the vote requirement. No ground was stated: the motion was "to seal the nonpublic sessions minutes for not longer than 99 years, but to the date of my death", followed by banter about the mover's death, and the minutes record it as "for not longer than 99 years or his death" (minutes), also without a ground. A term pegged to a member's life is not a period the statute contemplates. The subject of the session is not stated anywhere in the public record beyond the II(a) heading. The ten-year review in RSA 91-A:3, IV did not yet exist; it took effect October 3, 2023.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (2022 codification; source note ends 2021, eff. Jan. 1, 2022); draft minutes, item 6

The posted agenda lists six action items and no nonpublic session; the minutes add one as item 6. The meeting notice, not the agenda, is what RSA 91-A:2, II requires, and it covers "a nonpublic session" held at the noticed meeting, so the omission is not itself a breach. What the record lacks is duration: the minutes give no time for entering or leaving the session, and the recording passes from the entry roll call (1:33:16) to talk about signing documents (1:33:30) with nothing to show how long the board was out. The HR director nomination, listed as agenda item 3, was moved to after the session; the record does not say whether the two are connected, and this page does not assume they are. RSA 91-A:3, II(a) covers "promotion", while hiring is a separate ground, II(b).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (notice, including a nonpublic session); RSA 91-A:3, II(a) and II(b); agenda

OBSERVATION The chair acknowledges the SAU budget calendar slipped past December; the FY2024 SAU budget was adopted January 17, 2023

RSA 194-C:9, I provides: "At a meeting held before January 1, the school administrative unit board shall adopt a budget" (statute). The chair says the board "missed having a hearing for the say [SAU] in December, which is when we should have had it" (1:14:19) and proposes a board calendar to prevent a repeat. Two points of precision. First, RSA 194-C:9 requires adoption before January 1 and requires no public hearing; holding one exceeds the statute. Second, the draft minutes of the January 17, 2023 SAU 6 hearing and meeting (filed in the March 30 packet) record the FY2024 budget being amended to $2,396,000 and approved that night, after the statutory date. That defect belongs to the January 17 meeting; it appears here because the chair raised it and the board's response was a calendar item.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I (2022 codification; source note ends 2003, 279:1); draft SAU 6 minutes, January 17, 2023
