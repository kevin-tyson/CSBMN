MEDIUM The SAU board organized on July 1, outside the April 1 to June 1 window, and no secretary was chosen on the record

RSA 194-C:5, I provides that the SAU board "shall meet between April 1 and June 1 in each year" and "shall organize by choosing a chairperson, a secretary, and a treasurer". By the superintendent's account, "The last time we met was, believe, December 10th or so." (0:01:45), and the December 10, 2020 minutes are the ones approved tonight (0:04:15), so the board did not meet in the statutory window; it organized on July 1. On the record it re-elected a chair and a vice chair (0:02:07 to 0:02:32) and elected a treasurer (0:35:47 to 0:36:02); no secretary is chosen, though the clerk takes the roll and minutes. The election was opened and called by the superintendent, who is not a member, with the vice chair present (0:01:45 to 0:02:43); no rule examined for this page says who presides before officers are chosen, so that point is noted only. Graded MEDIUM, not HIGH: the whole window fell within the COVID-19 state of emergency, which lapsed at midnight on June 11, 2021, and the project's search for an emergency order extending RSA 194-C:5 was not exhaustive. A reviewer should confirm that no order or later minutes cure the gap before treating it as a missed statutory deadline.

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, I (SAU board meets April 1 to June 1 and organizes with a chairperson, secretary and treasurer; 1996, 298:3, unamended)

OBSERVATION The superintendent evaluation subcommittee had not met since the fall; its revived meeting is a public meeting

Erickson said the subcommittee last met "in the fall" and that "nothing has been done" on the planned changes to its evaluation tool (0:46:07, 0:47:02). It now has members from both boards and a meeting set for August 4 at 5:00 p.m. (0:51:47). A subcommittee of the SAU board is itself a public body: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) reaches "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", so the August 4 session needs 24 hours' posted notice and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II, and any evaluation discussion held in private must go through a nonpublic-session motion and roll call. Setting the date, time and room in open session is a good start; no notice or minutes of the August 4 session have been found in the district's shares, which hold nothing older than 2022.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2019 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1); RSA 91-A:2, II (notice and minutes)
