Claremont School Board Finance Committee, December 12, 2025

A Friday-morning working session of the board's Finance Committee, two members with the interim business administrator and the interim superintendent, five days before the FY27 budget went to the full board. The draft stood at about a 2% increase for a four-school model; the administrators said the prior budget had underfunded health insurance and that alternative-program staff were paid from other lines; the members steered the alternative program out of the Tech Center toward Bluff School. HIGH No minutes of this committee meeting exist in any district share located, nearly ten months on

The Finance Committee is a subcommittee of the school board and therefore a public body: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) reaches "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto". RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting", and, since 2025, 112:1 (eff. Aug. 22, 2025), minutes that state "the start time and end time of the meeting, and name of the person who produced the minutes". Five business days from Friday, December 12, 2025 ran to Friday, December 19. No minutes for this session were found in the FY 2025-2026 or 2025 packet archives, in the next two board packets (14. CSB 12.17.25 and 15. CSB 1.7.26), in the district's Claremont Finance Sub Committee folder (no files; folder last modified December 1, 2025) or in its 2025-2026 finance subfolder (created July 2, 2026, no files returned), or by Drive searches on the dotted date and on "finance committee" (re-checked 2026-10-03). The committee can produce minutes: the 12/3/25 board packet carries Copy of Finance 11.12.25.docx, and a public commenter quoted those November 12 minutes at the December 3 board meeting. Nothing for December 10 or December 12 followed. Severity follows the project's rule for missing records: a public body met, nothing on the record explains the absence, and no agenda or packet exists either, so the unmitigated (HIGH) limb applies rather than the MEDIUM limb for a meeting that was noticed and packeted with only its minutes missing. The recording itself is a full public account, which limits the practical harm, but the statute asks for minutes. Interim Superintendent Kennedy's own expectation on tape was minutes: "Just show me the minutes or whatever." (1:14:34). This is a negative from searches and listings that are known to be incomplete; it reads "not found", not "never existed".
