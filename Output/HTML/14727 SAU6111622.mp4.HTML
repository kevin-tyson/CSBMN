HIGH Votes were taken by voice while three members participated by Zoom, and the minutes give no reason for remote attendance

RSA 91-A:2, III, in the text in force on this date (2019 codification, unchanged until October 3, 2023), lets a member participate remotely only when physical attendance “is not reasonably practical”; “Any reason that such attendance is not reasonably practical shall be stated in the minutes”; and “All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote.” The COVID-era Emergency Order #12 relief ended with the state of emergency on June 11, 2021.

The minutes record Marjorie Erickson, Frank Sprague and Atonya Hart “present via Zoom” and state no reason for any of them. On tape the chair gives one for two of them (“Since Marjorie and Frank are unable to attend in person”, 0:00:57); none is given for Hart. All three votes of the meeting (agenda at 0:02:24, September 29 minutes at 0:02:56, adjournment at 1:34:40) were voice votes, not roll calls.

Mitigation: the votes were procedural and no objection is heard. The statutory wording is mandatory, so the grade follows the rule rather than the stakes. The record also conflicts on whether Shannon Popescu attended in person (see flag 3), which bears on the separate requirement in ¶III(b) that a quorum be physically present.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(a), (b), (e) (2019 codification; reason in minutes, quorum present, roll-call votes); Emergency Order #12 (2020); NHPR, June 10, 2021 (state of emergency ends June 11, 2021; reporting)

POSITIVE The full consultant study was posted in the public packet and presented in open session

The Organizational Study Report was placed in the public packet folder on the day of the meeting (Drive createdTime 16 November 2022, early afternoon), and its eleven findings, several of them critical of central-office practice, were presented and questioned in public session for more than an hour.
