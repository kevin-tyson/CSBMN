The committee is a subcommittee of the school board: the chair opens by naming himself as “the chair of this committee and vice chair of the Claremont Board” and Heather Whitney and Candace Crawford as its other members (0:00:04, 0:00:26), all three present. RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) makes any committee or subcommittee of a school board a public body, and RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes open to public inspection within five business days. OBSERVATION Filled behavioral-specialist positions drop out of the proposed operating budget, to be replaced through a paraprofessional contract that would go to voters as a separate article

Questioning the Maple Avenue lines, Sprague found “one, two, three behavioral support people” in the current budget (1:07:47); Henry: “There are no behavioral support specialists or the Haverhill support in any of these budget lines okay” (1:08:01). Crawford: “each elementary school this year has one behavioral specialist, right? In this proposed budget, there are no behavioral specialists for the elementaries. Is that true?” (1:08:36); Henry: “That’s true. Correct” (1:08:58). The superintendent explained the intent is to move these roles into the paraprofessional contract, “I can only say so much about this because we’re in an active negotiation” (1:09:00), and the superintendent said the contract’s cost “would be a warrant article” (1:12:04); Henry: “So it’ll be a separate Warner” [warrant article] (1:12:11).

Under RSA 273-A:3, II(b) only cost items of a bargained agreement go to the legislative body, and RSA 273-A:1, IV defines a cost item as a benefit whose implementation requires an appropriation. If the paraprofessional article failed, the positions now in the buildings would be in neither the operating budget nor an approved contract. The record shows the board saw the structure; it does not show the board weighing that contingency. Crawford also cut off Sprague’s on-air speculation about the contract figure (“Frank, you got to stop talking about that”, {L(665)}), consistent with keeping bargaining positions off the record.

Sources: RSA 273-A:1, IV (cost item); RSA 273-A:3, II(b) (only cost items to the legislative body)

Sources: RSA 273-A:1, XI (terms and conditions include hours); RSA 273-A:5, I(e)

OBSERVATION Portable classrooms to be bought with ESSER III money before September 2024, bid not yet started

The superintendent said portable classrooms for Disnard and Bluff were “funded in the, in the Esser monies” (0:06:11). Crawford asked whether ESSER had ended; Koski: “Not until September” (1:15:52). Tempesta: “We have plans from centipede. We have plans from Fall Mountain” (1:16:28) and “We still have to go out to bid” (1:16:43); the district could buy rather than lease, as “a nice, nice one time” (1:16:48).

ESSER funds lapse once their period of availability, including the Tydings carryover year, runs out, and a federally funded purchase must follow documented procurement procedures with a record of the procurement history. With ten months left and the bid not begun, the timing was tight. Whether the units were bought is outside this record.

Sources: 20 U.S.C. §1225(b) (one additional year of availability); 2 CFR 200.318(a), (i) (2023 edition: documented procurement procedures; procurement records)

OBSERVATION Fund balance floated as a source for curriculum purchases

Koski put the curriculum cycle at “250,000 per subject area” (1:20:20), then “$250,000 a year each year for curriculum” (1:22:27). Tempesta: “There’d be a good use of, allocates some money from a fund balance into that” (1:20:28), and “if we have a, say, a $400,000 fund balance” (1:22:49).

This is loose wording, not a decision, so it is an observation. Year-end unassigned funds a district retains under RSA 198:4-b, II may be spent only after a prior public hearing, and GASB 54 governs how any such amount is classified; money not retained is not available to the board at all. The audited fund balance and any retention vote would settle what was available.

Sources: RSA 198:4-b, II (2023 codification: retained funds; hearing before expending); GASB Statement 54, ¶6–17 (fund balance classes)
