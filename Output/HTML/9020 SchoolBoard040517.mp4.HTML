MEDIUM Three grants of $5,000 or more were accepted with no prior public hearing on the record

The board accepted $44,750 in one vote (0:50:44, 0:50:54): $20,250 in Title I reallocation money, $16,500 in McKinney-Vento funds and $8,000 in two early-learning grants of $3,000 and $5,000. The item was moved from updates to action items at the start of the meeting because "That's going to require to have the board approve" (0:01:38). If the district accepted these funds under RSA 198:20-b, the statute in force in 2017 provided that "For unanticipated funds in the amount of $5,000 or more, the school board shall hold a prior public hearing", with notice of its "time, place, and subject" published in a newspaper, and for smaller amounts that the board "shall post notice of the funds in the agenda and shall include notice in the minutes". No hearing is mentioned on this recording. The record leaves open three things that would settle it: whether Claremont voters had adopted the RSA 198:20-b article, whether the funds were within the budget the voters approved and so not "unanticipated", and whether a hearing was held at an earlier meeting. The system of care grant, "already approved" (0:50:32), is not part of this vote.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III (2016 codification; source note ends 2005, 188:1)
