Claremont School Board, August 3, 2016

Regular meeting dominated by the labor board ruling against the Stevens High School A/B block schedule: the board rejected a motion not to comply, voted to appeal, rejected keeping A/B pending a ruling and tabled the schedule decision to a special meeting. It also reappointed a member of the CCTV board and accepted about ten resignations. OBSERVATION The board was told it could keep the A/B schedule while it appealed; the appeal statute says an appeal does not suspend the order

The labor board had ordered the district to comply with an arbitrator's award against the A/B schedule (Valley News, Aug. 4, 2016, report by Patrick O'Grady on the Stevens schedule dispute). Vice chair Chris Irish moved "that we do not comply with the PLO, be ruling and continue to move forward with the A b scheduling while pursuing an appeal of the ruling" (1:04:44). Arguing for it, the chair said "It's not a court order." (1:22:18) and "part of that system allows us to continue on with the schedule as we have it and continue to appeal" (1:31:57). The motion failed on a roll call, 4-3 as the Valley News reported it (1:46:23); the board then voted to appeal, and a further motion to "stick with a B and till we get a ruling" also failed (1:57:46).

RSA 273-A:14 provides that review of a final labor board order is obtained "in the manner prescribed in RSA 541", and RSA 541:18 provides that "No appeal or other proceedings taken from an order of the commission shall suspend the operation of such order" unless the supreme court orders a suspension. Section 541:18 is written for "the commission" and reaches labor board orders through 273-A:14's incorporation; a reviewer should confirm that reading. On that reading, the chair's account was wrong: an appeal alone did not let the district continue the schedule. Irish's later point that nothing would be violated before the school year began (1:55:48) is a separate argument about timing. The board adopted neither noncompliance motion, so this is an observation about the advice members acted on, not a finding of a violation.

Sources: RSA 273-A:14 (2016 codification; source 1975, 490:2); RSA 541:18 (2016 codification; source note ends 1951, 203:16); Valley News, Aug. 4, 2016, report by Patrick O'Grady on the Stevens schedule dispute (reporting)

OBSERVATION The recording starts mid-meeting, and counsel's advice from a nonpublic session was repeated in public

The recording opens on the July 6 minutes (0:00:05); there is no call to order, roll call or Pledge on tape, and the chair later remarks that "some of us have been here since 530" (0:46:00). During the appeal vote Sprague said "I like the lawyers recommendation that it becomes a a negotiating chip as we move forward with negotiations." and the chair answered "But that was nonpublic." (1:48:55, 1:49:13). When and how that nonpublic session was held is not on this recording.

In 2016, RSA 91-A:2, I(b) excluded "Consultation with legal counsel" from the definition of a meeting, and a nonpublic session required a motion stating the exemption and a roll call in public session under RSA 91-A:3, I(b). Neither the earlier session nor any motion to enter it can be checked here. Repeating privileged advice in public is the board's choice to make, not a statutory violation, but it was done by one member without a decision of the board.
