Claremont School Board, August 4, 2021

Regular meeting at which the board adopted the administration's fall reopening matrix (masks optional except on buses) on a split voice vote, heard the summer school report and a detailed account of Stevens High School's 67% completion rate for the class of 2021, approved a senior trip to Puerto Rico, let out-of-district students play non-cut middle school sports for a fee pending a formal policy, and heard the chair correct his July statements about Education Freedom Accounts. On June 2 the board let the superintendent fill budgeted vacancies over the summer, the chair explaining "we're going to allow you to defer those nominations to, to August" (see the June 2, 2021 page, flag 2). RSA 189:39 provides that "Superintendents shall nominate and school boards elect all teachers". Tonight, asked for "any updates on hiring at all?" Sources: RSA 189:39 (superintendents nominate, school boards elect teachers; 1971, 371:2, unamended)

OBSERVATION A quorum plans to attend a community forum announced as a non-meeting

To let every member attend Monday's forum on elementary restructuring, the board agreed to "worn it as a non meeting" with members present but not discussing (0:34:53, 0:35:00). RSA 91-A:2, I, as it read in 2021, defines a meeting as "the convening of a quorum of the membership of a public body" for "the purpose of discussing or acting upon a matter or matters over which the public body has supervision, control, jurisdiction, or advisory power", and lists only four exclusions (bargaining strategy, legal counsel, a party caucus, circulating draft documents); the statute has no "non-meeting" category. Members who only listen may fall outside the definition, and posting notice that a quorum may attend is the cautious course. But the forum's subject, elementary restructuring, is a matter within the board's control, and if members discuss it there, the session becomes a meeting that needs minutes. The point is recorded so the forum's conduct can be checked against that line.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I (2019 codification; meeting defined, exclusions (a) to (d))

POSITIVE A full public accounting of the class of 2021's non-completers

After the principal said at graduation that about 30 seniors had not made it, the board asked for a follow-up, and Stevens staff presented it in open session: 88 of 130 projected graduates, about 67%, against 78 to 79% in 2017 to 2019 and a state average of 85 to 87% (0:52:52, 0:53:54, 1:17:19), with every one of the 43 non-completers placed in a category and the interventions and gaps described (0:54:25 to 0:57:24).
