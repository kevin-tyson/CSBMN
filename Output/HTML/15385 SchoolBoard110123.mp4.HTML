Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on November 1, 2023, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

MEDIUM Tuition-contract minutes sealed for 50 years with no stated finding, though RSA 91-A:3, II(k) requires them to be made public

The board entered nonpublic session under the tuition-contract exemption, RSA 91-A:3, II(k), on a 7-0 roll call (1:24:46) and on return voted to seal the minutes for 50 years on Sprague's motion (1:25:50). Two problems follow. First, RSA 91-A:3, III allows sealing only by a two-thirds recorded vote "taken in public session" when "it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism." The motion and the minutes state no such determination; the vote count clears two-thirds (5-2 on tape) but the finding is missing. Second, II(k) carries its own disclosure rule: "A contract negotiated by a school board shall be made public prior to its consideration for approval by a school district, together with minutes of all meetings held in nonpublic session". A 50-year seal is hard to square with that sentence, and the board voted the $16,000 FY2025 tuition rate in public about a minute after returning (1:26:35). The MEDIUM grade reflects that the rate vote is not itself the approval of a contract, and this record does not show when any tuition contract was approved or whether these minutes were released then; if a contract was approved with them still sealed, the II(k) point becomes a likely violation. The citation is also defective in every form: the agenda and minutes print "RSA91-A:,II (k)" with no section number, and the chair read it as "91-A colon, two k" and the enabling statutes as "RSA 119 or RSA 195-9" (statute: RSA 194 or RSA 195-A). The tally differs between tape and minutes too: on tape Sprague's vote is heard (Yes, daddy, a known ASR rendering of his Yes) for 5-2, while the minutes list six votes, 4-2, and omit him.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(k), III (2023, 189:1, eff. Oct. 3, 2023: roll-call entry; tuition-contract exemption and its publication duty; sealing requires a recorded two-thirds vote and a statutory determination)

MEDIUM The board set the district clerk's pay, which RSA 194:10 assigns to the district's annual meeting

On Gallagher's motion, seconded by Miles, the board raised the clerk's compensation to $300 per board meeting and $350 per special district meeting (1:19:35), an increase the superintendent put at "approximately five, 7000 more a year" (1:13:53). The superintendent described the post as elective ("It's elected. We can.", 1:15:41), and the flyer in the packet gives it election duties (overseeing the ballot process, certifying results, administering oaths). RSA 194:10 provides that "At its annual meeting each school district shall determine the salaries of its school board and other district officers". In an SB 2 district that is the deliberative session and ballot. Mitigation: the post had been vacant (Gallagher: "No one ran.", 1:16:19), the rate is framed as pay for a person appointed until an election, and a budget line approved by voters may already fund clerical support; none of that is shown in this record. The finding is that the vote fixes an officer's salary by board action without citing any authority for doing so.

Sources: RSA 194:10 (annual meeting determines salaries of school board and other district officers); Exhibit I- School Board Clerk Flyer 11.1.23.pdf

MEDIUM The minutes omit a member who voted three times, a seconder and a vote, and the October 18 correction never reached that meeting's minutes

The approved minutes list six members present ("Frank Sprague, Heather Whitney, Arlene Hawkins, Bonnie Miles, Candace Crawford, Jennifer Gallagher"). Whitney Skillen is missing, though she answers all three roll calls on tape (1:24:46, 1:25:34, 1:26:10) and the same minutes name her as seconder of the para motion. The motion to return from nonpublic is recorded with no seconder; on tape the chair seconds it (1:25:28). The seal tally omits the mover, Sprague. The masthead names the student members "Nicole Bouchard and Alex Barney" while the body welcomes "Nicole Bouchard and Kylee Plummer". RSA 91-A:2, II requires "the names of members" and "The names of the members who made or seconded each motion". Separately, the board approved the October 18 minutes subject to the correction "Bonnie Miles was not present at the last board meeting on 10.18.23", but the October 18 minutes in the district's share (last modified November 7, 2023) still list only Gallagher absent and omit Miles and Skillen. The speaker who asked for the correction (0:01:24) is labeled Gallagher in the dialogue file, and Gallagher was in fact absent on October 18; the chair's reply, "You're documented as being on there", fits Miles better, since the October 18 minutes already list Gallagher absent. The record does not settle it. Mitigation: every vote here except the seal was unanimous, and the seal clears two-thirds on either count.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2023 codification, in force from Oct. 3, 2023)
