MEDIUM The draft minutes reached the public share on the tenth business day, and the board then postponed approving them

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to be "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting" (statute). Five business days after Wednesday, April 19 ran to Wednesday, April 26, 2023. The draft in this meeting's packet folder was created on Drive at 13:09 UTC on May 3, 2023 (9:09 a.m. Eastern), with a modified time one minute earlier, so the file metadata gives no sign the document existed sooner. That is the tenth business day. The May 3 agenda then carries "Meeting minutes from April 19,2023 postponed until the next meeting." (agenda). The superintendent had told the board at 1:43:51 that the capital-reserve trustee needed "the active minutes" before releasing roof money. At the May 3 meeting the chair explained that the person who usually takes the minutes had been away on vacation (see the May 3 page). Further mitigation, which sets this at MEDIUM under the project's severity rule rather than HIGH: the minutes exist, are complete and name every mover and seconder, and a paper copy may have been available at the SAU office earlier; Drive is one place of inspection, not the only one.

OBSERVATION The board voted to draw up to $500,000, about the whole capital reserve, without the record showing its authority to direct the withdrawal

Under RSA 35:15, I the trustees hold a capital reserve "until such time as the town, district or county shall have voted to withdraw funds from such capital reserve fund or shall have named agents of the town, district or county to carry out the objects designated" (statute). A school board can therefore direct a withdrawal only if the district meeting named it agent to expend for that fund. Nobody at the table identified the warrant article that did so, and the minutes describe the board as "appropriating up to $500,000.00 from the Capital Reserve Trust Fund", appropriation being the voters' act. The superintendent put the reserve at "500,000 that we've had in the reserve" (1:32:51), so the cap equals the fund. The motion sequence was also loose: Sprague's $350,000 motion, seconded by Miles, was never withdrawn or voted, and the substitute passed on a voice vote (1:44:32). In mitigation, the board required an RFP with at least three bids returned to it for review, and the May 3 minutes report the bids out with a public opening on May 22, 2023. The fund's establishing article and its stated purpose (RSA 35:15, V limits spending to that purpose) would settle the question.

Sources: RSA 35:15, I and V (2022 codification; source note ends 2021, 105:2); April 19 minutes; May 3 minutes (bid opening)
