SAU 6 Board, December 10, 2020

Public hearing on the fiscal year 2022 SAU 6 budget, followed by the regular SAU 6 board meeting, held in person and by video during the COVID-19 state of emergency. The board adopted a budget of $1,772,143 and heard the superintendent's report on the Goshen tuition agreement. OBSERVATION The budget was presented without position detail, and the business administrator disclosed an omission from last year's budget

Erickson reported that Unity members could see salary lines but not "who's covered in each each one of those, items" (0:08:55), and Petrin pressed on the $61,871 business-office increase (0:10:52). The business administrator explained it as a transfer of one position plus a 0.2 position he had left out of the fiscal 2021 budget, "approximately a $20,000 number" (0:13:10), and offered a staff listing to members who wanted it (0:14:31). The explanation was given in public and the figures reconcile to the motion. No rule required position-level detail in an SAU budget presentation, so this is an observation; it records that the prior year's adopted SAU budget understated a cost that was being incurred, by the business administrator's own account.

Sources: Cablecast: SAU 6 Board Meeting of 12/10/20

POSITIVE The SAU budget was adopted before January 1, after a public hearing the statute does not require

RSA 194-C:9, I, in the text then in force, provides: "At a meeting held before January 1, the school administrative unit board shall adopt a budget". The board adopted the fiscal 2022 budget on December 10, three weeks ahead of that date, and held a public hearing first, with an invitation for comment in the room and online (0:23:42). Section 194-C:9 contains no hearing requirement. The motion's $1,772,143 is consistent with the increase the business administrator described ($45,139 over the $1,727,004 read as the fiscal 2021 figure, 2.6%).

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I (2019 codification; source note 1996, 298:3; 2003, 279:1)

POSITIVE The budget was circulated before the hearing and walked through line by line in public

The business administrator emailed the budget presentation to members beforehand (0:03:33), presented its structure and the allocation between the districts, and the technology director explained each technology line (0:15:32). The public-comment opportunity was offered both in the room and online (0:23:42). These go beyond what the SAU budget statute requires.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9 (2019 codification)
