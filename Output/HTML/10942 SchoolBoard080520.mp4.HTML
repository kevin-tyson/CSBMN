Claremont School Board, August 5, 2020

Regular meeting, mostly in person with one member by telephone and the public by phone and CCTV, devoted to the administration's fall reopening plan (the Cablecast listing titles it "Fall 2020 Back to School Plan"). The superintendent and assistant superintendent present a phased hybrid model (two days in school, three remote, cohorts of about twelve), health screening, masks all day with supervised breaks, and bus limits; district counsel explains liability. A motion for a full-open option plus a remote option, without a hybrid, fails; the board then votes 5-2 to support the administration in developing a hybrid schedule with metrics for changing phase. Two parents speak by phone and three letters are summarized. Built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on August 5, 2020, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule settles the point.

HIGH With a member participating by telephone, five of the board's seven votes were taken by voice, and the chair said so on the record

RSA 91-A:2, III, in the text in force from January 1, 2019 to October 2, 2023, governs meetings at which a member participates other than in person, and ¶III(e) provides: "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote." (statute text). Emergency Order #12 waived only the physical-quorum requirement of III(b) and the audibility-at-a-location requirement of III(c) (order text); it did not touch III(e). Towle participated by telephone for the whole meeting (0:02:36, 3:37:40).

The agenda (0:03:29), the June 3 minutes (0:04:03), the face-shield gift (3:40:17), the school calendar (3:42:32) and adjournment (3:42:41) were taken by voice. When Towle asked how to vote on the minutes, the chair answered "No, not roll call. Because we're all here, Carolyn. Except for you." (0:04:12). Only the two reopening motions were taken by roll call. The statute admits no exception for a meeting where most members are in the room.

Mitigation: none of the voice votes was contested, and the outcomes are unlikely to have changed; the school calendar, though, is a substantive decision. At the fully remote meetings of May 6 and June 3 the assistant superintendent and counsel had told the board that every vote must be by roll call (May 6, 2020 page; June 3, 2020 page).

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(a), (e) (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1; next amended 2023, 188:1); Emergency Order #12 (Mar. 23, 2020): waives III(b) and part of III(c) only
