MEDIUM Paraprofessionals assigned to IEP services were being pulled to cover classes, which the special education administrator called noncompliance

Asked whether special education paraprofessionals were working with their students under their IEPs, the special education administrator said "To the maximum extent. Yes, absolutely." and then: "when parents get pulled to do subbing, then you're actually pulling a student from. So you're essentially going into noncompliance. So that happens a lot" (0:59:31; parents is the transcript's rendering of paras). He added that the district then cannot bill Medicaid for that time (1:05:16). Zullo asked "Which is illegal, by the way, isn't it?" and the question was not answered directly; the superintendent turned to staff absence data (1:05:59). Federal regulations require that special education and related services be made available in accordance with the child's IEP. A paraprofessional written into a child's IEP and reassigned to substitute is, on the administrator's own description, a service not delivered. Mitigation on the record: the new substitute plan was adopted partly to stop this (0:59:31), and Medicaid billing has improved (1:00:16). Members estimated unclaimed Medicaid at about 10% of roughly $700,000 (1:02:00 to 1:02:20); no rule turns on that figure.

Sources: 34 CFR 300.323(c)(2) (2017 annual edition; services made available in accordance with the IEP)

OBSERVATION Line-item transfers are approved by administrators, while the statute names the governing body

Asked whether the board had transferred appropriations during the year, O'Neill said "And it's not really the board doing it. It's really the administration moving." (1:14:34); transfers are approved by "either either either Middleton on myself" and recorded on a form (1:14:57), and "as long as we don't overspend the appropriation, we really don't have to come to the board every time" (1:15:28). RSA 32:10, as amended effective August 15, 2017, provides that the governing body may transfer an unexpended balance between appropriations, that the total spent may not exceed the total appropriated, that records must let any citizen trace the transfers, and that a purpose includes individual line items. POSITIVE Public hearing set within the statutory window, with the deadline stated correctly and access arrangements offered

The superintendent told the board "We must have a public hearing no later than January 16th." (2:37:36); the board set it for January 3 at the Stevens High School auditorium so it could be televised (2:39:44). RSA 40:13, II-a, in the text in force in 2017, requires official-ballot districts to hold budget hearings on or before the third Tuesday in January, which in 2018 was January 16. LeClair stated that "the budget you pass tonight is the budget.
