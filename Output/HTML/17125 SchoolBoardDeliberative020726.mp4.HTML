MEDIUM Article 8, the petitioned budget cap, departs from the ballot wording RSA 32:5-f, IV prescribes, in two places, and went to the ballot that way

RSA 32:5-f, IV sets the wording of the adoption question. Its inflation-index form reads: "higher than _____ dollars per pupil cost times the average daily membership in residence of the school district as of October 1 of the year immediately preceding the proposed budget year plus an annual increase for inflation using (the index) published by (the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics or American City and County) as of October 1." (statute text). The petition as printed on the posted warrant copy fills the dollar blank with "the 2025-2026 fiscal year per pupil cost" and ends "as of January 1" (warrant copy). Counsel told the meeting about the first departure: "this is the part where this petition is different than the law, higher than a blank dollars" (2:44:55) and "if you adopt this, I can't provide certainty" (2:45:28). Sprague read the January 1 date aloud (2:29:32); counsel did not address it. The one amendment offered, substituting $50,000 (3:04:55), failed 80 to 121 (draft minutes), so the article goes to the March 10 ballot in its petitioned form. RSA 40:13, IV(a) bars amending "Warrant articles whose wording is prescribed by law"; counsel's view that the blanks remained open to amendment (3:03:30, 3:03:50) is his. Separately, the warrant copy prints "The School Board Recommends this Article (0) Yes (6) No (1) Abstain", which counsel called "misleading" and said the board would correct before the ballot (3:53:31). Adoption needs a 3/5 majority (RSA 32:5-f, I), which the warrant states. RSA 32:5-f, III also requires a public hearing on the question 15 to 30 days before the vote; for a March 10 vote that is February 8 to 23, after this session (see the February 18, 2026 page). The petitioners, not the board, wrote the wording; the defect is in the article's text, and its legal effect if adopted is the open question counsel raised.
