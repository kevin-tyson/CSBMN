MEDIUM The nonpublic motion, as transcribed, names paragraph II but no lettered exemption

RSA 91-A:3, I(b) required that "Any motion to enter nonpublic session shall state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II" and that the decision be by roll call. The motion as transcribed reads "RSA 91-A colon three sub paragraph two." (1:10:52), which identifies paragraph II but none of its lettered grounds. The project's attribution report reads the motion as II(a), but that letter is not in the transcript, and with no minutes there is no document to settle it. The automatic transcript may have dropped a spoken letter, which is why this is graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH. The minutes of the nonpublic session were due for disclosure within 72 hours unless sealed by a two-thirds recorded vote under paragraph III; none survive.

OBSERVATION The SAU 6 board says it has never adopted policies of its own

Sara Lowe, in her fourth year on the board, asked whether the board was "Way out of compliance" for never having looked at SAU-level policies (0:46:38). Zullo answered that it was not; the superintendent proposed taking up "an essay policy book" after the search; the chair agreed "we haven't looked at policies" and linked the need to a possible enlargement of the SAU (0:48:37, 0:48:55). This page verified no statute requiring an SAU board to adopt a policy manual, so the point is recorded as an observation. It bears on the Lempster discussion earlier in the meeting: a third district would join a unit whose own board had no written policies.

POSITIVE The superintendent search is published on the SAU website, with a timeline, survey results and planned monthly updates
