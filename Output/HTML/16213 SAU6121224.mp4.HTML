SAU 6 Board, Budget Public Hearing, December 12, 2024

Combined budget public hearing and meeting of the SAU 6 joint board (Claremont and Unity), rescheduled after the December 3 meeting failed for want of a quorum. After a brief nonpublic session the board voted, with no agenda item for it, a 3% raise and a two-year contract renewal for the superintendent and a salary parity adjustment for the assistant superintendent. At the hearing, which no member of the public attended, members rejected the administration's proposal to eliminate the curriculum director's SAU-funded share, and the board then adopted an FY2026 SAU budget of $2,755,723, a figure produced orally and found in no packet document, with one member opposed, plus up to $160,000 of fund balance to cover the restored position. MEDIUM No minutes of the meeting that adopted the SAU budget and the superintendent's contract terms have been published; they were approved four months later

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting", which made December 19, 2024 the deadline. No minutes of this meeting were found in the SAU 6 packet folders or the Claremont Meeting Minutes share. The next SAU 6 packet folder that exists, 6. SAU6 6.12.25, holds the April 10, 2025 draft minutes, which record: "Heather Whitney made a motion to approve the minutes from 12.12.24 as presented, Michael Petrin seconded the motion". So minutes were written and approved 119 days after the meeting, but the text was not published with them. Because of that gap the public record of this meeting is the recording alone: it does not show who seconded the budget motion (the chair's own row carries "Second discussion", 1:04:00), who cast the one recorded No (1:04:06), or who attended. RSA 91-A:2, II also requires the minutes to record "the names of the members who made or seconded each motion". Graded MEDIUM under the project's severity rule, not HIGH, because the meeting was noticed and packeted and the minutes demonstrably exist; the defect is that they were never made available.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2024 codification; minutes within five business days; movers and seconders); 4.10.25 DRAFT SAU6 Board Meeting Minutes (approval of the 12.12.24 minutes)

The posted agenda scripts a motion under "RSA91-A:3.II (a)" and quotes II(a), the compensation and discipline exemption. As spoken, the chair moved "under RSA 91. Dash a colon three a." (0:02:48), which names section 3 but not paragraph II; RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires the motion to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II", and the roll-call vote was taken (0:03:15 to 0:03:24). The recording then jumps: the chair announces the session at 0:03:34 and the next motion, a raise for the superintendent, comes at 0:03:57, 23 recording-seconds later. No nonpublic minutes and no record of a seal vote were found, and the meeting's own minutes are unpublished (flag 1). The recording cannot show what happened during the gap; the agenda's script and the 23-second splice are consistent with a return vote that was not recorded. Graded MEDIUM: the exemption claimed on the agenda fits the compensation decisions that followed, and the gap is in documentation, not shown misuse.

OBSERVATION The superintendent's raise and two-year contract renewal, and the assistant superintendent's salary adjustment, were voted with no agenda item, no dollar amounts and no count

The agenda's only compensation item is the nonpublic session. Immediately after it the chair moved "the 3% salary increase for the superintendent" (0:03:57); Crawford added the term and the chair restated it as "to renew your contract for two years" (0:04:26). The assistant superintendent motion began as "a $7,000 salary bonus" and was withdrawn and restated as a readjustment "to achieve parity with other districts" with no figure (0:04:47, 0:05:07); the $7,000 reappears only inside the later budget motion (1:02:03). Neither salary, nor the base the 3% applies to, nor the contract's other terms were stated in public, both votes were voice votes with no count, and no executed contract was found in any SAU 6 folder. The SAU board does hold this power: RSA 194-C:5, III says the board "shall fix the salaries of all school administrative unit personnel". Voting in public session after the nonpublic discussion is the right sequence. The concern is what a member of the public could learn from the notice and the record, which is very little; no statute located for this date requires an agenda item before a regular meeting acts.

Sources: RSA 194-C:5, III (SAU board fixes salaries); SAU6 Budget Public Hearing agenda 12.12.24

OBSERVATION The adopted budget total appears in no packet document, and no total or percentage increase was stated at the hearing

The packet folder was created at 3:59 p.m. Eastern on the day of the meeting, with the agenda at 4:01 and version 3 at 4:03, some two and a half hours before the hearing. Version 3 totals $2,597,521.45 gross against the FY2025 budget of $2,489,151.00, an increase of $108,370.45 (4.35%), but its printed headline is "Total Budget Increase ($16,629.55) Total Budget % Increase -0.67%", a figure net of $125,000 of grant revenue compared with an FY2025 total carrying no revenue offset. The chair repeated the sheet's message ("the budget increase is actually a decrease", 0:39:04) and then asked for the percentage, which the business administrator could not give (0:39:34, 0:39:43). The amount adopted, $2,755,723, was produced orally (1:03:14); it is $266,572 (10.71%) above the FY2025 budget and is in none of the packet's documents. No member of the public attended (1:01:42). No statute located requires an SAU budget hearing or prescribes its form (RSA 32:5's hearing rules govern districts, and RSA 194-C:9 governs SAU apportionment), so this is an observation; the hearing was the board's own commitment on its agenda, and the record shows the public could not have known what the board was about to adopt.

Sources: SAU6 FY26 Proposed Budget V3 (Drive createdTime 2024-12-12, 21:03 UTC); SAU FY26 Proposed Budget V2; RSA 194-C:9, I (SAU budget apportionment)

No rule is cited: no statute was located for this date governing an SAU's use of its own surplus, and the motion's own condition, Title funding first, is prudent.

Sources: the recording at the times shown; 4.10.25 DRAFT SAU6 Board Meeting Minutes (fiscal 2021 audit presented)

OBSERVATION The board adopted an SAU budget, apportioned to both districts, with one of Unity's five members present, as Unity's withdrawal advanced

Only Rocco Ruggeri answered the roll for Unity (0:00:41). The board was told Unity's withdrawal plan had been reviewed and approved by the state board and awaited Unity's March vote (1:07:16, 1:07:31). RSA 194-C:9, I apportions SAU costs half on average membership and half on equalized valuation, and RSA 194-C:8 provides weighted voting by pupils, used only "upon the demand of a majority of the members of any board present and voting"; no demand was made. Seven of twelve members made a quorum, so the vote was valid. The observation is that the budget Unity will pay a share of was adopted with Unity almost absent, and the single recorded No cannot be attributed to anyone (flag 1).

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I (apportionment); RSA 194-C:8 (weighted votes on demand); RSA 194-C:2, IV (withdrawal from an SAU)
