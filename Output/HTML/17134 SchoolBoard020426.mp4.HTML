MEDIUM A member says the business-administrator question came up in a nonpublic session entered only for tuition contracts

The board entered nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(k), "Consideration by a school board of entering into a student or pupil tuition contract" (agenda text, quoting the statute), from 7:03 to 7:17 p.m. (minutes). RSA 91-A:3, I(c) provides: "All discussions held and decisions made during nonpublic session shall be confined to the matters set out in the motion" (statute text). Later in the meeting Madden said: "as we heard, the nonpublic Matt brought up, that we should be making a decision on which way we're going with the business administrator" (1:08:18). The direction of the business office is not a tuition contract. Graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH because the only evidence is one member's sentence, the remark may refer to a passing mention, and no nonpublic minutes were found to check it against (flag 2). The approved public minutes record the business-office item without mentioning the nonpublic session.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), I(c), II(k) and III (motion states the exemption; discussion confined to the matters in the motion; tuition contracts; nonpublic minutes disclosed within 72 hours unless sealed); 2.4.26 Approved CSB Meeting Minutes

MEDIUM No nonpublic minutes were found and no sealing vote is recorded; the tuition rates were then set with no public discussion

RSA 91-A:3, III requires that "Minutes and decisions reached in nonpublic session shall be publicly disclosed within 72 hours of the meeting" unless sealed by a recorded two-thirds vote in public session with a statutory finding (statute text). The public minutes record entry and return by roll call but no motion to seal. The district's Unsealed Minutes share holds a 2026 Non-Public folder; its contents could not be listed from here (a parentId query returned nothing, which proves nothing), and title and full-text searches for nonpublic minutes of this date found none. II(k) also has its own disclosure rule: a negotiated tuition contract "shall be made public prior to its consideration for approval by a school district, together with minutes of all meetings held in nonpublic session" (agenda text). On return the board set FY2027 tuition rates of $15,000, $15,500 and $17,000 with no discussion (0:34:12, 0:34:35); the record does not say whether the rates relate to a contract under negotiation. Graded MEDIUM under the project's severity rule: the session was noticed on the agenda with the exemption printed in full and the public minutes exist, so only the nonpublic record is missing. "Not found" is the finding; this page does not say the minutes were never written.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), I(c), II(k) and III (motion states the exemption; discussion confined to the matters in the motion; tuition contracts; nonpublic minutes disclosed within 72 hours unless sealed); 2.4.26 agenda, item III.1; 2.4.26 Approved CSB Meeting Minutes

OBSERVATION Policy JLCK was adopted from an NHSBA sample whose rule table mislabels the minimum-standards provisions it implements

The second-read packet document is the NHSBA sample "Policy JLCK: Physical and Emotional Well-Being of Students", still carrying the sample's adoption notes, which say they "should be removed prior to adoption" (document text). Its revision note correctly ties the policy to "the requirement in Ed 306.04(b)(15) that schools have a policy addressing 'physical and emotional health needs of students.'" (document text), but its reference table labels Ed 306.04(b)(15) "Behavior Management and Intervention for Students" and (b)(23) "Meeting the Special Physical Health Needs of Students" (document text). In the rules in force since December 13, 2024, (b)(15) concerns physical and emotional health needs and (b)(23) daily physical activity. The agenda and the chair also called the policy by its old title, "JLCK special physical health needs of students" (1:05:04), so the motion did not name the text being adopted. The mislabeling does not change the policy's substance.

Sources: N.H. Admin. Rules Ed 306.04(b)(15) and (b)(23) (minimum standards: policies on physical and emotional health needs; daily physical activity), readoption #14150, eff. 12-13-24; Copy of 12.17.25 Update Policy JLCK (packet)
