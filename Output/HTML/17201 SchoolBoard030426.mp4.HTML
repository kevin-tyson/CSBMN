Claremont School Board — March 4, 2026

The last meeting of the 2025–26 board, six days before the March 10 ballot on an operating budget of $42,957,713 and a petitioned budget cap. Neither article is on the agenda, in the minutes, or anywhere on the recording. What the board did instead was spend an hour on mid-year reading and mathematics data, adopt two policies, and ask why the salaries it had just published to voters do not match the budget.
