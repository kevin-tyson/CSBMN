MEDIUM A three-hour nonpublic session ended with its minutes sealed for ten years, without the determination RSA 91-A:3, III requires, in a vote the recording does not carry

The board entered nonpublic under RSA 91-A:3, II(a) at 0:12:42 (6:43 p.m. per the minutes). The motion states the exemption ("We go into nonpublic for RSA 91-832A", the transcript's rendering of 91-A:3, II(a)) and the roll call follows (0:13:21 to 0:13:26). The recording ends there. The approved minutes record the return to public session at 9:54 p.m., then "Heather Whitney made a motion to seal the minutes for 10 years, Frank Sprague seconded the motion; roll call vote taken by Noelle Kronberg, all present voting in favor", and adjournment at 9:56 p.m. Five members were present, so the two-thirds vote was met.

RSA 91-A:3, III allows withholding only where, "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism". The minutes record no such determination, and the board's by-laws (Appendix D) say "The decision to seal minutes of non-public sessions must affirmatively state one of the statutory bases listed above." Because the vote happened after the CCTV recording stopped, the minutes are the only record of it. The minutes also report no decision reached in the session; RSA 91-A:3, III requires decisions to be disclosed within 72 hours unless sealed. Same defect as the February 5 seal.
