OBSERVATION The stated purpose of the nonpublic session reached past the exemption the motion cited

The motion cited RSA 91-A:3, II(c) (1:20:01), and a roll-call vote followed (1:20:18), which is what paragraph I(b) requires. II(c) covers "Matters which, if discussed in public, would likely affect adversely the reputation of any person, other than a member of the public body itself". The superintendent's stated reason fits part of that: building principals would describe situations in their schools, and "Because of the size of the community" students could be recognized (1:15:40). He also said the session would cover "the direction that we anticipate heading in the near future through the budget process" (1:16:06), and earlier described the topic as one to take up "publicly as well as privately and not public" in preparation for the budget (0:09:55). Budget direction is not a II(c) matter, and paragraph I(c) confines nonpublic discussion "to the matters set out in the motion".

What was said behind closed doors is not on the recording and no nonpublic minutes survive, so the page cannot say whether the discussion stayed within II(c). The chair's report on returning (1:20:59) describes no decision. This is an observation about a stated purpose, not a finding about the session.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), I(c), II(c) (2016 codification, in force for 2017)

OBSERVATION The special education director told the board some students were being "contained" rather than educated

Describing 5 to 7 students the district considers "extremely at risk", the director said "what we're talking about here is containment versus education. And so for a lot of these students, we're we're containing them, but we're not we're not educating them" (1:09:42), and that out-of-district placements across the state are full (1:08:44). Federal law requires that "A free appropriate public education is available to all children with disabilities" in the state, and that children with disabilities be educated with nondisabled children "To the maximum extent appropriate". The director's account is a candid description of capacity, not an admission tied to any particular child, and the board was told the district was planning a shared program with Newport (1:14:29). It is flagged because it is the district's own statement that its current arrangements fall short of educating some students, made in the same meeting in which out-of-district spending was presented as falling (2.1 million estimated for 2017 against 2.5 million the year before, 1:04:53).

Sources: 20 U.S.C. §1412(a)(1)(A) (free appropriate public education) and §1412(a)(5)(A) (least restrictive environment)
