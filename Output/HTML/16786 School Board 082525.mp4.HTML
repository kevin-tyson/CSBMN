Claremont School Board, August 25, 2025

Board meeting held three days before the first day of school and eleven days after the board announced the district's deficit on August 14. The board heard the acting business administrator and district counsel on a bank loan and on cuts, heard an hour and a half of public comment, then voted 4–1 with one abstention to authorize eliminating 20 positions and 4–2 to direct cuts to all non-mandatory extracurricular and co-curricular programs. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on August 25, 2025, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies.

MEDIUM The minutes give no actual start or end time, three days after the law began requiring both

RSA 91-A:2, II was amended by 2025, 112:1, effective August 22, 2025, so that minutes must include "the start time and end time of the meeting, and name of the person who produced the minutes" (statute text). This meeting was the board's first after that date. The draft and approved minutes name the person who produced them (Noelle Kronberg), which meets the third clause. They do not state when the meeting began or ended: the times they print, 6:30 PM beside the call to order and 8:30 PM beside adjournment, are the agenda's scheduled times carried into the minutes template. The recording runs from the call to order at 0:00:00 to the chair's adjournment at 2:15:04, about two hours and fifteen minutes, so an 8:30 p.m. end would require a call to order at about 6:15 p.m., a quarter hour before the noticed 6:30 p.m. The printed 8:30 is therefore unlikely to be the actual end time. Mitigation: the amendment was three days old, and the minutes were otherwise timely and complete (flag 8). This is graded MEDIUM as a documentation gap rather than HIGH because nothing in the record is concealed by it; the recording supplies the duration.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (minutes contents; start and end time and producer added by 2025, 112:1, eff. Aug. 22, 2025); approved minutes 8.25.25

MEDIUM The board authorized eliminating 20 positions "consistent with what we were presented tonight", an oral presentation whose total differs from the minutes, with no list in the packet

Hawkins's motion authorizes the interim business administrator "to eliminate the 20 positions consistent, consistent with what we were presented tonight." (2:01:29). What was presented was O'Hearn's spoken list (0:11:48, 0:12:27) and a spoken total "of $1,252,646" (0:13:11). No position list, count or cost sheet is in the packet folder, which holds only the agenda. The minutes give four category figures and a total of 1,354,686.77; the four figures they print add to $1,354,685.90, so the printed total is off by 87 cents, and it exceeds the spoken total by about $102,000. Neither the recording nor the minutes enumerates twenty positions; one commenter speaks of 19 (0:40:37). The board was also asked to cut the Medicaid clerk without a revenue figure: Peabody put the position's yield at about $500,000 a year (1:28:57), and Angell said "that's a number of we don't have" (2:06:08). RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to record the "final decisions" (statute text). A decision defined by what was "presented tonight" can be reconstructed only from the recording, and the two written and spoken totals disagree. Mitigation: the minutes list the position categories, and the category figures broadly match the spoken ones.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (minutes must include final decisions); approved minutes 8.25.25, item 3 and motion; packet folder 4. CSB 8.25.25 (one document)

OBSERVATION Both action votes were added at the meeting, and the agenda change itself was adopted without the vote the agenda calls for

The posted agenda lists every item as discussion, with no vote, and was last modified at 2:33 p.m. on the day of the meeting. At the meeting the chair added a reduction-in-force item and "a board vote" after public comment, and asked "Is there any objection to those changes" before proceeding (0:02:32). The agenda's own consent item reads "(vote required)" (agenda text); no vote was taken, and the minutes record only that the chair "requested" the changes. Madden's motion on extracurricular programs (2:13:25) appears in neither the agenda nor the chair's announced order. RSA 91-A:2, II requires notice of the meeting, not a final agenda, so this is not a Right-to-Know defect. It is noted because the two most consequential decisions of the meeting were not visible to anyone reading the posted agenda, and the agenda's own masthead warned that "the agenda may be modified before and/or on the day of the meeting" (agenda text). The board's by-laws were not checked for this page.

Sources: Amended CSB Agenda 8.25.25; RSA 91-A:2, II (notice of the meeting)

OBSERVATION The district's books could not be reconciled, and the size of the deficit was unknown when the cuts were voted

Angell told the room that about $5 million in prior-year bills were unpaid and that current-year money was paying them (0:33:22), and later that "There's no beginning balance in there. You can't reconcile it to the bank statement." (1:49:19). He promised the reconciliation to the governor's office within 30 days (1:49:44). The board therefore voted cuts sized to a cash problem whose amount no one at the table could state. Whether the district's FY2025 financial statements must disclose substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern is a question for those statements and their auditor: GASB Statement 56, ¶16–19 requires that evaluation when indicators such as negative financial trends exist. Nothing in this meeting's record is a financial statement, so no GASB departure is asserted. The documents that would settle the figures are the reconciled general ledger and the FY2023 to FY2025 audits.

Sources: GASB Statement 56, ¶16–19 (going-concern evaluation and disclosure)
