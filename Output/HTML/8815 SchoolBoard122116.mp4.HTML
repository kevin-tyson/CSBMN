HIGH A $5,000 grant was accepted with no prior public hearing on the record

The board accepted the Hope Foundation's $5,000 grant for Maple Avenue's Project Lead the Way Launch program by voice vote at 1:35:50. RSA 198:20-b, III(a), as it read in 2016, provided: "For unanticipated funds in the amount of $5,000 or more, the school board shall hold a prior public hearing on the action to be taken", with newspaper notice at least 7 days before. Nothing on the recording refers to a hearing: the grant was listed only as a discussion item, and the superintendent asked at the start of the meeting to add its acceptance to the agenda (0:02:03, 0:02:26). The grant sits exactly at the $5,000 threshold, and the statute's authority to accept such money at all depends on the district having adopted the enabling article in ¶I, which the recording does not mention. What would settle this: a hearing notice or hearing minutes, which do not survive in any district share (flag 2). The money was for students, and LeClair said it carried no district cost; the defect, if there is one, is procedural.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(a) (2016 codification; source note ends 2005, 188:1)

OBSERVATION Bargaining agreements were reached but their costs were kept out of the budget for later disclosure

The superintendent said the district had “reached agreements” with its bargaining units and would discuss them “more publicly in early January” (0:13:37, 0:14:07). O'Neill said the requested budget contains “no collective bargaining agreement cost” because every agreement was negotiated this year (0:54:19). OBSERVATION About $600,000 of the increase is paraprofessional positions the district said it could not legally cut
