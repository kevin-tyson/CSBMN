Claremont School Board Finance Committee — November 5, 2026

A morning meeting of the board's three-member Finance Subcommittee, with two members and the superintendent. MEDIUM No minutes, agenda or packet for this meeting are in any district share ten business days on

A subcommittee of a school board is a public body (RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d)), and RSA 91-A:2, II requires its minutes to be "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting" (statute); a draft is enough. The board's Policy BEDG § A applies the duty to "each of the school board's committees" and § C.3 repeats the five business days (policy). Policy DB says of this committee by name that its "Meeting minutes shall be posted" (policy). As of November 20, 2026 nothing for November 5 was found in the Meeting Minutes share's 2026 or 2027 folders, in the district's empty Finance Sub Committee folder, or in the November 18 board packet. The same was true of the committee's June 19 and August 12 meetings.

Graded MEDIUM under the project's rule for missing records, on the mitigated limb: the chair says the agenda was posted (0:00:48), the meeting was recorded and published by CCTV, and the committee's September 15 minutes plainly exist, since copies were being printed for approval (0:00:06). Minutes may exist at the SAU office. Three smaller gaps go with it: the recording has no call to order and seems to start about 25 minutes after the listed 10:00 a.m.; no one is identified as taking minutes; and the September 15 minutes are not in the public shares either.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d); RSA 91-A:2, II; Policy BEDG §§ A and C.3 (Meeting Minutes; adopted 12/2007, revised 6/2018 and 9/2025); Policy DB (Annual Budget; adopted 3/6/2019, revised 8/14/2025); Claremont Finance Sub Committee folder (empty)

MEDIUM The calendar agreed here puts the budget hearing on January 20, 2027, a day after the last day the statute allows

The chair read the hearing date twice (0:02:55, 0:03:06) and the superintendent kept it in the revised calendar (0:51:17), which went to the board as the FY28 Budget Development Schedule: "Jan 20 - Regular School Board Meeting - Public hearing on the budget" (schedule). For a district voting in March under the official-ballot law, RSA 40:13, II-a(c) requires budget hearings "on or before the third Tuesday in January" (statute). In 2027 that is January 19. Policy DB lists the same deadline: "Last day to hold at least one budget hearing" (policy). Ms. Whitney had just called that policy "highly structured" (0:03:46).

The date is still two months off and can be moved, which is why this is MEDIUM. A supplemental hearing may follow the first on seven days' notice, but the first must be held by the 19th, with notice posted by January 12.

Sources: RSA 40:13, II-a(c) (budget hearing by the third Tuesday in January); Policy DB, Budget Timeline Targets, January; FY28 Claremont Budget Development Schedule (1).pdf; Board page for November 18, flag 2
