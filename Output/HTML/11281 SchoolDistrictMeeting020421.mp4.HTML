Claremont School District Meeting, February 4, 2021

Second session of the 2021 Claremont School District annual meeting, held as a virtual meeting under House Bill 1129: the reading of voters’ written submissions (there were none), and the moving of Article 1 (election of officials) and Article 2 (the $37,785,406 operating budget) to the March 9 alternative ballot as posted. MEDIUM No minutes of this session were located

This session was the one at which the annual meeting acted on the warrant: both articles went to the March 9 ballot here. The clerk minuted the first session (2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting, filed in the November 3, 2022 special-meeting packet), but MAP.md records no minutes or other Drive material for this one. RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes open to public inspection “not more than 5 business days after the meeting” (statutory wording, 2019 codification).

Severity follows the project rule (html_briefing.md §5), mitigated limb: the session was noticed (the 2/1/21 minutes say the warrant and agendas were on the SAU 6 web page, and voters were mailed the schedule), the clerk demonstrably minuted the first session, and only the minutes are missing. Because the first session’s minutes surfaced in a 2022 packet, these may yet exist in a share or packet this page did not search; the finding is “not located”.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2019 codification); 2.1.21 Minutes from Virtual District Meeting; MAP.md section 140 (plain text, unpublished).

OBSERVATION Both articles went to the ballot on the moderator’s word, with no board motion, second or vote on the record

The second session is where, as the district’s attorney described the House Bill 1129 process on January 13, the moderator introduces each article, takes a motion and a second, and the board then debates it (show 11262, dialogue row 159), and the moderator then asked the board who would make the motions (show 11262, row 312). On this recording no motion, second or vote is taken on either article. After reading each article and the report that no submissions had arrived, the moderator closed discussion and said the article “will be moved to the alternative ballot as posted” (0:07:44, 0:10:40). Members read the article texts, and Petrin spoke on the budget (0:09:43); no one proposed an amendment.

Because no amendment was offered and no voter submitted anything, the outcome would likely have been the same with a vote. The point for the record is that the session that replaced the floor deliberative session produced no recorded action by the body that was to act for the voters. Whether the enacted law required a vote is not graded here: the chaptered text of 2020 N.H. Laws ch. 8 (HB 1129) was not read for this page, so this remains an observation. The first-session minutes also place Petrin’s budget remarks at February 1; they were made here (see the 2/1/21 page, flag 1).

Sources: Dialogue/11262 SchoolBoard011321.mp4.CSV (attorney’s description, rows 159 and 312); HB 1129 (2020), bill status page (chaptered text not read); Dialogue/11276 SchoolDistrictMeeting020121.mp4.CSV.

POSITIVE Voting instructions were complete and added walk-up and absentee options

The moderator closed with the full voting information on the record: hours, the three ward polling places, the identification procedure, that “Walk through voting will be allowed in all three voting places” for voters without a car, absentee voting for those “temporarily absent from the city” or unable to appear because of disability, and a supervisors-of-the-checklist session on February 27 for voters who could not register during the clerk’s office hours (0:11:40). This went beyond the drive-up minimum the attorney had described in January and carried out what Magoon had told the board on January 27 about voters without a vehicle.

Sources: Dialogue/11281 SchoolDistrictMeeting020421.mp4.CSV (rows 44 to 46); Emergency Order #12 (March 23, 2020).
