SAU 6 Board, Special Meeting, October 6, 2025

The joint Claremont and Unity board for SAU 6 met at 6:00 p.m., after the Claremont board's own special meeting. It went into nonpublic session on a roll-call vote, voted on return to seal those minutes for five years "on advice of our attorney", and then heard a long presentation by the New Hampshire School Boards Association's staff attorney on the roles and responsibilities of school boards. The interim superintendent announced three central-office resignations. Built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript and the posted agenda; no minutes have been found. MEDIUM The nonpublic minutes were sealed for five years on counsel's advice, with no statutory determination stated

Whitney: "I would like to make a motion on advice of our attorney, Sarah Friedman, to seal the minutes for five years." (0:06:45). Each member then answered Yes on a roll call, Ruggeri adding "The advice is to seal Minister five years per the attorney." (0:07:12). RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes and decisions to be "publicly disclosed within 72 hours" unless, "by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined" that divulgence "likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person" other than a member, or (under the paragraph's other limbs) would render the proposed action ineffective or pertains to terrorism. The recorded vote was unanimous and taken in public, so the two-thirds element appears met; what the motion lacks is the determination itself. Counsel's advice is a reason, not one of the statutory grounds. One of the exemptions invoked, II(c), is the reputation exemption, so a qualifying finding may well have been available; it was not made on the record, and no public minutes exist to show it was made elsewhere (flag 2).

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (disclosure within 72 hours unless sealed by recorded two-thirds vote with a determination; source note ends 2023, 189:1)

OBSERVATION Two lawyers gave the same board members different answers on whether the Claremont vacancy must be filled

Five days earlier, district counsel told the Claremont board that "the remaining members of the school board shall appoint someone to fill the vacancy" and that failure sends the appointment to the city council (Claremont School Board, October 1, 2025, recording show 16872). Here the NHSBA staff attorney answered Hawkins: "there's no absolute requirement that you fill it" (1:51:46), and that boards often leave seats open late in the year. RSA 671:33, II(a) says the school board "shall fill vacancies" and supplies the selectmen as the fallback when members cannot agree; it states no deadline and no consequence beyond that fallback. Both statements can be read as partial descriptions of the same text; neither speaker cited the selectmen fallback here.

Sources: RSA 671:33, II(a) (school board shall fill vacancies); Claremont School Board, October 1, 2025, flag 6
