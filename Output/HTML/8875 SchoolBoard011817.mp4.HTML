Claremont School Board, January 18, 2017

Regular board meeting, chaired by the vice chair with four of seven members present, at which the administration restored the high-school PE and alternative-education positions, the board worked through its line-item budget questions with staff, residents and students spoke for a PE teacher and the technical center's business program, and the board accepted Brent Ferland's resignation so the seat goes to the March ballot. The resignation vote was a voice vote with no tally announced, so the minutes are the only place the vote would be recorded. OBSERVATION Four different measures of the budget gap were used in one meeting, and the default budget figure was still not stated

Viewers heard the budget described as “about $1 million above this year's budget” (1:21:14), as having “an increase of over $900,000” (1:37:41), as 92,553 over the current year (2:35:05, 2:36:15), and as needing “roughly 970,000” in cuts for no tax increase (1:23:04). These are probably different things: O'Neill's figure is the operating budget against the current year, while the larger figures appear to be tax effects that include warrant articles and lost revenue. No one on tape reconciles them. The superintendent also warned again that the bargaining agreements fail if the default budget comes in below the proposed budget (1:21:43 to 1:22:01), but no default figure was given, a week after the hearing at which RSA 40:13, XI(a) required it to be disclosed (January 11 page, flag 1). No rule requires a single figure at a work session; the point is that a resident could not tell from this meeting how far apart the budget and the board's goal were.

Sources: RSA 40:13, XI(a) (2017 codification); Dialogue/8875 SchoolBoard011817.mp4.CSV

OBSERVATION The budget is being shaped in members-only working sessions whose records are not in the corpus

Much of tonight's discussion works from the spreadsheet five members built on Saturday, January 7 (1:10:39, 1:11:03), a session with no recording or minutes in any district share (January 11 page, flag 2). The presiding member proposed another session “just like we did on a Saturday. Just us.” (2:44:25), and members said it would need public notice (2:45:05, 2:45:30, 3:02:42). A gathering of a quorum to discuss the budget is a meeting under RSA 91-A:2, I, with notice and minutes required by ¶II. Arranging notice is the right step; the concern for a reviewer is that the decisive work on the budget is happening in sessions whose only public trace, so far, is members' summaries at televised meetings.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I and II (2016 codification)

POSITIVE The administration revised its recommendations after the hearing and stated the cost

A week after residents testified against cutting a PE teacher and an Owls teacher, the superintendent's revised memo took both reductions off the table (0:01:50) and stated the cost of restoring them: about $117,000 and 17 cents on the tax rate (1:48:02). That is the hearing working as intended. RSA 32:5, I provides that the body "after the conclusion of public testimony shall finalize the budget", and changing recommendations in response to testimony is within that process.

Sources: RSA 32:5, I (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 190:7)

POSITIVE The resignation was timed so the voters, not the board, fill the seat

Ferland asked to stay through the budget process. On counsel's advice, the board accepted his written resignation effective March 13, 2017, the day before the election (2:59:55, 3:01:37), so the remaining year goes on the March 14 ballot instead of to a board appointment. Under RSA 671:33, II(a) as it then read, "The school board shall fill vacancies occurring on the school board", and appointees serve "until the next district election"; Irish said “The voters should absolutely fill this position.” (2:56:50). The filing period announced, January 25 to February 3 (2:55:34), matches RSA 669:19, which runs filing from the seventh Wednesday before the election to the Friday of the following week, applied to school districts by RSA 671:19. The board acted early enough for the clerk to open the seat with the others.

Sources: RSA 671:33, II(a) (2016 codification; source note ends 2013, 114:1, in force until May 30, 2017); RSA 669:19 (2017 codification; source note ends 2011, 40:1)
