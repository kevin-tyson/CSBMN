Claremont School Board, April 2, 2025

A short public meeting around a 90-minute nonpublic session. The business administrator reported a current-year deficit of about $1.4 million on tape ($1.5 million in the minutes), driven by special-education tuition and contracted services, and imposed a spending freeze; the director of student services said a request for about $1 million more for out-of-district placements was made in July 2024 and never acted on. MEDIUM A projected overrun of about $1.4 to $1.5 million, foreshadowed by a July 2024 request the board never took up

The business administrator projected a current-year (FY2025) deficit of about 1.4 million on tape (0:21:40); the approved minutes print "roughly at $1.5 million deficit, largely based on encumbrances (tuition and contracted services)". She put special-education tuition about $1 million over and contracted services about $700,000 over, partly offset by about $300,000 of unspent salaries (0:21:53, 0:22:19), and imposed a freeze on non-essential spending. The director of student services said that in July 2024 the previous director had asked for about $1 million more for unbudgeted out-of-district placements, that the follow-up subcommittee meetings were canceled, and "So we never dealt with that issue." (0:25:37, 0:26:25).

RSA 32:8 bars a school board from incurring a liability "for any purpose in excess of the amount appropriated by the legislative body for that purpose, or for any purpose for which no appropriation has been made, except as provided in RSA 32:9-11" (statute text; 1993, 332:1, unamended). RSA 32:10 allows transfers between appropriations, and RSA 32:11 (Emergency Expenditures and Overexpenditures) requires an application for authority, which "shall be made prior to the making of such expenditure" except for a "sudden or unexpected emergency" (statute text). This meeting's record shows no transfer vote, no 32:11 application, and no figure for how the overrun will be covered beyond the freeze and the hope that encumbrances fall. Whether 32:8 is engaged depends on how the appropriation's purposes are defined and on what is finally paid, which this record does not show. Graded MEDIUM as a control weakness: a shortfall reported to a subcommittee in July 2024 was, on the director's account, not addressed until a deficit was announced nine months later. The administrator's statement that the deficit was "not because of anything that was within our control" (0:22:44) and the district's new placement processes (0:33:42) are the administration's mitigation.

Sources: RSA 32:8 (limitation on expenditures); RSA 32:10 (transfers); RSA 32:11 (emergency expenditures and overexpenditures); approved minutes 4.2.25, item III.2

MEDIUM The gift exhibit publishes a cheque's bank details and a student's name, from a donor who asked to remain anonymous

Exhibit A in the public packet folder is the donor's letter and an image of the $10,000 cheque. The letter is addressed by full name to a member of the Class of 2025, says "I prefer to remain anonymous, thank you.", and the cheque image carries the bank's name and the encoded number line at its foot. This page reproduces none of those details. The foundation's name was also printed on the agenda and read aloud at 0:01:20. RSA 91-A:5, IV exempts "confidential, commercial, or financial information" from disclosure; it permits withholding and does not require it, so this is a judgment the district could have made, not a statutory breach. The project has recorded the same kind of exposure in the 4/17/24 and 9/4/24 packets. The acceptance itself was noticed on the agenda and recorded in the minutes; at $10,000 it was below the $20,000 threshold at which RSA 198:20-b, III(a) requires a prior public hearing. Graded MEDIUM as a recurring control weakness in packet preparation.

Sources: Exhibit A- Donation Letter (1).pdf (described, not reproduced); RSA 91-A:5, IV (exemption permits withholding; 2024 codification); RSA 198:20-b, III(a) (prior public hearing at $20,000 or more; 2023, 38:1)

OBSERVATION Nonpublic session: an emergency-functions exemption cited with no matching matter recorded, and the one decision found only in the nonpublic minutes

The motion, as the minutes record it, cited "RSA 91-A:3, II (a (1, 2), c, i)". Exemption II(i) covers emergency functions "directly intended to thwart a deliberate act that is intended to result in widespread or severe damage to property or widespread injury or loss of life" (agenda text quoting the statute). The nonpublic minutes describe only two matters: a grievance hearing, "unanimously denied by the Board", and a personnel matter with no action. Neither is an emergency-functions matter, so either (i) was not used or its subject went unrecorded. RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires the motion to state the exemption relied on, and III requires decisions reached in nonpublic session to be disclosed within 72 hours unless sealed; no seal was moved. The nonpublic minutes file was created on April 3, 2025, the next day, and now sits in the district's Unsealed Minutes share (its 2025 Non-Public folder was created in January 2026), so whether it was public within 72 hours cannot be determined from Drive. The approved public minutes do not mention the grievance decision. The 22-minute recess "for the purpose of a non-meeting with attorney Matt Upton" was moved and voted by roll call (minutes); consultation with legal counsel is outside the definition of a meeting under RSA 91-A:2, I(b).

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), II(i), III; RSA 91-A:2, I(b) (consultation with legal counsel); 4.3.25 CSB nonpublic minutes Unsealed
