Claremont School Board, December 4, 2024

Regular board meeting. Two Stevens High School psychology students presented a survey of 194 students and staff linking stress to the new seven-period schedule; the assistant superintendent reviewed PSAT and SAT results; and the business administrator, in place of the noticed budget tutorial, reported that the FY2021 audit was nearly finished and that audits through FY2024 were targeted for June 2025, and set out a compressed Finance Committee calendar. A motion to endorse a school-funding letter to a state senator was withdrawn on a point of order and carried to the next agenda. No vote was taken all evening. MEDIUM The Finance Committee planned a December 12 "pre meeting" on the budget, described as preparation before going "out in public"

Henry asked to meet "just finance Committee" on Thursday, December 12 (1:07:22), and the chair, herself a committee member, described it as "just to do a pre meeting for, the 13th. So we can go over everything within the budget, make sure that we understand what we're doing before we, come out in public" and "Before we go out and publicly discuss what's within the budget." (1:09:11, 1:10:10). The committee is Sprague (chair), Crawford and Whitney. A subcommittee of a school board is a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which ends "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", and a quorum of it convened to discuss the budget is a meeting under RSA 91-A:2, I that needs 24 hours' posted notice and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. Two further gaps are in this record: the agenda's own Future Dates list only the December 18 Finance session, and the December 13 televised session was arranged on the floor, with Hawkins asking whether it would be "publicized" (1:09:39). This page does not establish whether the December 12 session took place, with how many members, or with notice; no notice or minutes of a December 12 Finance session were found in the December 4 or December 18 packet folders, and a Drive search returned nothing, which is not proof. Graded MEDIUM because the concern rests on a plan stated on tape, not on a session shown to have occurred unnoticed.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (subcommittees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, I and II (2024 codification; definition of a meeting, notice, minutes); _CSB Agenda 12.4.24 (Future Dates)

MEDIUM The fiscal 2021 audit was still unfinished in December 2024 and fiscal 2022 had just begun; if federal spending reached the threshold, the single audits were years overdue

Henry reported the fiscal 2021 audit almost finished, fiscal 2022 begun, and fiscal 2023 and 2024 targeted for June 2025 (0:59:43, 1:00:25); the chair restated the June 2025 target (1:01:03). The district's fiscal year ends June 30. Under the Uniform Guidance a non-federal entity that spends the threshold amount or more in federal awards in a year must have a single audit ($750,000 for these years), and the report is due the earlier of 30 days after the auditor's report or nine months after the fiscal year end, so the fiscal 2021 report was due by March 31, 2022. Whether Claremont crossed the threshold in each year is not stated in this record, which is why this is not graded HIGH. No New Hampshire statute located for this date sets a deadline for a district's own audit: RSA 21-J:19 lets a district hire an independent auditor and is permissive. Mitigation on tape: the staff are reconstructing missing records, the auditor will report to the board in public, and the business administrator expects the audits to be current by mid-2025.

Sources: 2 CFR 200.501(a), 2023 annual edition ($750,000 threshold); 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1), 2023 annual edition (nine months); RSA 21-J:19 (district may hire auditors; permissive); approved minutes 12.4.24, item III.3

OBSERVATION The SAU 6 board failed to make a quorum on December 3, a month before its budget deadline
