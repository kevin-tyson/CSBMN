Claremont School Board — June 7, 2023

A regular meeting of the seven-member board, generated from the meeting recording, the posted eleven-document packet (the richest of any meeting in this corpus so far) and the official minutes. Three things on this page reward a resident's attention. The board accepted a $5,500 donation from the Dick's Sporting Goods Foundation, and on June 7, 2023 that figure was over the threshold that triggered a mandatory prior public hearing with seven days' published newspaper notice: the threshold was $5,000, not the $20,000 the statute carries today. The board held the hearing. Nothing in the record shows the newspaper notice. Second, the packet's 2023–24 school year calendar prints "177 Student Days" (three below the 180 in RSA 189:1) and the board approved changes to it on the consent agenda without a motion, a second or a vote. Third, the packet's "Retreat Minutes May 16, 2020" are not from 2020: the people in the room, and the plans they discuss, place that retreat in May 2023. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.
