Claremont School Board — November 18, 2026

A regular meeting at the tech center with all seven members present. The business administrator handed the board a blank MS-25 to sign while the FY26 DOE-25 stays unfinished; the treasurer reported vouchers signed after the fact; a member described a stipend paid to a non-employee and the superintendent explained how one person had been able to create and approve grant payments. The superintendent called the district "still in crisis", moved the curriculum director into the middle school, and reported 22 tuition students with no current agreements. The board voted to hold the deliberative session on a weeknight, adopted three policies the night they were brought to the table, set a budget schedule whose public hearing falls a day after the statutory last day, and moved its school configuration item behind the nonpublic session. MEDIUM Three policies were adopted the night they were brought to the table, on an agenda that listed no policy action

The posted agenda's policy item reads "First reading: None" and "Second reading & adoption: None" (agenda). At 1:59:16 the superintendent says "We brought a few to the table. Is that okay?" The board then approved a first reading of DAF and adopted revisions to DGA (Authorized Signatures), DFA (Investment) and DFA-R in single votes (2:06:42, 2:08:18, 2:09:21). On DGA the first motion was for a first reading; Ms. Whitney said "We can approve if it's just an update." (2:05:13) and the motion was changed to adoption.

The board's own rules call for more. Policy BGAA § E.2 makes every new or revised policy subject to a first reading at a regular meeting; § E.5 puts the second reading and action "At the next Board meeting (or a later meeting if so agreed by the Board)" (policy). § G.1 allows action at any meeting "provided that public notice of the proposed action was given at least one week prior to the Board meeting" (policy), and § G.2 allows the second meeting to be waived "On matters of unusual or unexpected urgency", with the minutes to record the circumstances (policy). Policy BEDB says "The Board, however, may not revise Board policies, or adopt new ones, unless such action has been scheduled, or unless there is an emergency." (policy). No urgency was stated and no vote to waive the process was taken on the recording.
