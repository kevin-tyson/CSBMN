HIGH A $451,832 federal grant was given its required public hearing without the required notice

RSA 198:20-b, III(a), as in force in 2021: "For unanticipated funds in the amount of $5,000 or more, the school board shall hold a prior public hearing on the action to be taken. Notice of the time, place, and subject of such hearing shall be published in a newspaper of general circulation in the relevant municipality at least 7 days before the meeting is held." The hearing had been noticed for the donation ("the original item on the public hearing", 0:04:31). The grant was added on the night at the superintendent's request (0:02:39, 0:03:21), heard with no public comment and closed (0:05:21), and then accepted (0:57:57). A subject added at the hearing cannot have been in a notice published seven days before. Mitigation on the record: the superintendent reported that "legal counsel has met up and is okayed it" (0:03:51), offered to re-post for the next meeting (0:03:51), and described the 15 percent match as already budgeted (0:02:09). Counsel's reasoning is not on the record, and no notice survives.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I, III(a) and IV(a) (2019 codification; source note ends 2005, 188:1, eff. Aug. 29, 2005; in force until 2023, 38:1)

OBSERVATION The grant was in use before the board accepted it

The superintendent said the grant would fund "the same boards that Jeff and his crew have already started" (0:57:40), and on March 17 he had told the board SAU 6 had received the grant and the district was already installing boards under its technology plan (see the March 17, 2021 page). RSA 198:20-b, I lets a board "apply for, accept and expend" such money, and ¶III(a) makes the hearing "prior" to "the action to be taken". Whether grant money was spent before tonight's acceptance is not on the record; the award and purchase orders would settle it.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(a) (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION A "$10,000 threshold" was stated; the statute's was $5,000

The donation was described as "the $11,000 over the $10,000 threshold" (0:02:39). RSA 198:20-b, III(a) set the hearing threshold at $5,000, and ¶III(b) allows a board to set a lower notice amount only: "A school board may establish the amount of unanticipated funds required for notice under this subparagraph, provided such amount is less than $5,000." No effect tonight, since $11,160.92 exceeds both and was heard; it would matter for a gift between the two figures.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, III(a) and III(b) (2019 codification)

OBSERVATION About $3.7 million in CARES/ESSER money is described as received, with no acceptance step on this record

A first allocation of about $760,000 (0:27:52) and "approximately $2.9 million" more (0:23:41) are described as received, on a reimbursement basis (0:27:48), money "not not in the budget" (0:19:34). Federal money passed through the state during the fiscal year is the "unanticipated money from a state, federal or other governmental unit" that RSA 198:20-b, I addresses, with a prior hearing at $5,000 or more. No hearing or acceptance for either allocation appears here; they may have been accepted at earlier meetings, and no minutes survive to check.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(a) (2019 codification)

POSITIVE The board followed up on the needle exchange question within one meeting and closed an open-ended exception

Three weeks after the board learned on March 17 that no quarterly updates had arrived, the assistant superintendent reported that the program had left the hospital site on March 24, 2020 and distributed its quarterly reporting forms (0:58:35 to 0:59:25); the board then revoked its permission until the program is presented again (1:01:56 to 1:02:08). Under RSA 318-B:45 an exception for a drug-free school zone "may be granted by the applicable district school board", and the statute sets no reporting or review duty, so the follow-up and the recorded decision exceed its requirements.

Sources: RSA 318-B:45 (2018 codification; exception sentence added by 2018, 254:2, eff. Aug. 11, 2018)
