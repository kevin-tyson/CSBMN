Claremont School Board, Deliberative Session, February 8, 2023

First session of the 2023 Claremont School District annual meeting under SB 2. The body is the voters, with the moderator presiding; the board presents the warrant. Voters amended Article 2 to add back the $1,000,000 the board had cut on January 17, raising the operating budget from $37,345,312 to $38,345,312 on a secret ballot the minutes record as 41 to 27, and then restricted reconsideration. Items flagged for review

These flags are a reviewer's aid, not findings of violation and not legal advice. Each one names the rule it rests on, as that rule read on February 8, 2023, or is labeled an observation where no verified rule applies. A deliberative session is a meeting of the voters under RSA 40:13, not a school board meeting: there is no roll call, quorum, board public-comment period or nonpublic session to assess, and the rules on board meetings do not apply.

MEDIUM The Rules of Procedure misstate how a voter can challenge a vote

The handout the moderator reviewed (0:03:08) ends: "any voter who doubts the accuracy of any non-counted vote may require the Moderator to determine a vote by a counted show of hands or counted standing vote." RSA 40:13, IV makes RSA 40:4-b govern the first session, and 40:4-b gives that remedy to a group, not one voter, and a different remedy: a declared non-ballot vote questioned "immediately and before any other business is begun ... by 7 or more of the voters present" must be retaken "by secret 'yes-no' ballot." The sheet also omits RSA 40:13, IV(a) (articles whose wording is prescribed by law may not be amended) and IV(c) (no amendment may eliminate an article's subject matter; changing a dollar amount is allowed). No vote on the recording was challenged, so the misstatement affected nothing this night; it misinforms voters about their rights at every session that uses the sheet, and the project has found the same sheet in 2024.

Sources: RSA 40:4-b (questioning a vote; 1971, 524:1); RSA 40:13, IV (2022 codification; first session governed by RSA 40:4-40:4-b, 40:6-40:10); Rules of Procedure (packet)

OBSERVATION Three explanations of the default budget, and advice to set it for the vote margin

Seaman gave the session three accounts of the $36,342,948 default. First, that "The only difference between the proposed budget, the default budget is the Sri negotiated teacher contract" (0:24:00). Second, answering Pope, that the default fell $1,000,000 "because there were positions that were eliminated" (0:52:11), which is the RSA 40:13, IX(b) test ("salaries and benefits of positions that have been eliminated in the proposed budget"). Third, after the moderator had closed the session, that the board should "consider revising the default budget so that the difference between the two helps preserve the vote" (1:46:53). RSA 40:13, XI(b) does let the governing body adjust the default before the ballots are printed, but only "acting on relevant new information" and on an amended default budget form; the default's amount is fixed by IX(b), not by the margin between the two figures. Whether the board later changed the default is not in this record; the default budget form is in neither the February 1 nor the February 8 packet. See the February 1 page, flag 1.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) and XI(b) (2022 codification; default definition; adjustment by the governing body on relevant new information)

The transcript has no rows between 1:35:11 and 1:43:19, the balloting and count, so the moderator's announcement of 41 to 27 is not in the transcript; whether it is audible on the video was not tested. Most podium name-and-ward announcements were also lost, leaving 254 of 352 rows unidentified. The minutes fill both gaps, but they list floor speakers by position rather than in order, omit Frank Sprague's floor turn (0:55:45), list the Article 1 offices without the district clerk the warrant includes, and place the session in the Gymnasium where the notice and warrant say Auditorium. The observation is about the public record: for this session the approved minutes, not the recording, are the source of the result and of who spoke.

Sources: RSA 40:4 (moderator's duties); Approved Deliberative Session meeting minutes 2.8.23

POSITIVE The secret ballot was requested and run as RSA 40:4-a requires

The moderator announced at the outset that she had "already received a request for secret ballot" on Article 2 (0:04:34), confirmed in the room that each signer was present (0:12:38), and conducted the amendment vote by written yes-no ballot with registered-voter cards checked at the box (1:19:56). RSA 40:4-a provides that "5 voters who are present may make a request in writing prior to a vote" and that "the moderator shall conduct the vote by secret 'yes-no' ballot." Unlike the school board's own secret ballot on January 17, this one is lawful: RSA 91-A:2, II excepts school district meetings from its secret-ballot bar.

Sources: RSA 40:4-a (secret ballot on request of 5 voters; 2006, 117:1); RSA 91-A:2, II (2022 codification; exception for school district meetings)

POSITIVE The SB 2 calendar was met, and the draft minutes were posted within five business days

RSA 40:13, III places the first session "between the first and second Saturdays following the last Monday in January"; in 2023 that window ran February 4 to 11, and February 8 falls inside it. The warrant, signed January 23 to 25, reached the packet folder on January 27, before the last-Monday-in-January posting date of RSA 40:13, II-a(d) (January 30); the public notice is dated January 18. The draft minutes reached the session folder on February 14, the fourth business day after the session, within RSA 91-A:2, II's five business days, which apply because RSA 91-A:1-a reaches the legislative body of a school district.

Sources: RSA 40:13, II-a(d) and III (2022 codification); RSA 91-A:2, II (2022 codification; minutes within 5 business days); RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (public body includes a school district legislative body)
