Claremont School Board Finance Committee, January 5, 2024

A televised working session of the board's three-member Finance Committee two days after the budget hearing. The business administrator reports that she has spread the unexplained $688,426.17 reduction back across salary and benefit lines, with $16,830 she could not place; the members trade one proposed elementary social worker for an instructional coach, work through middle-school and Stevens staffing line by line, and leave the $36,313,408 total unresolved. They schedule a further meeting for Wednesday, January 10 at the superintendent's office, of which this corpus holds no record. The Finance Committee is a committee of the school board, and RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) makes "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof" a public body, so RSA 91-A:2, II's notice and minutes duties apply to it: notice at least 24 hours ahead, excluding Sundays and holidays, and minutes open to inspection within five business days, recording the members present, persons appearing, a brief description of each subject discussed and final decisions. OBSERVATION The $688,000 was reallocated by judgment, $16,830 was not located, and the committee worked without current-year actuals

Henry's account is that the $688,426.17 credit offset grant-funded staff her predecessor had budgeted locally, and that she has now moved it into the salary and benefit lines it belonged to, "based on the information that I had" (0:29:42), leaving $16,830 she could not place and took from an over-budgeted health-insurance line (0:30:04). The committee chair says he found "like a half $1 million" of similar entries (0:40:29), the business administrator says payroll encumbrances have not been updated for staff changes (0:44:37), and monthly year-to-date reports have "not done yet" (1:31:53). A member calls the resulting variances Fiction (0:46:12). None of this changes the bottom line voted, as Henry stresses, and no statute or rule verified for this page requires a board to receive year-to-date actuals during budget preparation; GASB's budget-to-actual requirements govern the annual financial statements, not budget drafts. The point for a reader is that the line-item comparisons the committee was using to judge the fiscal 2025 proposal were, on the business administrator's own account, unreliable in places, and that the fix was a reallocation she made on her own analysis and offered to document by journal entry.
