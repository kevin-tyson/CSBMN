MEDIUM The district's FY2015 audit was still in fieldwork in September 2018

The board was asked to approve "The board questionnaire for fiscal year 15 audit." (0:12:59), a document the auditors use during fieldwork, drafted for a board on which "no existing board members were on the board during the 1415 school year" (0:13:11). The roster supports that reading: the longest-serving members were sworn in March 2016. So the audit of the year ended June 30, 2015 had not been issued three years and three months after that year closed. Fiscal 2015 began July 1, 2014, before the December 26, 2014 start date of the Uniform Guidance audit rules (2 CFR 200.110(b)), so OMB Circular A-133 governed it: an entity expending $500,000 or more in federal awards needed a single audit, with the reporting package due "within the earlier of 30 days after receipt of the auditor's report(s), or nine months after the end of the audit period", here March 31, 2016. The record does not state the district's FY2015 federal expenditures, and RSA 21-J:19 makes the hiring of auditors permissive, so this page rates the gap MEDIUM rather than HIGH. The FY2015 audit report and the Federal Audit Clearinghouse filing would settle whether a federal deadline was missed. The SAU 6 business administrator said a week earlier that the unit's own audits were done through FY2017 (September 13, 2018 page).

Sources: OMB Circular A-133 (2007 revision), sec. 200(a) ($500,000 threshold) and sec. 320(a) (nine-month due date); 2 CFR 200.110(b) (2016 edition: Subpart F applies to fiscal years beginning on or after Dec. 26, 2014)

MEDIUM A $15,000 gift was accepted before the public hearing the statute requires

The board voted to accept $15,000 from the Jack and Dorothy Byrne Foundation for the regional technical center (1:03:45, 1:06:04). RSA 198:20-b, I lets a school board accept unanticipated money from "a private source" without a district meeting vote only where the district has adopted an enabling article, which the business administrator confirmed Claremont had done (1:20:02). Paragraph III(a), in the text in force in 2018, adds: "For unanticipated funds in the amount of $5,000 or more, the school board shall hold a prior public hearing on the action to be taken", with newspaper notice at least seven days ahead. The acceptance vote came first. The record mitigates it: the assistant superintendent raised the question immediately (1:06:06) and confirmed the hearing requirement the same evening (1:19:49), and on October 3 the board held the hearing and voted again (October 3, 2018 page). Whether seven days' newspaper notice preceded that hearing is not in the record.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2005, 188:1; $5,000 threshold until 2023)

OBSERVATION Search committee members were named by volunteering, after the chair proposed written ballots

The SAU 6 motion of September 13 called for the two Claremont members to be elected by the Claremont board (September 13, 2018 page). The chair proposed that if more than two volunteered, members write their choices on paper (1:20:25); Zullo asked "why does it need to be secret" (1:21:07) and the chair answered that it was about efficiency (1:21:18). RSA 91-A:2, II provides that, except for town meetings, school district meetings and elections, "no vote while in open session may be taken by secret ballot". No ballot was used: only Horsky and Zullo volunteered, and the board settled on them by consensus (1:22:51), so no vote was recorded at all. The same chair's later remark that the budget subcommittee might be "disagreeing behind closed doors, but not disagreeing here" (1:24:30) also bears watching: a subcommittee is a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), and RSA 91-A:2-a, I requires public bodies to deliberate "only in meetings". The October 3 page records how a later vacancy on the committee was filled.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification; secret-ballot sentence); RSA 91-A:2-a, I and II (2016 codification; source 2008, 303:4, unamended); RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (source note ends 2008, 354:1)
