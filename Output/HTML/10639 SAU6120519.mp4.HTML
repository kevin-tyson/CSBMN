MEDIUM The board set its budget hearing for January 2, after the date by which RSA 194-C:9 requires the SAU budget to be adopted

RSA 194-C:9, I provides: “At a meeting held before January 1, the school administrative unit board shall adopt a budget”. The exceptions do not reach SAU 6: ¶II covers single-district units, and ¶III covers districts that have adopted RSA 194-C:9-a, whose separate-warrant-article procedure under RSA 194-C:9-b “shall not apply to a school administrative unit that includes a city”, as SAU 6 includes the City of Claremont. Tonight the board did not adopt a budget; it moved a figure to a public hearing and then set that hearing for January 2, 2020 (1:21:32), so adoption could not happen before January 1.

The record shows the board knew a deadline existed and did not establish it. Benware asked for “the absolute drop dead date” (1:19:16) and again for “The deadline for this budget.” (1:20:21); the superintendent answered “We don't know that yet.” (1:19:23); the clerk said the SAU budget is completed “By the end of December” (1:19:28); Benware proposed January 2 “so we're safe” (1:19:47). Mitigating: no statute requires an SAU budget hearing at all, so the delay came from choosing a hearing, which is itself above the minimum (flag 5), and the business administrator called this “not a normal year”. This page grades the scheduling decision MEDIUM; the adoption itself happens on the January 2, 2020 page.

Sources: RSA 194-C:9, I-III (2019 codification; source note 1996, 298:3; 2003, 279:1); RSA 194-C:9-a (2019 codification); RSA 194-C:9-b, IV (2019 codification; not available to an SAU that includes a city)

OBSERVATION A member proposed approving the budget figure by individual emails to the chair; the board voted in public instead

Benware asked whether the business administrator could “send it to us via email, and can we ratify it to Mike of the board chair?”, with each member replying individually, “And if we all do that or at least a preponderance.” (1:12:16, 1:12:36). Zullo objected (“We can't all email him or.”), and the business administrator produced the figure on the spot so that a motion could be made in the meeting (1:12:30, 1:13:38). RSA 91-A:2-a, I requires public bodies to deliberate only in meetings, and ¶II provides that communications outside a meeting, “including, but not limited to, sequential communications among members”, “shall not be used to circumvent the spirit and purpose” of the chapter. Collecting a majority's assent one email at a time is the pattern ¶II describes. The board did not adopt the proposal, so this is an observation; it is recorded because the objection and the in-meeting vote are what kept the decision public.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2-a, I-II (2016 codification; source note 2008, 303:4, unamended through 2022)

POSITIVE A line-by-line SAU budget in public, a lower total, and a public hearing the statute does not require

The business administrator walked the board through staffing, total compensation, object codes and each department's lines (0:24:22 to 1:08:56), separated the cost-of-living placeholder as a visible variable (0:48:32), and explained each reduction.
