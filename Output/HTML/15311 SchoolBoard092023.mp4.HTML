Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, chaired by Vice Chair Frank Sprague with Chair Heather Whitney and Whitney Skillen absent. MEDIUM The nonpublic session was entered under a paragraph that does not exist as cited, and the exemption it describes applies only in an adjudicative proceeding that the record never identifies

The agenda heads the item "Non-Public Meeting Session- Title IV Section 91-A-3 I (j)", and the minutes repeat "Title IV Section 91-A-3 I ((j)". RSA 91-A sits in Title VI, and paragraph I of RSA 91-A:3 contains no lettered exemptions; the text the agenda goes on to quote is paragraph II(j). The motion on tape follows the agenda (0:05:53). RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires the motion to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II which is relied upon", so the citation itself is defective. The substance matters more: II(j) covers confidential, commercial or financial information exempt under RSA 91-A:5, IV only "in an adjudicative proceeding pursuant to RSA 541 or RSA 541-A". Neither the agenda, the motion nor the minutes names such a proceeding. Later in the meeting Nester says "I didn't know how much of this was going to be covered in nonpublic" while pre-K staffing is discussed, and Tempesta refers to "the just the student student issue" (0:13:04, 0:13:06), which suggests the session concerned a student matter; whether a proceeding under RSA 541-A was pending cannot be told from the record. The roll-call requirement was met: five members voted Yes by name (0:06:41 to 0:06:59).

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) and II(j) (2022 codification, in force before 2023, 189:1 took effect October 3, 2023); agenda, item IV.1; minutes, item II.1

MEDIUM The nonpublic minutes were sealed for ten years with no statutory finding stated

RSA 91-A:3, III requires nonpublic minutes to be disclosed within 72 hours "unless, by recorded vote of 2/3 of the members present taken in public session, it is determined that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism". The vote met the numerical test: a roll call in public session, five of five present voting Yes (0:08:24 to 0:08:30). But the motion was only "that we seal the minutes for ten years" (0:08:04), no member stated which of the three statutory grounds applied, and the minutes record only "a motion to seal the meeting minutes for 10 years". The determination the statute makes the basis for withholding is absent from both records. The ten-year term is not itself a statutory concept on this date; the review rule in RSA 91-A:3, IV took effect October 3, 2023.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (2022 codification); RSA 91-A:3, IV (2023, 189:1, eff. Oct. 3, 2023, for the later review rule); minutes

MEDIUM Twenty days after the statutory date for school district financial reports, the business administrator said FY2023 was not yet closed and the DOE-25 not yet begun

RSA 21-J:34, V provides that "School districts shall submit financial reports on or before September 1 of each year." On September 20 Henry described FY2023 as still open: items "need to be moved to grants", some expenses "may have belonged in fiscal belong in fiscal year 22", tuition payments are outstanding, and "I'm working on it, for the Doe 25. So hopefully I can really start getting started on that. So that's my goal next week" (0:26:04 to 0:26:48). Her surplus figure, "a little over $400,000", was given without a printout and "could fluctuate" (0:25:40). This page did not verify whether the DOE-25 is the report RSA 21-J:34, V refers to, or what the Department of Education's own DOE-25 due date was for FY2023, so it is graded MEDIUM rather than HIGH. Mitigation on the record: Henry started in early July 2023 after a business-administrator vacancy, and describes reconciliation work with Lori Mowrey (transcribed Lori Morey; the spelling follows a 2026 district banking resolution). Whatever was filed by September 1 could not have reflected a closed year.

Sources: RSA 21-J:34, V (2023 codification; source note ends 2007, 182:2)
