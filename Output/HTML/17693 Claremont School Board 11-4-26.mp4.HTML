MEDIUM Payments the treasurer did not authorize are still clearing the bank

The treasurer's report lists the payments that cleared in September; the red items are, in his words, "payments that were made that the current treasurer did not authorize." (0:04:09) and "probably the board never saw them either." (0:04:33). A member notes "It just seems that's continued since July." (0:08:58); the treasurer answers that "The number of those has gone way down since July." (0:09:16). The business administrator traces the cause to ACH payments processed before their vouchers were created, and says "So that process has already been changed going forward."
