Claremont School Board, December 20, 2023

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, five of seven members present. After a nonpublic session of more than two hours, the board hears six Claremont Middle School staff oppose a proposed CMS schedule, hears counsel on placing non-CTE programs in the technical center, ratifies the maintenance-and-custodial, secretaries' and paraprofessional agreements, amends the school calendar, and moves a $36,313,407.97 FY2025 budget to the January 3 public hearing. MEDIUM The motion to enter a two-hour nonpublic session named no exemption

The chair announced the session "for RSA 91. Dash a colon three comma two" and asked whether to read it out; the motion that followed was "that we move into nonpublic under the gist of that" (0:07:46). RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires the motion to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II" (statute). Neither the announcement nor the motion named a lettered ground; the agenda printed the text of II(a) and II(c), and the minutes record the motion as under "RSA 91-A:3,II(a,c)". The session then ran, by the minutes, from 6:39 to 9:08 p.m. The agenda's letters probably reflect what the body meant; the recorded motion does not say it.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b) (2023 codification; source note ends 2023, 189:1, eff. Oct. 3, 2023); agenda, item IV.1

MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed for 50 years with no statutory determination on the record

The seal was moved and seconded in public and carried five to nothing by roll call (0:08:50 to 0:09:12; minutes), which clears the two-thirds of members present that RSA 91-A:3, III requires. The paragraph also requires that the vote determine "that divulgence of the information likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism" (statute). No ground is stated on the recording or in either set of minutes. From October 3, 2023, RSA 91-A:3, IV also provides for review of sealed minutes no more than ten years after the vote to withhold them; a 50-year term does not displace that review.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III and IV (2023 codification)

MEDIUM Nearly half of the budget increase sent to hearing is the reversal of a $688,426.17 credit no one can allocate

The packet's budget report (printed 12/20/2023 2:06 p.m.) totals $36,313,407.97, an increase of $1,433,096.06 or 4.11% over the FY 23-24 budget column of $34,880,311.91, the figure moved at 1:05:15. Page 44 of the same report carries account 100.40.1100.113.5.00000, "Board Voted Expense Reduct", at ($688,426.17) in the FY 23-24 budget column and $0.00 proposed, an increase of $688,426.17. That one line is 48% of the total increase. Two days earlier the business administrator told the Finance Committee she did not know what the credit was allocated to and that her predecessor's spreadsheets "don't match up" with the budget (December 18 page). RSA 32:5, IV requires the budget form to carry comparative columns of prior-year appropriations and expenditures, and this report does; but a comparison column that balances only through an unallocated credit makes the reported line increases, and the share of the increase that is new spending, unreliable. Nothing said on the recording explains the line to the board or the public before the vote. The board's later reduction of the figure after the January 3 hearing is the statute working as written (RSA 32:5, I) and is not part of this flag.

Sources: Updated Claremont School District Budget 12.20.23, pages 44 and 55; RSA 32:5, I and IV (2023 codification); Finance Committee, December 18, 2023, flag 3
