Claremont School Board — December 6, 2017

Summary of a regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript. After a superintendent's report on substitute pay, head lice and Senate Bill 193, the board heard 36 minutes of citizens' comments on the budget and on two school incidents, questioned City Manager Ryan McNutt on the city budget and property valuations, accepted a $1,600 donation, and then took a line-by-line administration presentation of the reductions needed to meet its directive of a FY2019 budget $1.2 million below FY2018 (no vote; "Just a presentation"). The recording ends during the resignations item, before a nonpublic session that was still to come. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

OBSERVATION Agenda, packet and minutes for this meeting are not available online

The chair refers to "our agenda" (0:01:15) and the superintendent to documents "in your packet" (0:11:40), and the board approved the November 15 minutes at this meeting (0:01:29 to 0:01:54), so a notice, agenda, packet and a minutes process existed in 2017. None of it is in the district's online Drive shares, whose archive begins in 2022. RSA 91-A:2, II as it read in 2017 required minutes to be "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting" and to record members, persons appearing, a brief description of the subject matter and final decisions; it did not require online posting, so the absence is a limitation of what the district publishes today, not evidence that minutes were never kept. The December 6 minutes would presumably have been approved at a later meeting. The rule that minutes name movers and seconders took effect January 1, 2019 and is not applied here.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2016 codification; source note ends 2016, 29:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017, so the text in force throughout 2017); MAP.md section 49 (Drive check of 9/26/26), named here as a project file.
