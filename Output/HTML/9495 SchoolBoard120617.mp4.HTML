Claremont School Board, December 6, 2017

Regular meeting at which the administration presented, line by line, the reductions needed to meet the board's directive of a fiscal year 2019 budget $1.2 million below fiscal year 2018, after a superintendent's report, long citizens' comments and a discussion with the city manager. This is the full-length recording (show 9495); the same evening is also published in two parts. RSA 91-A:2, II, in the text in force throughout 2017, required minutes including the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions, open to public inspection within five business days. The recording shows the routine was in place: the chair works from an agenda ("So we have our agenda.", 0:01:15), and the superintendent refers to "in your packet, my superintendents report" (0:11:40).
