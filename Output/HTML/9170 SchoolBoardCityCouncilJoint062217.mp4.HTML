Claremont School Board & City Council, Joint Meeting — June 22, 2017

Quarterly joint meeting of the Claremont School Board and the Claremont City Council, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares, and the city's records were not inspected. The bodies heard the mayor's lead-initiative update and two citizens' comments, a Green Dot violence-prevention presentation, and a homelessness and childhood-poverty presentation by the assistant superintendent and a local judge, and set September 28 for the next joint meeting by consensus. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.
