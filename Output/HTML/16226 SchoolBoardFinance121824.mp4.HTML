Claremont School Board Finance Committee, December 18, 2024

Afternoon session of the school board’s three-member Finance Committee, televised by CCTV, hours before the regular board meeting the same evening. The administration presented a revised FY2025–26 draft with pre-K removed, $1,128,543 over after revenues; the committee worked through tax effects and settled, by consensus and without a motion, on asking the administration for about $200,000 more in cuts, roughly a 4–4.5% increase fully loaded. OBSERVATION The 4% target and the $200,000 instruction were set by consensus, and the board was later told the target had been 5%

No motion was made and no vote was taken. Sprague proposed 4% “Revenue included” (1:39:22, 1:39:41); Whitney restated it (1:39:56); Crawford converted it into “Just find me 200,000”, “about a four, 4.5% increase fully loaded” (1:54:27, 1:55:12); the superintendent accepted “our orders” (1:55:33). Under by-law 1.05 subcommittees “are advisory in nature” and the board decides, so an informal direction is within the committee’s role. The point is what the record shows afterward.

The approved January 7, 2025 minutes record the business administrator’s presentation as: “After revenues, total increase is $350,330 before the teachers contract and other warrant articles” and “The target was 5% and with the teachers contract, it is just under that”. The target on this recording is 4%, or about 4.5% fully loaded. The record reviewed for this page does not explain the difference; the committee met again on January 6, 2025 (Cablecast show 16246), and that session is not reviewed here. Both figures are reported here; nothing is assumed about how the 5% was reached.

Sources: By-law 1.05 (subcommittees are advisory); Approved CSB minutes 1.7.25

OBSERVATION Pre-K was taken out of the draft before any board vote, on the committee’s discussion, and the record does not say where the identified children would be served

Henry: “I did take out pre-K because you guys had talked about that” (0:02:55). The committee took no vote on December 13 or here. Asked what the law requires, the room answered: “You do not have to have a preschool” (Henry, 1:52:53); “We’re just obligated to pay to the for the services that are tied to their IEP” (Pratt, 1:52:39). Federal rules make the placement question one for each child’s team: 34 CFR 300.116 applies to “a preschool child with a disability” and requires the placement to be made by a group including the parents, determined at least annually, and as close as possible to the child’s home.

The committee’s figure for the program was “$430,104.13” (0:08:02). The board moved the budget to hearing on January 7, 2025, after the chair said there would be “no reduction in services embedded in IEPs” and that the cut affected “nearly $500,000 to provide services to 23 identified students” (both quoted from the approved January 7 minutes). The record contains no plan for where those children’s services would be delivered. The board, not the committee, made the decision, as the by-laws provide.

Sources: 34 CFR 300.116 (placements, including preschool children); Approved CSB minutes 1.7.25

OBSERVATION The committee set its target without the default budget, and a member’s misstatement about the default was corrected on the record

Asked for the default budget, Henry said “I’m working on that tonight” and gave only a range (1:33:14, 1:33:17). That evening the board was told it is “currently $39,791,261” (approved December 18 minutes). RSA 40:13, XI(a) requires the default budget to be disclosed at the first budget hearing, so the committee stage carries no statutory duty; the effect is that the 4% target was chosen before anyone had the figure that controls if the budget fails.

Whitney said the deliberative session “can’t cut the budget below the default” (1:34:18). Henry and Crawford corrected her: “Yes they can. SB two you can” (1:34:26). The statute supports the correction. RSA 40:13, IV allows warrant articles to be amended at the first session and provides that “An amendment that changes the dollar amount of an appropriation in a warrant article shall not be deemed to violate this subparagraph”; XI(b) bars amending only the default amount itself.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IV and XI(a)–(b) (last amended 2019, 192:2); Approved CSB minutes 12.18.24
