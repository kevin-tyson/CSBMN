HIGH The FY22 audit remains unfinished: 11 of 18 corrective actions drafted, board approval targeted for late October

The superintendent reports "we're at 11 out of 18" (0:27:49) and aims to have the rest drafted "by the end of October" so the board can approve them and the auditors can "finalize the 22 audit" (0:28:10). FY22 ended June 30, 2022. For a single audit, 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1) (2022 edition) set the reporting package due nine months after the period, March 31, 2023, and 2 CFR 200.511(c) has the auditee prepare the corrective action plan "At the completion of the audit" (regulation text). The FY22 findings include federal-grant compliance (finding 2022-018, per the September 2 draft plan), which points to a single audit, though the threshold test for FY22 is not shown in this record. Under RSA 198:4-d, ¶VIII as reenacted by 2026 N.H. Laws ch. 272, the FY26 audit report is due March 31, 2027, with FY23 to FY25 still behind it. The same condition is flagged on the September 2 page, together with the overdue DOE-25, which the October 7 agenda carries as an update item. Mitigation: the backlog predates this administration, and the corrective actions are being written in the auditors' format with named owners and dates.

Sources: 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1), 2022 edition; 2 CFR 200.511(c), 2022 edition; 2026 N.H. Laws ch. 272, RSA 198:4-d, ¶VIII; Corrective Actions - Claremont FY22 Audit - Sept 16 2026 DRAFT.docx.pdf

OBSERVATION The superintendent says scanned postings violate federal accessibility law; the federal web rule is not yet in force

At 0:14:22 the superintendent says that when documents are posted as scans the text cannot be read by OCR software, so "We are actually violating a federal regulation or law." The ADA Title II web-accessibility rule, 28 CFR 35.200, requires WCAG 2.1 AA conformance "Beginning April 26, 2027" for public entities of 50,000 or more and "Beginning April 26, 2028" for smaller entities and special district governments (regulation text). For Claremont that date has not arrived, so this page does not treat current scans as a violation of that rule; other disability-access duties were not assessed. The practical point stands: the treasurer confirms manifests are not yet available to the public (0:16:22), and the district's own corrective-action draft promises PDFs of approved manifests "uploaded to a public folder on the school district website" by December 31, 2026 (September 2 packet document).

Sources: 28 CFR 35.200(b) (compliance dates, as amended 2026); Corrective Actions draft, September 2, finding 2022-008
