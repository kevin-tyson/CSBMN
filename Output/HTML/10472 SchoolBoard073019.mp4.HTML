Claremont School Board, July 30, 2019

Short meeting called five days earlier at the SAU 6 board to act on money returned from the SAU's central-office cuts: the board approved hiring a science teacher and a social studies teacher for Claremont Middle School at about $89,669 each, ending a half-year schedule in both subjects, accepted three donations totaling $2,675.51, and tabled the reappointment of a district representative to the CCTV board so that it could be properly warned. MEDIUM No agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting survive in any district share

MAP.md records no Drive material for this meeting: neither share holds a year folder older than 2022, and the Meeting Minutes share starts at 2023. The board committed about $179,000 a year to two new positions and accepted three gifts, and the recording is now the only public account. RSA 91-A:2, II, in the 2019 codification, required minutes including "the names of members, persons appearing before the public bodies, and a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", required that "The names of the members who made or seconded each motion shall be recorded in the minutes", and required the minutes to be "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting". This page applies the MEDIUM limb of the project's severity rule because the record mitigates the gap: a clerk was present and the board approved what it called the June 30, 2019 minutes, as amended and with one abstention, at its next meeting on August 21, 2019. Their content, discussed during the approval, is this meeting's teacher vote, so the June 30 date is a misstatement for July 30. What is missing is any public copy. MEDIUM Three gifts were accepted at a meeting called outside the regular schedule, where the gift statute names "any regular school board meeting"

The board accepted $720 from the American Legion, $360 from the Polish American Citizens Club and $1,595.51 from the Children's Fund of the Upper Valley in one motion (0:21:50, 0:22:19). For unanticipated funds under $5,000, RSA 198:20-b, III(b), in the 2019 codification, provides: "the school board shall post notice of the funds in the agenda and shall include notice in the minutes of the school board meeting in which such funds are discussed. The acceptance of unanticipated funds under this subparagraph shall be made in public session of any regular school board meeting." This meeting was not a regular one. At the SAU 6 board five days earlier the presiding officer said Claremont would need to meet sooner than its next scheduled meeting to reallocate the funds for teacher hires (show 10463), and the chair here opens the minutes item with "We haven't met for a while." (0:02:04). Much of the statute was met: the acceptance was in public session, the amounts and donors were stated, and the chair read "Acceptance of donations" as an item, apparently from the agenda (0:19:41). The question is the type of meeting. The severity is MEDIUM because the departure is from the text of a binding rule but turns on reading regular in its ordinary sense, and because the record does not show whether the district had adopted the enabling article that RSA 198:20-b, I requires, or whether the board relied on some other authority. The minutes would show how the board recorded the acceptance. The board's next regular meeting, three weeks later, accepted two further gifts in the ordinary way (August 21, 2019).

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(b) (2019 codification; source note ends 2005, 188:1)

OBSERVATION The record does not show how the returned SAU money became authority to pay two teachers

The superintendent described the money as "that money" coming back from the SAU and proposed allocating "$89,669 for each position" (0:03:06); the SAU 6 board had voted on July 25 to cut two central-office positions and return $205,416 to Claremont (show 10463). The board's motion here approved "hire of two new teachers" (0:06:45), about $179,338 a year at the stated figure, with a remainder of "approximately $50,000" (0:03:38). The draft minutes, as read aloud and corrected on August 21, recorded the motion as one to accept $205,416 for fiscal year 2020 from the SAU for two additional teachers, then changed the second accept to allocate (show 10506). Nobody said whether the SAU would refund part of Claremont's assessment, reduce a later assessment, or send money that the district would book as revenue, and nobody named the budget lines involved. If the returned amount left part of the district's appropriation for the SAU assessment unspent, RSA 32:10 lets the governing body transfer an unexpended balance to another appropriation, provided the total spent stays within the total appropriated, and requires records that let any citizen requesting them "pursuant to RSA 91-A:4" ascertain the purposes transferred to and from. Nothing on the recording shows such a transfer being made or recorded, and no budget document survives. The positions also recur in future budgets. This is an observation: the mechanism may be entirely lawful, but the public record of it is missing. The election of the individual teachers is a separate step under RSA 189:39; the board voted on 33 teacher nominations on August 21.

Sources: RSA 32:10, I(a)-(b) and II (2019 codification; source note ends 2017, 127:4); RSA 189:39 (school boards elect all teachers)
