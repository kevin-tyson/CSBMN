Claremont School Board Finance Committee, December 1, 2023

A three-hour daytime working session of the board's three-member Finance Committee on the proposed FY2025 district budget: the technical-center director and the middle- and high-school principals walk through their budgets, the business administrator answers on the district-wide section, and she discloses a negative $688,426.17 line in the current year's budget that nobody can explain. The committee closes by setting three more sessions before the board's December 20 meeting. No notice, agenda or minutes for this session was found in either district share, so the page is built from the Cablecast recording's attributed dialogue, with the board's own November and December documents for context. HIGH A public body met for three hours on the district budget with no notice, agenda or minutes in any district share

The Finance Committee is a committee of the school board and therefore a public body: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) reaches any board of a school district "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof" (statute). All three members met and deliberated on the budget. RSA 91-A:2, II requires a notice posted "at least 24 hours, excluding Sundays and legal holidays, prior to such meetings" and minutes "open to public inspection not more than 5 business days after the meeting" (statute). The session was set aloud on the November 30 recording, which is not a posted notice, and the board's November 15 minutes list no Finance Committee dates under future dates. MAP.md records no notice, packet or minutes in either share (checked 2026-08-28, including the next board meeting's folder and keyword searches). The record offers two partial answers, neither of which is minutes: the business administrator asks at 2:54:52 whether Chelsea "did you get it all?", which suggests notes were being taken, and the December 6 approved board minutes report only that "Sessions have been recorded on CCTV" (minutes' wording). A recording is public, but it is not the minutes the statute requires. Graded HIGH under the project's rule for unmitigated absences: nothing says minutes were produced or where they went, and the deadline passed years ago. This matches the grading of the committee's December 6 and December 18 sessions.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (committees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, II (2023 codification; notice and five-business-day minutes); approved board minutes, December 6, 2023, item 4(b); draft board minutes, November 15, 2023 (future dates)

MEDIUM The current-year budget carries a negative $688,426.17 line nobody can explain, and line-level errors make the comparison column unreliable

The business administrator reports a line "called board voted expense reductions that was a negative number of $688,426.17 loaded into the budget", for which she "can't find any notes" and which she and her predecessor could not trace (2:00:19); she proposes to leave it as "one of those anomalies" (2:00:50) and supposes it "was the number that was used to make your budget balance" (2:01:41). The same session surfaces other lines in the FY24 base or the FY25 draft that the business office itself calls wrong: an unfilled guidance salary "left in the budget the previous year" (0:08:40), new lines showing a zero percent change (0:56:13), a special-education salary line that Whitney calls "A budgeting error" with Henry's assent (0:52:07), one social worker counted in two places (1:50:58), workers' compensation that "was a budgeting error" (2:38:00), and, by the high-school principal's account, a substitute line and $40,000 for town fields "taken out after I submitted the budget" (1:13:18, 1:13:36) with no record shown of who removed them. RSA 32:5, IV requires the budget form to carry comparative columns of prior-year appropriations and expenditures (statute); it does not require every line to reconcile, so no breach is shown. But a comparison column the preparer cannot tie to the appropriation is a control weakness, and the committee was using it to judge every FY2025 increase. The members asked for an account of the errors (flag 5), and the figure recurs on the committee's December 18 recording (see that page). The budget sent to hearing on December 20 is outside this page's record.

Sources: RSA 32:5, IV (2023 codification; comparative columns); Finance Committee, December 18, 2023 page

OBSERVATION A members-only caucus is planned for December 18 "to make sure we've got our stories straight"

Whitney proposes that the three members meet on December 18 "For a caucus" and adds "I would like it still to be recorded and we can have a discussion." (2:56:44); Crawford: "We need to talk among ourselves to make sure we've got our stories straight for the 20." (2:58:03). A quorum of the committee convened to discuss the budget is a meeting under RSA 91-A:2, I whatever it is called, and the caucus exclusion in I(c) covers only "A caucus consisting of elected members of a public body of the same political party who were elected on a partisan basis" (statute). School board members are elected on the non-partisan ballot that RSA 671:30 requires, so the exclusion is not available. The plan itself breaches nothing, and Whitney's insistence that it be recorded points the right way: the December 18 session was televised (show 15451). That recording ends with the chair saying the members need to caucus; see that page.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I and I(c) (2023 codification); RSA 671:30 (non-partisan ballot for school district officers); Finance Committee, December 18, 2023 page
