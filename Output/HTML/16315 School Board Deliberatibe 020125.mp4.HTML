Claremont School Board, Deliberative Session, February 1, 2025

First session of the 2025 Claremont School District annual meeting under SB 2. The board and administration presented the four warrant articles, led by a $42,933,564 operating budget (default $42,772,778); no voter spoke on any article, nothing was amended, and all four went to the March 11 ballot as written. Items flagged for review

MEDIUM The Rules of Procedure handed to voters misstate the remedy for a doubted vote

The Rules of Procedure sheet, which the moderator told voters “will govern how the meeting is conducted” (0:06:20), ends: “any voter who doubts the accuracy of any non-counted vote may require the Moderator to determine a vote by a counted show of hands” (Rules of Procedure text). That is not New Hampshire law. RSA 40:4-b lets a declared non-ballot vote be questioned only by seven or more voters, immediately, and then requires it to be retaken by secret yes-no ballot; RSA 40:4-a gives five voters a written request, before the vote, for a secret ballot. RSA 40:13, IV makes both sections govern the first session. The sheet also tells voters that articles “may be Amended from the floor” without stating the limits in RSA 40:13, IV(a) and IV(c) (no amendment of articles whose wording is prescribed by law; no amendment eliminating an article's subject matter). No vote was doubted and no amendment was offered this day, so nothing turned on the error. Graded MEDIUM because the sheet is the district's written statement of the voters' rights and the same wording has appeared in earlier years' sheets.

Sources: RSA 40:4-b (7 voters; retaken by secret ballot); RSA 40:4-a (5 voters; written request before the vote); RSA 40:13, IV (first session governed by RSA 40:4, 40:4-a, 40:4-b; amendment limits); Rules of Procedure.docx (1).pdf

OBSERVATION Default budget explained again without the eliminated-positions clause

As at the January 15 hearing, Henry read the RSA 40:13, IX(b) definition of the default budget and stopped after the one-time-expenditures clause (0:24:39, 0:25:09). The omitted words, “and by salaries and benefits of positions that have been eliminated in the proposed budget” (statute text), bear directly on this budget, which suspends the preschool program. Her account of what the default contains (0:25:29, 0:25:49) names contracts, bond payments and out-of-district placements and no reduction for eliminated positions, and the session packet, like the hearing packet, holds no default-budget form. The default “shall not be amended by the legislative body” (RSA 40:13, XI(b), statute text), so the session could not correct it in any case. The underlying question is graded MEDIUM on the January 15 hearing page; here it is recorded as an observation so the same point is not counted twice.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b), XI(a)–(b); Claremont Budget Summary (1).pdf

OBSERVATION Teachers' first-year cost and the board's recommendation tally differ between documents

Article 2's Note A, read aloud by Sprague (0:57:07), puts the first-year cost of the teachers' agreement at $516,836 (warrant text: “2025-26 $ 516,836”). The budget presentation (0:24:39: “there's 514 in there”), Henry's January 15 hearing figure and that hearing's budget summary all carry $514,836. Note A is what voters approve as the agreement's cost item, so a $2,000 difference between it and the budget line is worth reconciling; the appropriation total of $42,933,564 is the same in every document. Separately, the warrant prints the Board's recommendation on Articles 2, 3 and 4 as “(4) Yes (0) No,” and Crawford reads “4 to 0” (1:03:57), while the approved January 21 minutes record five members present “voting in favor” of moving the articles. RSA 32:5, V(a) requires only a notation of whether the governing body recommends an article, so neither point is a rule question.

Sources: 2025 Warrant (1).pdf; approved 1.21.25 minutes; RSA 32:5, V(a) (2024 codification)

OBSERVATION Voters were steered toward comments rather than questions, and none spoke

Before the articles, the moderator told the room “discussion should be limited to comments about the articles and not questions which should have been asked at an earlier public meeting” (0:54:35). RSA 40:13, IV describes the first session as “explanation, discussion, and debate of each warrant article” (statute text), and RSA 40:4 lets the moderator prescribe rules that “may be altered by the town” (statute text). No voter rose on any of the four articles, and no amendment was offered; the session's only floor business was the minutes approval and two motions to restrict reconsideration. The record does not show how many voters attended or whether anyone was deterred. Noted because the deliberative session is the voters' only chance to amend the budget.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IV; RSA 40:4 (moderator's rules may be altered by the meeting)

POSITIVE Elected moderator presided, the session ran inside the statutory window, and every vote was declared

The 2024 session was presided over by an “interim moderator,” an office New Hampshire law does not create. This year the elected district moderator, Tracy Pope, presided, as RSA 197:19 contemplates. RSA 40:13, III sets the first session between the first and second Saturdays after the last Monday in January (January 27, 2025), so February 1 is inside the window, and the warrant certifies posting “on or before January 27,2025” (warrant text), as II-a(d) requires. The moderator declared each vote aloud (RSA 40:4), explained each motion to restrict reconsideration before putting it (1:02:20, 1:11:48), a motion RSA 40:10 makes available at school district meetings, and the minutes record the mover and seconder of every motion. A draft of the minutes exists as a Drive file created Monday, February 3, two days after the session, within the five business days of RSA 91-A:2, II; because the 2025 minutes folder was assembled later, that date shows the draft existed, not when the public could first read it.

Sources: RSA 197:19 (district moderator); RSA 40:13, II-a(d), III; RSA 40:4; RSA 40:10 (restriction of reconsideration); RSA 91-A:2, II (2023 codification); draft minutes (Drive created 2025-02-03)
