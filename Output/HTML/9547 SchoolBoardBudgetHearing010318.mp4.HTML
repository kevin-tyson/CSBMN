OBSERVATION The default budget was disclosed by its tax effect only; its dollar amount is not on the recording

RSA 40:13, XI(a), as it read before the August 2018 amendments, provided: "The default budget shall be disclosed at the first budget hearing held pursuant to RSA 32:5 or RSA 197:6." This appears to be that first hearing. The superintendent said that if the default budget were approved "it would be an increase of $0.24" per thousand, and explained why it was larger than the proposed budget (0:22:26, 0:22:59). The default budget's dollar amount is not read aloud during the hearing, and the handout that may have carried it does not survive. The figure was stated later that night at the regular meeting, where the transcript renders the figure as 35,111,787 and the business administrator puts it about $191,000 above the operating budget. Whether the disclosure at the hearing met XI(a) depends on what the handout showed. The superintendent's two tax figures also differ in sign: "a reduction of $0.03" for the main budget (0:22:26) and "the increase would be $0.03" with the special articles (0:23:24).

Sources: RSA 40:13, XI(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 7:1-4 and 190:1-3, 8-10, so in force until August 2018)

OBSERVATION The projected loss of adequacy aid follows the statutory phase-out of the stabilization grant

The business administrator projected adequacy falling from "12 million 341 for 91" to "12 million 077963" [$12,341,491 to $12,077,963], a reduction "driven by the reduction of the stabilization grant by the state" (0:28:28). RSA 198:41, IV(d), as it read in 2017, set a stabilization percentage that "shall be 96 percent for fiscal year 2017, and shall be reduced by 4 percent" for each fiscal year thereafter. The revenue loss the superintendent described as the State "downshifting" is in this respect a scheduled reduction written into statute. The page cannot verify Claremont's grant amount or the "225,000 a year for 20 years" figure; the transcript's numbers are internally consistent ($12,341,491 less $12,077,963 is $263,528, and $263,528 less the $50,000 tuition increase is the $213,528 decrease he cites at 0:28:57).

Sources: RSA 198:41, IV(d) (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 8:4, 5 and 85:9, eff. July 18, 2016)

OBSERVATION The hearing date met both statutory windows

For an official-ballot district voting in March, RSA 40:13, II-a(c) required budget hearings "on or before the third Tuesday in January", which in 2018 was January 16. RSA 32:5, I, in the text then in force, required the hearing "not later than 25 days before each annual or special meeting", with notice "at least 7 days in advance". The first session was announced for Wednesday, February 7, 2018 (see part 2), 35 days after this hearing. The posting of the seven-day notice cannot be checked because no notice survives.

Sources: RSA 40:13, II-a(c) (2017 codification); RSA 32:5, I (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 190:7, in force until Aug. 11, 2018)
