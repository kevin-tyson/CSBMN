Claremont School Board, Budget Public Hearing (part 1 of 2) — January 3, 2018

First of two recordings of the public hearing on the fiscal 2019 (2018–19) school budget, summarized from the CCTV recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no notice, agenda, budget handout or minutes are online. The superintendent presented a $31,148,257 budget (the board's December 20 figure) and the business administrator reconciled it to $31,455,410 with the collective bargaining agreement ratified on December 20; public testimony followed, and when the chair called a break during a heated exchange four members voted to "overrule the chair" and the hearing continued. Part 2 is Cablecast show 9548. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

OBSERVATION The default budget was disclosed only as a tax effect on the recording

RSA 40:13, XI(a), in the text in force on this date, provided: "The default budget shall be disclosed at the first budget hearing held pursuant to RSA 32:5 or RSA 197:6." (The wording "presented for questions and discussion" came later, in 2018.) The superintendent said approval of the default "would be an increase of $0.24" and that "The default budget is larger" than the proposed budget (0:22:26). The default's dollar amount is not spoken on the recording; it may have been on the handout, which is not online. A default larger than the proposed budget is consistent with the statute's definition in IX(b) (prior-year appropriations adjusted for contracts and obligations, less one-time expenditures), since the proposed budget was about $21,837 below the prior year's by the business administrator's figures (0:25:57). Whether disclosure in tax-rate terms and a handout satisfied XI(a) depends on documents that are not online.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) and XI(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 7:1-4 and 190:1-3, 8-10).

POSITIVE Timely hearing, printed figures for the public, and figures that reconcile

RSA 40:13, II-a, as then in force, required budget hearings "on or before the third Tuesday in January", January 16 in 2018; this hearing was January 3, and the deliberative session (announced for February 7 in part 2) was more than 25 days later, as RSA 32:5, I required.
