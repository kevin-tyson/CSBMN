Claremont School District, Deliberative Session, February 3, 2016

First session of the 2016 annual school district meeting under the official-ballot (SB 2) law: the voters heard the superintendent's budget presentation and sent nine warrant articles to the March 8 ballot without amendment. The elected moderator was absent and the meeting chose a temporary moderator. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript; no warrant, rules sheet or minutes survive in the district's online shares. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

MEDIUM No minutes or posted warrant for this session survive in any district share

MAP.md records no Drive material: the district's shares hold no year folder older than 2022, and the Meeting Minutes share starts at 2023. The clerk "shall keep a true record of all the doings of each meeting" under RSA 197:20, and RSA 91-A:2, II required minutes open to inspection within five business days. This page applies the MEDIUM limb of the project's severity rule because the recording shows the clerk keeping that record: the meeting accepted "the minutes from the annual school District meeting of February 4th, 2015" (0:08:22). The statute of the time did not require online posting, and the shares do not reach back to 2016. Without the minutes, the record of who spoke rests on the transcript, where several names are garbled.

Sources: RSA 197:20 (2015 codification; source note ends 1963, 120:5); RSA 91-A:2, II (2015 codification)

MEDIUM The district's attorney, not the clerk, presided over choosing a temporary moderator

With the moderator absent, RSA 197:20 provides that the clerk "shall act as moderator of any meeting until a moderator pro tempore shall be chosen". The clerk was present, but the district's attorney opened the meeting, proposed the election, and called for the second, discussion and vote (0:00:21 to 0:01:42); the clerk took the mover's name and administered the oath. The attorney cited "RSA 6716", which does not match the provision on point. The meeting itself chose the temporary moderator by voice vote, as the statute contemplates, no one objected, and the irregularity lasted under two minutes before any warrant business. It is graded MEDIUM, not HIGH, because the choice was the voters' and no article was acted on under the attorney. The clerk's oath ran "until March 5th, 2016" (0:03:09), a date that matches neither this session nor the March 8 ballot.

Sources: RSA 197:20 (clerk acts as moderator until a moderator pro tempore is chosen); RSA 671:33, III (2015 codification: in a vacancy "the school district clerk shall serve as moderator")

OBSERVATION The paraprofessionals' cost item on the warrant is $13,699 less than the figure given at ratification

When the board ratified the paraprofessionals' agreement on January 9, the business administrator gave its total cost as $143,449, including $42,241 for the insurance change and $38,206 for added work days. Article 3 as read here asks for "$129,750", made up of $91,569 in wages and $38,181 in insurance (0:59:42). The added days are not mentioned. The record does not explain the change, which may have come at the January 19 budget hearing (not in this corpus). Under RSA 273-A:3, II(b) only the cost items go to the voters, so the warrant figure is the one that counts; the point is that the two public statements of the cost do not match.

Sources: January 9, 2016 meeting page; RSA 273-A:3, II(b)

It's just my opinion." (1:31:22, 1:32:05). No statute verified for this page forbids it; it is noted because the presiding officer's neutrality is what the voters rely on.

Sources: Cablecast: SCHOOL BUDGET DELIBERATIVE SESSION 2-3-16

OBSERVATION Motions to approve articles that go to the ballot regardless

Each article was taken up on a motion "to approve" or "to accept" and carried by voice vote, beginning with the election of officers in Article 1 (0:44:43). Under RSA 40:13, VI, as it read in 2016, "All warrant articles shall be placed on the official ballot for a final vote, including warrant articles as amended by the first session", so these votes decided nothing; the first session's power is to explain, debate and amend. The moderator did read the warrant's statement that the final vote is by ballot (0:43:45). A viewer could still take "the ayes have it" on Article 2, with nays heard, as approval of the budget.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IV and VI (2015 codification; source note ends 2014, 190:1-3, 8-10, eff. Sept. 9, 2014)

POSITIVE Each cost item explained, and the budget article told voters it carried a contract

The business administrator broke down every collective bargaining article into wage cost, insurance savings and net figure before the vote (0:50:19, 0:59:00, 1:06:49, 1:10:52), and board members who sat at the bargaining table said so (1:08:17, 1:11:51). Article 2's text stated the administrators' $38,890 and that a favorable vote also approves that cost item (0:46:07). Folding a contract into the budget limits the voters' choice (see the January 9, 2016 page), but this disclosure tells them what they are approving. The session also fell inside the statutory window, the first and second Saturdays after the last Monday in January (January 30 to February 6, 2016).

Sources: RSA 40:13, III (2015 codification); January 9, 2016 page, flag 2
