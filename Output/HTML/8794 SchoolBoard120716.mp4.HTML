Asked whether a roll call was required, the superintendent answered that the board does not do that; a member disagreed, and the presiding officer took a roll call (1:32:49 to 1:33:29). RSA 91-A:3, I(b) required that a motion to enter nonpublic session state the specific exemption "on its face" and be decided by roll call vote. OBSERVATION Two $500 grants accepted in public at a regular meeting; the minutes half of the notice rule cannot be checked

The board accepted two ExxonMobil Educational Alliance grants of $500 each by voice vote (0:29:08, 0:30:10), and the superintendent's reference to "the footnote" indicates that the agenda listed them. RSA 198:20-b, III(b), as it read in 2016, provided that for unanticipated funds under $5,000 the board "shall post notice of the funds in the agenda and shall include notice in the minutes", and that acceptance be made "in public session of any regular school board meeting". The acceptance meets the public-session requirement on the recording; the agenda and minutes requirements cannot be confirmed because neither document survives. The statute also presupposes that the district had adopted the enabling article under paragraph I, which the record does not show.

Sources: RSA 198:20-b, I and III(b) (2016 codification; source note ends 2005, 188:1)

OBSERVATION The board kept "equal access" on the strength of counsel's summary, while the statute's text does not use the word

Legal counsel's note, as read by the superintendent, says the statute requires districts "to grant equal access" (1:18:58). RSA 193:1-c, I, as amended effective March 26, 2016, says such pupils "shall have access to curricular courses and cocurricular programs" and that district policy "shall not be more restrictive" for them than for its own pupils; the word equal does not appear. The vice chair and Adrian preferred the statutory wording; the board advanced the policy as written, the vice chair voting yes while noting his objection (1:27:57). Counsel's summary and the statute point the same way in practice, so this is a drafting choice, not a defect; the superintendent offered to get clarification by phone (1:27:07).

Sources: RSA 193:1-c, I-II (2016 codification; source note ends 2016, 4:1, eff. March 26, 2016); November 16, 2016 meeting page, flag 4
