SAU 6 Board, September 29, 2022

Regular meeting of the joint Claremont and Unity board of School Administrative Unit 6. HIGH Claremont's fiscal 2020 audit, due in 2021, was still unfinished and was pushed to the end of October 2022

Seaman reported that the auditor asked to move "the date for the Claremont audit, which was supposed to have been done by tomorrow to the end of next month", for Claremont's 2020 audit, with Claremont's 2021 and 2022 audits to follow (0:36:55, 0:38:02). Fiscal 2020 ended June 30, 2020. Under 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1), a single audit is due "within the earlier of 30 calendar days after receipt of the auditor's report(s), or nine months after the end of the audit period" (regulation text), that is March 31, 2021, and OMB Memorandum M-21-20 allowed fiscal years ending through June 30, 2021 a further six months, to September 30, 2021. The single-audit requirement applies when a district spends $750,000 or more in federal awards in the year (2 CFR 200.501(a)); the recording does not give Claremont's fiscal 2020 federal total, though the same report describes Title II, III and IV grants and ESSER money. Unity's 2020 audit was only in final draft. Mitigation stated on the record: the auditor attributed the delay to losing two senior staff and to grant reconciliation, and Seaman said the plan keeps fiscal 2022 on schedule, "as is required" (0:38:02).

Sources: 2 CFR 200.512(a)(1), 2022 annual edition (earlier of 30 days or nine months after period end); OMB M-21-20 (Mar. 19, 2021), App. 3, sec. IX (six-month delay for fiscal years ending through June 30, 2021); 2 CFR 200.501(a), 2022 annual edition ($750,000 threshold)

MEDIUM A member took part remotely, but votes were by voice and the minutes give no reason for the remote participation

A member identified as Whitney Skillen joined remotely ("You probably can't see me. I don't think you can see me. There I am now.", 0:20:34), answered the roll (0:01:44) and, per the draft minutes, seconded the adjournment. RSA 91-A:2, III, in the form in force from January 1, 2019 to October 2, 2023, allows a member to participate remotely only when attendance in person is "not reasonably practical", with the reason "stated in the minutes", and provides that "All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote." (statute text). Every vote at this meeting (agenda, three sets of minutes, adjournment) was by voice, and the draft minutes do not mention remote participation at all. The COVID emergency order that once relaxed these rules ended in June 2021. Mitigation: a physical quorum was present, so the remote participation did not decide whether the board could act.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(a) and (e), 2022 codification (source note ends 2018, 244:1): remote participation, reason in minutes, roll-call votes
