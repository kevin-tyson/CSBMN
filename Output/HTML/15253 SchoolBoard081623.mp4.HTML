Claremont School Board, August 16, 2023

Regular meeting with four of seven members present. HIGH The FY2024 report of appropriations voted (MS-22) was still unsigned four months after its statutory due date

RSA 21-J:34, II requires the governing body of each school district to "certify the appropriations voted" and states: "This report shall be filed within 20 days of the close of the meeting" (statute). The form itself prints "Form Due Date: 20 Days after the Annual Meeting" (Exhibit D). Claremont's 2023 annual meeting closed with the official-ballot vote on Tuesday, March 14, 2023, so the report was due Monday, April 3, 2023. The packet copy carries a DRA generation stamp of August 9, 2023, its total of $38,345,312 matches the budget the voters approved, and on August 16 the business administrator asked the board to sign it so she could upload it: "It is late, but it's getting done", adding of next year's "This one might be late" (0:24:17). That is 135 days after the due date. Mitigation on the record: the business office turned over in the interval (Henry describes "a new administration", 0:08:13), and the board signed the same night. The filing date at DRA is not in this record.

Sources: RSA 21-J:34, II (2023 codification; source note ends 2007, 182:2, eff. Apr. 1, 2007); Exhibit D-MS22 CSB.pdf

MEDIUM Nonpublic minutes sealed for ten years with no statutory determination stated, and no times recorded for the session

RSA 91-A:3, III, as it read on this date, lets a public body withhold nonpublic minutes beyond 72 hours only by a recorded two-thirds vote of the members present, taken in public session, that determines divulgence "likely would affect adversely the reputation of any person other than a member of the public body itself, or render the proposed action ineffective, or pertain to terrorism" (statute). The vote here was in public and unanimous by roll call of the four members present (1:08:47 to 1:08:58), which meets the vote requirement. No determination was stated: the motion was "to seal the minutes for ten years" (1:08:25), and the minutes record it the same way. The session entered under II(c), the reputation ground, so the needed finding may be implied, but the statute asks for it to be made. The session is not on the recording: the return roll call begins 38 recording-seconds after the entry vote (1:07:19 to 1:07:57), so the tape was stopped or edited, and neither the tape nor the minutes give the session's start, end or length. The ten-year review in RSA 91-A:3, IV did not yet exist; it took effect October 3, 2023.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, III (2022 codification); minutes, item 7

OBSERVATION The handbooks were approved "with our notes and comments" incorporated, so the adopted text was not before the board

The motion approved three handbooks subject to changes the board described in discussion (0:59:20); Koski summarized them afterwards to be sure he had them all (0:59:54), and the minutes record them only in part (one bullet ends mid-sentence: "The Claremont School Board would like to have SHS and CMS handbooks be", minutes). The changes include substantive policy text: putting the board's bullying policy in the CMS and Stevens handbooks, and changing an elementary interim-measures clause from may to shall (0:55:20). Members said the handbooks arrived late for review (0:58:45). No rule requires the board to see final text before approving a handbook; the point is that the public record does not show what was adopted.

Sources: minutes, item 5; packet Exhibits E to G (appendix)

POSITIVE The board fixed its budget-season dates five months ahead, inside the SB 2 calendar

RSA 40:13, II-a puts budget hearings in official-ballot districts "on or before the third Tuesday in January", and RSA 40:13, III sets the first session "between the first and second Saturdays following the last Monday in January" (statute). For 2024 those fall on January 16 and February 3 to 10. The board voted a January 3 hearing and a February 7 deliberative session (0:31:04), both inside the windows, and announced them publicly in August so that, in the chair's words, "the public knows when our budget public hearing is going to be" (0:29:47).

Sources: RSA 40:13, II-a and III (2023 codification; last amended 2019, 192:2)

POSITIVE The nonpublic motion stated its ground, every nonpublic vote was by roll call, and the minutes reached the public share on the fifth business day

RSA 91-A:3, I(b) requires a motion to enter nonpublic session to state the specific exemption and to be decided by roll call. The motion read the text of II(c) into the record (1:06:17); a member asked for a roll call (1:06:40); entry, return and seal were each taken by roll (1:06:45, 1:07:57, 1:08:47). The minutes were created on Drive Wednesday, August 23, 2023, the fifth business day after the meeting, inside RSA 91-A:2, II's limit.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b); RSA 91-A:2, II
