Claremont School Board — June 2, 2021

Regular meeting summary generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. OBSERVATION The board authorized the superintendent to fill teaching vacancies over the summer, with its own election of teachers deferred

RSA 189:39 provides that "Superintendents shall nominate and school boards elect all teachers employed in the schools in their school administrative unit". At 2:08:41 the chair described the motion as letting the superintendent "fill staffing" and "defer those nominations to, to August", because the board did not plan to meet in July. Horsky's motion (2:09:17) authorized him "to fill vacancies as required" through August 18, 2021, and was narrowed at 2:11:22 to budgeted positions after Whitney asked whether new positions were included. The superintendent's answer mentioned possible additional ESSER-funded teachers (2:09:44), which the amendment excluded.

Read with the chair's explanation, the arrangement keeps the board's election but moves it after the hire, so teachers may be at work before the board has elected them. Whether a board may delegate in that way is a legal question this page does not resolve; the statute's text puts the election with the board, and no provision authorizing interim appointment was identified. In the board's favor: the authority was voted in public, limited in time and to budgeted positions, and the chair stated that nominations would come back to the board. Whether they did, and when, is a question for the August pages and minutes.

Sources: RSA 189:39 (source note ends 1971, 371:2; in force in 2021): superintendents nominate and school boards elect all teachers.

OBSERVATION A past state monitoring visit cited the district for purchasing documentation under its federal grants; the report is not public

Reading the FY2022 general assurances into the record, the assistant superintendent said that when she started, "they were coming out to do an audit. We were not doing sole source documents. We weren't reporting out anything over $10,000. We have to get permission from them to spend it." and "So since they came out and cited us on it, we need to make sure that we're following all those procedures." (1:27:06). She connected the fix to policy DAF, revised "last year". Federal award recipients must keep documented procurement procedures and records under the Uniform Guidance procurement standards. The monitoring report, its date and findings, and the corrective action plan are not in any available record, so the public cannot see what was cited or whether it was closed. Recorded as an observation: the disclosure itself, made unprompted in open session, is to the administration's credit, and she describes corrective action already in place.

Sources: 2 CFR 200.318 to 200.327 (Uniform Guidance procurement standards; general standard, current text); the monitoring report and policy DAF were not located.

POSITIVE The vacancy was filled promptly, in public, after the applicant was heard, with the chair disclosing his own shortcut

RSA 671:33, II(a), as it read in June 2021, provided that "The school board shall fill vacancies occurring on the school board" and that "Appointees of the school board shall serve until the next district election". The chair described that rule accurately (1:11:23), had consulted an NHSBA attorney, and told the board he had "acted unilaterally" in advertising the seat without first convening the board (1:06:35). The applicant spoke in open session and members questioned him (0:10:45); members debated the process and whether to reach past candidates; the appointment was moved and voted at the next regular meeting after the death, and the new member was sworn in on the spot. The statute sets no procedure beyond the board's vote, so the advertisement, the public hearing of the applicant and the chair's candor about his own step exceeded what it required. One gap: the vote was by voice with no audible tally or named seconder (1:13:54), so only the minutes could confirm it.

Sources: RSA 671:33, II(a) (2019 codification; source note ends 2017, 3:2; amended later by 2021, 91:318, eff. July 1, 2021, and 2021, 42:1, eff. July 16, 2021): school board fills vacancies until the next district election.
