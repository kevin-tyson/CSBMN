Claremont School District, Deliberative Session, February 8, 2018

First session of the 2018 annual school district meeting under the official-ballot (SB 2) law. On a written request by five voters, amendments were decided by secret ballot: the voters added $307,153 to the operating budget, 99 to 43, so that it went to the March 13 ballot at $34,920,410, and rejected a further $175,000 for technology, 66 to 67. The other three articles went forward unamended. No warrant, rules sheet or minutes survive in the district's online shares; the page is built from the recording's dialogue transcript. OBSERVATION The amendment result was announced with the wrong figure, and the first motion to restrict reconsideration was left hanging

The moderator put and announced the first secret ballot as an amendment of Article 2 "to the amount of $34,613,257" (1:15:21, 1:16:10), which is the original figure; the amendment raised it to $34,920,410 (0:35:14). The bottom line she later declared, $34,920,410 (1:39:17), is correct, so the slip did not change what went to the ballot. The chair then moved to restrict reconsideration of Article 2 (1:16:35); the moderator told the meeting this meant voters could not "further discuss or amend this article" (1:16:59), but after advice from an unidentified voice (1:17:28) she reopened the article and took a second amendment (1:19:45). No vote on the first restriction motion is audible. RSA 40:10, I allows a meeting "to restrict reconsideration of any one or more votes previously taken at that meeting, or warrant articles previously considered", so what a restriction covers depends on how the motion is worded and voted. The meeting did restrict reconsideration by a voice vote after the second amendment failed (1:39:42 to 1:40:04). The minutes would show whether the first motion was ever voted.

Sources: RSA 40:10, I and V (1996, 64:1, eff. July 1, 1996; unamended): restriction of reconsideration; applies to school district meetings under RSA 197; RSA 40:13, IV (2017 codification): the first session is governed by RSA 40:4, 40:4-a, 40:4-b, 40:4-f and 40:6 to 40:10

OBSERVATION Voters heard opposite accounts of whether the budget was cut; the figures on the record allow a check

The superintendent said the budget "really was reduced more than $2 million" (0:12:25), counting reductions from the administration's recommended budget plus the teachers' agreement absorbed inside it. Board member Irish said "There is no $1.7 million cut" and "We didn't cut anything from last year's budget" (1:07:46, 1:08:08). Both can be true, because they measure from different starting points, but neither speaker stated the comparison the warrant itself carries. As read by the chair, Article 2 was $34,613,257 and the default budget $35,111,787 (0:31:22). In 2018 RSA 40:13, IX(b) defined the default budget as the prior year's operating-budget appropriations, adjusted for debt service, contracts and other obligations previously incurred or mandated by law and reduced by one-time expenditures. The proposed budget was therefore $498,530 below that adjusted prior-year figure, and with the $307,153 amendment it stood $191,377 below it. Those differences come from the two figures read aloud; the default budget worksheet, which does not survive, would show the line-by-line comparison.

Sources: RSA 40:13, IX(b) (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 190:1-3, 8-10, eff. Sept. 9, 2014: default budget definition before the 2018 eliminated-positions clause)

POSITIVE Voters' procedural rights were honored: secret ballots on request, a close vote recounted, and a misstatement corrected before the vote

Five voters asked in writing for a secret ballot on the budget article and its amendments, and the moderator granted it (0:34:12). RSA 40:4-a, which RSA 40:13, IV applies to the first session, provides that five voters present may request a secret ballot "in writing prior to a vote", and the moderator then conducts the vote by secret ballot. Both amendment votes were taken that way (1:14:34, 1:37:11). The one-vote margin on the $175,000 amendment was counted "Several times. Not just twice, but several." (1:38:49). When a board member told the meeting that the amendment would push the budget past the default (1:32:43), a voter asked for clarification, and the business administrator and moderator corrected the point before the ballot (1:36:01, 1:36:38). Registered voters were seated apart from nonvoters (0:00:35) and district counsel was present.

Sources: RSA 40:4-a (2006, 117:1, eff. July 9, 2006): secret ballot on written request of 5 voters; RSA 40:13, IV (2017 codification)
