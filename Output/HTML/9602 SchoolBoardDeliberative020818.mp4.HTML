Claremont School District, Deliberative Session — February 8, 2018

First (deliberative) session of the Claremont School District's annual meeting under the SB 2 official-ballot system, summarized from the CCTV recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no warrant, rules sheet or minutes are online. On a secret ballot of 99 to 43, voters added $307,153 to the Article 2 operating budget, restoring the teachers' contract increase the board had left out, and sent it to the March 13 ballot at $34,920,410; a second amendment adding $175,000 for technology failed 66 to 67 on a secret ballot counted several times. Articles for $141,000 of security upgrades (unlocking about $561,980 in state infrastructure money), a $190,000 bus lease-purchase and a $100,000 middle school roof repair went forward unamended. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

Flags are a reviewer's aid generated by comparing the session against the state law in force on February 8, 2018. A deliberative session is a meeting of the voters, so school-board rules (public-comment periods, nonpublic sessions, board roll calls) do not apply to it; RSA chapter 40 as extended to school districts does. They are not findings of violation and nothing here is legal advice. Each flag cites the rule it rests on; where no verified rule applies, the flag says so and is recorded as an observation. Six flags follow, ordered by severity. No HIGH or MEDIUM flag was found.

OBSERVATION No minutes, warrant or rules sheet for this session are online

RSA 197:20 makes the district clerk keep "a true record of all the doings of each meeting". The district's public Drive shares begin in 2022 and hold nothing for this session (MAP.md section 59, checked September 26, 2026). That is a limit of what the district posts online, not evidence that no record was kept: at this session the voters approved the minutes of the 2017 session (0:06:24 to 0:06:46), and the 2019 session approved these (see the February 6, 2019 page). The warrant, the default budget form and the rules handout the moderator read from (0:04:33) are also not online, so the article texts and figures on this page are as read aloud and transcribed. Graded an observation under the project's rule for missing records, consistent with the 2019 page.

Sources: RSA 197:20 (1963, 120:5; unamended).

OBSERVATION The first secret ballot was put and declared using the unamended figure

The amendment on the floor would raise Article 2 "from $34,613,257 to $34,920,410" (0:35:14). When the moderator put it to the secret ballot she described it as a vote "to amend article two to the amount of $34,613,257", adding that it "would be adding $307,153 to the budget" (1:15:21), and she declared the result the same way: "to amend article two the main budget two $34,613,257 passes 99 in favor, 43 against" (1:16:10). $34,613,257 is the figure the article already carried. The stated effect, adding $307,153, was right, and the moderator later declared Article 2 to the ballot at the correct amended total of $34,920,410 (1:39:17), so voters who followed the whole session were not misled about the outcome. A ballot question that names the wrong total is still worth noting, because the question on the paper, which is not online, is what each voter answered. No statute is relied on; the transcript may also have garbled the figure.

Sources: the recording at 0:35:14, 1:15:21, 1:16:10 and 1:39:17. No statute relied on.

OBSERVATION The technology amendment failed by one vote after repeated counts

The $175,000 amendment failed 66 to 67 on a secret ballot (1:38:49, 1:39:05); the moderator said the ballots were counted "Several times. Not just twice, but several." RSA 40:4-a, which RSA 40:13, IV makes applicable to the first session, sets the secret-ballot procedure used here and also provides for a recount on the request of 5 voters when the margin is 10 percent or less; a one-vote margin qualifies. No recount request is heard, and the moderator moved directly to the article's final figure (1:39:17). The repeated counting is to the moderator's credit; the point recorded here is only that the statutory recount route existed and was not used, and whether anyone was told of it is not on the recording.

Sources: RSA 40:4-a (secret ballot and recount; source note ends 2006, 117:1, so the current text was in force in 2018); RSA 40:13, IV (2017 codification).

OBSERVATION Voice votes were taken on Articles 3 to 5, though every article goes to the ballot regardless

The moderator's opening statement of the rules said articles do not require a motion and are "automatically presented" to the ballot as written or amended (0:04:33). RSA 40:13, VI, as then in force, provides: "All warrant articles shall be placed on the official ballot for a final vote, including warrant articles as amended by the first session." She nonetheless called voice votes on Articles 3, 4 and 5 and declared Article 3 Passes. (1:54:20, 1:54:55, 1:57:28, 2:03:23). No amendment was offered to those articles, so nothing turned on the votes, but a voter could take them as having some effect. The same practice appears on the 2019 page. Noted only.

Sources: RSA 40:13, VI (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, so the text in force on this date).

OBSERVATION The superintendent and district staff spoke in favor of the technology amendment

After answering a tax-rate question (1:25:14), the superintendent argued for the $175,000 amendment: "My concern is that you don't keep you to reinvest, invest in technology" (1:25:30) and "we can save money by not investing just like that" (1:26:12). A middle school staff member also spoke in favor, saying "I work at the middle school" (1:29:33). RSA 659:44-a, in force from January 1, 2017, provides that "No public employee, as defined in RSA 273-A:1, IX, shall electioneer while in the performance of his or her official duties", and defines electioneering as acting "in any way specifically designed to influence the vote of a voter on any question or office". Two things cut against applying it. RSA 40:13, IV makes the first session one of "explanation, discussion, and debate of each warrant article", and the superintendent was there in that role; and the staff member spoke from the floor as a voter. Whether any of these remarks was "in the performance of" official duties and "specifically designed to influence the vote" rather than to explain it is not settled by the record or by any source this page verified. Elected board members presenting their articles fall outside the statute (RSA 273-A:1, IX(a)). Recorded for the reader, as on the 2019 page, not as a finding.

Sources: RSA 659:44-a, I and III (source note ends 2016, 176:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017); RSA 40:13, IV (2017 codification).

POSITIVE A voters' secret-ballot petition was honored, rules were read, last year's minutes approved, reconsideration restricted, and the session held inside its window

The moderator read the rules, including written amendments and time limits (0:04:33 to 0:06:17), and put the 2017 session's minutes to the voters (0:06:24). A written request from five registered voters for secret ballots on Article 2 and its amendments was read at 0:34:12 and honored for both amendments (1:14:34, 1:37:11); RSA 40:4-a lets 5 voters present require a secret yes-no ballot by a written request made before the vote, and RSA 40:13, IV applies it to the first session. Reconsideration of Article 2 was restricted by vote once amendments were finished (1:39:57); RSA 40:10, I allows a meeting "at any time during a meeting, and without notice in the warrant" to "restrict reconsideration of any one or more votes previously taken at that meeting", and ¶V applies it to school district meetings. An earlier attempt at 1:16:35 was set aside when an adviser pointed out that further amendments could still be offered, which protected the voters' right to amend. RSA 40:13, III, as then in force, placed the first session "between the first and second Saturdays following the last Monday in January, inclusive of those Saturdays"; with January 29 the last Monday, the window was February 3 to 10, and February 8 falls inside it.

Sources: RSA 40:4-a (source note ends 2006, 117:1); RSA 40:10, I and V (1996, 64:1; unamended); RSA 40:13, III and IV (2017 codification).
