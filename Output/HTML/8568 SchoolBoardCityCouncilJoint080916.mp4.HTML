OBSERVATION The school board gave its chair authority to sign a schedule agreement whose final wording did not yet exist, in a meeting of about one minute

Six days after the August 3 meeting tabled the Stevens High School schedule question (see the August 3, 2016 page), the chair called a separate school board meeting to order at 0:00:00, explained that "we're still waiting on some final wording" and that he had not heard whether the teachers had approved, and Irish moved to "give the chairman authority to sign the agreement when it comes to pass with the teachers." No second is audible, and the vote is a voice vote with no audible dissent (0:00:47). The board then adjourned. Nothing on the recording states the agreement's terms, whether it took the tabled motion off the table, or whether it carried a cost.

No statute identified requires a school board to approve the final text of such an agreement itself, so this is an observation. It matters for a reader because the August 3 debate turned partly on money (the superintendent on show 8558 spoke of "the 8% additional compensation of $157,000" for high school teachers under the A/B schedule): if the signed agreement contained a cost item, RSA 273-A:3, II(b) sends cost items to the legislative body. The agreement and the school board's own minutes of this meeting are not posted anywhere this project could reach. On the joint side, the subcommittees were likewise formed by consensus, with leads named but no vote of either body (1:18:06, 2:05:57).

Sources: RSA 273-A:3, II(b) (source note ends 2013, 244:1, so in force in 2016): only cost items go to the legislative body; August 3, 2016 page.

POSITIVE Members and a citizen put the new subcommittees' notice and minutes duties on the record, and a suggestion that they would be non-meetings was rejected

Before adjourning, Councilor Pope asked whether the subcommittees "need to be noticed" (2:07:11) and was told "Yes. That's correct." Irish then suggested that because none would include a quorum of either full body, "That will be the non meeting" (2:07:28); the mayor answered "No" and "It has to be." Bernie Fulton closed the public comment by reminding both bodies that "subcommittees of public bodies are public", with minutes and notice (2:08:38). That is the rule as it stood in 2016: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) made "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto" a public body, so a quorum of the subcommittee itself triggers RSA 91-A:2, II notice and minutes. Whether the joint subcommittees then met with notice and minutes is not in any record this project could inspect; the November 15, 2016 joint meeting page is the next point in the story.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (source note ends 2008, 354:1, so the same text governed in 2016); RSA 91-A:2, II (2015 codification, in force through December 31, 2016): 24-hour notice; minutes within 5 business days.
