OBSERVATION The chair was authorized to sign a schedule agreement with the teachers before its final wording existed

Six days after the board tabled the Stevens schedule question to a special meeting (August 3, 2016 page), the chair opened a short school board session and reported "we're trying to hammer out an agreement with the scheduling with Stevens, and we're still waiting on some final wording", adding that he had not heard whether the teachers had approved it (0:00:09). Irish moved "that we give the chairman authority to sign the agreement when it comes to pass with the teachers" (0:00:38); it passed by voice vote with no further discussion and the board adjourned (0:00:47, 0:01:04).

No rule this page verified forbids a board from delegating signature on a pending agreement. Three points for a reviewer: the board approved a text it had not seen, on the schedule it had debated for more than an hour and a half six days earlier; no roll call or attendance was taken for this session on tape (the joint roll call follows it, 0:01:07); and whether the session was noticed separately from the joint meeting cannot be checked without the notice. The terms of the agreement, and whether the chair signed it, are not in this record.

Sources: Cablecast: JOINT MEETING OF CITY COUNCIL & SCHOOL BOARD 8-9-16

POSITIVE Members recognized that the new joint subcommittees are public bodies that must be noticed

The two bodies agreed to form subcommittees on targeted vocational training, marketing and poverty (1:18:06, 2:06:05, 2:06:25). Councilor Pope raised notice: "when you were actually having an official meeting, I believe they need to be noticed" (2:07:11). Irish suggested that without a quorum of either board a subcommittee session would be "the non meeting"; the mayor answered "Fold? No." and then "It has to be." (2:07:42), and public commenter Bernie Fulton added that "subcommittees of public bodies are public" (2:08:38).

That is the law as it stood: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) extends the definition of a public body to "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", whatever the number of board members on it. Members correcting the point on the record, before any subcommittee met, is the kind of practice that keeps later meetings lawful. The chair also offered to share board packets with the council (1:51:21).

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (2015 codification; source note ends 2008, 354:1)
