Claremont School Board, September 17, 2025

Board meeting with the chair absent and Vice Chair Michael Petrin presiding, a week after the board tabled the administration's closure plan. The elementary principals withdrew the K–2 / 3–5 plan for lack of bus drivers and presented two alternatives; after about 55 minutes of public comment, most of it for keeping Bluff open, the board voted 4–1 by show of hands to move Bluff's classes intact, with their teachers and paraprofessionals, into Disnard and Maple Avenue, targeting October 20. The board also named two members to approve payment manifests, adopted eleven policies, and heard the names of five applicants for its vacant seat. Interim Superintendent Kerry Kennedy appeared for the first time. The page is built from the CCTV recording's dialogue transcript, the agenda and packet, and the draft and approved minutes. MEDIUM A 6:00 p.m. nonpublic session was noticed, and no record shows whether it was held

The agenda opens with "I . 6:00 PM Non-Public Meeting Session: RSA 91-A:3,I & II" followed by the text of exemption II(a). The recording begins with the 6:30 call to order (0:00:07) and never mentions a nonpublic session, and neither the draft nor the approved minutes records one: no motion, no roll call, no times, no nonpublic minutes, and no statement that it did not take place. If it was held, RSA 91-A:3, I(b) required a motion in public session stating the exemption and a roll-call vote, and RSA 91-A:3, III required minutes and disclosure of decisions within 72 hours unless sealed; if it was not held, the record should say so. The agenda's citation of paragraph I, which contains no exemptions, repeats a defect this project has recorded in other Claremont paperwork. Graded MEDIUM because the record cannot show whether a violation occurred, only that the session is unaccounted for.

MEDIUM The minutes reached Drive 47 days after the meeting and omit the start time, end time and author that the law has required since August 22, 2025

RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes open to inspection "not more than 5 business days after the meeting", which for this Wednesday meeting was September 24, 2025, and since 2025, 112:1 requires "the start time and end time of the meeting, and name of the person who produced the minutes." The draft, 08. draft CSB meeting minutes 9.17.25.pdf, was created on Drive on November 3, 2025; the approved Google Doc on January 3, 2026. Neither gives a start or end time or an author. The draft renders the principals as "Melissa Lewis (MARY LEWIS), Mark Blood, Bluff Principal (name unintelligible)", which suggests it was written from the recording; the approved version corrects the names. The minutes name no public speaker, summarizing about 55 minutes of comment in one sentence; RSA 91-A:2, II requires the names of "persons appearing before the public bodies", and whether that reaches citizens' comment is not settled by the text. Mitigation on the record: the clerk was absent (0:00:55), and the September 10 draft was posted the next morning, so the delay is specific to the clerk's absence. Graded MEDIUM, not HIGH, because the minutes exist, record movers, seconders and the names on the split vote, and were approved; the five-day deadline was nevertheless missed by about six weeks.

MEDIUM The Policy subcommittee met the afternoon of the meeting; no notice or minutes of that session were found

Hawkins: "The policy subcommittee met this afternoon" (2:10:58), and the board then adopted eleven policies on her motion. A subcommittee of a school board is a public body under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which ends "or any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto", so it needed 24 hours' notice and minutes under RSA 91-A:2, II. No notice or minutes were found in the September 17 packet or by Drive search; a negative from search alone does not prove none exists. Graded MEDIUM because the subcommittee's product, the policies, was presented and adopted in public session, and the policies themselves had been in members' packets the week before.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (subcommittees are public bodies); RSA 91-A:2, II (notice and minutes)

POSITIVE The board restored member sign-off on payment manifests

Angell told the board that the manifest review he considers standard "didn't actually happen" and that "there are no members that are designated" (2:02:25, 2:03:00).
