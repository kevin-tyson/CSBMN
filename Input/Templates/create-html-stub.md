The Claremont Schoolboard Meeting Navigator is an experimental service.

The goal of CSBMN is to provide an interface that integrates meeting videos, minutes, agendas, and other associated documents. The video is sourced from the CCMC Video On Demand (VOD) service. The documents are sourced from the board's Google Drive. AI is used to create webpages that group the video and the documents for a specific meeting and to analyze and summarize the discussion and the document contents. 

-   Extract transcript from the video using Adobe Premier
-   Transform the transcript into a dialogue using the transcript-to-dialogue skill *NB Names are extracted from the transcript using lexical and liguistic techniques and hence cannot be interpreted as authoratative.*
-   Find the publicaly accessible URLs for the video and the documents.
-   Read the dialog, agenda, minutes, etc.
-   Create a meeting description page with links to the places in the video where specific topics are discussed. Flag any items of conern given current state and federal legislation and regulation.
