Claremont School Board — August 21, 2024

The first Claremont School Board meeting this project has a recording of since 20 June. The meeting immediately before it, the full regular session of 7 August 2024, has draft minutes and a packet folder but no recording, no Cablecast show and no section in this project’s map. The consent agenda here approves those minutes. On the night, the board learned that a newly created administrative post had already been offered and accepted before the board created it, two weeks after the district’s own attorney had told this board, at the meeting there is no recording of, what that meeting’s draft minutes record as “the Board has to approve any new positions, even if it is within the budget”. It then entered nonpublic session under RSA 91-A:3, II(j), a ground available only in an adjudicative proceeding under RSA 541 or RSA 541-A and one that the board’s own by-laws do not list, on a motion that stated no exemption at all, declined to seal the minutes, and left no nonpublic minutes in any district share this project has searched. It elected thirty-one teachers and approved a job description that are in no public packet; voted $1,500 out of a budget line nobody named; changed a regular meeting date in perpetuity by voice vote; and adjourned with the Unity-withdrawal item, headed “Discussion and Vote”, undone for want of time.
