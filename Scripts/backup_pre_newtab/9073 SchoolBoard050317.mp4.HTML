OBSERVATION The bargaining subcommittee was formed by volunteering, without a vote on the record

Asked for "representation from the Claremont School Board who will serve on the collective bargaining subcommittee" (2:40:33), the chair volunteered and Sprague was named (2:41:18, 2:41:33); no motion or vote followed. A subcommittee of the board is itself a public body. The law in force, however, excluded "Strategy or negotiations with respect to collective bargaining" from the definition of a meeting, so the subcommittee's negotiating sessions with the association were not meetings requiring notice and minutes. The observation is limited to the formation: the record of who was appointed, and by what act, would be in the minutes, which are not online.

Sources: RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d) (source note ends 2008, 354:1, so in force throughout the corpus): a public body includes "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto"; RSA 91-A:2, I(a) (2016 codification, text in force during 2017): exclusion for "Strategy or negotiations with respect to collective bargaining".

POSITIVE A wrong weekday in the published notice of the May 11 SAU 6 board meeting was caught and a correction requested
