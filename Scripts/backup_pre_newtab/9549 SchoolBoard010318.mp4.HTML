Claremont School Board (part 1 of 2) — January 3, 2018

Part 1 of the regular meeting held after the FY2019 budget public hearing, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. After the hearing the board reconsidered its December 20 budget of $31,148,256.95, debated whether to fold in the $307,153 teachers' contract, and rejected Alex Herzog's amendment to $31,455,410 on a 3-4 roll call; the motion to send $31,148,256.95 to the deliberative session was made here and voted in part 2. OBSERVATION The teachers' contract was taken up in a closed session on December 20, and no recording shows where the ratification vote was taken

The speaker the dialogue file takes to be LeClair says the agreement "came to you in a non public meeting" after the December 20 board meeting and was "one of two items discussed in that" (0:12:51). The December 20 recording (show 9507) ends with a roll-call vote to leave public session in which the superintendent's citation is transcribed as 91 a two Roman numeral one, a, that is RSA 91-A:2, I(a); at the budget hearing (show 9547) the superintendent said the board approved and ratified the agreement on December 20. RSA 91-A:2, I(a), as it read in 2018, takes only "Strategy or negotiations with respect to collective bargaining" outside the definition of a meeting, and RSA 91-A:3, II lists no collective-bargaining ground for a nonpublic session. A vote to ratify is neither strategy nor negotiation, so it belongs in public session. Nothing on any recording shows the vote itself: the board may have returned to public session and voted after the December 20 recording stopped. The December 20 minutes, approved at 0:07:42, would settle it but are not online. Recorded as an observation because the record is incomplete, not because a lapse is shown.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I(a) (2017 codification; source note ends 2017, 165:1 and 234:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2018); RSA 91-A:3, I(b)-(c), II (2017 codification; source note ends 2016, 30:1 and 280:1); the Claremont School Board recordings of December 20, 2017 and the January 3, 2018 hearing (shows 9507 and 9547).

OBSERVATION Officials said a court decision requires Claremont to carry the teachers' contract inside the operating budget; the decision is not identified

O'Neill told the board the district is "one of the only, if not the only district" required to put the teachers' contract in the operating budget, and Rapp attributed this to "a Supreme Court decision" (0:13:19, 0:14:39). That is why the hearing figure rose by $307,153 and why the board had to choose between absorbing the contract and adding it. RSA 273-A:3, II(b), unchanged since 2013, provides that "Only cost items shall be submitted to the legislative body" for approval; the statute requires the voters to approve the cost items, and this recording does not show how Claremont's placement of them inside the operating-budget article squares with the usual separate-article practice, or which court decision officials meant. This page does not verify either claim. Readers comparing Claremont's budget votes with other districts' should know that, by the officials' own account, the bargained raise and the operating budget rise or fall together here.

Sources: RSA 273-A:3, II(b) (current text; source note shows the paragraph last amended 2013, 244:1, eff. Sept. 22, 2013, so the same text governed in 2018).

OBSERVATION The nonpublic session held before the hearing is not on the recordings: its motion, stated exemption and roll call cannot be checked

The chair opens this recording by asking for "a motion to come out of nonpublic, which we had before our budget hearing" (0:00:08), and the exit passes by voice. The motion that opened the session is on neither this recording nor the hearing recording (show 9547 begins at the hearing's call to order). In 2018 RSA 91-A:3, I(b) required the motion to "state on its face the specific exemption under paragraph II" and a roll-call vote, and 91-A:3, III required the minutes and decisions of the session to be disclosed within 72 hours unless sealed by a recorded two-thirds vote. None of that can be tested from the district's online record; the minutes of this meeting were kept (approved January 31) but are not posted.

Sources: RSA 91-A:3, I(b), III (2017 codification; in force for all of 2018).

OBSERVATION Minutes were kept and corrected on the record, but none for this period are online

The board approved its December 20 minutes after correcting who called the question (0:07:13, 0:07:42), and on January 31 approved this meeting's minutes with a correction. RSA 91-A:2, II as it read in 2018 required minutes naming members and "persons appearing before the public bodies", with "a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions", open to inspection "not more than 5 business days after the meeting" (the mover-and-seconder requirement came only on January 1, 2019). The recording shows the minutes existed, so nothing here suggests a lapse; but neither district Drive share reaches back before 2022, and the minutes for this meeting could not be located online (MAP.md section 55). Graded on the project's online-availability limb. The clerk's repeated difficulty hearing motions (0:06:18, 1:02:34) is noted because it bears on the accuracy of those minutes.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification; the text in force for all of 2018).

POSITIVE The budget was revisited in public after the hearing, and each member's vote was recorded by roll call

RSA 32:5, I, as it read in January 2018, required the governing body to hold its hearing and "after the conclusion of public testimony shall finalize the budget to be submitted to the legislative body"; paragraph II barred inserting an amount or purpose not disclosed at the hearing. The board did exactly the first: it reopened the figure by a public motion to reconsider (0:11:30), debated it for most of an hour, and took roll calls on closing debate and on the amendment (1:02:58, 1:04:02), so every member's position is on the record. The only proposed increase, the $307,153 contract, had been presented at the hearing, as O'Neill notes at 0:13:19. Whatever one thinks of the outcome, the process met and exceeded the statute's minimum.

Sources: RSA 32:5, I-II (2017 codification; source note ends 2014, 190:7, in force until Aug. 11, 2018).
