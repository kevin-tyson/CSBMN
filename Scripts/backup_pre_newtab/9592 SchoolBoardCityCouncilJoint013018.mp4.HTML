Claremont School Board & City Council, Joint Meeting — January 30, 2018

Quarterly joint meeting of the Claremont School Board and the Claremont City Council, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares, and the city's records were not inspected. The bodies heard a Kimball Union Academy proposal to test pets for lead, announced the governor's February 8 signing of SB 247 in Claremont, agreed by consensus to make the community's online presence a joint priority, asked for costs of an in-town program for out-of-district day students by April 17, and heard the mayor's plan for joint letters on HB 413 and HB 525. OBSERVATION The bodies adopted priorities, gave staff direction and approved joint letters by consensus, without a motion or vote

Every outcome of the evening was reached by consensus: support for the lead project (0:23:51), online presence as a joint priority with a plan requested from the superintendent and city manager (1:11:13), cost analyses for April 17, and joint letters on HB 413 and HB 525 to be drafted by the mayor and signed by the city manager, the superintendent, the board chair and the mayor (1:58:53, 1:59:19). RSA 91-A:2, II requires minutes to record the "final decisions" of a meeting; whether a consensus that directs staff or authorizes a letter in both bodies' names is such a decision is not settled by the statute's text, and no rule verified for this page requires a formal vote. The observation is that the record of what the two bodies committed to exists only on this tape.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, II (2017 codification).
