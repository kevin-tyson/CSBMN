SAU 6 Board — November 14, 2024

The joint Claremont–Unity SAU #6 School Board's forty-eight-minute November meeting, generated from the recording's dialogue transcript, the ten-document posted packet and the draft minutes. Four things make this night worth reading. The board carried out the sealed-minutes review its own new policy required and released eight sets of nonpublic minutes by a public vote, and left off the list the one seal whose stated term had already run out. It handed the superintendent the authority to set next year's staff raises within a band, a power RSA 194-C:5, III gives to this board in the word shall. It adopted six policies at a single first reading, every one of them still carrying the sample publisher's instruction to delete its own drafting notes before adoption, two of them with the names of responsible officers left as blanks. And when the recording stopped, a second board meeting began in the same room, before five of the same people, with no camera, no packet and forty minutes of nonpublic session whose minutes have never been produced. Body
SAU #6 School Board: the joint board of the Claremont and Unity school districts, twelve seats as printed on the agenda masthead (seven Claremont, five Unity). It is a "public body" under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which names school administrative units expressly, together with "any committee, subcommittee, or subordinate body thereof, or advisory committee thereto". RSA 194-C:5, III places in this board the power to "fix the salaries of all school administrative unit personnel" and the authority to "remove superintendents and other administrators". It is a different body from the Claremont School Board, which met separately later the same evening; see flag 3.
