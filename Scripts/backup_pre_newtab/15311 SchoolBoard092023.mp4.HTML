Claremont School Board — September 20, 2023

Regular meeting summary generated from the meeting recording, the five-document posted packet and the official minutes. Eighty minutes, five of seven members, the chair and the policy chair both away and the vice chair in the chair. The substance is a twelve-minute public walkthrough of how Claremont's school tax rate is built: appropriations, revenues, net assessment, adequacy aid and the statewide education tax, every figure of which ties to the packet and to the statutory formula. Around it: a nonpublic session entered on a citation that names the wrong title and the wrong paragraph and sealed for ten years with no stated finding; a newspaper story about a payroll that failed and technical-centre bills that went unpaid, answered in two minutes and closed with no motion; the first read of an employee-rights policy; a listening session on the state's minimum standards postponed out of the window in which the board could have influenced them; and the previous meeting's minutes postponed for want of a draft: minutes that, three years on, do not exist.
