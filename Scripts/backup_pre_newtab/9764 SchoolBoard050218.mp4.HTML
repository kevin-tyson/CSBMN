Claremont School Board — May 2, 2018

Regular meeting of the Claremont School Board, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares, and the recording begins partway through the superintendent's report. Vice chair Rebecca Zullo presided and announced new citizens'-comment rules adopted at the board's April 22 retreat. Most of the meeting was a long debate on Steven Horsky's proposed no nit head lice procedure, with four members of the public speaking, ending in a motion to open the policy for review. The business administrator presented a shortened expenditure report projecting a balance of about $500,000, and the board accepted a $1,000 grant for field trips.
