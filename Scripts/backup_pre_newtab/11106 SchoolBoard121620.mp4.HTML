Claremont School Board — December 16, 2020

Regular meeting summary generated from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. A hybrid meeting held during the COVID-19 state of emergency: the board corrected its December 2 minutes to show a motion passing 4 to 3, heard a report on the Goshen anchor-school tuition agreement, and debated an administration proposal to move Stevens High School fully remote for the third quarter. The chair's motion to support the proposal went to a roll call whose answers and result are not on the recording. Timestamps link to the same moment in the Cablecast recording.

OBSERVATION A remote member said she had been sending written notes to the board during the deliberation

Carolyn Towle, taking part remotely, said at 1:29:31: "I've been sending in notes as we've been discussing, I, you know, I figured everybody was reading them". The recording does not show where the notes went (the meeting's public chat, which viewers could see, or messages to members only) or what they said. Written contributions that members read but the public cannot are part of the deliberation the public is entitled to follow: RSA 91-A:2, III(c) as it read in 2020 required that "Each part of a meeting required to be open to the public shall be audible or otherwise discernable to the public at the location specified in the meeting notice", and the same subparagraph provides that "No meeting shall be conducted by electronic mail or any other form of communication that does not permit the public to hear, read, or otherwise discern meeting discussion contemporaneously at the meeting location specified in the meeting notice." If the notes were in the public chat, no issue arises; if they went to members privately, they were not discernible to the public. Neither can be determined from the available records.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, III(c) (2019 codification; source note ends 2018, 244:1, in force until 2023, 188:1): each part of a public meeting "audible or otherwise discernable to the public"; dialogue row at 1:29:31.

OBSERVATION Board members heard staff at a same-day roundtable capped at three members, and that input shaped the debate

The superintendent reported "roundtable forums with, with folks, with staff members" that afternoon, attended by the chair and other board members, and explained "We can only have three." (0:40:02). The chair (1:06:32), Petrin (1:09:39) and the superintendent (0:46:18) then drew on what was said there. RSA 91-A:2, I defines a meeting as "the convening of a quorum of the membership of a public body"; three members of a seven-member board is not a quorum, so on the superintendent's account the roundtable was not a meeting and needed no notice or minutes. The cap shows awareness of the rule. The point for a reader is that the staff views that shaped the Stevens debate were given in a session the public could not attend and that left no public record.

Sources: RSA 91-A:2, I (2019 codification, in force until 2023, 188:1): "meeting" is the convening of a quorum; dialogue rows at 0:39:28 and 0:40:02.
