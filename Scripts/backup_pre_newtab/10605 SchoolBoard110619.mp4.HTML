Claremont School Board — November 6, 2019

Public hearing and regular meeting, summarized from the Claremont Community Television recording and its speaker-attributed transcript; no agenda, packet or minutes for this meeting are posted in the district's online shares. The board held a hearing on a warrant article asking voters to put $650,000 of restored state aid into a special education expendable trust fund, then sent the article to the November 21 special district meeting, cut its regular schedule to one meeting a month on a 4-2 vote, and accepted the gift of an organ. MEDIUM The district's counsel later said no notice of this hearing was posted on the SAU 6 website, which the special-meeting statute names as a required posting place

This hearing was held under RSA 197:3-a, the statute for a special meeting on changed state education aid. As it read in 2019, paragraph IV required the hearing "at least 14 days prior" to the meeting, with notice of its "time, place, and subject" posted in at least two public places, "one of which shall be on the school district's website, if such exists, at least 7 days prior to the hearing." The 14-day rule was met (November 6 to November 21). The website rule is disputed: four weeks later, on December 4, the chair read a statement from the district's attorney saying that "no notice of the public hearing held on November 6th, 2019 was posted on the Saw six website." The chair acknowledged at 0:00:24 that the process was "a little bit rushed" because the board learned of the deadline only at its previous meeting.

Why MEDIUM and not HIGH: the defect is mitigated on the record. Counsel argued on November 20 that the SAU 6 site is not a Claremont School District website, so the "if such exists" clause may not have been triggered, and the board then used the curative procedure of RSA 40:16 and RSA 31:5-b, holding a second special meeting on December 12, 2019. Whether notice was posted in two physical places is not in any available record.

Sources: RSA 197:3-a, IV (2019 codification; source 2013, 197:1, unamended): hearing 14 days before the meeting, notice on the district website "if such exists" 7 days before the hearing; RSA 40:16 (2019 codification): special meeting to cure procedural defects.

OBSERVATION Food service reimbursement claims were missed for months over two prior years and could not yet be filed this year

At 1:06:11 the business administrator said the state could not accept the district's food service application because of a state IT problem, so "we have not submitted as of yet any of our reimbursements". In the exchange that follows, which the dialogue file leaves unidentified, someone confirms: "We missed the reimbursement a month period, a period of months over two years, because that's a monthly report." On November 20 he reported the application accepted and claims submitted for September and October. The amount of reimbursement lost in the earlier years is not stated. This page did not verify the federal claim-filing deadline, so it records the point as an observation of lost revenue and control weakness rather than a rule violation.
