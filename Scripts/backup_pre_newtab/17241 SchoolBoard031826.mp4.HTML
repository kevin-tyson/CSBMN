The organizational meeting eight days after the March 10 ballot: two new members, a new chair, a 3–3 vote that kept the vice chair, every standing subcommittee reconstituted, a by-laws item the minutes record as approved although no vote was taken, and the MS-22 signed at the only meeting before its filing deadline. Date

Seven at-large seats. POSITIVE The moderator vacancy was filled by the route state law provides, and the clerk stated that route correctly

RSA 197:26 lets the school board fill vacancies in district offices “except that of moderator”.
