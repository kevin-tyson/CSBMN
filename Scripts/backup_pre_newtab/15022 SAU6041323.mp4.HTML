Body
SAU #6 School Board: the joint board of the Claremont and Unity school districts. It is a "public body" under RSA 91-A:1-a, VI(d), which names school administrative units expressly. It employs the superintendent and the central-office staff and adopts the SAU budget; the schools themselves answer to the two district boards. Not the Claremont School Board, which met separately on April 5 and would meet again on April 19.
Board composition
Twelve seats as printed on the agenda masthead: seven Claremont (Horsky, Sprague, Miles, Skillen, Hawkins, Gallagher, Heather Whitney) and five Unity (Ruggeri, Erickson, Popescu, Simpson, Hart). Officers changed too: Steven Horsky replaced Marjorie Erickson as chair on March 30 by paper ballot, 6–5, and Rocco Ruggeri replaced Frank Sprague as vice chair the same night. Nine members were present; three Unity members were absent.
