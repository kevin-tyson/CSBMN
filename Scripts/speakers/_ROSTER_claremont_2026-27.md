## Diarization habits seen on Premiere transcripts
- One or two catch-all clusters absorb "Yes/Yeah/Right" interjections and sentence onsets. Map the whole cluster to Unidentified and override the clearly identified segments.
- Roll-call "Yes" votes come without the clerk's name reading, so the voter can't be identified.
- The clerk's roll call often lands in a catch-all cluster with members' 'here' answers; label the segment Noelle Kronberg and say so in Role.
- 9/2/26 (17566): a chair-recognized member's turn can land in another member's cluster (Petrin's parking remarks sat in Lavalette's). When the chair names someone and a different cluster answers, check the draft minutes before trusting the cluster.
