## A-II. Statutes cited for what they do not say, or that do not exist

**A2 · The phantom RSA chapter 92-A, in agendas, minutes and aloud** — *Class A · Systemic*
There is no RSA chapter 92-A; Title VI runs 91-A, 91-B, 91-C, 92, 93-A, and chapter 92 is
"Tenure and Oath of Office in Certain Cases". The 12/14/23 SAU 6 agenda cites it; the 1/11/24
agenda repeats it and **the minutes repeat it too**, in a sentence the clerk composed —
"motioned to move into non-public under RSA 92-A:3,II"; the 2/15/24 agenda and minutes carry it
again; and on show 15580 at 0:02:37 the chair **speaks it aloud, corrects herself once, and
lands on the wrong chapter both times**. The briefing directs that it be reported as a defective
citation, never silently repaired to 91-A. It stops in SAU 6 paperwork after 15 February 2024 —
that clearance covers nonpublic-session citations only.
Source: `html_briefing.md` §15d, §20, §22e. **Systemic** (three agendas, two sets of minutes, one spoken instance).

**A3 · Claremont by-laws Appendix D counts the nonpublic grounds four different ways** — *Class A · One-off document, standing effect*
Appendix D of the by-laws adopted 5 June 2024 says RSA 91-A:3 "sets forth eight grounds" in
prose, then **enumerates nine**, then calls them "the **nine** statutory bases" two paragraphs
later, then letters only **six** (a)–(f) in its compliance checklist — fusing II(d), II(e) and
II(g) into one item and continuing the same letter series g–o for procedural steps. The statute
has **twelve** live grounds, (a)–(m) with (f) repealed. Appendix D also states the 72-hour rule
and a nine-item minimum-content list for nonpublic minutes, citing policy BEDG.
Source: `html_briefing.md` §22e, §26b. **One-off** (one document, but governing from 5 June 2024).

## C-I. Legal analysis

**C1 · The mover/seconder clause was wrongly dated, which would have produced two opposite classes of error** — *Class C · Systemic, corrected 2026-08-29*
Addendum 10 stated that RSA 91-A:2, II's "names of the members who made or seconded each motion"
clause was **new — added 2023, 188:1 and amended 2025, 112:1** — and told agents not to apply it
before those dates. **That was wrong.** The clause has been in force since **2018, 244:1,
eff. January 1, 2019**: the 2017 codification lacks it, the 2019 codification carries it, and
re-verified three times in wave 1. What 2025, 112:1 added was the start-time/end-time and
minutes-producer requirements, which genuinely must not be applied before **22 August 2025**. Two
consequences of the error, both stated in the briefing: agents were about to **credit districts
with voluntary compliance for something the law required**, and to **miss real defects in
2019–2023 minutes that omit movers or seconders**.
Source: `briefing.md` Addendum 10; `html_briefing.md` §11; 2017 and 2019 Justia codifications. **Systemic, corrected.**

**C2 · RSA 32:5 paragraph numbers were wrong by one across shipped pages** — *Class C · Systemic, cleared*
Earlier pages placed the gross-basis requirement at ¶IV and the comparative columns at ¶V. The
verified mapping is: public hearing/25 days/7 days' notice/"shall finalize the budget" = **¶I**;
gross basis = **¶III**; comparative columns = **¶IV**; special warrant articles = ¶V. **All 40

**C3 · RSA 91-A:2, ¶I's lettering was wrong in the project's own guidance** — *Class C · One-off, corrected*

**(b)** consultation with legal counsel · **(c)** a caucus of members of the same political

and the substantive argument survives — **RSA 671:30** requires the non-partisan ballot system for
school-district officers (unamended since 1979, 321:1, eff. 21 Aug 1979), so a NH school board has
no party caucus to hold and the exemption is unavailable.
