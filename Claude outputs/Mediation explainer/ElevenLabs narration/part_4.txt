Why does this matter beyond one office? In Claremont, the voters act as the school district's legislature. In February of twenty twenty-six, residents debated the budget for four hours at the deliberative session. In March, they decided it by ballot. Each of those votes assumed that voters could find out what the district had done.

New Hampshire's constitution says government should be open, accessible, accountable and responsive, and that public access to its records shall not be unreasonably restricted. The Right-to-Know Law begins by calling openness essential to a democratic society.

In eighteen thirty-five, Alexis de Tocqueville wrote that town meetings are to liberty what primary schools are to science. They put self-government within reach. That worked when a farmer could know the town's business first-hand. A district with a budget near forty-three million dollars, and recordings back to twenty fifteen, is past that point.
