# Conflicts of interest and participation disclosure

This project reports on proceedings in which its maintainer has personally
participated, and it depends on an organization whose board he sat on. Both are
disclosed here rather than left for a reader to discover, because a transparency
project that is not transparent about itself has no standing to ask anything of
anyone else.

Every generated page carries its own maintainer disclosure at the point of use,
stating whether the maintainer appears in that meeting's record and, where he
does, on what terms. This file is the roll-up. It was compiled on 2026-09-01 by
searching all 127 dialogue CSVs, all 127 transcripts and all published pages for
the maintainer's name, and it should be regenerated the same way whenever new
meetings are processed.

Kevin Tyson, Ward 2, Claremont, New Hampshire. Sole maintainer of this

He holds no elected or appointed office in the Claremont School District, SAU 6
or the City of Claremont. This project is not funded by, run on behalf of, or

## Standing conflicts

### Claremont Community Television (CCTV) / Claremont Community Media Center (CCMC)

Serves on the **CCTV/CCMC Board of Directors**, and has since the City Council
appointed him to **Seat 3 on 13 July 2022**. The board roster in the district's
21 May 2025 packet shows that appointment's term expiring 31 May 2025; he has
continued to serve on the board since that date and serves on it now. CCTV and
CCMC are one organization under two names.

This is the most structurally significant item in this file. CCTV/CCMC produces
and publishes every recording in this corpus, and it publishes this project's
output as the Claremont School Board Meeting Navigator, an experimental CCTV/CCMC
service. Each page in the project links to a CCTV/CCMC recording and deep-links
into it by timestamp. For every meeting held since 13 July 2022, the maintainer
has sat on the board of the organization that made the record the page is built
from, and he sits on the board of the organization that publishes the pages.

*Corrected 29 September 2026.* Earlier versions of this file, of `ReadMe.md`, of
the Data Quality Report and of 37 meeting pages gave this board service as
ending on 31 May 2025. It did not end. Each of those statements now says that the
maintainer still serves on the board. Because the earlier end date took meetings
after May 2025 out of this disclosure, five pages for later meetings at which
CCTV/CCMC's own business came up (3, 10 and 12 December 2025; 5 and 12 August
2026) had no board disclosure; they now carry one. Pages covering meetings where
CCTV's own contract, funding or operations were before the school board carry an
expanded disclosure to that effect. See in particular the pages for 5 June 2024,
21 August 2024, 20 November 2024 and 18 March 2026.

### Education Options (EdOpt)

the record at the 1 October 2025 meeting and clarified his role at the 5 November

This is not an incidental fact. It became a subject of the board's own
deliberations: the chair relayed community emails questioning whether the

Pages reporting those meetings quote those objections in full and do not soften

## What this means for the reader

Where the maintainer is a participant, treat the page for that meeting with the
skepticism you would apply to any account written by someone in the room. Where
the meeting concerns CCTV, treat it with the skepticism you would apply to an
account written by a sitting director of the organization under discussion.

The mitigations, such as they are:

* The pipeline is automated and applies the same procedure to every meeting.
* Every characterization deep-links to the timestamp in the official recording,
  so a reader can check it against the source in one click.
* Objections raised against him on the record, including the EdOpt objections,
  are quoted in full on the pages that report them.
* The generation procedure is in this repository and can be audited and rerun.

These are mitigations, not cures. A reader who concludes that the pages covering
the vacancy proceedings of 2023 and 2025 should be read as an interested party's
account is drawing a reasonable conclusion, and this file exists so that they
can draw it with the facts in hand.

If this disclosure becomes incomplete, that is a defect in the project. Open an
issue.
