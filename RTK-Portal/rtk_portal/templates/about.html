How the portal works

The law

RSA 91-A:4, I gives every citizen the right to inspect and copy governmental records. When a record is not immediately available, the District has five business days to make it available, deny the request in writing with the specific exemption, or state in writing how much time it needs and why, with an itemized cost estimate if a charge applies (RSA 91-A:4, IV). Board Policy EH names the Superintendent of SAU 6 custodian of District records, and Procedure EH-R(1) sets the steps, fees and waivers. A business day here is a weekday that is not a New Hampshire legal holiday under RSA 288:1 and not a day the Superintendent's office has posted as closed.

The queue

The portal handles one request at a time. A request is open from the moment staff start on it until they send a response or close it, and no other request is taken up meanwhile.

The next request is chosen by round, then by time received. A request's round is set when it is verified: round 1 if its requester has no other request open, round 2 if they have one, and so on. Every round-1 request is handled before any round-2 request. So if you have several requests open, your second and later ones wait until other requesters' earlier requests are handled, and no single requester can fill the queue.

The statute's clock does not wait for the queue. When a waiting request nears its fifth business day, staff can send the five-business-day statement for it without changing its place.

What is published

  • Every verified request gets a public number, its time of receipt, its due date, its status and its round.
  • The request text is published once you verify, with phone numbers and email addresses removed. Text that may identify a student or another private person is held until staff publish a redacted copy.
  • Every written response the District sends through the portal is published with your email address removed, and your name removed unless you chose to show it.
  • Unverified requests are never published and are deleted after {{ settings.pending_ttl_hours }} hours.
  • The public record of events is hash-chained so that anyone can check it has not been altered.
