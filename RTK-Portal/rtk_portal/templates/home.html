What happens after you file

  1. You confirm your email. A six-digit code arrives by email. The request is filed, numbered and logged when you enter it.
  2. It joins the queue. Requests are handled one at a time, oldest first, except that a requester's second and later open requests wait until everyone else's earlier requests are handled.
  3. It is triaged. The portal checks the request against RSA 91-A, the Superintendent's procedures (Policy EH and EH-R(1)) and the public knowledge graph, and drafts a response.
  4. Staff review and send. A person at the Superintendent's office edits and approves every response. Nothing is sent automatically.
  5. Everything is published. The request, its dates and the response go in the public log. Your email address never does.

Read the full rules, including how the queue works, what is published, and the statutes behind each step.
